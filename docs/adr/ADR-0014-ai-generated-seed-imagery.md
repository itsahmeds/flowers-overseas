# ADR-0014 — Seed catalogue imagery is AI-generated under a locked style guide

## Context
84 seed products need consistent, rights-clean imagery before any florist exists, inside a Phase 0 budget of tens of euros and a five-week calendar. Competitor images are excluded (copyright and duplicate-content risk).

## Options considered
1. **Licensed stock** — €300–800; inconsistent styles; same images as competitors; not our bouquets.
2. **Lahore florist shoot** — €300–600 + 2–3 days; consistent and owned; European seasonal stock differs; still not the destination florist's work.
3. **AI-generated with a locked style guide** — ~€50–150; very consistent; owned outputs (generator terms checked); must be labelled as example arrangements and replaced by real delivery photos over time.

## Decision
Option 3 for the catalogue; option 2 kept as an optional small shoot for brand/hero imagery where AI output looks synthetic. Accepted recommended default.

## Consequences and the trade-off accepted
Easier: 84 consistent images in days; no licensing. Harder: a human review gate at import for impossible flowers; an honesty label on every PDP ("example arrangement, your florist hand-makes each one"); images are placeholders that real delivery photos must progressively displace.
