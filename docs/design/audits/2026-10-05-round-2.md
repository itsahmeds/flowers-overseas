# Design round 2 — every remaining page, the laptop band and the phone (2026-10-05)

**Why.** The founder, 2026-10-04: *"i asked to redesign all the pages not just a few"*, *"we will
redesign each page.. including the product page too... home page too"* and *"make sure the sizing of
page is good for both computer and phone.. cuz current pages on demo site feel big on my macbook"*.
On 2026-10-05, reviewing the first drawings of this round: *"the redesigned home page looks junior"*,
*"some pages look cluttered.. UI UX doesnt feel good and state of the art... the product page feels
cluttered too and mobile view?? ughh"*. Round 2 was redrawn to that bar: one 12-column grid, one job
per first screen, no tilt, at most one decorative device, large photographs, and a phone design of its
own.

**How it was checked.** Every artboard was rendered with Playwright at 1440 × 900, 1280 × 800,
1470 × 830, 1512 × 860 and 1512 × 945 (desktop) and at 390 × 844 and 360 × 640 (phone), with the
annotation hidden and the artboard set to the window's width, so the clause 9 fluid steps resolve as
they will in the browser. A DOM probe recorded the H1's box and computed size, the primary action's
box, the photograph's box, every marked left edge, horizontal overflow, and any text that touches or
overlaps a photograph. Each desktop artboard's **Laptop band** row prints those numbers. Before and
after PNGs are not committed (the scratchpad folder `design-r2/after/`).

   point at round 2. DOM order changed on "About us" (who runs it moves up).

   menu. A4 asked for them in a second notice-bar row; they stay reachable, but A4 needs a conforming
   line. The basket is not drawn (A20: no control without a page).

   the artboards keep 20.
6. **Spec 003 A16.** The popup is a top sheet on the phone (R10) where A16 says bottom sheet, and the
   highlight is browser language only where A16 keeps A14's country signal. Both need conforming lines.
7. **Spec 004 §14 A23** binds the PR 185 listing artboards, and clause 5 and AC-34 to AC-36 name the

   grid. **R8** keeps the ranking disclosure above the grid; the phone list puts it under the grid.
   **AC-32**'s breadcrumb becomes a back link on the phone.

    not-open line now sit above both columns.
