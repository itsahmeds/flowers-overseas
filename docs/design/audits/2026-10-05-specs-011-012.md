## The pattern

- **Florist (phone first).** The minimal header is the logo and one Menu (language, sign out). A
  thin demo line on every page. One H1, then the content, then a docked bar with the one action the
  order's state allows, 56 px tall. The answer-by time is the only blush on the screen; the payout
  is the only leaf-wash. Desktop puts the same screens on the 12-column grid: the list on columns
  2 to 11, the order on 2 to 7 with the action card sticky on 9 to 11.
- **Admin (desktop first).** A 232 px side bar (brand, environment chip, navigation for the role,
  the signed-in person), 14 px tables with 44 px rows, 36 px controls, `tabular-nums`, chips for
  status, a dashed Demo marker, poppy only for the screen's single primary action. At 390 the side
  bar becomes a top bar with Menu and every table becomes a three-line row list.
- **Public pages.** The round 2 chrome; a quiet cream head band (no ink band: that is the landing's);
  the walkthrough two by two on desktop, each screen beside its words.

## How it was checked

Rendered with Playwright, annotation markers hidden, each artboard set to the window's width, fonts
loaded. The probe checks horizontal overflow, text overlapping text (clipped to its phone frame),
text off screen, text clipped by its box, and empty vertical gaps over 120 px inside each drawn
screen (the first screen, every state screen, the state band).

**Result: 28 artboards at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800 (desktop) and 390 × 844 and 360 × 640 (phone),
56 renders, 0 findings.** Earlier runs found and this round fixed: a long URL in the annotation
pushing the 390 artboards to 536 px; the order assignments and the florist's zones tables
overflowing at 390 (now row lists); the delivery photo at 800 px tall on desktop (capped at 420 px
wide); phone-frame content overlapping the walkthrough text (frames now clip and the screen is a
360 px phone scaled with `zoom`); a 129 to 177 px gap above the footer on the apply and walkthrough
pages; the admin user's name pinned to the foot of the side bar (now under the navigation).

Each desktop annotation carries a **Laptop band** row (H1 size and position, primary action) at
1280 × 800, 1470 × 830, 1512 × 860 and 1512 × 945. The apply form's button sits below the fold on
every laptop by design (a 13-field form); everything else that has a primary action shows it in view.

Before/after images are not committed: every page is new, so the "before" is no page. The labelled
`NEW-PAGE-*.png` images (desktop and phone first screens side by side) are in the session scratchpad
`design-011-012/`.

## Decisions taken here (the founder can overrule any)

1. **No action panel on the admin order** (spec 012 §2.6, AC-19). The work order asked for buttons
   for the allowed transitions; the approved spec says v0 renders none and spec 020 adds the panel
   through `orderService.transition`. The artboard says "Changes to orders are not available in
   admin yet." The florist inbox does draw only the allowed transitions (spec 011 AC-29).
2. **"Florists", not "Partners", in admin.** Spec 012 §2.2 allows the internal words in admin, but
   the banned-word scan's `admin-*` exemption lands with TASK-217. Routes, tables and permissions
   keep their names in `<code>`; links write `{florists}` for the `partners` segment and `{base}`
   for `FLORIST_BASE_PATH`.
3. **Em dash removed** from Appendix A's "Delivered — add the photo": drawn as "Delivered, add the
   photo". Spec 011 Appendix A needs the same edit.
4. **Florist sign-in drawn at 1440 too** (Appendix B asked for the phone only; the work order wants
   both). On the phone the language links sit under the card so the header is the logo alone.
5. **The typed-key confirmation keeps its button enabled**; a mismatch is refused with a message
   (spec 004 §14 A20: no disabled control).
6. **"Make active" is absent, not disabled,** until the four conditions hold; the card lists which
   are missing.
7. **Payouts** (approver, 2026-10-05). The inbox and the demo florist's mapping show example
   figures, 149, 129, 189 and 219 zł, annotated as such: real payouts come from the florist's agreed
   price (the seed's mapping, TASK-026, then admin). 149 zł is also spec 011 §13 Q7's default. No
   blank bar is drawn: the founder rejected it on the for-florists card.
8. **Admin times** show in the viewer's `admin_profile.time_zone`, always named. Spec 012 §5.1
   makes it a per-user column with the default `Europe/Tallinn`, so "times in Europe/Tallinn" in the
   side bar is the drawn user's setting, not a constant. There is no screen to change it in v0
   (only the column and `pnpm admin:grant`); a later admin spec should add one.
9. **Two former markers drawn as values** (approver, 2026-10-05): the inbox stage chip "Being made"
   and the buyer projection of `no_partner_available`, "we're finding a florist". Both are new
   wording, founder copy pending.

## Escalations

1. **The landing was not redrawn.** Spec 011 Appendix B item 1 asks for the landing with the status
   line, the two-line payout example, the FAQ as visible headings and the guide links; the round 2
   `for-florists-*` (PR 189) predates spec 011 and draws a docket, "Apply to join" (Appendix A says
   "Apply to work with us") and draft copy. The work order kept it out of scope. TASK-210 builds the
   landing from it, so it needs one redraw pass, or the founder approves the round 2 version and
   Appendix A follows it.
2. **Three short sections or two.** Spec 011 §2 names three on the walkthrough (replacing a flower,
   declining, paid); Appendix A writes two and folds declining into step 2. Two are drawn.
3. **Stacked on PR 189.** This branch starts from the round 2 design branch; merge 189 first.
4. **The scan exemption for `admin-*`** (TASK-217) and the phone frame's `zoom` (TASK-210) are
   implementation notes, recorded on the artboards.
