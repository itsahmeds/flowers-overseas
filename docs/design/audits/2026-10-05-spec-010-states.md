## How it was checked

Each artboard was rendered with Playwright, with the annotation hidden and the fonts loaded: desktop
at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800, phone at 390 × 844 and 360 × 640. The probe checks five things:

- horizontal overflow;
- text overlapping text;
- text off screen;
- text clipped by its box;
- empty vertical gaps over 120 px inside the page, each state screen and the state band.

**Result: 8 artboards, 32 renders, 0 findings.**

The probe is the spec 011/012 probe with two refinements:

- **Clipped text.** Text that an ancestor clips on purpose is measured only where it shows. That covers the
  date and size rails that scroll sideways, the line-clamped description and the phone frames. A rail or a
  clamp is not reported as clipped.
- **Photographs.** A full-bleed photograph (`role="img"`) counts as drawn content, not as background.

On `origin/main`, the same probe found 45 findings across these eight files. Each was fixed:

- the checkout's mobile state band, three columns at 390 (the cards overlapped);
- the for-florists `.tag` class, which collided with the shared `.tag` and pushed "Example" off screen;
- gaps above the footer and between sections;
- the empty photo band on the live confirmation, now 16∶9;
- the product H1's line box over the price, now 10 px apart;
- 100 px of foot padding under the phone sheet, not 120.

Each desktop annotation's **Laptop band** row comes from this run. The product's "Try a demo order"
and the landing's "Apply to work with us" are in view on every laptop. The checkout's Continue is
below the fold by design: the step has eight inputs, the notice and the calendar, and the sticky
recap keeps the total in view.

## Decisions taken here (the founder can overrule any)

1. **The em dash is removed from two spec strings** (ruling R9, as the briefs already say).
   - "Place demo order — nothing is charged" is drawn "Place demo order. Nothing is charged." This is
     TASK-206's default.
   - "Continue — {total}" is drawn "Continue, £45.90".
   - The footer's support line is drawn without its em dash, as round 2 already draws it.
2. **"Try a demo order" is an ink outline button, not poppy.** It starts a rehearsal, not a purchase.
   The line under it keeps saying that ordering is not open. Poppy returns for the live "Continue".
3. **The demo banner is the round 2 band with its words replaced.** There is no "Demonstration" pill.
   The bold title does that work, and it adds no new string.
4. **The step names in the segmented control are new:** "Recipient", "Card and you", "Check". The H1s
   are Appendix A's.
5. **On desktop, step 3 puts the total, the VAT split, the currency line and the place button in the
   order recap.** The letter holds the summary. This avoids printing the total twice. On the phone the
   sticky bar carries the action.
6. **The daily cap replaces the button with a notice.** It is not a disabled button (spec 004 §14 A20).
7. **Add-on prices are a placeholder bar on step 2.** Spec 005's `addonPriceProjection` does not exist
   yet, and we print no number we do not have.
8. **The live confirmation is kept as the first screen, marked future.** The demo confirmation is a
   whole state screen below it. Phase 0 shows only the demo one.
9. **The landing's application form is removed.** It lives on `for-florists-apply-*`, as Appendix B
   item 1 asks ("the landing only").
