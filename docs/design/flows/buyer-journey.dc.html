Buyer journey · home → track · [internal]
Purpose
The Phase 0 buyer journey end to end, one thumbnail per screen, with the decision the visitor makes, the trust moment that carries them past it, and the data the screen reads. Read left to right, row by row.
The shape of it
Destination and date are asked once, on the home finder, and the answer travels in the URL (locale prefix, destination country segment) and one cookie (display currency). There is no IP redirect anywhere in this flow, ever (ADR-0006), and no step re-asks a question an earlier step answered.
Price identity
The all-in price shown on the product page — VAT and delivery included, with the date's surcharge already applied — is the same integer the checkout total and the payment charge use. There is no configuration of the flow in which a cost appears later than the tier chip that first showed a price (plan/07 §4, drip-pricing ban).
What is real in Phase 0
Steps 1–4 render. Step 5–7 are behind the checkout.demo_guard flag (on by default): the form renders, no order is placed and no card is charged. Steps 8–9 exist as designs for spec 010/015 and as the confirmation the demo shows a florist.
Owning specs
004 chrome and system · 007 country page and hub · 008 shop, category, occasion · 009 product page · 010 checkout · 015 order state and tracking
007 · what changed
Steps 01–03 are redrawn as the journey spec 007 creates: home finder → country page → (only when a florist exists) shop. Step 02 loses its waiting-list exit and gains region grouping and the two destination states; step 03 is drawn in its guide state, because that is the state every one of the seven destinations is in while hasActivePartners() answers false. The consequence is worth stating plainly: in Phase 0 the journey stops at step 03, and steps 04 onwards are reachable only behind the demo guard. That is the correct shape for a site that indexes guides and sells nothing yet.
Round 2 · what changed
Destination and date are settled before the basket, not after it: the finder asks for both on step 01, the grid on step 04 carries the date control above it and badges every card against the destination cutoff, and step 05 numbers the three decisions and prints the fee on every date chip. Steps 06–07 are data entry only — the total cannot move. The field is split on this and the half that defers the date (Interflora UK, Bloom & Wild) reveals “not available on that day” after the buyer has committed (benchmarks/00-summary.md, pattern 1).
