Consent and locale · [internal]
Purpose
The three surfaces of the first visit — the locale chooser, the language-suggestion dialog and the consent sheet — with their states and their order. They are drawn together because they are the only elements in the design that can want the screen at the same time, and because the order between them is the design (spec 003 §14 A14).
The rule they exist to keep
No IP redirect. Ever. (ADR-0006) A visitor’s language is never decided for them: / lists the locales, a language or country mismatch is a suggestion they answer once, and the chosen locale is in the URL. The country is read only by hints.ts, served only by a no-store /api/geo, and stored nowhere. Currency is a cookie; the destination country is in the shop URL.
Order
Sequence first, layers second: the language dialog is asked and answered, and only then is the consent sheet rendered (spec 003 §14 A14). The dialog is a modal <dialog>, so it is in the browser’s top layer and needs no z-index at all; the named scale still orders what is left — --layer-header 100 < --layer-banner 200 < --layer-overlay 300 (consent sheet, and the skip link when focused).
Copy status
chooser.*, suggestion.* and a11y.* exist in messages/en.json and are quoted verbatim below. There are no consent.* keys yet — every string on the consent sheet is marked [slot] and is owned by the spec that ships it.
Owning spec
003 (locales, chooser, suggestion dialog — §14 A14, TASK-119) · 004 (layers, chrome) · consent sheet and settings: not yet specified — see docs/design/README.md

—Z-order and first-visit sequence
layer-overlay · 300Consent sheet · the focused skip link. Dismissible, focus-trapped, above everything.
top layerLocale suggestion dialog. Pushes nothing (zero shift), holds the screen until it is answered, and is answered once.
layer-header · 100Sticky header. scroll-padding-block-start keeps a focused anchor target clear of it (WCAG 2.4.11).
pageEverything else. No component writes a raw z-index.
Land on / or a locale page→ Locale suggestion, if a language or country hint says another locale→ Consent sheet (first visit only), once the question above is answered→ The page, in the locale that is in the URL

The order is deliberate, and it changed on the founder’s ruling of 2026-09-16 (spec 003 §14 A14): the language question is asked first and the consent sheet waits for it, because a consent decision recorded in a language the visitor is about to leave reads as the site ignoring the answer — and because two sheets stacked at the bottom of a phone is the interstitial that costs ranking. The wait is a flag on <html> set during hydration and released the moment the dialog closes, and it fails open twice over: if the dialog never arrives, the sheet appears anyway. Neither surface may be the reason a crawler sees different content — both are additive to a fully server-rendered page.
