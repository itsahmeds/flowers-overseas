Purpose
How a Warsaw florist meets us, applies, and on a demo call signs in on staging and handles a first example order on their phone. Phase 0 exit signal 9 depends on it.
What changed (spec 011 Appendix B item 6)
Step 03 is the staging inbox reached through the one email sign-in (spec 011 §13 Q2, accepted): there is no per-order /v/{token} link. Step 04 drops the public-use consent question (§13 Q6: photos stay private until spec 029's recipient consent) and the substitution field (spec 027). The walkthrough has its own page now. “Not in Phase 0” names specs 026 and 027.
What is honest here
No count of florists, no testimonial, no shop logo. The payout example is two lines, the buyer's price from the catalogue and the agreed amount, labelled as one example. Every inbox order on staging is synthetic.
