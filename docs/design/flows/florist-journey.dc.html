Purpose
How a Warsaw florist meets us, applies, and on a demo call signs in on staging and handles a first example order on their phone. Phase 0 exit signal 9 depends on it.
What changed (spec 011 Appendix B item 6)
Step 03 is the staging inbox reached through the one email sign-in (spec 011 §13 Q2, accepted): there is no per-order /v/{token} link. Step 04 drops the public-use consent question (§13 Q6: photos stay private until spec 029's recipient consent) and the substitution field (spec 027). The walkthrough has its own page now. “Not in Phase 0” names specs 026 and 027.
What changed (spec 016, 2026-10-14)
Steps 06 to 11 add Phase 1: the offer by email with the one-tap link (and the founder's WhatsApp copy, offer and stop message only), the reminder and the timeout that re-offers the order to the next florist, the confirmation after capture, the stop message and the inbox's withdrawn stage, the photo reminder, and the founder's no-florist alert with the grace window. Templates: wireframes/email-*.
What is honest here
No count of florists, no testimonial, no shop logo. The status line is drawn as “We are opening country by country, starting in Poland.” (founder copy pending; it replaces spec 011's “choosing” line, which the founder's ruling bans). The payout example is two lines, the buyer's price from the catalogue and the agreed amount, labelled as one example. Every inbox order on staging is synthetic.
What changed (spec 026a, 2026-10-22)
Step 03 now happens on the live site at flowersoverseas.com/vendor: the inbox moved from /demo/vendor-inbox to /vendor in every environment, and a florist of an active or paused shop signs in on production. The founder grants access in admin by entering the florist's contact email and switching the shop to active; the florist page shows “Not signed in yet” or “First signed in on {date}”. Step 04 notes the paused shop (Decline only). No public link to /vendor.

Decision
Phone a florist and reroute to them (spec 020a's Reroute dialog calls manualReroute), cancel, or let the window run out. The admin side is drawn in flows/admin-order-actions.dc.html.
Grace window
Due today: 2 hours. Not due today: until 10:00 destination time the next morning (21:00 → 10:00; 06:00 → 10:00 the same day). Both end by the routing deadline (16-2). With routing.auto off, nothing cancels automatically.
Trust
The buyer has paid; a cancel refunds in full, and B5 says only “We could not deliver this order. We have refunded {amount}.”
