Until then the reason is a line of text, not a disabled button (spec 004 §14 A20). Desktop: the record on two thirds, the summary card sticky on the last third.

Spec 030b · the order’s Money panel · desktop 1440 · 2026-10-23 · [internal]
What changes
Spec 030b §2.8 and §5.3.1 item 3: /admin/orders/[id] gains a read-only Money panel for finance.view, placed in the main column after Payments. Everything drawn above is spec 012’s page with spec 020a’s Actions card; this block redraws none of it. The page above is a demo order, which takes no money, so it never shows the panel: the panel is drawn here on live sample orders.
Panel
Status chip (Final, Provisional or Rate missing) with each reason as text; the “Accountant to confirm” line naming the open questions (AC-18); the 11 lines of orderMargin (gross at ECB, FX cost, settled, refunds, VAT, net sale, florist cost, fees, disputes, margin, margin %), each converted figure with its rate, source and date (AC-21, AC-31); the balance transactions by provider reference. No control writes (AC-31).
Permission
finance.view (admin only). For ops, finance and content the panel and every figure in it are absent from the HTML, with no placeholder (AC-2, AC-31).
Reads
orderMoney(orderId): one order’s facts, its payment_balance_txn rows and the rates it needs.
States
Final (F1) · provisional, florist cost pending · provisional, fee pending and no florist · fee not available (Mollie) · rate missing · a refund and its VAT estimate · test order · absent for ops.
Data drawn
Sample figures. F1 is spec AC-13’s fixture exactly: 45.90 GBP, settled 53.02 EUR with fee 1.58, florist 149.00 PLN paid 27 Oct, margin 12.45 EUR, 25.36%.
