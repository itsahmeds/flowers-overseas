Amber Hour

229.00 PLNIncludes VAT and delivery

Orange roses hand-tied with eucalyptus and a few sprigs of seasonal foliage, wrapped in kraft paper. Eighteen stems as standard, with twelve and twenty-four stem sizes on the same page. Warm rather than loud, so it reads as well for a birthday as for a thank you. The roses are always in it; the greenery and filler stems follow the morning market. Every order will be made by hand and delivered in person by our florist in the recipient's city.
