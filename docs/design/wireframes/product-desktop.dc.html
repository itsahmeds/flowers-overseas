Product page — one template, three picker states, every state on one artboard · [internal]
Purpose
The last page of the Phase 0 browsable site, and the only one that answers the question the whole business turns on: when will it arrive, and how do you know? One bouquet, one destination, one all-in price, and a delivery calendar that is allowed to say only what the destination's own operational data supports.
URL
/{locale}/{countrySlug}/{product}/{productSlug}: /en-gb/poland/product/amber-hour · /de/polen/produkt/amber-hour · /pl/polska/produkt/amber-hour. The product segment is the localised path word from locales.data.ts; the slug is one authored ASCII slug shared by all four locales (§13 Q1), because a bouquet name is a proper noun and is not translated. Composed only by 008's productPath(), which is localePath() underneath.
Exists
iff the country is published and the product is status: 'active' and an active country_price row exists for that (product, country) and the product has a slug in that locale. Everything else is a hard 404. productPageExists() is the only answer to that question, and the route, generateStaticParams, the sitemap builder, 008's card-link renderer and the crawl test all call it. This is the one route in the Phase 0 site with dynamicParams = true — 84 products × 7 countries × 4 locales is 2 352 URLs, so the top 24 per (locale, country) are prebuilt and the rest are generated on first hit.
Index
noindex,follow and in no sitemap, in every locale, through spec 007's indexability() — this spec writes no noindex branch of its own. A PDP is indexable only when corridorState(iso2) === 'live', which needs an active florist, and no destination has one. The products.xml sitemap child is therefore empty and, being empty, absent.
Reads
productView() — the one view model the page, the JSON-LD builder and the sitemap row all read. Under it: getProduct, listTiers / defaultTier, priceProjection(locale, …) for every amount, dateSurcharges() for every fee, listAddons(iso2), deliveryWindow() and pickerState() (spec 009's new modules/geo/delivery), upcomingOccasions() for the date markers, and topProductsForPrebuild() for the related row.

Block order
breadcrumb → destination line → gallery → <h1> → description and care → tier selector → delivery date picker → delivery facts → add-on price list → price summary → trust → related products. That is the DOM order, and it is the mobile order. Desktop re-flows the gallery into a left column with CSS only; nothing is reordered in the markup, so a screen reader and a crawler read the sequence above.
States shown
unavailable (the Phase 0 default for all seven destinations — no dates at all) · preview (the full computed calendar, every date unselectable, labelled) · live (Phase 1: selectable, earliest preselected, cutoff line) · past-cutoff · surcharge date · holiday date with its reason · occasion-marked date · unavailable-for-every-date · no-photo (72 of 84 products, so the common case) · stale FX · single-tier · the demo summary with the honest sentence where a CTA would be · the sticky mobile summary (drawn in full on product-mobile).
Removed in this round
Three things the TASK-059 draft carried and spec 009 §13 deletes: (a) the “Order within 3 h 20 m” countdown and the relative “today” chip label (Q4 — a page cached for an hour cannot hold a countdown that is wrong after a minute, or a “Today” that is wrong after destination-midnight); (b) the “Continue” button and the add-on checkboxes (Q6 and spec 010 — there is no basket, and a control that changes nothing is a worse lie than no control); (c) the withdrawal-right sentence and the photo-on-delivery promise (Q7 — lawyer-gated and spec-027-gated), together with the “our florist in the recipient's town” claim, which is gated on an active florist.
The one island
DateAndTierEnhancer, ≤2 048 B Brotli, no fetch, no XHR, no WebSocket, no dynamic import, no timer. It swaps the summary total between numbers already in the HTML and announces the change on aria-live="polite". Every state on this artboard renders with JavaScript disabled; the island is an ergonomics improvement over a working GET form, never a dependency.
Not on this page
No rating, star, review, review count or testimonial; no florist name, florist photograph or florist count; no delivery photograph; no “X sold”; no badge; no countdown; no relative day label; no strike-through or “was/now”; no add-to-basket, wishlist or quick-view; no add-on checkbox; no withdrawal notice; no Product, Offer, AggregateOffer, aggregateRating or review in the markup. Most of these are unexpressible: ProductViewSchema has no field for them.

Deleted in this round, and why

Three things the draft carried that this page may not say

The countdown and the “today” chip“Order within 3 h 20 m for Thursday” and a chip labelled today. Both are functions of now on a page served from cache for up to an hour: the countdown is wrong within a minute and a cached “Today” is wrong after destination-midnight. They would be false statements rendered by us at scale in four locales, which is worse than a less urgent page. Absolute dates and an absolute cutoff replace them (§13 Q4); they return in Phase 1 with the shorter revalidate window and the scheduled cutoff purge.
“Continue” and the add-on checkboxesThere is no basket in Phase 0, so there is nothing to continue to and nothing to tick. The button is replaced by the “ordering is not open yet” sentence and the checkboxes by a priced read-only list (§13 Q6). Spec 010 adds both, and it adds them to a page where a tier, a date, a surcharge and one total are already one view model.
The withdrawal notice and the photo promise“There is no 14-day right of withdrawal on made-to-order flowers (CRD Art. 16(d))” is a legal disclosure, is lawyer-gated (plan/13 B4) and belongs to spec 010 and the legal page set. “Our florist photographs it at the door” is gated on spec 027. “Our florist in the recipient's town” is gated on an active florist. A block whose backing is missing renders nothing at all — not a heading, not a placeholder (§13 Q7).
What the markup says
SchemaBreadcrumbList mirroring the visible six-item trail, and nothing else (§13 Q8). No Product and no Offer in Phase 0: a Product without an Offer earns no rich result and adds a “missing field offers” warning, and an Offer on a page with no purchase path advertises a transaction we cannot perform. The typed Product + single Offer builder is written and tested here against offerProjection(), so Offer.price === renderedDisplayPrice by construction, and it emits the day a live country has a purchase path — a data flip. AggregateOffer, aggregateRating, review, Event and LocalBusiness are forbidden and asserted absent in every locale.
Indexability and canonicalOne engine, one descriptor: 007's indexability(). Indexable iff the page exists and corridorState(iso2) === 'live' and isProductIndexable() and isLocaleIndexable(locale) and isIndexingEnvironment() — so every PDP in every locale is noindex,follow today. Self-canonical per (locale, country, product); no cross-country canonical, because the country block genuinely differs and a shared canonical would be a lie about which page serves which destination. hreflang is en + en-gb only until the catalogue is translated, because a locale with no reviewed description gets no alternate.
Parameters?tier= and ?date=, parsed by a strict zod schema. An unknown parameter, an unknown tier, a malformed date, a date outside the window or a date that is not selectable falls back to the default rendering — never a 404 and never an error page, because a stale link pasted into a chat message must still show the bouquet. Every parameterised URL is noindex,follow with a canonical to the bare URL, appears in no sitemap and is never an <a href>: it exists only as the result of submitting the form.
Caching and the islandThe bare URL is ISR at revalidate 3600, tags product:{sku}, catalog:{iso2}, catalog:{iso2}:{locale}, sitemap. A live PDP drops to 300 s and adds cutoff:{iso2}. No response varies by header, none sets or reads a cookie, and the body is byte-identical with and without fo_locale, fo_currency and fo_consent — which is why the price is the locale's default currency and not the cookie's. DateAndTierEnhancer is ≤2 048 B Brotli inside the 131 072 B budget, reads only the embedded DateTotals table (≤4 096 B) and the form's own DOM, and makes no request of any kind — no fetch, no XHR, no WebSocket.
