Purpose
The founder reads every new review, publishes it, withholds text that names a person or is abusive or illegal, removes only what is not genuine, replies, and reads the Phase 1 exit metric (average buyer rating, target 4.5). A rating or a word is never edited or buried.
URL
/admin/reviews (tabs ?status=, filters rating, country, waiting time) · /admin/reviews/[id].
Index
Never: as every /admin page (noindex, no-store, robots disallow).
Permission
reviews.moderate (admin, ops). The order link shows only with orders.view. Others get 403 and no write (AC-16).
Reads
review with its denormalised columns (country, product, florist, delivered month, display name, locale), the moderation columns, our reply; the metric: the average of every first-party rating not removed, two decimals, with its count.
Writes
moderateReview(reviewId, ModerationAction): publish · publish_without_text {reason} · remove {reason, note} · reply {text}. Spec 012's audit trigger records each (AC-17).
States
Queue, pending tab, oldest first, one over 72 hours (first) · one review, pending (second screen) · publish without text · remove with a missing note · reply · published with a reply · text withheld · removed · redacted · queue empty · no permission.
Layout
Desktop: the queue on two thirds with the metric card on the last third; the review on two thirds with the action card sticky on the last third. One primary action: Publish; Remove is the danger outline. Mobile: the metric first, rows not a table; on the review the actions come first.
