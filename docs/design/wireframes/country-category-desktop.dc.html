Empty · unreachable, and why

A country category exists only when countProductsIn(category, iso2) ≥ 6 (spec 008 §13 Q7), so an empty grid cannot be rendered: Orchids (3) and Sunflowers (4) have no URL in Poland at all, and the sibling row never offers them. Nothing to draw.
