Before you order

How it reaches Poland

You order from us in your own language and currency. Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person. Nothing crosses a border, so there is no customs form and nothing to pay on arrival.

States
Empty is unreachable, by constructionA category page exists only where six products do, so the empty grid has no URL to render at. The state the implementer has to get right is the one above it: a category that falls below six — because a price row expired or a product was deactivated — stops being generated and its URL becomes a 404 at the next build, with its products still reachable from the shop root. It is never left as a thin page and never turned into a noindex one.
Near-identical listings across destinationsThe same fifteen bouquets will one day sit at seven country URLs. What keeps them apart is on the page: the destination's own price column, its currency, its occasion dates, its VAT wording and the authored country link in the breadcrumb — and the fact that a country page is indexable only once that country is genuinely operational, so the count stays at one for a long time.
