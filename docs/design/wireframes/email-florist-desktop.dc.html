Florist emails V1 to V5 · desktop 1440 (emails at 600) · spec 016 · [internal]
Purpose
The five florist emails of spec 016 §5.2 (V1 to V5). Each one but V4 carries the one-tap link that signs the member in and opens the order (ADR-0019; spec 011 §12). None carries the recipient's name, street, phone or card message (16-3).
Sent
test and live orders (16-11), one email per partner_member of the shop, in that member's locale_code (en, pl, de). WhatsApp shops also get the W copy through the founder (V1 and V4 only), and the email always.
URL
The one-tap URL is spec 012's {base}/sign-in/confirm?t={token}, callback {base}/orders/{assignmentId} stored server-side. {base} is FLORIST_BASE_PATH (/demo/vendor-inbox in Phase 0; spec 026 sets the production path).
Index
Not a page. Emails are not indexed and carry no schema (spec 016 §6). Every link: spec 018's tracking URL, the buyer's locale home, the florist one-tap URL, or /admin/orders/{id}; no tracking parameters. Open and click tracking are off on the Resend domain (click tracking would rewrite the sign-in links).
Reads
the assignment (product, tier, add-ons, date, town and postcode, payout in the florist's currency, sla_expires_at in the destination zone); never the buyer, never the recipient.
States
V1 offer · V2 reminder · V3 confirmed · V4 withdrawn after acceptance · V4 timed out · V5 photo reminder.
Data drawn
As on florist-inbox-*: Amber Hour · 18 stems + Vase, Thu 8 Oct, 00-001 Warszawa, answer by 15:40 (Europe/Warsaw), “You receive 149 zł” (an example payout, spec 011 §13 Q7).
Copy
Founder copy pending Inbox strings are spec 011 Appendix A verbatim; the rest is proposed under emails.florist.*, listed per email.
