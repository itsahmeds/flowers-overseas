Questions people ask about this day

Including the two nobody answers

When is Women's Day in Poland, and when do I have to order?8 March, every year. Order by 14:00 Warsaw time on 7 March for delivery on the day; 8 March itself is the busiest flower day in Poland, so the earlier date is the safe one. The date and the cutoff on this page are the destination's, not yours.
Can I send it without my name on it?Yes. Leave the card message blank or sign it however you like — our florist prints what you write and nothing else, and we never add our own note. We still need your email for the receipt and the delivery photo, and the recipient never sees it.
Can it go to an office?Yes, and it is the most reliable address on a weekday. Add the company name and the floor; our florist leaves it at reception if the recipient is out, and the photo shows who took it.
Is the price on this page the price I pay?Yes. Delivery and Polish VAT are already in it. Add-ons are separate lines, never pre-ticked, and the total on the product page is the total at checkout.

States

UpcomingThe order-by chip is prominent. A countdown appears only when it is derived from real cutoff data — no invented urgency, ever (plan/07 §4).
Order-by passedThe chip becomes “Too late for 8 March — soonest delivery Mon 10 March”, in the danger ink and in those words. The grid stays: the flowers are still sendable, just not for the day.
Out of seasonThe page stays live and indexable with next year's date shown as next year's date. Deleting a seasonal page every year throws away the link equity it took a year to earn.
No date for this countryThe occasion has no occasion_country row: the page does not exist, returns 404, and is absent from the sitemap and the occasion hub's country table. It is never rendered with the buyer's own date as a substitute.
