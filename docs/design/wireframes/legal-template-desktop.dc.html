Legal page template
Purpose
One template for every legal page: terms, privacy, cookies (with the settings block), withdrawal and cancellation, Impressum, company information. Per locale, versioned, and dated.
URL
/en-gb/legal/terms · /en-gb/legal/privacy · /en-gb/legal/cookies · /en-gb/legal/cancellation · /de/impressum · /en-gb/legal/company
Index
yes. They are real pages a regulator, a payment provider and a cautious buyer all look for.
Reads
the versioned document for (page, locale), its effective date, and company.ts for the identity block. The cookies page additionally reads and writes the visitor's consent record.
Why one template
six pages × four locales × versions is 24+ documents. A single shell with a prose column, a version header and a “what changed” line is the only way that stays maintainable and the only way a superseded version stays readable.
Owning spec
007 (the shell) · the documents themselves are founder/lawyer content, tracked in docs/compliance/
