How it works

We are not taking orders yet. This page describes how ordering will work when we open.

We do not ship flowers. When you order from us, a florist in the recipient's own town makes the bouquet and hands it over in person. You deal with us from start to finish: in your language, in your currency, at one price.

Four steps, and who does each

  1. You order from us.

    Choose the country, the town, the day and a bouquet. The price you see includes VAT and delivery, and it is the price you pay.

  2. We pass it to our florist there.

    We will send your order to a florist we chose ourselves in the recipient's town, in their language, with your card message. We handle the payment, the translation and any question that comes up.

  3. Our florist makes it that morning.

    From what is fresh in their shop that day. If one flower is not available, they use one of the same or higher value, style and colour, and we tell you what changed.

  4. It is handed over in person.

    Our florist calls the recipient if they need to and delivers the flowers by hand. If anything goes wrong, you talk to us, and we put it right.

Why we work this way

A bouquet in a box has spent a day or two in a van. Flowers bought and arranged in the recipient's town do not travel at all. Nothing crosses a border, so there are no customs forms and nothing for the recipient to pay at the door.

What we promise

One price.
VAT and delivery are in the price of every bouquet. Extras such as chocolates are separate lines you choose; we never add them for you.
A guarantee.
If your flowers don't arrive fresh and in good condition, we replace them or refund you in full. Our guarantee page sets out exactly when.
A person to talk to.
By phone or WhatsApp. Our help page says how quickly we reply.

What we do not promise

A copy of the picture.
Each bouquet is made by hand from what is fresh that morning, so it will be close to the photo, not identical. Some pictures on our site are example arrangements, and we say so beside them.
Every country yet.
We have written guides for seven countries in Europe. We will open each one when a florist there has agreed to work with us, and not before.
