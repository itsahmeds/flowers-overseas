Account emails T14 and T18 · mobile 390 (emails at 375) · spec 018 §14 A-founder-2026-10-22, 2026-10-22 · [internal]
Purpose
The two emails the account adds: T14, the sign-in link (first sign-in and every later one, the same template, so nothing reveals whether an account exists), and T18, an occasion reminder sent only with its own consent.
Sent
T14 by the buyer surface on spec 012's core (mail tag buyer_sign_in), in the locale of the page where the link was asked for. T18 by reminders.daily (07:00 UTC), once per reminder whose next occurrence is exactly its lead time away, in preferred_locale (else the latest live order's locale, else en). Both through the one Mailer, in spec 016's layout (email-layout-*) with the buyer frame.
Copy
Spec 018 C.15 verbatim: emails.account.signIn.*, emails.account.reminder.*. Slots: the footer's “why” line for T14, the preview lines, the trader identity (spec 041, company.ts).
Escalation
emails.account.reminder.body inserts {occasion} from account.reminders.occasion, whose values are capitalised (“Birthday”), so the sentence would read “Maria's Birthday is on 14 March.” It needs a lower-case form (and its own forms in de and pl). Drawn lower-case.
Honesty
T18 shows no price and no product (AC-57); it links to the destination's occasion page. Neither email says anything about how an order reaches our florist.
