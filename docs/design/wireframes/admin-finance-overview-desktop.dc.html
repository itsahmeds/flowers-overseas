Admin · finance overview · desktop 1440 · spec 030b · 2026-10-23 · [internal]
Purpose
The founder’s Monday page (spec 030b §4): pick a range and see what we sold, what each country and florist brought in, what we owe florists and what we kept, with every figure that is not final counted and one click from its reasons. Calm by design: one statement of totals, three tables, no charts (§3). Desktop: totals statement on two thirds, counts and the owed position on the last third, then the three tables full width.
URL
/admin/finance?from=2026-09-29&to=2026-10-28&basis=paid&mode=live: dates, codes and modes only, no personal data (§5.2). No query means the last 30 days ending today in the viewer’s zone.
Index
Never: X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, Cache-Control: no-store, private, robots meta, Disallow: /admin/, no public link (AC-3, §6).
Permission
finance.view (admin only); the CSV buttons need finance.export (admin only). ops, finance and content get 403 and no menu entry (AC-1, AC-2).
Reads
portfolioOverview(range): finance_order_facts, payment_balance_txn, rateBook, finance_partner_position, labels; at most 8 SQL statements for any range (AC-22). Arithmetic in orderMargin and rollUp, integer minor units.
Rendering
SSR no-store; the range form is a plain GET form, so the page works without JavaScript (§5.4). Live or test is a radio pair inside the form.
Formatting
Reporting columns name EUR in the header and show the number only; buyer-currency amounts carry their ISO code (formatMoney, currencyDisplay: code); never summed across currencies. Numbers right-aligned in tabular figures. Percentages through formatPercentBp as Intl renders them for en (25.36%). Rates through formatRatePpm, six decimals, with the source tag (ECB or Stripe) and the date.
Rates beside totals
Each order’s rate, date and source show on the order list and its Money panel. A total adds many orders at many rates, so the statement carries one “rates used” line, linked to the order list, instead of a single rate (orchestrator ruling on AC-21, 2026-10-23).
States
Default 30 days (first screen) · range refused (three field errors) · empty range · rates missing · all provisional · test orders · loading · tax questions confirmed · other roles.
Data drawn
Sample figures, not real orders. 8 synthetic live orders across Poland and Germany and 4 sample florists, computed with §5.2’s rules (half away from zero, largest remainder) from sample ECB rows; F1 of AC-13 is FO-7K2M-Q9XD with exactly its figures. The unassigned order’s 100% margin is what the rules give a provisional order with no fee and no florist yet, and is why the provisional count sits beside the totals. “Today” is Wednesday 28 October 2026.
