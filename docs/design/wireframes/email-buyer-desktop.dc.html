Charge at once. The payment is taken when the order is placed, and no buyer email describes how an order reaches our florist. A cancellation we make reads “We could not deliver this order. We have refunded {amount}.” Specs 013, 016 and 018 as amended in PR 220 are the source; strings they do not carry are proposed.
Purpose
The buyer emails of spec 016 §5.2 (B1 to B8, B7 retired), each drawn as the client shows it (From, To, Subject, preview line), with its plain-text part, its build notes and every state its trigger can produce. The layout they share is email-layout-*.
Sent
live orders only (16-11: test sends florist and founder emails, never buyer ones). Rendered on the worker at send time by React Email (emails/buyer/*), sent through spec 012's one Mailer, recorded in notification_delivery (no address, name or subject stored).
URL
None. Links: the tracking URL /{locale}/track/{token} from tracking.linkFor (spec 018) and the locale home.
Index
Not a page. Emails are not indexed and carry no schema (spec 016 §6). Reads
the order (reference, product lines, date, recipient name and city only, amounts and currency, locale_code, terms_version), succeeded refund rows, the delivery photo (B4), the invoice PDF (B8). Amounts are read, never recomputed: what was authorised, charged or refunded, in that currency.
