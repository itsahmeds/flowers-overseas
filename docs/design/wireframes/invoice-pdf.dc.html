Invoice and credit note · the A4 PDF · spec 018 launch slice, 2026-10-14 · [internal]
Purpose
Our own invoice for every live order, issued when the money is taken, and a credit note for every succeeded refund. A document, not a page: drawn at A4 (794 × 1123 px at 96 dpi) inside the 1440 frame. There is no 390 artboard: the phone opens the same PDF; the HTML equivalent comes with the account order detail (task 4, after launch).
Status
Spec 018 approved (2026-10-14). Profile principal/PL (active seed): Polish labels plus the buyer's language, invoice in the charged currency, VAT per rate also in PLN at NBP table A of the business day before the tax point (18-5). 🧾 The accountant can change every label, the series and the PLN line by data (AC-18).
Where
No URL of its own. renderPdf(invoiceId) stores it in the private bucket (invoices/{yyyy}/{id}.pdf); the buyer gets it by spec 016's B8 email and through /en-gb/account/invoices/[invoiceId]?t=… (signed invoice link; Content-Disposition: attachment, private, no-store, noindex, no-referrer; never through a tracking token, AC-23).
Reads
The invoice snapshot and the profile only, never live order rows: issuer (company.ts + tax_registration), buyer (order.buyer_snapshot: name, country), supply and issue dates, lines (product name untranslated plus the localised tier descriptor), order.vat_breakdown per rate, totals, the NBP rate with its source and date.
States drawn
Invoice (one 8% bouquet, one 23% add-on, the free printed card, paid in GBP: AC-17's fixture) · credit note for a partial refund of the add-on (row 30), referencing the original number, negative per-rate amounts summing to the refund (AC-21). Not drawn: a full credit note (the same with every line negated, netting to zero per rate), the agent/PL receipt (title and issuer statement from its profile).
Build
PDFKit, tagged: true, /Lang pl (one language when the buyer is in pl), Noto Sans subset embedded (Latin Ext: “Zażółć gęślą jaźń”, “Größe”, AC-22), reading order: header, meta, seller, buyer, table, PLN line, payment line, fine print. 56 px margins, 11.5 px body, numbers tabular and end-aligned, one 2 px ink rule under the header and the total. Ink and rules only; no airmail edge, no photo. The wordmark is the committed SVG.
Numbers
Example amounts. VAT per rate = round_half_even(gross × rate / (10 000 + rate)) on the group total: 8% of £45.90 → £3.40; 23% of £6.00 → £1.12. Gross per rate sums to order.buyer_total_minor (£51.90) or the invoice refuses (INVOICE_TOTAL_MISMATCH). The NBP table number, the rate and every PLN amount are placeholder bars: we have no rate to print. Numbers follow 18 Q4 (FO-PL/2026/000001, credit notes FOK-PL/2026/000001).
Copy
Every label is proposed (dotted underline), Polish first, English after the slash; title and issuer statement come from invoice_profile.title_key and issuer_statement_key. Seller identity: placeholder bars and [slot]s until the OÜ is registered (company.registered false → INVOICE_ISSUER_INCOMPLETE, so no invoice is issued before then). Sample buyer “Sam Sample” is checkout-samples.ts's.
Owning spec
018 · AC-15 to AC-23 · task 3 · 016 (B8 sends it) · 013 (payment.captured_at, refund rows).
