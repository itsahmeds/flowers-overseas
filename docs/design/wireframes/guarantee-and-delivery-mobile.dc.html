If it is not right, we fix it

One promise in one sentence, then the detail. We would rather write the exceptions down than have you find them at the wrong moment.

Fresh for seven daysIf the flowers do not last a week in water, tell us within eight days and we replace them or refund you. We do not ask for the flowers back.
Delivered on the date you choseIf we miss the date, you choose: delivery the next day with the delivery cost back, or a full refund.
Made by a real local floristIf the bouquet that arrives is not made by the florist we routed it to, that is our failure and a full refund.
The photo, or your money back on the deliveryThe photo of the delivery is part of what you paid for. No photo, no delivery charge.
Substitution

The exception that matters most

A florist buys what is fresh that morning. When a specific flower is not available they substitute it for one of the same value, the same style and the same colour, and they tell us what they changed. We put that note on your order and in the delivery email.

What they never do: substitute down in value, change the colour palette you chose, or replace a named flower silently. If the substitution is not one you would have accepted, the guarantee above applies.

Withdrawal and refundsMade-to-order flowers are perishable, so the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply (CRD Art. 16(d); UK CCRs reg. 28(1)(c)). We say this on the product page, at the payment step and here.Our own voluntary window: cancel free of charge any time before we send the order to the florist. After that the guarantee is the route, not a cancellation.The full text lives on /legal/cancellation; this page is the plain-language version and links to it.
