Our guarantee

We are not taking orders yet. This page describes how ordering will work when we open.

If the flowers are not right, we fix it. Here is exactly what that means, including the exceptions, so you know before you order rather than after.

Fresh-flower promise

If your flowers don't arrive fresh and in good condition, send us a photo within 72 hours of delivery and we'll replace them or refund you in full.

How long cut flowers last after that depends on the room, the water and the care they get, so we do not promise a number of days.

Delivered on the day you chose

If our florist cannot deliver on the day you chose, for a reason that is ours and not the recipient's, we refund you in full.

If nobody is home

Our florist calls the recipient first, which is why we ask for their phone number. If the flowers still cannot be handed over, we will agree a second attempt with you at no charge. If the address given was wrong, we will try to reach the recipient and tell you the options before any extra charge.

Substitutions

Our florists work with what is fresh that morning. If a flower is not available, they use one of the same or higher value, in the same style and colours, and we tell you what changed. If the main flower is not available, for example the roses in a rose bouquet, we will ask you first. If we cannot reach you within two hours, the florist uses the closest match.

The photo

When the flowers are handed over, our florist photographs them at the door and we email you the picture.

Cancelling

You can cancel free of charge until our florist accepts your order. For most cards and wallets we only hold the payment until then, so nothing is charged; if your payment method charges straight away, we refund it in full. Once a florist has accepted, the flowers are being made for you and the order can no longer be cancelled. The guarantee above still applies.

Your legal rights

This promise comes on top of your legal rights, not instead of them. If the flowers are faulty, the law gives you rights against us as the seller, and nothing on this page limits them.

Why the 14-day right to cancel does not apply

Most things bought online come with 14 days to change your mind. The law makes an exception for goods that perish quickly, and fresh flowers are one of them. We say this on the product page and again before you pay; the full wording is on our withdrawal and refunds page.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. We start a refund on the day we agree it; your bank decides how quickly it shows on your statement.

How to use the guarantee

Message us on WhatsApp or call us with your order number and what went wrong. If the flowers did not arrive fresh and in good condition, send the photo within 72 hours of delivery. A person reads it and decides, not a form.
