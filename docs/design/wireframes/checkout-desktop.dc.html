Purpose
Recipient → you → pay. Three steps on one URL, guest by default, state saved server-side per step so refresh and back never lose data, and every step ≤8 fields on mobile.

Phase 0 demo guard
checkout.demo_guard is on by default. The form renders and the total is real arithmetic, but no order row is created, no payment intent is opened and no card is charged. The guard state is drawn as the first thing on this artboard because it is the state the 16 October demo runs in.
Reads
order draft, the destination's address format, add-on prices for the destination, payment methods for the buyer's country, the signed 30-minute price quote, VAT breakdown by rate.
Price identity
the total equals the product page's all-in price plus only the lines the buyer chose. Nothing is added at this step that was not visible earlier.

States

Validation errorThe field's border-block-end turns danger, the message sits under the field linked by aria-describedby, focus moves to the first error, and the step's heading gains a count. Colour is never the only signal.
Quote expiredThe 30-minute signed quote lapses: the total is recomputed, the difference is stated in words before payment, and the buyer confirms the new amount. A silent re-price is drip pricing.
Payment declinedThe step stays on the page with the draft intact, the reason is shown in plain language, and an alternative method is offered. Nothing the buyer typed is lost.
Date became impossible while typingThe cutoff passes mid-checkout: the summary shows the new soonest date and asks for a confirmation before payment rather than quietly delivering a day late.
Sticky total on mobileA sticky bar carries the running total and the primary action on the 390 artboard, so the number is never scrolled away from the button.
AccessibilityNo time limit except the payment session, which is extendable. Usable at 200% zoom. Each step is a <form> with a fieldset per group and a real submit button; the progress indicator is an ordered list with the current step marked aria-current.
