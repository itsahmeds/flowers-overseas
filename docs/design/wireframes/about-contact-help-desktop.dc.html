Page C · /en-gb/help

Questions we are asked

How does sending flowers abroad work?You order here; a vetted florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet and delivers it by hand the same day or on the date you chose. Nothing is shipped.
Is the price really final?Yes. VAT and delivery are included in the price on the product page, and the total at payment is the same number. Add-ons are separate lines you choose.
What if nobody is home?The florist calls the recipient's phone number — which is why we ask for it — and agrees a place or a second attempt. We tell you what happened.
Can I send flowers today?In the cities we cover, yes, if you order before the country's cutoff. Every product page shows the soonest date it can actually arrive.
What if the flowers are not what I chose?The florist substitutes to the same value, style and colour and tells us; we tell you. If it is not a substitution you would have accepted, the guarantee applies.
Can I cancel?Free of charge until we send the order to the florist. Made-to-order flowers are perishable, so the 14-day withdrawal right does not apply — we say so before you pay.
How do I track my order?The link in your confirmation email needs no account. Lost it? Enter your order number and email at /track and we will send it again.
Which countries can you deliver to?Poland today. Six more have a corridor guide and a waiting list. We open a country when we have florists there.
Per-country FAQsEach corridor page carries its own five questions; this page links to them rather than repeating them, so neither is a duplicate of the other.
