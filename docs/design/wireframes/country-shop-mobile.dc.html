Before you order

How it reaches Poland — three sentences

You order from us in your own language and currency. Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person. Nothing crosses a border, so there is no customs form, no paperwork and nothing to pay on arrival.

Cities we coverWarszawa · Kraków · Wrocław · Gdańsk · Poznań · ŁódźCity pages exist only where florists do; until then these are text, not links (spec 004 AC-14).
Occasions in PolandAll Saints 1 Nov · Andrzejki 29 Nov · Wigilia 24 Dec · Women's Day 8 Mar · Name days, all yearDates come from the destination's calendar, not the buyer's.
What you payOne total in your currency. We pay our florist in złoty. There is no hidden fee and no percentage on top.Taken from Interflora PL's “nie pobieramy ukrytych opłat” — a promise we can keep because delivery is already in the price.
Three sentences and three link groups, which is the whole of the below-grid content: it answers the cross-border question and carries the internal links to the money pages. It is not word count.
States

Empty — a live country with no priced productThe grid is replaced by one sentence and a link to the country guide. It never renders an empty grid, a “0 results” header or a fake product.This state is a data hole worth an alert: resolvePrice finding no active row for a live country is a warn plus a Sentry message, not a blank card.
Unavailable for the chosen dateA product the destination cannot make on the selected date stays visible with the reason in words (“soonest Fri 11 Sep”), because hiding it looks like a smaller catalogue and lying about it is worse.
CurrencyThe chip shows the display currency and carries an aria-label spelling it out. The switch is a server-side repaint from an embedded price table (≤6 currencies, ≤512 B per tier) — no client fetch, and the destination's own currency is always available.
SchemaItemList of the products in document order. Each card's price is visible text only; the purchasable Offer lives on the product page, so a from-price never becomes a structured-data price.
