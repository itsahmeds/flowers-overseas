Semantics
One <h1>. The sentence is a <form method="get"> whose action is the A21 clause 4 route under /api/ (303: to the country occasion page when the destination is published and the page exists, else the country shop root, else the destinations hub; no-store, noindex), labelled by its heading; each choice is a real <select> with a visually hidden <label>; it works with JavaScript off. Only country (ISO code) and occasion have a name; the occasion select reads the six home tiles in src/config/occasions.ts, in registry order, by id (birthday, nameDay, anniversary, sympathy, justBecause, newBaby), each worded by a new message key; who it is for has no name and never reaches a URL (plan/07). The seven destinations are listed in collator(locale) order; an unpublished one is a disabled option labelled “not yet”, never colour alone (A21 clause 4, AC-11). On submit, the ≤1,024 B home island may write the relationship key to sessionStorage (fo-who). field-sizing: content fits each select to its choice; where unsupported the select is as wide as its longest option, which is acceptable. Stamps are an <ul>; a stamp with a page is one <a>, a stamp without is a <div> with no arrow and no hover. The postmark and the signoff are aria-hidden. Every photograph ships as spec 006's Photo (<img srcset sizes>, per-locale alt); the drawing paints it as a background only because artboards carry no <img>.

How we send your flowers

Three people touch your order. None of them is a courier.

You order from us in your own language and currency. Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person.

  1. You choose the town, the day and a bouquet.

    Every price is final, and any order-by time we show is on the recipient's clock, not yours.

  2. A florist a few streets away will make it.

    Made that morning with what is in season locally. Substitutions are like for like, and we tell you.

  3. Delivered by hand. You get the photo.

    A picture will arrive when the flowers do, so you know it happened.

Nothing crosses a border, so no customs and no box.
