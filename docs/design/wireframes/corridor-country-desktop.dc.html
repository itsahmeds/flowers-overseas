Questions about Poland

Five, and they are the corridor's own

Can flowers arrive today?Yes in the cities we cover, if you order before 14:00 Warsaw time. After that the soonest date is shown on every product.
What if the exact flowers are not available?The florist substitutes to the same value, style and colour and tells us; we tell you, and the substitution note appears on your order. If you are not happy, the guarantee applies.
Who delivers it?A florist with their own shop in the recipient's town. We visited them before opening the corridor.
Does the price include everything?Yes: VAT and delivery are in the price you see. Add-ons are separate lines and never pre-ticked.
