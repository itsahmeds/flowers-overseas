Practical, for Poland

The four things that go wrong

A complete Polish addressStreet and building, then flat number after a slash (Marszałkowska 12/34). Five-digit postcode with a dash: 00-590. City last. Polish characters are kept exactly as you type them — ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż all survive to the florist's screen and onto the card.The single most useful piece of country content anywhere in the field (InternetFlorist). It is also our checkout's field order for this destination.
Hospitals and care homesGive the ward and the room, and the patient's full name as the hospital has it. Polish hospitals usually accept flowers at reception, not on the ward; our florist leaves them there and the photo shows who signed. Intensive care does not accept flowers at all.Per destination, authored, and reviewed before the page publishes.
Funerals and cemeteriesTell us the chapel or cemetery and the hour of the service; we deliver at least 60 minutes before it. All Saints (1 Nov) is the single busiest day in Poland and orders close two days ahead. A wreath needs the family name for the ribbon.Highest intent, highest operational risk. Phase 1 scope is an open question for the founder.
Offices and no-one homeAdd the company and the floor; reception is the safest weekday address. If nobody answers, our florist calls the recipient on the number you gave, then tries a neighbour, then keeps the bouquet cool and delivers it the next morning at our cost.We never write “we are not responsible”, and we never charge for a second attempt at our own failure.
This block is why the page is not thin: it is four answers that are true only of Poland, written once, and it is the content the field replaces with a swapped place name.
Questions about Poland

Five, and they are Poland's own

Can flowers arrive today?Yes in the cities we cover, if you order before 14:00 Warsaw time. After that the soonest date is shown on every product.
What if the exact flowers are not available?The florist substitutes to the same value, style and colour and tells us; we tell you, and the substitution note appears on your order. If you are not happy, the guarantee applies.
Who makes it and who brings it?Our florist in the recipient's town makes your bouquet the morning it is delivered. We chose every one of them ourselves, and they hand it over in person.
Does it have to clear customs? Will the recipient be asked to pay anything?No, to both. Nothing is posted and nothing crosses a border: our florist in Poland buys the flowers in Poland and delivers them in Poland. There is no customs form, no import duty and nothing to pay at the door.
Does the price include everything?Yes: VAT and delivery are in the price you see. Add-ons are separate lines and never pre-ticked.
