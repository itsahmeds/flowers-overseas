Country occasion — one destination, one dated occasion · [internal]
Purpose
What we can make for one occasion in one destination, with that country's date for it printed on the page. Mother's Day is a different day in every country a buyer might send to, which is the whole problem this page type exists to solve.
URL
/{locale}/{country-slug}/{occasions}/{occasion-slug}: /en-gb/poland/occasions/mothers-day · /de/polen/anlaesse/muttertag · /pl/polska/okazje/dzien-matki.
Exists
iff the shop root exists and observed === true for that (occasion, country) and countProductsFor(occasion, iso2) ≥ 6 and the occasion has an authored slug. In Poland today that is Mother's Day alone among the dated occasions: All Saints has 5 tagged products, Women's Day 5, Easter 5, Christmas 2, Teachers' Day 0. Those dates are printed on the shop root and on the hubs; none of them has a page.
Index
noindex,follow in Phase 0, as every country-scoped page here.
Reads
The same listingView(), narrowed by the occasion facet, plus occasionDate(rule, year) for the dated line — computed, never a literal, and never copied from a message string.
States shown
The dated line · toolbar default and sorted · the grid (all seven products, one page, so pagination does not render) · pagination in its multi-page form, drawn for the implementer · the sibling-occasion row · the breadcrumb through the occasion hub.

Mother's Day flowers for Poland

Mother's Day in Poland is Wednesday 26 May 2027.It is a fixed date there — 26 May, whatever the weekday — and not the Sunday that Britain, Germany, Italy and the Netherlands keep.The dated line, and the reason this page type is worth having: the date is occasionDate(rule, year) over Poland's own rule, formatted by formatDate in the page's locale. There is no date literal in any component or message string, and a wrong Mother's Day is worse than no page.
