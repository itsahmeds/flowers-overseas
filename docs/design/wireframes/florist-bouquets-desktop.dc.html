Status
Awaits the founder’s approval of the amendment’s defaults (§13 Q15 to Q28).
Purpose
A signed-in florist sees the bouquets they make for us and the amount they receive for each size, with each bouquet’s state, in their own language; nothing about what the buyer pays or about other shops (§14.2 C). Read only. Desktop: cards three across in spec 011’s ten-column measure.
URL
{base}/bouquets, where {base} is spec 026a’s FLORIST_BASE_PATH. The header gains the link “My bouquets” beside the orders list (AC-56).
Index
Never: X-Robots-Tag: noindex, Cache-Control: private, no-store, under the florist path’s Disallow; no public link (spec 026a §6).
Who
A member of a florist that signInPolicy admits at that request (requireFloristContext); rows are that florist’s own partner_catalog_mapping rows only, read under the florist’s row-level security through a strict output schema with no field for a retail price, a margin or another shop (AC-52, AC-53, AC-55).
States
Four states on the first screen (on sale, not on sale yet, paused, no longer sold) with the support line, because company.supportEmail is not set today · with the support email: Ask for a change per card · a paused shop (spec 026a’s notice, every row Paused) · empty · English · menu open with the link. No loading state: server-rendered, no client JavaScript.
Data drawn
Sample bouquets and amounts for the sample florist of the admin artboards; product names and codes are the catalogue’s. Photos are grey slots: the real one is the shop’s approved primary photo. The phone number is company.phoneDisplay.
