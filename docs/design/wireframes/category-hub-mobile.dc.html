Category hub — no destination, and therefore no money · [internal]
Purpose
The page for a buyer who has searched “rose delivery” with no country in mind. Its job is to get the destination chosen: it shows where we can make roses first, then what we make, and it quotes no price because a price without a destination would be wrong for every destination but one.
URL
/{locale}/{shopCategory}/{category-slug}: /en-gb/flowers/roses · /de/blumen/rosen · /pl/kwiaty/roze. No country segment. /{locale}/flowers with no category is a 404: no country-less categories index ships (§13 Q4).
Exists
iff the category has at least one product in at least one published country and an authored slug in that locale and an authored intro in that locale. Empty is therefore impossible.
Index
This is one of the three page types in spec 008 that becomes indexable at the indexing flip — hubs and the occasions index, in indexable locales, once their intro is reviewed and they link to at least one page that exists. They are honest before any country is operational, because their content is authored copy plus real per-country facts rather than a grid pretending to be a shop. Phase 0: still noindex,follow, because the indexing environment does not exist yet.

Sending roses

Roses are the one flower that means the same thing in every country we deliver to, and the one where the number of stems says more than the colour. We make them as hand-tied bouquets, as arrangements in a box and, for a funeral, as a spray — and our florist in the recipient's town makes yours on the morning it is delivered. Tell us where it is going and we will show you what she can make there, at one price with delivery and VAT already inside it.

The authored intro: 40–120 words, human-written, ≥60%% token-distinct from every other hub intro in this locale, and free of a price literal and of any delivery-timing claim. seed:check fails the file that breaks one of those rules, and an intro that is not yet reviewed leaves the page noindex rather than unpublished.
First, the destination

Where are they?

We show no price on this page, because the price depends on where the flowers are going. Choose a destination and every price you see there includes VAT and delivery.The one sentence the no-money state is required to carry. A cross-country minimum converted at today's rate is a price no configuration matches — it is the exact shape the Price Indication Directive and the drip-pricing ban exist to prevent — so the hub shows no money at all and says why, rather than showing a “from” we cannot honour.

Countries first, then products — the hub's job is the destination (§13 Q4). The list is in collator(locale) order, which is why France precedes Germany here and would order differently in Polish. Poland is first because it is the only published destination and the only one with a page; the rest follow it in collator order.
Six of the seven destinations are text, not linksA destination gets a link from a hub only when its own page exists. Poland is published, so it has a shop and a link; Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Romania and Spain are demo, have no shop page at any URL, and are therefore named in words with their state said plainly. The hub never links at a 404 and never pads its list back out with destinations we cannot serve.
