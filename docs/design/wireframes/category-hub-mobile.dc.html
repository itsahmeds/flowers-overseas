Why there is no price here

One sentence, said out loud

A rose costs what it costs in the town it is made in, so we cannot show you a price until you tell us where it is going. Pick a destination and every price on the next page is the whole price — delivery and VAT in, nothing added at checkout.

The field's answer to this page is a price with an asterisk. Ours is no price and a reason, which is the only version compatible with “price shown = price charged”. It is also the only page type in the set that is allowed to show a product without a price.
