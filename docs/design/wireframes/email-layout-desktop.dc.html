Email layout · three audience frames and the parts · desktop 1440 (emails at 600) · spec 016 · [internal]
Purpose
The one email layout (emails/layout.tsx) in its three audience frames, and every part the templates are built from. One layout, an audience prop: buyer (the full footer: who we are, help, the trader identity, why you got this), florist (help and why), founder (internal). Templates: email-buyer-*, email-florist-*, email-founder-*.
Widths
A single 600 px column on a --color-paper-2 ground (desktop clients), 48 px inline padding; at 375 px (phones) the card fills the screen, 20 px padding, the button full width. One column always: no side-by-side blocks, nothing that needs a media query to stay readable.
Type
Fraunces 400 headings and Alegreya Sans body from our own origin by @font-face (never Google Fonts, ADR-0016); clients that refuse web fonts fall back to Georgia and the system sans, and the layout is checked in both. The wordmark is the committed lockup as a 2× PNG with alt="Flowers Overseas" (proposed: SVG is blocked by Gmail), drawn here as the SVG mark plus Newsreader text.
Colour
The v2 tokens, nothing new. Escalation: React Email needs literal colours in inline styles, and CLAUDE.md lets a colour be written only in globals.css's @theme. Proposed: emails/theme.ts generated from globals.css (oklch to sRGB hex) by a script and checked in CI, so the email palette cannot drift. color-scheme: light is declared; clients that force dark mode invert the card, checked in the visual snapshots.
Spacing
Logical properties in the source (spec 016 §5.3). Outlook ignores padding-inline, so React Email's components emit the table-cell padding; the rendered HTML is the build's concern, the source stays logical. lang and dir from the locale config on the root.
Parts
eyebrow · heading · body · facts list (label above value at 375) · amount box · refund line (“Refunded {amount} on {date}”, leaf, under the amount in every buyer email once a refund has succeeded) · information box (sage), stop box (blush), alert band (butter) · answer-by badge · payout row · primary button (the one action; poppy pill, 52 px, a bulletproof table button in the build) · note under a button · attachment row · legal block (14 px, ruled) · offers table · paste block.
Plain text
Every template has a plain-text part (AC-21): the same words in the same order, links written out in full on their own line, the footer after --. Drawn beside each email.
Never
No tracking pixel, no click tracking, no unsubscribe link (all transactional, spec 016 §8), no recipient address or phone anywhere, no name, address or phone in a subject line, no em dash.
Index
Not a page. Emails are not indexed and carry no schema (spec 016 §6). Every link: spec 018's tracking URL, the buyer's locale home, the florist one-tap URL, or /admin/orders/{id}; no tracking parameters. Open and click tracking are off on the Resend domain (click tracking would rewrite the sign-in links).
Copy
Founder copy pending Footer: company.description, company.support.label, company.operatedBy verbatim; company.support.hours with its em dash drawn as a full stop. proposed emails.layout.buyerWhy “You receive this email because you placed order {reference} with us.”, .floristHelp “Call us if anything about an order is unclear.”, .floristWhy “You receive this email because your shop takes orders from Flowers Overseas.”, .opsWhy, .audience.florist “For our florists”, .audience.ops “Internal”. The trader identity renders only once the company is registered (isCompanyRegistered()); a live buyer email cannot be sent before then, because legalReadiness() requires it.
