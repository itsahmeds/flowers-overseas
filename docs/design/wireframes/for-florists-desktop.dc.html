We find that buyer, take the order in their language, handle the payment and stand behind the promise. You make the bouquet from what is fresh and deliver it in your town. For each order you receive a fixed amount that we agree with you before you start.

What we do

  • We find the buyer and talk to them in their language.
  • We take the payment; card fraud, refunds and disputes are ours.
  • We answer the buyer's questions before and after delivery.
  • We send you each order with everything you need to make and deliver it.

What you do

  • You accept or decline each order; saying no when you are full costs you nothing.
  • You make the bouquet from what is fresh that morning.
  • You deliver it in person on the date.
  • You add one photo of the delivered bouquet.
  • You tell us before you deliver if you have to replace a flower.

When you are paid

Every week, by bank transfer in your currency, for the orders you delivered that week.

What we never ask for

  • a fee to join
  • a monthly fee
  • a minimum number of orders
  • working only with us
  • your own customers
  • your bank details before we have spoken

How an order reaches you

  1. We email you a link that opens the order.
  2. You accept or decline.
  3. You make and deliver it.
  4. You add one photo from the same page.

Questions florists ask us

Do I have to work only with you?

No. You keep your shop, your customers and any other work you do.

Is there a fee?

No. There is no fee to join and no monthly fee.

What if I am full on a day?

Decline the order. It costs you nothing.

Who is the customer?

We are. The buyer pays us, and any question or complaint comes to us. You never chase a payment.

Do I see the buyer's details?

No. After you accept, you see what delivery needs: the recipient's name, phone number and address, the card message and the date.

What happens to the recipient's details after delivery?

You use them only to deliver that order and do not keep them afterwards. Our agreement with you says so.

In which language do orders arrive?

In Polish, German or English, as you choose.

Do I need a registered business?

Yes. We work with registered flower shops and florists, and we ask for your registration number when we speak.

How do I start?

Apply here, or call or message us first. We talk, agree what you receive for each bouquet, and send you a test order before a real one.
