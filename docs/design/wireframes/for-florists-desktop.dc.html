Orders from abroad, made by you

Somebody in London wants flowers delivered to their mother in Warszawa. We find the buyer, take the order in their language, handle the payment and the promise. You make the bouquet and deliver it, and you are paid a fixed amount per order that you agree in advance.

What each of us does

We handleThe buyer and their language · the website and the photography · the payment, the refunds and the chargebacks · VAT and the invoice · the delivery promise and the guarantee · the buyer's questions before and after · finding the order in the first place.
You handleAccepting or declining each order · making the bouquet from what is fresh · delivering it locally on the date · one photo of the delivered bouquet · telling us if you substituted something · saying no when you are full, with no penalty.
