Basket email R1 · basket-reminder · desktop 1440 (emails at 600) · spec 034 in spec 016’s email layout, 2026-10-25 · [internal]
Purpose
One email, about two hours after a buyer who ticked the box stopped, that takes them back to their basket. No price, no discount, no countdown, no recipient, no name: it is safe even at a mistyped address.
Sent
Only with the box ticked on checkout step 3 (“Your details”, spec 010 §14 A-founder-2026-10-25a; spec 034 says step 2) and flag marketing.basket_reminder on; 2 to 20 hours after the last activity; once per basket and at most once per address in 7 days; through spec 012’s Mailer, stream ’marketing’, from MARKETING_MAIL_FROM (Q7, Q8: a new ADR for Resend until the CRM).
URL
None. Links: /{locale}/basket/restore/{token} (opaque 256-bit token, 7 days), /{locale}/basket/unsubscribe/{token}, help and privacy. No tracking parameters.
Reads
BasketReminderEmailPropsSchema: 1 to 5 lines (product, size, country, thumbnail, date kept, moved or open), the two URLs, help, privacy, company. No field exists for a price, an amount, a time left, a cutoff, a stock count, a discount or a person’s name (AC-12).
States
one line, its date kept (first screen) · three lines: kept, moved (the chosen date can no longer be met, so the earliest date now), open (no date chosen) · lines that can no longer be delivered are left out; none left: not sent · not sent for every skip reason · RTL pseudo-locale.
Copy
Verbatim from spec 034 Appendix A (email.basketReminder.*, reviewed: false, founder batch); the help link label reuses footer.link.helpAndContact; footer identity from company.ts (slots until filled). No em dash, no pressure word.
Example data
The basket of basket-*: Amber Hour (date kept, Thursday 8 October), Vistula Red (the Thursday has passed its cutoff: Saturday 10 October), Quiet Blush to Germany (no date yet). Images are the products’ public thumbnails.
Gaps
A1 Spec 034 places the box on step 2; on the four steps it sits on step 3 under the email (drawn in PR 417 on checkout-*). A2 The email names no greeting and no sign-off; drawn without them (no buyer name by design).
AC
spec 034 AC-6, AC-11, AC-12, AC-13, AC-14, AC-15
Owning spec
