404 and 500 · [internal]
Purpose
The two pages nobody designs and everybody sees. Localised, useful, and with a way back that is a destination picker rather than a “go home” link.
URL
any unmatched path (404) · any unhandled error (500) · a deliberately removed URL (410)
Index
404 and 410 return their status codes and are noindex by construction. A soft 404 — a “not found” page returning 200 — is the failure mode this design exists to prevent.
Reads
the locale from the URL where there is one, and the country registry for the destination picker. Nothing else: a 500 page that needs a database cannot render when the database is why it is a 500.
Copy
errors.notFound.*, errors.serverError.* and meta.notFound.* / meta.error.* all exist in messages/en.json and are quoted verbatim below.
