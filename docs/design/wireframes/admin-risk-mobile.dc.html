Purpose
Everything needed to decide one held order: every signal with its points and a one-line reason, the Radar facts, the deadline and what happens at it, and three decisions. Nothing about the recipient.
URL
/admin/risk/{reviewId}; the dialogs are native <dialog> elements on it (a bottom sheet on the phone), no URL of their own.
Index
Never: X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow and Cache-Control: no-store, private on every response, the admin layout's robots meta tag, Disallow: /admin/, the Cloudflare bypass, no sitemap, no public link (spec 021 §5.4, AC-19). No locale prefix; ids only in the path.
Permission
Page, Approve, Cancel and refund: risk.review (admin, ops). Cancel, refund and block: also risk.blocklist (admin only); without it the button is not drawn and a sentence says why, and a forged post answers 403 and changes nothing (AC-21, AC-22). Buyer email and name: orders.view_pii only; that view writes one audit_log read row (AC-19).
Reads
The review, its assessment (signals as { id, points }, score, rules_version, Radar level, score and outcome, liability shift) and its order. Labels and explanations come from admin.risk.signal.{id}; no signal value is stored, so none is shown.
Writes
Each button opens its dialog; the dialog posts ReviewDecisionSchema { reviewId, decision: approve | cancel | cancel_block, reason, expectedStatus: open }. Approve: order.risk_released, routing starts (AC-20). Cancel and refund: cancelOrder(orderId, admin:{id}, risk_cancel), full refund, no Stripe fraud reason (AC-21). Cancel, refund and block: reason fraud_confirmed, Stripe reason fraudulent, email and card hashes on risk_blocklist (source = admin). A post-routing review on a paid order: Cancel and refund reads "Refund in full" and refunds the remaining balance (spec 021 §5.2).
Reasons
From decision_reason. Proposed split: Approve offers looks_genuine, buyer_contacted, other; the two cancels offer stolen_card_suspected, card_testing, blocklist_match, efw, buyer_contacted, other. deadline_default and superseded are written by the system only. The schema has no note field, so none is drawn.
States
open, before routing (first screen) · without orders.view_pii · without risk.blocklist · decided (who, when, reason) · released or cancelled at the deadline · closed without action (superseded) · after routing with no deadline (in_production) · dialogs: approve, cancel and refund, cancel refund and block, already decided, order changed (superseded on submit), pending ("Approving…", not drawn twice), done toasts.
Accessibility
Focus moves to the chosen reason when a dialog opens; Escape and "Keep reviewing" close without acting; a refusal banner takes role="alert"; the score is text, never colour alone.
