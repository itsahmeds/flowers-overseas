We do not ship flowers. We send them.

A bouquet in a box is a bouquet that has spent two days in a van. So we do it the other way round: you order here, and a florist with their own shop in the recipient's town makes it that morning and delivers it by hand.

The relay

Four steps, and who does each

01 · youYou orderIn your language and your currency, with the all-in price and the delivery date fixed before you pay.
02 · usWe route itTo a florist we have visited, in the recipient's town, with the capacity for that day. We handle the language, the money and the promise.
03 · the floristThey make itThat morning, from what is fresh in their shop. If something is unavailable they substitute to the same value, style and colour, and tell us.
04 · the florist and usYou get the photoThey photograph the delivered bouquet and we email it to you, so you know it arrived and what it looked like.
What we promise, and what we do not

The price you see is the price you payVAT and delivery are in it. Add-ons are separate lines and never pre-ticked. Nothing appears at the payment step that was not visible before.
A photo of the actual deliveryNot a stock photo of a similar bouquet. The florist's own photo of what they handed over.
7 days of freshnessIf it does not last a week we replace it or refund you.
A person to talk toWhatsApp and phone, with hours, not a form that disappears.
We do not promise a bouquet identical to the pictureIt is made by hand from what is fresh that morning. Where an image on our site was generated rather than photographed, we say so on the page.
We do not promise a time of dayOnly a date. A florist delivering by hand cannot honour a two-hour window, and pretending otherwise is how a good delivery becomes a complaint.
We do not deliver everywhere yetOne country today, six with a guide and a waiting list. We open a country when we have florists there, not when we have a page for it.
We do not have reviews yetSo we show none. Not a sample, not a seeded one, not a five-star average of nothing.
