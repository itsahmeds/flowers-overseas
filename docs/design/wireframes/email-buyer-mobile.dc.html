Buyer emails B1 to B8 · mobile 390 (emails at 375) · spec 016 · [internal]
Purpose
The eight buyer emails of spec 016 §5.2 (B1 to B8), each drawn as the client shows it (From, To, Subject, preview line), with its plain-text part, its build notes and every state its trigger can produce. The layout they share is email-layout-*.
Sent
live orders only (16-11: test sends florist and founder emails, never buyer ones). Rendered on the worker at send time by React Email (emails/buyer/*), sent through spec 012's one Mailer, recorded in notification_delivery (no address, name or subject stored).
URL
None. Links: the tracking URL /{locale}/track/{token} from tracking.linkFor (spec 018) and the locale home.
Index
Not a page. Emails are not indexed and carry no schema (spec 016 §6). Every link: spec 018's tracking URL, the buyer's locale home, the florist one-tap URL, or /admin/orders/{id}; no tracking parameters. Open and click tracking are off on the Resend domain (click tracking would rewrite the sign-in links).
Reads
the order (reference, product lines, date, recipient name and city only, amounts and currency, locale_code, terms_version), succeeded refund rows, the delivery photo (B4), the invoice PDF (B8). Amounts are read, never recomputed: what was authorised, charged or refunded, in that currency.
States
B1 held · B1 paid now · B2 · B3 · B4 · B5 no florist · B5 buyer cancelled · B5 hold lapsed · B5 money taken · B6 partial · B6 full (“Closed”) · B6 two refunds · B7 · B8 invoice · B8 credit note · not sent (test, demo, flag off).
Data drawn
Example values, as on confirmation-*: FO-7K2M-9QXA, Amber Hour · 18 stems, Thursday 8 October, the sample recipient “Maria Przykładowa”, £45.90 (en-gb), the masked address. Refund amounts (£12.00, £8.00, £4.00) and dates are examples. No real person, no real order.
Copy
Founder copy pending No email copy exists yet: spec 016 has no Appendix A. Each email's Build list marks every string verbatim (with its key and source) or proposed (with the key this artboard proposes). Lawyer-gated sentences are [slot]s. No em dash anywhere; first person; present tense.
AC
spec 016 AC-12, AC-14, AC-18, AC-21, AC-24, AC-25, AC-26, AC-37
Owning spec
