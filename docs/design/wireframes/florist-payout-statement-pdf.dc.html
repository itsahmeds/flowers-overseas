Florist payout statement · the A4 PDF · spec 030a · [internal]
Purpose
What a florist keeps: the amount we send for each order, by statement number, in their currency and language. A remittance statement, not an invoice (§13 Q1): it carries no VAT rate or VAT amount, and says so. Drawn at A4 (794 × 1123 px at 96 dpi) inside the 1440 frame; no 390 artboard, the phone opens the same PDF.
Where
No URL. The payouts.statement_email job renders it once, at send, stores it private at payouts/{yyyy}/{payoutId}.pdf as a media_asset (visibility = 'private'), sets statement_media_asset_id and attaches it to the statement email. Admin opens it through a 5-minute signed URL. Never linked from a public page.
Reads
The payout's own rows only: statement number, kind, period (or the single date of a prepaid or per-order payout), sent_at, currency, and each payout_line: order_reference(), the order's delivery date in the florist's zone, product name and tier from product_translation in the florist's locale (fallback en), amount, and the note of a manual line. The florist's legal_name and code. Never a buyer or recipient field, the card message, the retail price, the public_token or a VAT figure (AC-22, AC-23).
Index
Not a page: private bucket, short signed URL, so it cannot be crawled (§6).
States drawn
Weekly statement with an order whose add-on was priced by an adjustment line (the note shows under the order reference) · prepaid statement, one order, the single-date label. A per-order statement is the prepaid layout with its photo date.
Build
Spec 018's PDFKit setup, shared with the invoice: tagged: true, /Lang from the florist's locale (en, pl or de), a title, the embedded Noto Sans subset (Latin Extended: “Kwiaciarnia pod Lipą”, “łodyg”), A4, reading order header, meta, parties, table, payment, fine print (AC-30). Same 56 px margins, 11.5 px body, tabular end-aligned numbers and 2 px ink rules as invoice-pdf.dc.html. Ink and rules only.
Language
Drawn in English. Ships in the language of the florist's earliest owner member, else the country default (pl for Poland, §13 Q10); dates and amounts through the i18n formatters in that locale (“149,00 zł” in pl). The pl text is read by a native speaker before the first live statement.
Copy
Shared with the statement email, keys email.floristPayoutStatement.* (reviewed: false). Verbatim from spec 030a §7: paymentMethod “We send this amount to your bank account through Wise, in {currency}.” and notInvoice “This is a payout statement, not an invoice.” Every other label is proposed (dotted underline). deleteReminder is the lawyer's to confirm with the florist agreement (§13 Q12). Our legal identity is placeholder bars and [slot]s until the company is registered.
Data drawn
Synthetic, matching admin-payout-detail-*: 149.00 + 397.00 (2 × 149.00 + 99.00, AC-3's shape) + 20.00 = 566.00 zł.
