Before you order

Questions people ask first

All help topics
Will the bouquet look like the photo?+

Close, not identical. Florists work with what is fresh that morning and substitute like for like in colour and value. Every change is noted on your delivery photo.

What if nobody is home?+

The florist calls the recipient first. If they cannot be reached, we agree a redelivery the same or next day at no charge.

Is the price really final?+

Yes. Delivery and VAT are inside the price you see. Your card is charged in your currency for exactly that amount.

Who delivers, and when?+

A vetted independent florist in the recipient's town, by hand, on the day you choose. Order by 14:00 local time for same-day in Warsaw.

What if the flowers do not last?+

Seven days from delivery. Tell us and we redeliver or refund, your choice, no photo required.
