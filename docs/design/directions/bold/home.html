How we send your flowers

Three people touch your order. None of them is a courier.

You order from us in your own language and currency. Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person. Nothing crosses a border, so there is no customs form, no paperwork and nothing to pay on arrival.

  1. 1You choose the town, the day and a bouquet.

    Every price is final, and any order-by time we show is on the recipient's clock, not yours.

  2. 2A florist a few streets away accepts it.

    Made that morning with what is in season locally. Substitutions are like for like, and we tell you.

  3. 3Delivered by hand. You get the photo.

    A picture arrives when the flowers do, so you know it happened.
