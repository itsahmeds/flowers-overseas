How we send your flowers

Three people touch your order. None of them is a courier.

You order from us in your own language and currency. Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person. Nothing crosses a border, so there is no customs form and nothing to pay on arrival.

  1. 1

    You choose the town, the day and a bouquet.

    Every price is final, and any order-by time we show is on the recipient's clock, not yours.

  2. 2

    A florist a few streets away accepts it.

    Made that morning with what is in season locally. Substitutions are like for like, and we tell you.

  3. 3

    Delivered by hand. You get the photo.

    A picture arrives when the flowers do, so you know it happened.

Before you order

Questions people ask first

Our help pages are being written. Until they are up, the answers below are the whole of what we promise.

Will the bouquet look like the photo?

Close, not identical. Our florists work with what is fresh that morning and substitute like for like in colour and value. Every change is noted on your delivery photo.

What if nobody is home?

Our florist calls the recipient first. If they cannot be reached, we agree a redelivery with you, at no charge.

Is the price really final?

Yes. Delivery and VAT are inside the price you see. Your card is charged in your currency for exactly that amount.

Who delivers, and when?

Our own florist in the recipient's town, by hand, on the day you choose.

What if the flowers do not last?

Seven days from delivery. Tell us and we redeliver or refund, your choice, no photo required.
