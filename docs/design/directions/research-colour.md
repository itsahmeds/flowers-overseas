# Colour and emotion: research for direction A

For the founder, 3 October 2026. The question: which colours make a daughter in London trust us with flowers for her mum in Kraków, and feel something while she does it? Each claim links to its source. **Study** means peer-reviewed research. **Practitioner** means a trade source or a competitor's live site. **Pop** means a figure that goes round marketing blogs without a published method.

**Short answer.** Use a calm, light, warm ground (letter paper) and one dark, cool ink for words and prices. Add one saturated colour, a deep **peony** pink, only for what you act on. Stay away from poppy red, red-and-white, and purple-and-black.

## 1. What research says about colour, warmth, trust and urgency

| Finding | Kind | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| In a study of 4,598 people from 30 nations, people link the same colours to the same feelings: **red with love and anger, pink with love, joy and pleasure, yellow with joy, black with sadness and fear, white with relief.** Nations that are close in geography or language agree more ([Jonauskaite et al. 2020, *Psychological Science*](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797620948810); [BPS summary](https://bps.org.uk/node/1441)). | Study | Pink carries love without red's anger. A UK buyer and a Polish mum are close enough to read colours the same way. |
| **Brightness and saturation predict feeling more strongly than hue does.** Brighter colours feel more pleasant, and more saturated colours are more arousing ([Valdez & Mehrabian 1994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7996122/)). | Study | Calm comes from light, low-saturation grounds. Keep the one saturated colour for the action. |
| Shoppers in a **blue** store made more simulated purchases and put off fewer than shoppers in a **red** one ([Bellizzi & Hite 1992](https://doi.org/10.1002/mar.4220090502); [summary](https://makinggood.ac.nz/media/1264/leerao_2010_colour.pdf)). A relaxing (blue, lower-chroma) screen makes a page **feel faster** ([Gorn et al. 2004, *J. Marketing Research*](https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/publications/waiting-for-the-web-how-screen-color-affects-time-perception/)). | Study | A cool, dark ink for text and prices supports calm, decisive buying. |
| Hue maps onto brand personality: **red reads as exciting and blue as competent.** Purchase intent is highest when the colour fits the personality the brand claims ([Labrecque & Milne 2012, *JAMS*](https://colab.ws/articles/10.1007%2Fs11747-010-0245-y)). | Study | We claim "trustworthy and caring", so a competent blue ink with one warm, affectionate accent fits that claim. |
| How appealing a site's colours are is a **significant driver of trust and satisfaction**, and the effect differs by culture ([Cyr, Head & Larios 2010, *IJHCS*](https://experts.mcmaster.ca/scholarly-works/58866)). | Study | Colour is part of the trust promise, not decoration. |
| **Nostalgia and physical warmth are linked:** people are more nostalgic on cold days, and nostalgia makes a cold room feel warmer ([Zhou et al. 2012, *Emotion*](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/342612)). | Study (our inference) | The study is about temperature, not colour. Warm paper tones suit a "letter home" mood, but this is our reading of the study, not a tested result. |
| Red's effect **depends on context.** It raises caution in situations where someone is being judged and attraction in romantic ones. Older colour studies often failed to control hue, lightness and chroma, and many were underpowered ([Elliot 2015 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4383146/)). | Study | Treat any single "red sells" result with care. |
| **Prices shown in red read as savings**, at least for men making a low-involvement choice ([Puccinelli et al. 2013, *J. Retailing*](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jouret/v89y2013i2p115-125.html)). | Study | A red price looks like a sale. Ours is one honest, all-in price, so prices stay in ink. |
| "**85% of shoppers buy because of colour**" ([traced to Colorcom/CCICOLOR via Kissmetrics](https://www.ragan.com/85-percent-of-consumers-buy-products-based-on-color/)), "blue means trust", and "this button colour converts best". | Pop | No published method. Practitioners who test buttons find that the winner is whatever **contrasts most with its surroundings**, not any particular hue ([EyeQuant](https://www.eyequant.com/blog/button-color-on-websites)). |

## 3. Cultural meanings in Poland and the UK

**Poland**
- **White and red are the national colours by law**, white over red ([Act of 31 January 1980, via FOTW](https://fotw.info/FLagS/pl.html)). A red-on-white scheme, or red-and-white airmail stripes, reads as the flag. Read this way, it is national-flag kitsch for a diaspora brand.
- **All Saints' Day (1 Nov):** families take **chrysanthemums** and *znicze* candles to family graves across the country ([Stars and Stripes](https://europe.stripes.com/your-community/poland/all-saint%E2%80%99s-day-poland.html)). Small potted chrysanthemums read as "cemetery" ([Euroflorist PL](https://www.euroflorist.pl/blog/jakich-kwiatow-nie-wysylac)). The All Saints' stamp stays on plain paper, never purple or black.
- **Purple and black are mourning colours:** Polish funeral Masses use purple or black vestments ([Konicki](https://www.konicki.com/2010/03/18/poland-and-austro-hungarian-history-in-one-funeral/)), and violet is the colour of Advent and Lent ([Liturgy Brisbane](https://www.liturgybrisbane.net.au/learn/liturgy-lines/the-colour-purple/)). Black stands for "endings and death" ([Euroflorist PL](https://www.euroflorist.pl/blog/jakich-kwiatow-nie-wysylac)).
- **Yellow flowers** can signal jealousy or betrayal in romance, and yellow carnations once meant "you disappointed me". Mixed bouquets soften this ([Euroflorist PL](https://www.euroflorist.pl/en/blog/flower-etiquette)). That makes it a rule for imagery (no all-yellow romantic bouquet), not for the interface.
- **Red poppies** are the song *Czerwone maki na Monte Cassino*, treated as an unofficial second anthem ([Wikipedia: Feliks Konarski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feliks_Konarski)).
- **Red roses** are for partners and wrong for teachers or bosses ([Euroflorist PL](https://www.euroflorist.pl/blog/jakich-kwiatow-nie-wysylac)). This matters for the Teachers' Day stamp.

**UK**
- **The red poppy means Remembrance.** It is worn from about 28 October to 11 November ([Woman & Home](https://www.womanandhome.com/life/what-side-to-wear-poppy/); [Yorkshire Post](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/read-this/why-red-poppies-are-worn-for-remembrance-season-and-what-the-alternative-colours-mean-2998681)), the same fortnight as All Saints'.
- **Red and white flowers together** mean "a death on the ward" to many nurses ("blood and bandages"). A 1980s Folklore Society survey found the belief widespread ([Plant Lore](https://www.plant-lore.com/?p=2107); [phrases.org.uk](https://phrases.org.uk/bulletin_board/54/messages/309.html)).

A buyer in London sending to Kraków reads both sets of codes, so the palette has to pass in both countries.

## 4. Accessibility

- **The rules:** body text needs 4.5:1 and large text 3:1 ([WCAG 2.2, 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)). Control edges and focus rings need 3:1 ([1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)). Colour is never the only signal ([1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)).
- **Colour blindness:** up to **8% of men and 0.5% of women** of northern European descent have red-green colour deficiency ([PMC review 2025](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12385717/)). Sons send flowers to their mums too.
- **Warm off-whites cost contrast.** A grey that passes on white can fail on cream. Our caption ink was darkened to `#566077` so it still clears 4.88:1 on the darkest wash (apricot).
- **Measured pairs** (WCAG relative luminance): ink on paper 13.3, ink-2 7.4, ink-3 5.9 (≥ 4.88 on every wash), peony on paper 6.5, white card text on peony 6.9 (9.1 on hover), airmail blue on paper 6.9, stem green on paper 5.9, apricot on the ink band 11.1.
- **Simulated colour blindness** ([Machado et al. 2009](https://www.inf.ufrgs.br/~oliveira/pubs_files/CVD_Simulation/CVD_Simulation.html)): for deuteranopes, peony and stem green collapse to nearly the same tone (1.08:1). So "included" is always a word, a selection always has a shape (dot, tick or 2 px ink border), and Send is a filled pill while links are underlined text. For protanopes, peony turns grey-blue, and Send stays clear by its shape and white label.
- **Prices** are always in ink, bold, and never peony, red or green, so they never look like a discount. **Send** is the only filled peony thing on a screen, which gives it the isolation effect that button tests actually measure.

## 5. Recommendation: "Letter home"

| Token | Hex | Its emotional job |
|---|---|---|
| **Letter paper** | `#FBF6EE` | Warm, bright ground. It feels like a letter from home: calm and pleasant (bright, low saturation). |
| **Blue-black ink** | `#1E2A44` | The ink letters are written in. Text and prices read as competent and steady, with blue's calm. |
| **Peony** | `#A3285A` | Affection: pink means love and joy across 30 nations. Used once per screen for Send and the things you chose. It is not flag red, poppy red or sale red. |
| **Airmail blue** | `#2B5596` | The journey: links, the postmark, and the buyer's own words, as if written in a blue pen. |
| **Stem green** | `#2F6B47` | The logo's own green, used for "included" and "made locally". It is quiet reassurance. |
| **Apricot** | `#F6DFC4` | Sunlight on paper: stamps, hints, and the warm text on the dark band. Warmth without a yellow-flower message. |

Supporting tints: paper-2 `#F4EBDE`, card `#FFFDF9`, peony wash `#F6E1E7`, airmail wash `#E2E9F3`, rule `#E2D8CB`, ink-2 `#46516B`, ink-3 `#566077`.

**Rejected 1: Poppy, cornflower and sunflower (the "A + C" brights).** Poppy red means Remembrance in the UK and Monte Cassino in Poland, and both peak in the All Saints' fortnight. Its red-and-white airmail stripe reads as the Polish flag and as "blood and bandages". Saturating every colour raises arousal when the buyer needs calm to trust a cross-border payment.

**Rejected 2: Heather and cream (lavender and plum on cream, black type).** It looks pretty and suits a florist, but purple with black is Polish funeral and Lenten dress. Interflora and 1-800-Flowers already own purple.
