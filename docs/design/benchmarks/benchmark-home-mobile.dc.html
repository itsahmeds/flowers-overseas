The changes, argued
#Change, kind and argument
M1The utility strip collapses from three rows (113 px) to one (44 px). Rearrangement The delivery-dates sentence keeps its position; the current language and the currency chip join it on the same line, as Bloom & Wild's single "UK (£)" control and Euroflorist's single "NL" control both do. Saves 69 px, and nothing that was said stops being said.
M2The three other language links leave the mobile strip for the footer. Removal The stronger argument the brief asks for: all four locales stay reachable, crawlable, server-rendered and one tap away — the footer already carries every one of them as an ordinary <a>, so hreflang reciprocity, the sitemap and ADR-0006 are untouched, and no IP is read to decide anything. What changes is only their position on a 375 px screen, where four side-by-side language links consume a 33 px row above the logo that no competitor spends on anything. Desktop keeps all four in the strip.
M3The telephone number and its hours leave the mobile strip for the footer and the help page. Removal It costs 44 px — 5.4% of the phone viewport — at the most valuable position on the site, above our own name. None of the four sites measured puts a telephone number above its logo on a phone. It remains in the desktop strip, in the footer and on the contact page, so a visitor who wants a human still finds one in one scroll. A secondary point worth the founder's eye either way: the number rendered on production is a +1 (213) United States number, on a European site quoting CET hours.
M4The hero photograph goes from 300 px to 240 px and the three finder fields plus the button fit above the fold. Rearrangement With M1–M3 the button lands at roughly 748, with its foot at 798. A page whose one call to action has never been seen without a scroll is the clearest conversion defect this study found, and it is fixed by moving pixels, not by removing anything.
M5The category row becomes real links, as on desktop. Rearrangement Same argument as H1: the four items shown at 375 are <span> elements today and pass nothing to the live Poland pages.
M6The 56 px category row itself: kept. Kept It is the only route from the home page into the shop, and once M5 makes it work it is the most valuable 56 px on the screen.
