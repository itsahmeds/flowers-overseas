The changes, argued
#Change, kind and argument
LM1Chrome 245 → 176, by the same three moves as the home page. Rearrangement + Removal See M1–M3 on benchmark-home-mobile.dc.html: the strip collapses to one row, the three other language links and the telephone number move to the footer, and both remain reachable. Saves 69 px on every page, not only this one.
LM2The duplicated intro sentence goes; the unique one joins the summary line. Removal Same argument as L1, and worth 96 px here rather than 72: at 375 that paragraph is four lines, and its first sentence is printed verbatim again in the "How it reaches Poland" block further down the same page.
LM3The ordering-is-not-open notice becomes a chip directly under the <h1>. Rearrangement It goes from four lines at 503 to two lines at roughly 270, which is higher on the page and harder to miss. The fact is not softened: "You cannot order yet" keeps the first position in the sentence, and the price caveat moves into the summary line under it. Same argument as L2, and the same constraint honoured — this notice may never disappear and does not.
LM4The second heading and the ordering sentence collapse into one --text-xs summary line. Rearrangement 60 px of ordering sentence plus a 25 px heading plus their gaps become one two-line strip that also prints the count. Every word of the ordering sentence survives. id="our-selection" moves to the <h1>.
LM5The card photograph goes from 199 px to 209 px and the label from three lines to two, taking the photograph share from 55.0% to about 66%. Rearrangement 55.0% is the lowest card in the study, against Bloom & Wild's 66.4% and Interflora's 75.2%, and the cause is measurable: the 52-character honesty label wraps to three lines in a 160 px column and takes 60 px of a 362 px card. Setting it at --text-xs, the size the label style already uses elsewhere, costs it one line and costs the buyer nothing.
LM6Net effect: the grid moves from 779 to about 382, and the first price from 1023 to about 600. Rearrangement Two complete product photographs and two prices are then above the fold, where today 33 px of one photograph is. That puts us level with Bloom & Wild (386) instead of level with Interflora (745).
LM7Two cards across, not one: kept, and flagged as a founder choice. Kept Both comparators go one-up at 375 — Bloom & Wild at 327 px square, Interflora at 359×436 — which buys a much larger photograph per product and halves how many a thumb passes per screen. Two-up with LM5 reaches roughly 66% photograph, which is Bloom & Wild's ratio at half the size. The evidence does not decide this one, so it is drawn two-up and named here rather than changed quietly.
