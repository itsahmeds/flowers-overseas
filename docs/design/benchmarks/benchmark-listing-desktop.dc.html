The changes, argued
#ChangeKindThe argument
L1The intro paragraph's first sentence — "Our florist in the recipient's town makes the bouquet the morning it is delivered and hands it over in person." — is deleted from the top of the page.RemovalThe stronger argument: it is not lost, because it is already printed verbatim, word for word, 2,700 px lower on the same page, as the second sentence of the "How it reaches Poland" block. We were saying the same sentence twice on one document and charging the buyer 72 px of the first screen for the first copy. Its companion sentence, "every price is one number with VAT and delivery already inside it", is unique and is kept — it moves into the line under the heading, where it is read by everyone who reads the count.
L2The ordering-is-not-open notice moves from a 48 px paragraph below the intro to a Chip on the heading's own line, and loses its second clause.RearrangementEvery fact survives: that ordering is not open, that we are still choosing florists in Poland, and — in the heading line beside it — that the prices shown are the prices we would charge. It gains prominence rather than losing it: at the heading's baseline it is inside the first thing anyone reads, where at 431 it was the fourth paragraph of a stack. The chip is the shipped Chip primitive in its accent tone. This notice may never disappear and does not.
L3The <h2> "Everything we can make for Poland" and the ordering sentence merge into the <h1> block as one 20 px line carrying the count, the all-in-price fact and the ordering fact.RearrangementTwo headings 257 px apart say nearly the same thing; the second exists to carry id="our-selection", which spec 008 AC-9 fixes and which moves to the <h1> unchanged. Every word of the ordering sentence is kept: "our own order, not a ranking by sales, popularity or payment". Saves 137 px, adds a number (84) the page already computes and did not show at the top.
L4Net effect: the first photograph moves from 649 to about 343, and the first price from 1026 to about 700.RearrangementThe preamble goes from 466 px to 160, which puts us between FloraQueen (222) and Bloom & Wild (276) rather than behind Interflora (435). A full row of photographs and the first row of prices are then inside the fold, which is true of FloraQueen and of nobody else in the set.
L5The card, the 4:5 photograph, the "Includes VAT and delivery" line and the "Example arrangement" label: unchanged on desktop.KeptMeasured at 70.1% photograph, inside the field's range of 66.4–74.4 and above Bloom & Wild's. There is no evidence for a change and the label is a founder ruling and a spec 006 requirement.
L6The breadcrumb: unchanged.KeptIt costs 20 px and none of the four comparators prints one on a listing page. It stays because it is the BreadcrumbList source and ranking is priority 1. Recorded here so the founder can see it was measured and kept on purpose.
