## 2. What we take

1. **Fee printed on every selectable date.** FloraQueen is the only site in the set that puts the money next to the day: "Tomorrow 10 September €8,90". Source: floraqueen.com/products/a-pure-heart-mom (2026-09-09). Already our rule in `plan/04` §7 — this confirms nobody else does it, so it is a differentiator, not a copy.
2. **Numbered, gated steps for destination → date → size.** InternetFlorist's "1. Enter Your Delivery City / 2. Select Delivery Date / 3. Select Your Size" with Add-to-Basket disabled until a city is chosen is the cleanest expression of "one decision per screen" in the field (`competitors-global-and-serp.md` §1). We already gate on destination; adopt the explicit numbering and the disabled-CTA affordance.
3. **Cutoff stated in the recipient's time zone.** 1-800-Flowers: "by the following times in your recipient's time zone: M-F: 3pm, Saturday: 2pm, Sunday: 12pm" (1800flowers.com/customer-service-faq, 2026-09-09). Every European site states its own zone. Ours must say "14:00 Warsaw time".
4. **Stem-count tiers, not adjectives.** Interflora UK "18 Red Roses £91 / 24 Red Roses £115" and 1-800-Flowers "12 or 18 long stem" are checkable; FloraQueen's Classic/Premium/Delux and Euroflorist's Prisvennlig/Standard/Premium are not.
5. **Substitution in plain language on the PDP itself.** Fleurop ("Alle Fotos sind Beispielfotos, jeder Strauß ist ein Unikat…") and Euroflorist PL/NO do this; FloraQueen, Interflora UK and Bloom & Wild bury it or omit it.
6. **A regional-holiday warning attached to the date picker.** Fleurop's warning that the destination region may have a local holiday on the chosen date is exactly the cross-border failure mode we own; ours can be factual per destination rather than a generic "it might be".
7. **Delivery-confirmation promise stated on the PDP.** Euroflorist NO: "Du mottar en SMS med leveringsbekreftelse når blomstene er levert." We promise a photo, which is stronger — say it in the same place.
8. **Genuine per-product rating next to the price.** Bloom & Wild's "4.85 (369)" / "4,77 (227)" is the only per-product review presentation in the set that looks like it belongs to the product. Gate ours at ≥3 verified product reviews (`plan/04` §7).
9. **Explicit "what the price does not include".** Euroflorist PL says "Cena nie obejmuje wazonu" and lists what *is* included (card, chocolates). Even with all-inclusive pricing we should name the excluded thing (vase) rather than let the photo imply it.
10. **An availability state, not a dead end.** FloraQueen's "This product is currently unavailable in <country>" overlay plus a link onward is better than a silent empty grid — matches our demo-country honesty rule (`plan/04` §6).

## 3. What we drop and why

1. **Struck-through "regular price" on every tier, permanently** (FloraQueen: €100,00 → €62,90 on every size). A reference price that was never charged is a misleading omission under the Price Indication Directive and the UCPD/Omnibus rules; it is also exactly the "invented metric" the founder ruled out (`docs/design/homepage-v1/README.md`, round 6). No struck-through prices unless the higher price was genuinely charged for the required prior period.
2. **A shared review pool presented as this product's reviews** (FloraQueen's 1,770 reviews repeating across products). Omnibus requires that reviews presented as originating from consumers who used the product actually do. Our rule is real-only, product-scoped, verified-buyer, hidden below threshold.
3. **Delivery fee revealed only after the date is chosen, or only at checkout** (1-800-Flowers "will be shown" at date selection; Interflora UK "product prices exclude delivery charges"; Fleurop "zzgl. Versandkosten"). Drip pricing — banned by the UK DMCC and against `plan/07` §4. Price shown = price charged, VAT and delivery in.
4. **A percentage service fee disclosed in fine print** (Euroflorist NO "serviceavgift på cirka 5–10 %"). Honest that it exists, but "circa 5–10 %" is not a price. Ours is inside one number.
5. **Substitution "without notice"** (Interflora UK T&Cs). We commit the opposite in `plan/04` §7: equal-or-higher value in the same colours and style, and we never swap the main flower without telling the buyer.
6. **Date and address deferred to basket/checkout** (Interflora UK, Bloom & Wild, 1-800-Flowers, Aquarelle). It produces the "sorry, not available tomorrow" reveal after the buyer has committed. Date lives on the PDP.
7. **Client-rendered PDP body, price and schema** (Interflora UK ~2.9 KB server HTML; Bloom & Wild ~4 s to content; 1-800-Flowers date/add-ons). Breaks priority #1 and mobile CWV. Server-render title, canonical, hreflang, price, schema.
8. **Adjective tiers with no visible difference** (Classic/Premium/Delux, Prisvennlig/Standard/Premium, Mały/Średni/Duży). Stems instead.
9. **Fake-ish scarcity and generic urgency** ("Send love today", "Need flowers to arrive like, right now?!", "BESTSELGER" on a fixed middle tier). Countdown only from real cutoff data (`plan/07` §4).
10. **Add-on prices that only appear at the next step** (Blume2000 pattern; Interflora's robots-blocked `/finishing-touches`). Add-on prices sit on the PDP with images, defaulting to off.

## 4. Open questions for the founder

1. **Bestseller labelling.** Euroflorist marks the middle tier "BESTSELGER" permanently. Do we show a bestseller badge at all before we have real order counts per destination? (Current design gates "Most sent this week" on real orders — should tier badges follow the same gate?)
2. **Vase policy.** Every competitor excludes the vase and says so. Do we sell vase as an add-on (per `plan/04` §7) *and* state "vase not included" on the default view, or photograph bouquets without vases entirely?
3. **Same-day on the PDP.** FloraQueen offers no same-day at all; Interflora/Euroflorist/Poczta all lead with it. Is same-day promised on the PDP from Phase 1 for Poland only, or held back until partner capacity is measured?
4. **Struck-through pricing for genuine promotions.** If a founding-customer offer runs, do we show a prior price (with the compliant 30-day reference) or only "Founding offer − £X"?
5. **Photo-proof wording strength.** Aquarelle promises a photo *at dispatch* and a 48-hour complaint window; we promise a photo *on delivery*. Do we also commit to a complaint window on the PDP, and how long?
