Premium interactions: the morph, the card preview, soft loading, the phone gallery and the route line · system · spec 042 · 2026-10-20 · [internal]
Purpose
The founder (2026-10-19): “anything else that we missed motion or design or animation wise?”, then “fix and do all this tooo.” and “we will only launch after that bro.” Spec 042 turns six ideas into limits and values; the founder approved it on 2026-10-20, verbatim: “all defaults, keep going”. This sheet fixes the look of the five that build now, each with its frames and its reduced-motion form, so 042-1, 042-2, 042-4, 042-5 and 042-6 can be planned. It continues system/motion.dc.html's numbering: motions 14 to 18.
Not drawn
Idea 3, the second photo on hover, is not drawn. It waits on spec 042 §13 Q6 (are the 84 approved detail photographs good enough as the second photo?), which has no default and is the founder's call, and on 042-V (the detail photographs at the hero's width ladder). When Q6 is answered, its cell goes on system/motion-premium.dc.html beside motion 6, which it layers on.
Where it applies
Motion 14: a product card on any listing, hub or home rail, to that product's page, from lg (64rem, 1,024 px) up. Motion 15: the product page's card section and checkout step 2. Motion 16: every photograph drawn through MediaAsset. Motion 17: the product page gallery below lg. Motion 18: the country guide page's first screen (/{locale}/send-flowers-to/{country}), from md (48rem, 768 px) up. A system sheet: no URL of its own, no index status. The page artboards that carry these in place: wireframes/product-desktop.dc.html, wireframes/product-mobile.dc.html, wireframes/checkout-desktop.dc.html, wireframes/checkout-mobile.dc.html, wireframes/corridor-country-desktop.dc.html, wireframes/corridor-country-mobile.dc.html (each gains a “Spec 042” band).
Data it reads
Real catalogue photographs, the R2 files the site serves: Amber Hour fo-bq-001-hero and fo-bq-001-detail, Baltic Dawn fo-bq-003-hero, Blush Terrace fo-bq-021-hero, with their seed/data/alt/en-gb.json alt text. The placeholders and fill colours come from seed/data/media-lqip.json, which pnpm media:lqip has not generated yet: the placeholder is drawn as a blurred copy of the same file, and the fill as --color-photo-stop-3, a stand-in for each asset's own mean colour. The detail photograph is drawn from its 384 px file, the only one that exists until 042-V gives it the hero's ladder. Prices are the ones system/motion-premium.dc.html and wireframes/country-shop-desktop.dc.html draw for these three cards; the phone sheet repeats wireframes/product-mobile.dc.html.

Reused verbatim, no new key (spec 042 L11): product.card.legend “What should the card say?”, product.card.printed and checkout.card.preview “Printed on our card · included”, catalog.addon.card.description (the sample sentence), checkout.card.label “Card message”, checkout.card.previewLabel “Preview of the printed card”, checkout.card.signAs “Sign as”, checkout.card.counter, a11y.media.gallery “Product images”, a11y.media.imageOfCount “Image {index} of {total}”, media.provenance.aiExample, catalog.price.allIn “all in”. States
Each motion: rest, the frames of the motion, its end, and the stills: reduced motion, no JavaScript, an unsupported browser, a phone, a first load. Loading is motion 16 itself. No motion here has an error state: each one fails to the page as it is today.
Binding, all five
L1 PropertiesOnly transform and opacity animate; the route also animates stroke-dashoffset on its inline SVG. The morph replaces the UA group keyframes (which animate width and height) with transform-only keyframes (spec 004 §14 A-founder-2026-10-20a clause 2).
L2 CLS0. Every moving layer is absolutely positioned inside a box that exists at rest.
L3 LCPOn a first load, a reload and a JavaScript-off load the LCP element paints at its final position and opacity in its first frame; nothing adds blocking="render". The morph is the one exception, on a card click from lg up only (A-founder-2026-10-20a).
L5 BytesPer idea, Brotli: morph 400 B JS (deferred, pages with cards only), 400 B CSS, 60 B HTML per card · card preview 160 B in the product island, 160 B in CardCounter, 300 B CSS · soft loading 200 B CSS, 4,096 B HTML per page · gallery 400 B in the product island or 0 B in CSS, 300 B CSS · route 300 B CSS, 1,024 B uncompressed SVG. All six: 2,048 B CSS. Route budgets unchanged (131,072 B; 133,120 B on product pages); one product island, at most 2,048 B.
L7 Reduced motionUnder prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: no transform, position or stroke change over time, nothing iterates, at most an opacity change of 120 ms. Each cell draws its reduced form.
L10 Tokens--duration-fast 120, --duration-base 200, --duration-slow 320, --duration-draw 600 ms; --ease-standard, --ease-emphasised, --ease-photo. No raw duration or colour.
L9, L12, L13No text, link, alt, canonical, robots, hreflang, sitemap or schema value changes or moves to the client. Every photograph and placeholder is an example arrangement under the existing label. The card is printed, never called handwritten. Nothing reads IP, Accept-Language or a geo header.
On the canvas
Each stage loops so it can be watched; in the build each motion plays once per trigger. The small frames under each stage are the instants named in their captions. The canvas honours the viewer's own reduced-motion setting too. Pointer and finger marks are drawn for the reader and do not ship.

Motion 14 · idea 1 · task 042-1 · from lg up

The card's photograph carries the buyer into the product page

Only the photograph moves. The rest of the old page goes at once and the new page is there at once: no root crossfade. The card's 4∶5 box and the hero's 4∶5 box share a ratio from lg up, so a uniform scale lands it exactly, with no stretch and no crop.

TriggerActivating a product card's link (click or Enter) that starts a same-origin document navigation, at a viewport of lg or wider, under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, in a browser with cross-document view transitions.
Opt-inBoth documents: @view-transition { navigation: auto; } inside (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) and (min-width: 64rem). The root carries view-transition-name: none on both, so nothing but the photograph is captured.
The nameThe product hero carries the product's name. On the old page a pageswap handler (≤ 400 B, the deferred motion module) puts the same name on the activated card's photograph box only, never a static name per card: a product can sit in two home rails, and two boxes with one name abort the transition. A click before the handler loads navigates as today.
The flightCustom group keyframes, transform only: from the card's box, translate() plus one uniform scale() about transform-origin: 0 0 (100% 0 under dir="rtl"), to none on the hero's box. --duration-slow 320 ms, --ease-emphasised, once. Its end box equals the hero's box within 1 px (AC-7).
The imagesBoth snapshots fill the group with object-fit: cover. The old one (the card's photograph) stays under; the new one (the hero) fades in over it, opacity 0 to 1 in --duration-base 200 ms --ease-standard. If the hero has not decoded at reveal, the card's photograph carries the flight and the hero's fill colour (motion 16) sits under both.
CornersBoth ends are --radius-photo 18 px. In flight the radius scales with the snapshot (about 9 px at the start); accepted, because the keyframes are transform only.
Everything elseThe old page's other cards are not captured and are not seen during the flight. The thumbnails, the name, the price and the form on the new page are at rest from the first frame. Focus lands as on any navigation.
Click · old page
Card 3 under the pointer, in motion 6's held state. pageswap names its photograph box.
0 ms · new page
The new page is painted. The photograph starts at the card's box; the hero's box (dashed, drawn for the reader) is empty under it.
About 160 ms
Half the time, most of the way: the emphasised curve front-loads, so the photograph is near the hero by 160 ms and settles softly.
320 ms · rest
The group ends on the hero's box and the hero itself shows: opacity 1, transform none (AC-44 as amended).
Reduced motion

No view transition at all: the opt-in sits inside no-preference. The product page loads as today, hero at rest in its first frame.

Phone and tablet, below lg

No morph (advisor fix F2): the opt-in sits inside min-width: 64rem. A card tap navigates as today and the hero paints at rest. Phones get it only by a later amendment, after spec 023's field data shows its mobile LCP cost.

Not from a card

A first load, a reload, an entry from another site, a typed URL, the header, the breadcrumb, pagination, Back and Forward: no name is set on the old page, so nothing flies, and the hero keeps A23 clause 11 exactly as written.

Unsupported browser

Firefox, and Safari before 18.2: the @view-transition rule is ignored and the card link navigates exactly as today. Without JavaScript the same: pageswap never runs, so no name exists.

Motion 18 · idea 6 · task 042-6 · the country guide page, from md up

A line draws itself from you to the place you are sending to

Once, half a second after load, a dashed arc draws from an unlabelled dot at the inline-start (you, wherever you are: we never guess it) to a dot beside the country's dates. It sits in the margin beside the H1, never over its words, and says nothing in words. It is decorative: aria-hidden, no text, no link.

WhereThe country guide page's first screen only (spec 042 §13 Q8 (a), default), at md and up, in the gutter between the H1's column (1 to 6) and the dates (9 to 12), its top 40 px under the eyebrow. Its box never meets the H1's box (AC-19). Hidden below md (display: none).
WhatOne inline SVG, viewBox 180 × 120, at most 1,024 B: a 2 px --color-accent dashed arc (dash 6, gap 7, round caps); the origin, an unfilled 5 px dot with a 1.5 px --color-ink-2 ring, never labelled; the destination, a filled 5 px --color-accent dot. No <text>, no <image>, no <a>. The H1 already names the country (Q8 (b), default: no origin country).
The drawMotion 5's mechanism: a mask path in the page colour (--color-paper) covers the dashed route and unrolls by stroke-dashoffset, 500 ms after load, --duration-draw 600 ms, --ease-emphasised, once, ending drawn. Then the destination dot's opacity 0 to 1, --duration-base 200 ms. CSS only: it runs with JavaScript off too.
RTLmirror-in-rtl: on /ar-XB the origin is at the inline-start, the right, and the arc rises to the left (AC-5).
Not the LCPAn SVG path is never an LCP candidate, and the H1's text never moves.
