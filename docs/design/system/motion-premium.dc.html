Hover and press: now and premium, side by side · system · 2026-10-19 · [internal]
Purpose
The founder (2026-10-19): “hover still doesn't have that animation yet bro.. the interaction with the site doesn't feel premium.” Ruling: “premium A”, the approved hover (motion 6 on system/motion.dc.html) and a more premium version drawn side by side, both moving, before anything is built. The founder chose the right column, 2026-10-19, verbatim: “go with the premium proposed version”. Spec 004 §14 A-founder-2026-10-19e adopts every value of its table and its token. The right column is approved and binding; the left column is what shipped before and is superseded. TASK-277 builds the right column pixel for pixel.
Where it applies
Every product card link on every listing (country shop, country category, country occasion, the category hubs, the home rows): ProductCard's a[data-fo-card-motion]. Every pill button: Button variants primary, accent, secondary, danger and the notice actions in layout/noticeShell.ts. No URL of its own; a system sheet, not a page; no index status.
Data it reads
None. Three real catalogue cards exactly as wireframes/country-shop-desktop.dc.html draws them at en-gb, Poland: Amber Hour (fo-bq-001), Baltic Dawn (fo-bq-003), Blush Terrace (fo-bq-021), the R2 files the site serves. Real copy only: catalog.price.allIn “all in”, media.provenance.aiExample, nav.send “Send flowers”. No [slot] is left.
States
Rest · hover in · hover held · hover out · press · release (all looped in §1 and §2, live in §3) · the priority card, a coarse pointer, reduced motion and keyboard focus (§4).
Binding, both columns
Transform and opacity only (spec 004 AC-44, T-48 scans every @keyframes and transition-property). The shadow is a pre-painted layer whose opacity changes; box-shadow never animates. The photograph scales inside its clipped box; the lift is a transform, so nothing reflows and CLS stays 0. No motion on the priority card (the page's LCP photograph) or its ancestors: it keeps carrying no data-fo-card-motion. Hover motion only on (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). prefers-reduced-motion: reduce turns every transform off; the name's line and the pill's layer show by opacity, at once (§4).

Token
--duration-hover: 500ms, the only new one, approved with the column (A-founder-2026-10-19e clause 3). It is written as a literal on this sheet; the build adds it to docs/design/system/tokens.css and src/app/globals.css in one PR. The 500 ms exception (clause 4, now in the Binding of system/motion.dc.html): only the hover-in and hover-out of a moving card's link, photograph and shadow layer, in rules whose selector contains [data-fo-card-motion]. Never the name's line, a pill, anything without a pointer's hover, anything at first paint, or the LCP element and its ancestors.
For the implementer
ProductCard gains two aria-hidden elements: a shadow layer as a sibling of the photo's clipped ratio box, outside the clip, inside the link (so it lifts with it), and the name's line inside the heading after the <bdi>. Both are omitted on the priority card. The shipped CSS keeps transition-property to transform (link, image, line, pill) and opacity (shadow layer, pill layer). No will-change left on at rest. Hover-out uses the same duration and curve as hover-in.
On the canvas
§1 simulates the pointer resting on card 1, then 2, then 3, 2 s each, both columns in step, so the two are seen at the same instant. §2 loops a hover, a press and a release every 3.2 s. §3 is the real CSS: move a mouse over it. The two small frames under each column are rest and held. No mobile artboard: a phone has no hover, and its press (§4) is unchanged apart from the release.

The first photograph on the page. No motion in either column: no lift, zoom, shadow layer or drawn line, and no transition on it or its ancestors (AC-44). The name still underlines on hover with text-decoration, as today.

Phone and touch · coarse pointer
Send flowers

No hover motion at all: the card does not lift, zoom or cast a shadow on a tap. The pill keeps its press, scale(0.98); in the premium column it settles back over 200 ms instead of 120 ms.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: no lift, zoom or press, and the shadow layer stays at opacity: 0. The name's line rests at scaleX(1) with opacity: 0 and on hover shows at opacity: 1 at once, with no transition; the pill's layer shows at once. No transform changes under reduce (A-founder-2026-10-19e clause 5; A24 clause 9 (c)).

Keyboard focus

Unchanged in both columns: the shared focus ring (--focus-ring, --focus-offset) around the whole card link or the pill. Focus starts no motion.
