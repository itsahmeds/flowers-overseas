# Spec 042, premium interactions (draft, PR 284)

Advisor memo, 2026-10-20. Not legal advice.

## 1. In one sentence
Six small motion ideas, fenced by strict byte and Core Web Vitals limits that protect ranking well, but the spec misreads the photos we already have, lets the photo morph touch phone LCP on our money pages, and its defaults leave iPhones with dots that never follow the swipe.

## 2. Verdict
**Build now:** 2 card preview, 4 soft loading, 5 phone gallery, 6 route line (cheap, CSS or tiny, no LCP risk). **Build now on desktop only, phones later:** 1 morph. **Later, not a launch blocker:** 3 second photo on hover. **Drop:** none.

Fixes (each checkable):
- **F1 §1, §5.3.5, §13 Q6, §12 042-3.** "Most products carry one photograph" is wrong: `seed/data/media.json` has an approved `productDetail` photo for 84 products, but `media-variants.json` gives each only a **384 px** variant (heroes go to 828). Restate Q6 as "are these detail photos good enough as the second photo?", and make 042-3 and 042-5 depend on a `media:variants` run that gives detail photos the hero ladder, not on new imagery.
- **F2 §5.3.1, AC-8.** Put `@view-transition` inside `(min-width: lg)` as well as `no-preference`; phones get the morph only after spec 023's RUM shows the LCP cost. Choose the `pageswap` option (name on the clicked card only), and add to AC-8 a page where one product appears twice (home rails), since two elements with one name abort the transition [inferred, from the View Transitions spec].
- **F3 §13 Q4.** Change the default to **(b) display face on both**, unless spec 011 makes every florist print in Caveat. The preview must show what is printed.
- **F4 §13 Q7.** Default (a) means iPhones never see the dots follow, because Safari has no `::scroll-marker` (MDN: not Baseline). New default: if bytes are short, the gallery dots' scroll listener wins over idea 2's 160 B fade.
- **F5 §5.2, AC-15.** `pnpm media:lqip` reads the published variants, not `.local` originals (those exist only in the main checkout). Measure AC-15's 4,096 B cap on the home too, not only a 12-card listing.
- **F6 §5.3.3.** No hover layer for TASK-250's three products (FO-PT-005, FO-GS-002, FO-GS-010) until that task closes.

## 3. The four hats
- **Building:** (1) Seven breaker PRs now gate launch (§12). (2) Ideas 2 and 5 share about 1,100 B of product-page headroom (§5.3.0); measure before building. (3) F5.
- **Google:** (1) Idea 1 on phones: a 120,000-pageview RUM test found view transitions add about 70 ms to mobile LCP, about 5 ms on desktop (third-party blog). Lab Lighthouse and AC-9 in CI cannot see it; CrUX can, on product pages. A new site has little CrUX data yet [inferred], so the risk grows with traffic. (2) Otherwise no concern: no URL, canonical or schema change, and L9 with AC-6 keeps the crawl identical.
- **Law and compliance:** (1) Card face honesty (F3): a preview in a hand face, printed in another face, could read as misleading [inferred; a lawyer is not needed if F3 is taken]. (2) Wrong photos (F6). (3) Accessibility: L7 and AC-4 cover reduced motion; no concern.
- **Customer:** (1) iPhone dots (F4). (2) A 384 px photo swiped full width on a phone looks soft (F1). (3) On a phone the keyboard may push the preview off screen [inferred]; the designer should check on a real iPhone.

## 4. Questions the founder should ask
1. **Q1:** Is the morph on desktop first, phones after we can measure it, acceptable? (Recommended: yes.)
2. **Q2:** Can launch go with 2, 4, 5 and 6, leaving phone morph and idea 3 for after? (Recommended: yes.)
3. **Q4:** Will every florist print the card in Caveat? If not, pick (b).
4. **Q6:** Are the 84 approved detail photos good enough as second photos once enlarged?
5. **Q7:** If bytes run short, do the gallery dots come before the preview fade? (Recommended: yes.)

## 5. What looks right
The shared limits (L1 to L13), per-idea byte caps and the colour-only fill on the LCP image protect ranking well. A CSS-only route line that reads no IP, and a one-line kill switch per idea, are the right shape.
