# Spec 043, site clarity: advisor memo (2026-10-20)

Subject: `specs/043-site-clarity.md` (draft, PR 292) and its §14 pointers in specs 004, 007 and 008.
This memo is advice, not approval and not legal advice.

## 1. In one sentence
A sound, low-risk clean-up that keeps every indexable URL as it is and points the header at the
country you are already shopping in; two small fixes make it safe to build and quicker to see.

## 2. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES
1. **§5.3 A, "How it stays static":** add "the header slot has a page or catch-all for every route
   under `[locale]`, so a client-side navigation never keeps the previous page's row", and add that
   case to T-03. Next.js keeps an unmatched slot's old content on a client-side navigation and only
   uses `default.tsx` on a full load ([Next.js docs](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/file-conventions/parallel-routes)).
   Today every public link is a plain `<a>` (`next/link` only in `AdminChrome.tsx`), so it does not
   bite yet, but one future `<Link>` would leave a Poland header on the home.
2. **§12 "Order and dependencies":** say plainly that task 1 (header) and task 3 (photographs) do
   not wait for TASK-239 or TASK-241. TASK-239 is still `todo` behind TASK-228 to 232, so as written
   the founder's "fix that up" could wait weeks for its two most visible parts.

## 3. The four hats
**Building**
- Task 1 copies the `@switcher` slot (four route files plus `default.tsx`): proven here, but the
  route tree is mirrored by hand, so a new page type needs a new slot file (fix 1 guards it).
- Task 3 is tied to spec 004 A-founder-2026-10-19b's build row, which has no row in `TASKS.md` yet.
- No ADR contradicted; no data, migration or new key.

**Google**
- C2 follows `plan/02` §11 (authority to country money pages) and changes no URL, canonical,
  sitemap or hreflang (§6, AC-6). Pages stay prerendered with no `Vary` or cookie (AC-4): good.
- While countries are not live (ADR-0007), the indexable guides (`/en/send-flowers-to/poland`)
  will link their header to `noindex` country pages instead of the five indexable hubs. Hubs keep
  links from the home, hubs and footer (AC-6), so the loss is small `[inferred]`; it turns into a
  gain the day a country goes live.

**Law and compliance**
- Price display holds: one all-in price per card, "every price here is the price you will pay" kept
  on page 1, the site-wide "Prices include delivery and VAT" bar on every page (§8).
- Photos-first is a new sort rule; the ranking line ("the order we chose ourselves") stays true.
  A lawyer should confirm that line once, before launch, as part of the wider consumer-law review.

**Customer**
- No concern with the model: Roses on a Poland page now shows Poland's roses with prices.
- "Our selection" will name both the header item (the whole shop) and the home row (five bouquets):
  fine, but the founder should see it is the same words for two things.
- The US WhatsApp number in a European shop costs trust on a phone (Q7).

## 4. Questions the founder should ask (mapped to §13)
1. Q1: Am I happy that, on a country's pages, the header's Roses, Plants, Birthday and so on stay in
   that country? (Recommend A, the default.)
2. Q3 and D: How long can "Ordering opens soon" stand before it reads as untrue? (Default no date:
   agree, but set yourself a month to revisit.)
3. Q4: Is "Our selection" right for both the header item and the home row? (Default approve.)
4. Q7: When will we have a Polish or Dutch WhatsApp number? (Default keep: agree only until launch.)
5. Outside the spec: is there a task row for the two gift sets priced the same as their bouquets
   alone (§1.1)? That is a money error a buyer would see.

Q2 (no cookie), Q5 (drop "P.S."), Q6 (A, photos first) and Q8 (say the state once): agree with the
defaults.

## 5. What looks right

"Guide · not delivering yet" seven times, "Our picks until real orders start", 20 mixed chips).
The fixes remove words rather than add pages, so the ranking risk is close to nil.
