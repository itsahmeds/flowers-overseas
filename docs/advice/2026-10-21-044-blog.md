# Advice: spec 044, blog in the CMS (PR 312)

Not legal advice; a lawyer or accountant has not reviewed this.

## 1. In one sentence
The spec puts blog posts in the admin on the same approve-then-publish machinery as the bouquets, and it is sound on ranking and honesty; it needs five small fixes, and the founder should know nothing ships until five unbuilt CMS tasks (TASK-292 to TASK-296) are done.

## 2. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES
Approve once these are made. "044 defaults" stays valid except where marked.
1. **§2.3 rule 5 and AC-6.** The relay rule has no exemption and the word lists include everyday words ("partner", "held", "hold", and "offer", "take", "accept" near "florist"; `src/config/honesty.ts`). A funeral or name-day post will trip them. Change: the refusal names the matched word and sentence, and AC-6 gets a fixture ("flowers for your partner") that asserts the message shows the word. Default unchanged.
2. **§2.2 authors and Q4.** Add: `display_name` is the person's own name, and the author form records "this is my own name". Reason: outreach is signed "Adam Demir", and `plan/02` §12 says "no fabricated personas". Clarifies Q4; **changes Q4 only if you want a pen name on posts.**
3. **§2.3 rule 3.** Also require 70 % distinctness from the guide text of each tagged country's corridor page. Post 1 ("how to send flowers to Poland from the UK") would otherwise compete with the Poland money page. Tightens Q6 slightly.
4. **§12 "Requirements on other specs".** Name TASK-295 (removal-guard exemption) and TASK-293 (`blog_post_revision` kind in `attest()`) and put the clauses in their briefs now; both are `todo`, so it costs nothing today and rework later.
5. **§2.7 and Appendix A.** Add one plain end-of-post link to the corridor guide of the first country tag, so a phone reader has a next step. **Changes Q13: ten strings, not nine.** No price, no buy control, Q5 untouched.

## 3. The four hats
**Building**
- Every dependency is `todo` (TASK-292, 293, 295, 296, 299). The blog cannot be live until that chain is built (§12, Q12).
- Task 1 of §12 (migration, four triggers, ledger kind, rules, markdown, shared function, seed) is likely too big for one PR. Split data and triggers from rules and markdown `[inferred]`.
- Blog unpublish needs an exemption in spec 012b's removal guard (§2.6); a second spec's rule changes an approved spec.

**Google**
- Same text at `/en` and `/en-gb`, each self-canonical, with reciprocal hreflang (§6, AC-9). That matches the plan and Google's guidance for same-language regional variants ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)). Google says untranslated versions can count as duplicates, so it may show one of the two; low risk.
- Slice A posts get links in from the footer and index only; the corridor-to-posts link waits for slice B (§2.10).
- Server-rendered, no client JavaScript, `BlogPosting` equals visible text, 500-word minimum, no `Offer` or `FAQPage`: no concern.

**Law and compliance**
- Price display holds: price literals are refused and no product card exists in slice A (§2.3, §8). No concern.
- Authorship honesty: see fix 2. The RoPA row and takedown runbook are planned (AC-21). No PII is added; author pages will use a number in the URL.
- Sign-off: the privacy and lawful-basis wording for author names (§8 flow 1) is for your lawyer after the domain is live.

**Customer**
- A reader finishing a post has only in-body links (fix 5). The "not open yet" line under every post is honest but will meet every reader first (§2.7).
