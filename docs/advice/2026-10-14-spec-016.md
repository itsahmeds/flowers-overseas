# Spec 016: routing and notifications (absorbs 017), draft of 2026-10-14

**1. In one sentence.** This spec picks the florist, re-offers when one declines or goes quiet, and sends every order email; without it no paid order can be fulfilled. It follows A6 and specs 010 to 012 closely, but it needs fixes first: one email-retry bug, one night-time routing bug and one rollback contradiction would each cause failed sends or wrongly cancelled orders once real orders start.

**2. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES.** Approve once these are in:
- **F1 (§5.2 Notifications; AC-19, T-19).** A retry re-renders the email, and a florist email mints a fresh sign-in token each time, so the body changes. Resend refuses a reused `Idempotency-Key` with a different body (`409 invalid_idempotent_request`) and keeps keys for 24 hours ([Resend](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys)). As written, a failed florist send is retried until it goes `dead`. Fix: treat that 409 as "sent". Also say that a repeat which finds the `notification_delivery` row still `queued` sends again rather than skipping it, because "a repeat is a no-op" would drop an email after a crash between insert and send. Add both cases to T-19.
- **F2 (§5.2 `offerWindow`; AC-4, T-4).** "Same-day: offer now, SLA 30 min" is listed before the night rule. A same-day order paid at 06:00 would then be offered to sleeping florists every 30 minutes, run out of candidates (5 offers) by about 08:30 and be cancelled. Fix: the night rule wins. A same-day order secured 20:00–07:30 is offered at 07:30 with its SLA ending at 08:00. Add that case to AC-4.
- **F3 (§12 Rollback; §5.2 `order.no_partner_expire`).** "Turn `routing.auto` off (exhausted orders wait for the founder)" is not true. With it off, every order goes to `no_partner_available` and is cancelled 2 hours later, and before spec 020 nothing can reroute it. Fix: while `routing.auto` is off for a country, the expiry waits until the routing deadline. Or change the rollback text to say plainly that the switch cancels and refunds.
- **F4 (§12 Dependencies).** Add the spec 010 amendment (place writes `order.locale_code`) as a dependency of tasks 2 and 3. Without it, the `'en'` default sends every buyer email, Polish ones included, in English, and `en-gb` buyers get the wrong variant.
- **F5 (§12 Tasks).** Split task 1 (20 ACs, the migration, jobs, the withdrawal handler and an edit inside spec 011's files) into (a) the pure functions plus `canServe`, (b) the jobs, the handler and `manualReroute`, and (c) withdrawal plus the inbox stage. As one PR, it is too much to review and break in two rounds.
