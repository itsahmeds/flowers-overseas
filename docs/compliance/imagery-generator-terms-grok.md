## Answers

This record quotes short passages only. The full text is at the x.ai URLs in the Terms read row, as read on 2026-09-30.

1. **Generator and model.** `generator: "xAI Grok Imagine"`. The manifest names no model version,
   so `generatorModel` is `Grok Imagine` as well, until xAI publishes one.
2. **Commercial use — allowed, per the FAQ.** The FAQ says outputs may be used "as you wish,
   including for commercial use". The Terms say nothing that restricts it by plan tier.
3. **Ownership — ours.** The Terms define Input and Output together as "User Content" and say the
   user retains ownership of it, to the extent the law allows. The FAQ: "You own the Inputs and
   Outputs."
4. **Licence we grant xAI.** Irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide and royalty-free, over what we submit
   (the prompts). The prompts are already public in this repository, so this discloses nothing.
5. **Attribution — the Terms require it; we do not give it. This is a founder decision, not a
   reading of the terms.** Section 4 of the Terms says a user of Output is "required to obtain our
   permission and attribute" it, as detailed in the Brand Guidelines. The Brand Guidelines ask for
   "Created with Grok" to be displayed "wherever the Grok-generated material is published". The FAQ
   softens this to a request ("We do ask that you attribute"). **Founder, 2026-09-30:** keep the
   Grok photos and show no "Created with Grok" line. The risk accepted is that xAI treats the
   attribution clause as binding and asks for the images to be credited or taken down. The
   mitigation, if that happens, is to regenerate the 62 assets from their committed prompt records
   in ChatGPT, whose terms need no attribution (`imagery-generator-terms.md` clause 5).
6. **"Obtain our permission".** Read literally, the same sentence also asks for permission to use
   Output. The FAQ says the opposite. This record does not resolve the conflict; it is part of the
   risk the founder accepted in clause 5.
7. **Exclusivity — none.** The Terms warn that outputs "may not be unique". Accepted, as for
   ChatGPT: the images illustrate a style, not the delivered product.
8. **AI disclosure.** The Terms say a disclosure that the content is AI-generated may be required
   or applied by xAI. Our honesty label (spec 006 AC-17) already discloses it on every page.
9. **Training on inputs.** The prompts may be used for training unless "Improve the model" is off
   (grok.com: Settings → Data; app: Settings → Data Controls). No personal data is in the prompts
   (spec 006 §8).
10. **C2PA.** Present on every Grok original. The re-encode strips it (`derivativeC2pa:
    "stripped"`), as for ChatGPT; the originals keep it in the Drive store and in
    `.local/imagery/originals/`.
11. **Restricted use.** Nothing in the consumer Terms read touches flowers, funerals or product
    illustration.
12. **Governing law.** For European consumers the Terms choose Irish law and Irish courts. We use
    Grok as a business, so these consumer protections may not apply to us; noted, not relied on.
