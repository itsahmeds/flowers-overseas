## 5. What the files themselves say (read from the PNG C2PA manifest, 2026-09-18)

Every delivered original is a PNG with a single `caBX` (JUMBF) chunk carrying a C2PA 2.2 manifest
(`org.contentauth.c2pa_rs 0.79.2`). The claim generator is **"OpenAI Media Service API"**; the
`c2pa.created` action names `softwareAgent` **`gpt-image` version `2.0`** with
`digitalSourceType = trainedAlgorithmicMedia`, and a `c2pa.watermarked.unbound` action records the
SynthID watermark. The pipeline note: `seed/media-variants.ts` re-encodes to AVIF/WebP/JPEG with
`sharp`, which does **not** carry a PNG `caBX` chunk across — see the carry-forward in
`imagery-generator-terms.md` §5.
