## Escalations

One dated bullet per escalation: the question, who it went to, the answer or `open`.

- 2026-11-04 (builder CSP262, to the orchestrator; **answered**): **which response carries step 3's policy?** Step 3 shares `/{locale}/checkout` with steps 1 and 2 and the `demo`/`closed` screens, and the page decides the screen and the mode from the draft row and the flags (database). A page cannot set a response header, and the proxy runs before the page and reads no database (spec 001 §11; `tests/unit/csp.test.ts` "keeps the policy out of src/proxy.ts"). So no mechanism can give step 3 in `test`/`live` the Stripe policy while steps 1, 2, `demo` and `closed` at the same URL carry none, as AC-2 reads. Options: **(a, recommended)** amend AC-2: where a Stripe step is registered (both Stripe keys in the env, which the proxy can read), every `/{locale}/checkout` page response carries `payStepCspHeader` (nonce, `'strict-dynamic'`, Stripe origins; enforced under `CSP_REPORT_ONLY=false`); deployments without the keys keep today's policy byte for byte; no HTML outside step 3 names Stripe. Cost: steps 1 and 2 may frame and connect to three Stripe origins; gain: a nonce on every checkout step, as spec 010 §5.4 already asks. (b) give step 3 its own address (e.g. `/{locale}/checkout/pay`), amending spec 010's "three steps on one URL". (c) read the draft's mode in the proxy, amending spec 001 §11. Any option also puts the policy on the **request** headers (Next reads the nonce from there to nonce its own scripts) and drops the csp.test.ts proxy assertion.
  - **Answer (orchestrator, under the founder's delegation, 2026-11-04): option (a).** When both Stripe keys are configured, every `/{locale}/checkout` page sends `payStepCspValue`; without both keys, checkout keeps today's policy. Built in PR 494.
