## What a cookie is {#what-a-cookie-is}

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep, so that it can remember a choice you made when you come back. Your browser's own storage works the same way, and we treat it exactly like a cookie: everything on this page covers both.

## The cookies we use {#the-cookies-we-use}

"Set today" means this website writes it now. "Planned" means a later part of the website will write it. We list it now so that nothing is ever set that this page has not named first.

**The country you choose.** When you choose a country to send flowers to, we keep that choice in the fo_dest cookie for 180 days, so that we can show you that country's prices and offer it first next time. It is set only when you choose a country, never just because you opened a page, and it contains nothing but the country's two-letter code. We do not ask for your consent for it, because it is strictly necessary to give you the service you asked for: prices for the country you chose (ePrivacy Directive, Article 5(3); in the UK, regulation 6(4)(b) of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003). You can choose another country at any time.

## The three groups {#the-three-groups}

- **Essential:** what the website cannot work without, such as the language you chose. These are set without asking.
- **Analytics:** how many people visit, and which pages help them send flowers. Set only if you say yes.
- **Marketing:** measuring which advertising brought you here. Set only if you say yes.

Only essential cookies are set without a choice from you. The table above shows which cookies are set today.

## Your consent record {#your-consent-record}

When you choose Accept all, Reject all or Save choices, your browser keeps your answer in the fo_consent cookie, and our server records the same answer so that we can show what was chosen.

The record contains four things: a random code your browser makes up, which does not identify you; the date and time; your answer for analytics and for marketing; and the version of this policy you answered. It contains no IP address, no browser details and no page address.

## Changing your mind {#changing-your-mind}

You can change your answer at any time, here or from "Cookie settings" at the foot of every page. A new answer replaces the old one from that moment.

## What else this website records {#what-else-this-website-records}

Besides cookies, this website records three kinds of information about visits.

### Hosting and edge logs

When you open a page, the services that host and deliver this website record technical details of the request: your IP address, your browser's user agent, the time, the page address and how the server answered.

- **Why:** to serve the website and to keep it secure, for example against abuse.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in running a website that works and is secure (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Who handles it:** Railway, which runs the website; Cloudflare, which delivers and protects it; and Vercel, while it remains our standby host.
- **How long:** for the period each of them keeps its logs on the plan we use.

### Server-side error monitoring

When something goes wrong on our server, an error report may be sent to Sentry: what failed, where in our code, the time, and the general type of browser. Cookies, form contents and IP addresses are removed before a report is sent.

- **Why:** to detect and fix errors.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in a website that works (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Who handles it:** Sentry, in its European Union region.
- **How long:** 90 days.

### Consent records

Your cookie choice, as described above under "Your consent record".

- **Why:** to be able to show what you chose.
- **Legal basis:** our legal obligation to be able to demonstrate consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) with Art. 7(1)).
- **Who handles it:** today the record is written to our hosting logs, so the services named under "Hosting and edge logs" handle it.
- **How long:** our policy is five years. Today the record sits in the hosting logs and is deleted when they are, which is sooner.

**Where this information goes.** The hosting and edge logs may be transferred to the United States under the providers' data-processing terms, which rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Today the consent records are kept in those same logs, so they follow the same transfer. Error reports stay in Sentry's European Union region.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for a copy of what we keep about you, ask us to delete it, or object to our using it. Call us or message us on WhatsApp at {phone}.

**Complaints.** If you think we have handled your personal data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work: for example the UODO in Poland or, in the UK, the ICO.
