## What this website processes today {#today}

We are not taking orders yet, so today this website processes very little.

### Hosting and edge logs

When you open a page, the services that host and deliver this website record technical details of the request: your IP address, your browser's user agent, the time, the page address and how the server answered. We use them to serve the website and to keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). They are handled by Railway, which runs the website, Cloudflare, which delivers and protects it, and Vercel, while it remains our standby host, for the period each keeps its logs on the plan we use.

### Error monitoring

When something goes wrong on our server, an error report may be sent to Sentry, in its European Union region. Cookies, form contents and IP addresses are removed first. We use it to detect and fix errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Reports are kept for 90 days.

### Consent records

When you answer the cookie question, we record your answer with a random code that does not identify you, the date and time and the version of the cookie policy. We keep it to be able to show what you chose. Legal basis: our legal obligation to demonstrate consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) with Art. 7(1)). Our policy is to keep it for five years; today it sits in the hosting logs and is deleted when they are, which is sooner.

### Analytics

We have prepared Google Analytics, but it is switched off. If we switch it on, it runs only if you accept analytics cookies, and this notice will first be updated to say so.

### Occasion reminders

An occasion-reminder sign-up exists but stores nothing: an address sent to it is checked and then discarded, and no email is sent.

### Our database and file storage

Our database, run by Neon in Frankfurt, and our file storage, run by Cloudflare in the European Union, are set up but contain no personal data yet.

## What we will process when orders open {#when-orders-open}

This part describes the future: none of it happens yet.

- **Your details** (name, email address, phone number and country), to take, confirm and deliver your order. Legal basis: the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Invoice records are kept as long as accounting law requires; other personal details are pseudonymised after three years.
- **Payment.** Your card details go straight to our payment provider and never reach us. We keep only a reference to the payment.
- **The recipient's details** (name, address, phone number and your card message), to deliver the gift. Legal basis: the legitimate interest, yours and ours, in delivering a gift (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **The delivery photo**, as proof of delivery for you. Kept for two years.

Before the first order, this section will name every company that handles these details.

## If you received flowers from us {#recipients}

Someone sent you flowers through us and gave us your name, address and phone number, so that our florist could deliver them and call you if needed. We use these details only to deliver the gift, we share them only with the florist who delivers it, and we never use them for marketing. We delete or pseudonymise them 90 days after delivery.

You can ask us what we keep about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact details above.
