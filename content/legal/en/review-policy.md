## Who can write a review {#who-can-review}

Only someone who ordered from us can review us. A day after an order is delivered, we send its buyer one email asking how we did. The link in that email works for that one order, once, for 30 days. We ask every buyer whose order was delivered, whatever happened after the delivery. We never choose whom to ask by what we expect them to say.

## How we check a review {#how-we-check}

Every review is tied to a delivered order, so we know it comes from a real buyer. Before a review appears on this website, a person on our team reads it, within 3 working days. We never change a buyer's stars or words.

## What we publish, and when {#what-we-publish}

We publish the stars, the words as they were written, the month of delivery and the country the flowers went to. We show the buyer's first name only if they asked us to; otherwise the review says "Verified buyer". We never publish a surname, an email address or the recipient's details. Reviews are listed newest first, and low ratings are shown like every other.

## When we withhold a comment or remove a review {#moderation}

We publish every genuine review. If a comment contains personal details, abusive language or unlawful content, we publish the stars without the comment and say why. The stars still count in the average. We remove a review only if it is not genuine, is a duplicate, or comes from a test order, and we keep a record of why. We never withhold or remove a review because of its rating or because it is critical.

## Our replies {#replies}

We may reply to a review. Our reply appears under it, marked as ours.

## We do not pay for reviews {#no-rewards}

We never pay for a review, and we never offer a discount, voucher, gift or prize draw for one. Writing a review never changes anything about an order.

## Trustpilot {#trustpilot}

Our review email may also bring a separate invitation from Trustpilot, an independent review website. A review written there is published by Trustpilot under its own rules, not by us. We send the same invitation to every buyer we ask, whatever their rating on this website.

## Reporting a review {#report}

Every review has a "Report" link. It opens a WhatsApp message to us with the review's number, at {phone}. Tell us what is wrong and we will look at it and answer you.
