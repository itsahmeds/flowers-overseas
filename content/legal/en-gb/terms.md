## Who sells {#who-sells}

These terms apply between you and {legalName} ("we" or "us"), registered with the {registryName} under number {registrationNumber}, with its registered address at {address}.

We are the seller of every order placed on this website, and we are responsible for your order from your payment until the flowers reach the door. The flowers are made and delivered by our florist in the recipient's town.

## How an order becomes a contract {#contract}

You place an order by paying for it. Your card or wallet is charged when you order. The contract between you and us is formed when we confirm your order, which we do once your payment has gone through. If your payment does not go through, there is no contract and nothing is charged.

## Prices {#prices}

The price shown is the price you pay: it includes VAT and delivery. Anything you add to an order is shown as its own line, with its price, before you pay. We charge no fee for the payment method you choose. You pay in the currency shown on the page you order from.

## Delivery {#delivery}

You choose the delivery day when you order, and our florist delivers the flowers by hand on that day. If our florist cannot deliver on the day you chose, for a reason that is ours and not the recipient's, we refund you in full.

If nobody is home, our florist calls the recipient first, which is why we ask for their phone number. If the flowers still cannot be handed over, we agree a second attempt with you at no charge. If the address you gave was wrong, we try to reach the recipient and tell you the options before any extra charge.

## Substitutions {#substitutions}

Our florists work with what is fresh that morning. If a flower is not available, they use one of the same or higher value, in the same style and colours, and we tell you what changed. If the main flower is not available, we ask you first. If we cannot reach you within two hours, the florist uses the closest match.

## Cancelling {#cancelling}

Your card or wallet is charged when you order. You can cancel free of charge until we start preparing your order, and we refund you in full to the payment method you used. We start preparing your flowers on the morning of the delivery day. Once we have started, the order can no longer be cancelled.

If we cannot deliver an order, we cancel it and refund you in full.

Fresh flowers are excluded from the usual right to cancel a purchase made online, because they deteriorate rapidly (the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, regulation 28). Our withdrawal and refunds page sets out what we do instead, and how the right to cancel applies to an add-on that does not wilt, which is not settled yet.

## Our guarantee and your legal rights {#guarantee}

If your flowers don't arrive fresh and in good condition, send us a photo within 72 hours of delivery and we will replace them or refund you in full. How long cut flowers then stay beautiful depends on the room, the water and the care they get, so we do not promise a number of days.

Our guarantee comes on top of your legal rights, not instead of them. If the flowers are faulty, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you rights against us as the seller, and nothing in these terms limits them.
