## Fresh flowers and the right to cancel {#fresh-flowers}

When you buy something online in the UK, you usually have 14 days to cancel. Fresh flowers are an exception, because they are made for you and they wilt.

In legal terms: the right to cancel does not apply to goods which are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly (the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, regulation 28).

## Cancelling before we start {#cancelling}

We give you more than the law asks. Your card or wallet is charged when you order. You can cancel free of charge until we start preparing your order, and we refund you in full to the payment method you used.

We start preparing your flowers on the morning of the delivery day. Once we have started, the order can no longer be cancelled.

## If we cannot deliver {#if-we-cannot-deliver}

If we cannot deliver an order, we cancel it and refund you in full, and we tell you that we have done so.

## How a refund reaches you {#how-a-refund-reaches-you}

A refund goes back to the card or wallet you paid with. We start it on the day we agree it, and where the law says a refund is owed, we pay it within 14 days. How quickly it then shows on your statement depends on your bank or your wallet provider.

## Add-ons that do not wilt {#add-ons}

Some add-ons you can choose with your flowers do not wilt. Whether the usual right to cancel applies to them, and how it works for an add-on delivered together with flowers, is not settled yet. This section will say so when it is.
