# Phase 0 imagery requirements — the founder's generation sheet (2026-09-16)

Everything on this page is derived from committed data: `seed/data/media.json` (the 31 assets), `content/imagery/prompts/*.json` (prompt, negative prompt, declared seed per asset), `content/imagery/style-guide.md` (the locked style), `seed/budgets.ts` (byte caps) and `seed/data/media-variants.json` (the width ladder). Spec 006 §13 Q3 fixed the Phase 0 set at **12 products × 2 assets + 7 homepage slots = 31 images**. Nothing else needs a picture for the 16 October demo: corridor pages render no image (spec 007), hubs and listings reuse the product heroes, the delivery band stays a placeholder by rule (no real delivery photo exists and a staged one is forbidden), and the other 72 products render the honest placeholder.

## 0. Before generating anything

1. **Pick the generator and file its commercial-use terms** in `docs/compliance/imagery-generator-terms.md` (ADR-0014's condition; TASK-081). Every prompt record and asset row currently says `generator: to-be-confirmed` — replace both fields in all 13 prompt files, then re-run the hash (`content/imagery/style-guide.md` §5). Prefer a generator that accepts a **seed** and a **negative prompt** and returns ≥ 2000 px on the long edge without upscaling; if it cannot take our declared seed, record the run's actual seed in the prompt record.
2. Generate into `.local/imagery/originals/{assetId}.png` (git-ignored) and keep a copy in your own store. Never commit an original.
3. Review each image against the checklist below; only then `pnpm media:variants` derives the 15 files per asset and rewrites the manifest, and `pnpm seed:check` enforces the caps. Committed derived bytes must stay ≤ 6 MB in total.
4. Approving an asset is a data edit: `reviewState: approved` + `reviewedBy` + `reviewedAt` in `seed/data/media.json` (TASK-080 lands the commit).

## 3. Negative prompt (every product asset)

> hands, fingers, arms, faces, people, shop interior, shopfront, premises, signage, text, lettering, numbers, watermark, logo, price tag, greetings card message, fused or impossible stems, extra petals, melted or waxy petals, plastic or silk flowers, wilted flowers, wrong flower species, implausible stem count, harsh flash, heavy vignette, cluttered background, props, tiled or repeated pattern

The homepage records carry their own negative prompts inside `content/imagery/prompts/homepage.json`.

## 4. Review checklist (reject on any one)

- A hand, a face, a person, a shopfront, signage, any text or lettering.
- Stem count implausible for the default tier (the tier is the price).
- Colour that would not pass as the product's colour facet.
- Wrong species, fused or impossible stems, melted or waxy petals, plastic look.
- Wrap that is neither kraft nor white; background not warm-grey seamless; light not from the left; margin under 8 % on a 4:5 asset.
- Funeral piece not on neutral stone; plant pot not plain terracotta or white.

## 5. Explicitly **not** needed now

- Delivery / doorstep / handover photographs — forbidden until a consented real one exists (spec 006 AC-8, `plan/07` §1.2).
- Florist portraits, shop interiors, team photos, partner logos, review avatars — fabricated trust, never.
- Country or city photography for the corridor pages — spec 007 renders none.
- The remaining 72 products and all in-home context shots — after R2 (spec 006 §2.6).
- A site-wide Open Graph card — not specified anywhere yet; if you want one, it is a 1200 × 630 brand image and needs a one-line spec 004 §14 amendment (flagged, not assumed).
