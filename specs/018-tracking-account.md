## 1. Problem

After a buyer pays, the product is a promise: a stranger in another country will make and deliver flowers. `plan/04` §9 and §13 say the tracking page is where that anxiety is replaced by a timeline, a florist's first name and, at the end, the photograph. Spec 010 places the order and shows a confirmation for as long as the checkout cookie lives (24 hours). Spec 002 §14 A6 now fixes every status the order can be in and what the buyer is told about each one (A6.4). Spec 016 will send emails that need a tracking link, and nothing yet answers that link.

Two legal duties also land here. We are the seller of record (ADR-0004, `plan/06` §4 Model A), so every paid order needs our own invoice, and every refund a credit note (`plan/06` §2.2, `plan/07` §4). The invoice's content depends on the accountant's `supply_model` answer, which is still open, so `plan/09` says the templates are "data-driven from day one". GDPR rights also need a self-service path (`plan/07` §1.5): export, erasure and a lawful address book. The schema already carries the flags (`customer.redacted`, `recipient.contact_redacted`, `retention_policy`, spec 002 §8), but no route uses them. The founder's ruling F4 on spec 002 A6 (2026-10-12) adds one more duty to this spec: once a refund has succeeded, the buyer sees "Refunded {amount} on {date}" next to the status, because A6.4 shows a refunded order as "closed", never "cancelled". This spec builds the post-purchase surface and the minimal account, covering roadmap items `018` and `019`, with a two-week build budget.

## 2. Scope

**Tracking**
- A **signed tracking link** `/{locale}/track/{token}`. The token is an HMAC-signed credential over `{ publicToken, version }`. It carries no personal data, no order reference and no date. It can be rotated (version bump, audited) and stops working 90 days after the delivery date, or 90 days after the order is cancelled (§13 Q7).
- The **tracking page**, as drawn in `docs/design/wireframes/track-order-*.dc.html`. Its headline is the current status's `order_status.buyer_projection` (spec 002 A6.4). Its timeline is a projection of `order_event`. It shows the florist's first name and city only after `assignment.accepted`, a re-route line after `assignment.declined`, `assignment.timed_out` or `order.manual_reroute`, the delivery photo through a token-checked proxy, the substitution note, the cancelled state, and the contact path.
- **Refund lines (A6.4, advisor F4, D5).** For every refund on the order whose `refund.status = 'succeeded'`, one line "Refunded {amount} on {date}" beside the status, in every state, partial and full alike. The same lines appear in the account. A partially refunded order keeps its fulfilment headline (D1); a fully refunded one reads "closed" (D5).
- A **calendar file** `/{locale}/track/{token}/calendar.ics`.
- The **lookup page** `/{locale}/track`. A buyer enters the `FO-XXXX-XXXX` reference (spec 010) and the email, and we email the link. The response is the same whether or not the pair matched.
- No recipient field on any token page. The view models are strict allowlists (AC-4).

**The live confirmation (an addition to spec 010's page)**
- Spec 010 owns `/{locale}/checkout/done`, its cookie, its recap and its `demo` and "no longer available" states (010 AC-29). This spec adds a **post-purchase block** to its `test` and `live` states only: the masked buyer email, the four-step "what happens next" list, the free-cancellation-until-acceptance sentence (A6 D4), the contact path, and tiles for the tracking link, the calendar file, the invoice state and sharing, then the one-tap account nudge. The `demo` state is unchanged.

**Invoices**
- One **invoice** per live order, issued when money is taken (§5.2), with gap-free numbering per series and an immutable snapshot.
- One **credit note** per succeeded refund.
- **Data-driven profiles** keyed on `(supply_model, destination country)`: title, issuer statement, label languages, which line kinds carry VAT, series, and an optional second VAT currency. Switching profile is a data change (AC-18).
- **Issuer identity** from `src/config/company.ts`, plus the OÜ's VAT registrations held as data.
- **The PDF**: VAT by rate, tagged for accessibility, embedded fonts, stored privately, hash-checked.
- **Download** by the signed-in owner, or through a purpose-scoped signed link in emails. Never through a tracking token.

**Customer account (minimal)**
- **Sign-in** as a third surface on spec 012's sign-in core (`createAuthSurface`), with spec 011's link design. No sign-in code of our own.
- **Order history**: every live order of the signed-in customer. The guest orders placed with the same email are the same `customer` row (spec 010's `upsertGuestCustomer` matches on the normalised email). Each order has a detail page with the recipient recap until the recipient sweep runs, the refund lines, and its invoice and credit-note downloads.
- **Free cancellation before acceptance** (A6 D4, row 13): a "Cancel for free" control on the account order detail and on the live confirmation, offered only while the status is in set E. Never on the tracking page, which is shared (§13 Q10).
- **Saved recipients** only after an explicit, unticked "save" action, recorded in `consent_log`. Saved recipients are exempt from the 90-day sweep and get their own retention row.
- **Privacy requests**: self-service export (JSON) and erasure for signed-in buyers, a verified email link for guests, a 30-day due date with an SLA alert, and spec 002's pseudonymise-and-keep-the-invoice rule.

**Cross-cutting**
- `noindex` on every route here, by header and meta; `no-store`; no sitemap entry; `robots.txt` gains `/*/track/` and `/*/account/` (spec 010 already adds `/*/checkout`).
- Locales en, en-gb, de and pl. WCAG 2.1 AA. Rollout behind the flags `account` and `invoice.issue`.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Order transitions, refunds, voids and the cancellation handler.** Spec 013 owns the payment side and the events that report it; spec 010 owns the engine. This spec calls `transition` once, for the buyer's cancellation (row 13), and otherwise only reads.
- **Address or date edits, redelivery, complaints (`order.disputed`), substitution approval (T3), the delivery-failed message (T12).** The pages print the contact path. Spec 020 and spec 016's §13 Q7 follow-up own them. `delivery_failed` and `redelivery_scheduled` show the buyer "on its way", as A6.4 projects them, with no extra line.
- **Sending B1–B6 and T1–T13, and attaching the invoice to them.** Spec 016. This spec exposes `tracking.linkFor(orderId, locale)`, `invoices.getIssuedPdf(orderId)` and `orders.buyerRefundLines(orderId)` for it. It sends only its own four emails (§5.2).
- **Live polling** (`plan/05` row 66). The page is `no-store`; a reload is current.
- **The recipient loop** `/r/{code}` and recipient-side DSAR self-service (spec 029, or the manual runbook until then); the consented "deliveries like these" photo band (spec 027/029). The band is omitted, not shown as placeholders.
- **Marketing preferences and passkeys** (Phase 3, `plan/05` row 56). The admin UI for privacy requests, invoice re-issue and token rotation is spec 020; Phase 1 has an alert, a runbook, a CLI and SQL.
- **Accounting export** (spec 039). Payouts and self-billing (spec 030).
- **Reviews**, invited from the photo email (specs 016, 022), never from these pages.

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer** who has just paid, I see what happens next and get one link I can keep, so that I stop worrying whether a stranger got my order.
- As a **buyer**, I open the tracking link from any device, without an account, and see every real step with its time, my florist's first name once they accept, and the photo, so that I know the flowers arrived.
- As a **buyer who was refunded**, I see "Refunded €12.00 on 14 November" beside the status, so that "closed" does not read like "you lost your money".
- As a **buyer who lost the link**, I type my order reference and email and get the link by email. Someone guessing at that form learns nothing.
- As a **buyer who changed my mind before a florist accepted**, I cancel for free from my account or the confirmation, without writing to anyone.
- As a **buyer**, I receive a proper invoice from a registered EU company with VAT shown by rate, in a language I read, and a credit note when I am refunded.
- As the **founder**, when the accountant answers the Model A or B question, I change data, not code. No invoice number is ever skipped or reused, and no demo or test order ever takes one.
- As a **returning buyer**, I sign in with a link and see every order I placed with that email. I save a recipient's address only when I choose to.
- As a **buyer**, I download everything you hold about me, or erase my account, without writing to anyone. You keep only what the law makes you keep, and you tell me so.
- As a **recipient**, my name, address and phone never appear on a page that the sender may forward.
- As **Googlebot**, none of these URLs is in a sitemap, every one says `noindex`, and `robots.txt` asks me not to fetch them.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

One migration, `NNNN_post_purchase.sql` with `NNNN_post_purchase.down.sql`, numbered when the task lands. It runs after spec 002's chain through TASK-025 (including A6's seed in `0007` and the triggers in `0008`, PR 210) and after spec 010's `checkout_draft`.

- **`order`** (spec 002 §5.1, spec 010 §5.1 A) gains:
  - `tracking_token_version smallint NOT NULL DEFAULT 1`;
  - on `public_token`: `DEFAULT translate(rtrim(encode(gen_random_bytes(16), 'base64'), '='), '+/', '-_')` (16 CSPRNG bytes, base64url, pgcrypto), `NOT NULL`, and `CHECK (public_token ~ '^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{22}$')`. Existing rows are backfilled first. The default means spec 010's `place` writes nothing for it (a note for TASK-202's brief, §12);
  - `UNIQUE INDEX order_reference_idx ON "order" (order_reference(id))`, where `order_reference(uuid)` is an `IMMUTABLE` SQL function that returns exactly what spec 010's `FO-XXXX-XXXX` derivation returns. The lookup (AC-8) and the account route resolve a reference through it. No column is added: spec 010 already decided that the reference is derived from the id (§13 Q13).
- **`fulfillment_partner`** gains `display_first_name text NULL`: the name the buyer sees ("Anna", `plan/04` §13), set with the florist's agreement. When null the page says "your florist in {city}" and never invents a name.
- **`recipient`** gains `saved_at timestamptz NULL`, `saved_consent_log_id uuid NULL FK consent_log`, `last_used_at timestamptz NULL`, and `CHECK ((saved_at IS NULL) = (saved_consent_log_id IS NULL))`.
- **`tax_registration`** `(id, country_iso2, vat_id text NOT NULL, valid_from date NOT NULL, valid_to date NULL, UNIQUE (country_iso2, valid_from))`: one VAT registration of the OÜ per row (EE now; PL under Model A).
- **`invoice_series`** `(key PK, prefix text NOT NULL, year smallint NOT NULL, next_number integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 1, UNIQUE (prefix, year))`. Default format `FO-PL/2026/000001`, credit notes `FOK-PL/2026/000001` (§13 Q4).
- **`invoice_profile`** `(key PK, supply_model CHECK IN ('principal','agent'), destination_iso2, document_kind CHECK IN ('vat_invoice','receipt_on_behalf'), title_key, issuer_statement_key NULL, label_locales text[] NOT NULL, vat_bearing_line_kinds text[] NOT NULL, series_key FK invoice_series, credit_series_key FK invoice_series, vat_currency_code NULL FK currency, vat_rate_source CHECK IN ('nbp_previous_business_day') NULL, active bool NOT NULL, UNIQUE (supply_model, destination_iso2) WHERE active)`. Seed: one active `principal/PL` row (`vat_invoice`, labels `['pl','{buyer}']`, VAT-bearing `product`, `addon`, `surcharge`, VAT currency `PLN` from NBP, §13 Q5) and an **inactive** `agent/PL` fixture row (AC-18).
- **`invoice`** `(id, kind CHECK IN ('invoice','credit_note'), order_id FK, refund_id NULL FK refund, corrects_invoice_id NULL FK invoice, series_key, number text UNIQUE NOT NULL, issued_at timestamptz NOT NULL, supply_date date NOT NULL, currency_code FK currency, total_minor bigint NOT NULL, profile_key FK, snapshot jsonb NOT NULL, snapshot_version integer NOT NULL, pdf_object_key NULL, pdf_sha256 NULL, rendered_at NULL)`, with `UNIQUE (order_id) WHERE kind = 'invoice'` and `UNIQUE (refund_id) WHERE kind = 'credit_note'`. A `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` trigger raises `INVOICE_IMMUTABLE`, except the one-time `NULL → value` write of the three render columns (the pattern of spec 002's `order_event` trigger). The money pair passes spec 002's `db:check`.
- **`privacy_request`** `(id, kind CHECK IN ('export','erase'), customer_id NULL FK, email_hash bytea NOT NULL, channel CHECK IN ('account','email_link','admin'), status CHECK IN ('received','verified','in_progress','waiting_open_orders','completed','rejected'), received_at, verified_at NULL, due_at timestamptz NOT NULL, completed_at NULL, export_object_key NULL, export_expires_at NULL, note NULL)`. `email_hash` is HMAC-SHA-256 under `SIGNED_LINK_SECRET`, so the table holds no address. `due_at = received_at + 30 days` (GDPR Art. 12(3)).
- **`retention_policy`** gains `saved_recipient` (delete 730 days after `last_used_at`, §13 Q6), `privacy_export` (delete the object at `export_expires_at`) and `privacy_request` (keep 3 years, Art. 5(2) accountability).
- **`retention.sweep`** (spec 002 TASK-025) changes one predicate: the 90-day recipient rule skips rows with `saved_at IS NOT NULL`. `order.recipient_snapshot` is still pseudonymised at 90 days.
- **`feature_flag`** rows `account` (global, default off) and `invoice.issue` (default off, `country` scope rows).
- **Row-level security**: `invoice`, `privacy_request` and saved `recipient` rows are customer-scoped through `customer.user_id = app.user_id` (spec 002 §5.5). `tax_registration`, `invoice_profile` and `invoice_series` are admin and system only; `app.role = 'public'` reads zero rows (spec 002 AC-17 is extended to them).
- **Rollback**: the down file drops the five tables, the `order_reference` index and function, the flag rows, the three retention rows and the four columns; restores `public_token` without default, `NOT NULL` or check; and restores the old sweep predicate. Spec 002's round-trip test executes it. Once a live invoice exists, the tables are kept and a forward fix is required (§12).

### 5.2 API / server actions / jobs / events (zod schemas named)

Modules follow `plan/01` §5; each exports from `index.ts` only.

**The order machine, read only (spec 002 §14 A6).** This spec adds no status, no `order_transition` row, no catalogue event and no outbox target outside A6.6's 36 names. It reads `order_status.buyer_projection` and `is_terminal` from the database, never from a copy in code, and its tests take the machine from `tests/fixtures/order-machine.ts` (A6.8). Three sets are used below, all from A6:
- **E** (before acceptance) = `authorised`, `paid_unrouted`, `routed`, `no_partner_available`, `manual_reroute` (A6.3).
- **Settled** = `closed` plus the terminal statuses of A6.4 (`chargeback_won`, `chargeback_lost`, `payment_failed`, `abandoned`, `cancelled`, `cancelled_refunded`).
- **Cancelled projection** = the statuses whose `buyer_projection = 'cancelled'`.

**`src/lib/signed-link.ts`**: `sign(purpose, payload, expiresAt?)` and `verify(purpose, token)`. `Purpose = z.enum(["track","invoice","privacy","export"])`. HMAC-SHA-256 under `SIGNED_LINK_SECRET`; the MAC input starts with `purpose + ":v1:"` (domain separation), is truncated to 16 bytes and compared with `timingSafeEqual`. `expiresAt` sits inside the MAC. A token is `base64url(payload) + "." + base64url(mac)`; one failure value for every cause. Distinct from spec 016's florist links and spec 011's sign-in tokens, which have their own secrets.

**`modules/orders`, read projections** (no new command):
- `TrackingToken = z.object({ publicToken: z.string().regex(/^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{22}$/), version: z.number().int().positive() }).strict()`.
- `tracking.linkFor(orderId, locale)`; `tracking.resolve(token) → { ok: true, orderId } | { ok: false }`. Fails for a bad MAC, an unknown `publicToken`, a stale version and an expired order (delivery date + 90 days, or the first entry into a cancelled-projection status + 90 days).
- `getTrackingView(orderId) → TrackingView` (`.strict()`, the AC-4 allowlist).
  - **Headline**: the current status's `buyer_projection`.
  - **Timeline steps**: the seven non-cancelled projections in order: `placed`, `confirmed`, `with_florist`, `being_made`, `on_its_way`, `delivered`, `closed`. Each `order_event` with a `to_status` maps to that status's projection. A step up to and including the current projection is *done* or *current*, dated at the first event that reached it. A step after the current projection is *future* and undated, even if an earlier event reached it (a `closed` order that is then disputed, row 27, shows `delivered` as current and `closed` as future).
  - **Cancelled**: when the current projection is `cancelled`, the timeline ends with a dated cancelled line after the last step reached.
  - **Re-route line**: one dated line "We are offering your order to another florist" for each `assignment.declined`, `assignment.timed_out` (rows 8, 9) or `order.manual_reroute` (row 11). It names no florist and no city (AC-5).
  - **Florist card**: present only after an `assignment.accepted` event.
  - **Refund lines**: `buyerRefundLines(orderId)`.
- `buyerRefundLines(orderId) → BuyerRefundLine[]`, `BuyerRefundLine = z.object({ amountMinor: z.number().int().positive(), currencyCode: CurrencyCode, succeededAt: z.date() }).strict()`. One item per `refund` row of the order with `status = 'succeeded'`, oldest first. `succeededAt` is the `occurred_at` of the `order.refunded_partial` or `order.refunded_full` event (rows 30, 31, 32) whose payload names that refund (a requirement on spec 013, §12). Exported for spec 016's buyer emails (§13 Q11).
- `getConfirmationBlock(orderId) → ConfirmationBlock` for spec 010's `test` and `live` confirmation states: masked email, current step, tracking link, invoice state, `canCancel` (status in E).
- `rotateTrackingToken(orderId, actor: Actor)`: bumps the version and writes `audit_log`. Phase 1 caller: `pnpm orders:rotate-tracking --order <id>` (audit channel `cli`); spec 020 adds the admin button.

**Buyer cancellation (A6 row 13, D4).** `cancelOrderAsBuyer({ orderId })`, a server action reachable from the account order detail (session owner) and from spec 010's confirmation (draft cookie owner). It calls `orderService.transition(orderId, 'order.cancelled', 'buyer', { reason: 'buyer_cancel', via })` inside one transaction. The engine refuses a status outside E (`ORDER_TRANSITION_ILLEGAL`) and checks `buyer` against row 13's `allowed_actors` (spec 010 §14 A1.5, AC-44). The void or refund that follows is spec 013's cancel handler, an outbox subscriber; this spec moves no money. A refusal renders "Your florist has already accepted; contact us" with the contact path.

**`modules/invoices`** (new; `plan/01` §5 gains the row):
- `issueInvoice(orderId)`: one transaction. Refuses unless `order.mode = 'live'` (`INVOICE_NOT_LIVE`, consuming no number and raising no alert) and unless a `payment` of the order has `captured_at IS NOT NULL` (`INVOICE_NOT_CAPTURED`, retried). Then `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the series row, allocates the number, inserts `invoice` with its snapshot, and enqueues `invoice.render`. Idempotent: an existing `kind = 'invoice'` row for the order is returned unchanged.
- `issueCreditNote(refundId)`: the same pattern for a `refund` with `status = 'succeeded'`; refuses `INVOICE_CREDIT_NO_INVOICE` (with an alert) when the order has no invoice.
- `renderPdf(invoiceId)`: PDFKit (`tagged: true`, `lang`, embedded Noto Sans subset, A4) from the snapshot and profile only, never from live order rows. Stores to the private bucket (`R2_PRIVATE_BUCKET`, spec 011) at `invoices/{yyyy}/{id}.pdf` through spec 002's `Storage` seam and writes the key and sha-256.
- `getIssuedPdf(orderId)` for spec 016.
- `InvoiceSnapshotV1 = z.object({ issuer, buyer, supplyDate, issueDate, lines, byRate, totals, currency, vatCurrency, vatRate, profileKey, orderReference })`, with `buyer` from spec 010's `order.buyer_snapshot` (name and country as entered).
- Typed refusals: `INVOICE_NOT_LIVE`, `INVOICE_NOT_CAPTURED`, `INVOICE_ISSUER_INCOMPLETE` (`company.registered` false, or no `tax_registration` for the profile's country), `INVOICE_SUPPLY_MODEL_UNSET`, `INVOICE_NO_PROFILE`, `INVOICE_FLAG_OFF`, `INVOICE_TOTAL_MISMATCH`, `INVOICE_CREDIT_NO_INVOICE`. Each raises inside the transaction, so no number is consumed.
- **VAT arithmetic.** `order.vat_breakdown` is the source of truth. Lines group by `vat_rate_bp`; gross per rate comes from the order; VAT per rate is `round_half_even(gross × rate / (10 000 + rate))` on the group total; net is gross minus VAT. The invoice asserts that gross per rate sums to `order.buyer_total_minor` and equals `vat_breakdown`, else `INVOICE_TOTAL_MISMATCH` and an alert. It never prints a number the buyer was not charged.
- **NBP adapter** behind `VatRateProvider`: table A (`api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/{ccy}/{date}`) for the last business day before the tax point; value, source and date stored on the snapshot. Called only when the profile sets `vat_currency_code`.

**Outbox subscriptions** (registered in spec 010's `outboxTargets(event, mode)`; for `mode <> 'live'` every list below is empty):

The job reads the money fact from `payment.captured_at`, never from the event |
| `order.refunded_partial` | 30 | `invoices` → `invoice.credit {refundId}` | partial refund settled (D1) |
| `order.refunded_full` | 31, 32 | `invoices` → `invoice.credit {refundId}` | full refund settled, including the self-loop after `cancelled` or `cancelled_refunded` |
| `order.closed`, `order.chargeback_won`, `order.chargeback_lost`, `order.payment_failed`, `order.abandoned`, `order.cancelled`, `order.cancelled_refunded` | the arrows into Settled | `privacy` → `privacy.resume {orderId}` | an erasure waiting on this order can finish |

No subscription names `order.refunded` (removed by A6.6) or a diagram arrow label (ruling R8). `order.chargeback_lost` creates no credit note (§13 Q12).

**Other jobs** (pg-boss, one zod payload each, idempotent on their key): `invoice.render {invoiceId}` (5 retries, then alert); `privacy.export {requestId}`; `privacy.erase {requestId}`; `privacy.sla_check {}` (daily).

**Account sign-in: a third surface on spec 012's core (`src/lib/auth/account.ts`).** Spec 012 task 1 builds the core (`createAuthSurface`, the adapter over `withSystemContext`, `confirmSignIn`, the `Mailer`, the `auth_email_send` limit of 5 links per address per hour, `AUTH_SECRET`). Spec 011 sets the link design: `{surfaceBase}/sign-in/confirm?t={token}`, no address in any URL, a `GET` that uses nothing up, a `POST` that consumes, single use, one hour. This spec configures only:
- `basePath` `/api/auth/account`; pages under `/{locale}/account/sign-in`; `session.strategy = "database"`; `maxAge` 30 days rolling;
- cookies `__Host-fo-account.session-token`, `__Host-fo-account.csrf-token`, `__Host-fo-account.callback-url`, each `Path=/`, `Secure`, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`. `Path=/` because the account lives under four locale prefixes and a per-locale path would sign the buyer out on a language switch; `__Host-` is allowed because the path is `/` (spec 012 §2.2 explains why the admin and florist surfaces cannot use it). No public route reads them (AC-36);
- the allow predicate `accountSignInPolicy(env)`: a link is sent only to an address whose non-redacted `customer` has at least one order with `mode = 'live'` (outside production also `'test'`). Every other address gets the identical page and no email;
- `sendVerificationRequest` → the T14 template; after the first verified sign-in, `customer.user_id` is set on the matching non-redacted `customer`;
- `requireAccountContext()` → `{ userId, customerId }`; `toDbContext` → `{ role: 'customer', userId }`.

**Server actions** (Zod at the boundary; each re-checks the session or cookie on the server):
- `saveRecipient({ recipientId })`, `removeRecipient({ recipientId })`;
- `requestExport()`; `requestErasure({ confirm: z.literal(true) })`, which needs a session created less than 15 minutes ago, or else sends a fresh sign-in link with `callbackUrl` set to the privacy page and changes nothing;
- `cancelOrderAsBuyer({ orderId })` (above);
- `lookupTracking({ reference: z.string().regex(/^FO-[0-9A-Z]{4}-[0-9A-Z]{4}$/), email: z.email().max(254) })`;
- `requestGuestPrivacy({ email })`; `sendAccountLink()` for the confirmation nudge, which sends the sign-in link to the order's email, read from the draft cookie, so nothing is typed.

**Rate limits.** The email-sending actions (lookup, guest privacy, account nudge) use the core's `auth_email_send` counter, keyed by the hashed address: lookup 3 per hour, guest privacy 3 per day, sign-in 5 per hour (spec 012). No IP is read or stored, as in spec 012. Per-IP limits are Cloudflare's (spec 040 rule 3, POSTs 60 per minute per IP). Over a limit the response is the same localised page, with no email sent.

**Emails** (React Email, every locale, plain-text part, through spec 012's `Mailer` and its non-production capture): T14 `account_sign_in`, T15 `privacy_verify`, T16 `tracking_link` (the lookup answer), T17 `privacy_export_ready`. None contains PII beyond the address in `To:`. None is sent for an order with `mode <> 'live'` outside the test environments.

**Erasure semantics** (spec 002 §8 "DSAR"):
- `customer` is pseudonymised (`name`, `phone_e164` and `address` rows cleared, `email_normalised` → `redacted+{id}@invalid`, `redacted = true`), `marketing_consent = false`.
- `users`, `accounts` and `sessions` rows are deleted, and saved recipients are deleted.
- For every order in Settled: recipient contact, `recipient_snapshot` and `buyer_snapshot` are pseudonymised, and delivery photos are deleted unless the status is `chargeback_open` or `disputed` (Art. 17(3)(e), §13 Q8).
- Orders, payments, refunds, invoices and their PDFs are kept, flagged by `customer.redacted`, because accounting law requires it (`plan/07` §1.2).
- An order not in Settled moves the request to `waiting_open_orders`; `privacy.resume` finishes it when the last such order enters Settled.
- `consent_log` is kept for 5 years.

**Export**: `PrivacyExportV1 = z.object({ schema: z.literal("privacy-export/v1"), generatedAt, customer, orders, savedRecipients, consents, invoices, refunds, privacyRequests })`. Orders carry the buyer projection, never internal statuses. No `payment.risk_score` or fraud signal (§8). Stored at `exports/{requestId}.json` in the private bucket; the link is signed with the `export` purpose and lasts 7 days; the sweep deletes the object at expiry.

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **New data flows**, recorded in RoPA (`docs/compliance/ropa.md`) by the closing task:

  - the NBP rate fetch: no personal data.

The confirmation and the account repeat the free-cancellation-until-acceptance promise, and the cancel control makes it self-service in all five states of E (A6 D4). ## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Tokens and token pages**
- **AC-1** `signed-link.verify(purpose, token)` accepts a token from `sign` with the same purpose and returns one failure value for: any single-byte change in payload or MAC; a token signed for another purpose; a payload past its `expiresAt`. The comparison calls `timingSafeEqual`.
- **AC-2** A tracking token decodes to exactly `{ publicToken, version }`. Every `order.public_token`, including one inserted by spec 010's `place` without naming the column, matches `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{22}$`. After `rotateTrackingToken`, the old token fails, the new one resolves, and one `audit_log` row records the rotation.
- **AC-3** For each of A6.4's 26 statuses, reached with `walkOrderTo` (spec 002 A6.7 b), `/{locale}/track/{token}` returns 200 with server-rendered HTML whose headline is that status's `buyer_projection` read from the fixture `tests/fixtures/order-machine.ts`. The timeline marks the steps up to the current projection as done or current, each dated at its first entry in the destination's zone with the short zone name, and later steps undated. A cancelled-projection status ends the timeline with a dated cancelled line. Each `assignment.declined`, `assignment.timed_out` and `order.manual_reroute` event adds one dated re-route line.
- **AC-4** `TrackingView` is `.strict()` and its key set equals the allowlist: reference, projection, timeline, refundLines, deliveryDate, destinationCity, floristFirstName, floristCity, substitution, photoState, canShare, demo. For a fixture order whose recipient name, phone, street, postcode, card message and buyer email are unique marker strings, the HTML of the tracking page in every state, the `.ics` body and the not-working page contain none of the markers.
- **AC-5** No florist name or city renders, and no re-route line contains a city, before an `assignment.accepted` event. After it, `display_first_name` and the partner's `city_label` render; with `display_first_name` null the page reads "your florist in {city}".
- **AC-6** When a `delivery_proof` exists, the page renders an `<img>` with `src` `/{locale}/track/{token}/photo` and the localised alt text. That route returns the object bytes with the asset's MIME type and `Cache-Control: private, max-age=300`, whatever `public_use_consent` is, and 404 for any token failure. When retention has deleted the photo, the page shows the dated deletion sentence.
- **AC-7** An invalid, a forged, a rotated and an expired token (delivery date + 90 days; first entry into a cancelled-projection status + 90 days) each return 404 and the same body for a given locale. The body offers the lookup form and contains no order data.
- **AC-8** `POST /{locale}/track` returns byte-identical HTML for a matching reference and email, a wrong email, an unknown reference and an over-limit request. In the `demo` state none of this renders and spec 010 AC-29 still passes.
- **AC-10** Refund lines (A6.4, F4): for fixtures in a status from each projection with `refund` rows in each of `requested`, `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed` and `cancelled`, the tracking page, the account list and the account detail render exactly one "Refunded {amount} on {date}" line per `succeeded` refund, oldest first, and none for the others. A `refunded_full` order's headline reads the `closed` key, never `cancelled`; an `in_production` order with a partial refund reads `being_made`.

## 13. Open questions

The spec is written against each recommended answer and stays `draft` until the founder rules. Each can be answered in one word.

- **Q1 — How long after the delivery photo may a buyer say "not what I ordered"?** Both tracking artboards print "[slot] N hours". **Recommended: 72 hours**, the same window after which the order closes by itself (spec 002 A6 row 26), held once as data (`company.ts` `complaintWindowHours`) so the pages, the guarantee page and the closing job print the same number. Answer: "72", or another number.
- **Q2 — Is the lookup page `/{locale}/track` kept out of Google?** **Recommended: yes (noindex).** It is a thin form; keeping it out lets one rule cover all of `/*/track/`. `plan/05` row 22 had it indexable.
- **Q3 — Is the account at `/{locale}/account`, with one sign-in that survives a language switch?** **Recommended: yes.** It renders in four languages, `plan/01` draws it under the locale, and its cookies sit on the whole site but are read only by account pages (AC-36). `plan/02` §4.1's unprefixed `/account/*` is corrected (below).
- **Q4 — When is the invoice issued, and how are series numbered?** 🧾 **Recommended: at the first "money taken" event** (card: capture when the florist accepts, which is also when the contract forms; BLIK, P24, iDEAL: at payment), one series per VAT-registration country per year (`FO-PL/2026/000001`), credit notes in their own series (`FOK-PL/…`). The accountant's answer changes data, not code.
- **Q5 — Invoice language and the PLN VAT line?** 🧾 **Recommended: Polish labels plus the buyer's language on one document, the charged currency as the invoice currency, VAT per rate also in PLN at NBP table A of the business day before the tax point.** The alternative (buyer language only, no PLN line) is one profile edit.
- **Q6 — Saved recipients: on what basis and for how long?** ⚖️ **Recommended: the buyer's explicit, unticked save, under legitimate interest, deleted after 24 months without an order to that recipient or when the recipient objects.** The alternative is no address book in Phase 1.
- **Q7 — How long does a tracking link work?** **Recommended: until 90 days after the delivery date, or 90 days after cancellation**, in step with the recipient sweep. A signed-in buyer keeps the history.
- **Q8 — On erasure, do we keep delivery photos as evidence?** ⚖️ **Recommended: only while a complaint or chargeback on that order is open** (Art. 17(3)(e)). The alternative keeps every photo for its full 2 years: stronger evidence, weaker erasure.
- **Q9 — The share tile: the phone's share sheet or a WhatsApp link?** **Recommended: the share sheet** (WhatsApp is one tap in it) with copy-link as the fallback. A `wa.me/?text=` link would pass the tracking link through Meta's redirect.
- **Q10 — Where can a buyer cancel for free before a florist accepts?** (A6 D4 allows it; spec 013's draft hands the button to this spec.) **Recommended: in the account and on the confirmation page, not on the tracking page**, because the tracking link is meant to be shared and whoever holds it could cancel. Alternative: on the tracking page too.
- **Q11 — Does every buyer email that shows a status also show "Refunded {amount} on {date}"?** (A6.4 gives F4 to this spec; the emails are spec 016's.) **Recommended: yes**: this spec supplies the refund lines and their wording, and spec 016 prints them in its emails.
- **Q12 — When we lose a bank chargeback, do we issue a credit note?** 🧾 **Recommended: no automatic credit note.** The order keeps its invoice; the accountant decides the treatment, and the runbook records it. Alternative: a full credit note on every lost chargeback.
- **Q13 — Order references must never clash.** Spec 010 derives `FO-XXXX-XXXX` from the order id; the lookup needs each reference to name one order. **Recommended: yes, add a database uniqueness check, and if a new order would clash, spec 010's `place` picks a new id and retries** (a one-line note for TASK-202). Alternative: a separate numbered reference, which would re-decide spec 010.
