## 1. Problem

**Why this spec is not "014 Mollie adapter".** The roadmap reserves `014` for a Mollie adapter (`plan/09` Phase 1), and ADR-0005 names Mollie as the fallback. Both assumed an Estonian OU (ADR-0004, `plan/06` §1). The company is now **Flowers Overseas LLC, a Wyoming (US) LLC** (ADR-0021, open PR 348). Mollie onboards only merchants based in the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, and requires an IBAN or UK bank account; a business registered anywhere else cannot sign up ([Mollie help: Can I use Mollie's services in my country?](https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002116105)). `plan/06` §1's own table already said "US LLC: Stripe US; Mollie no". So a Mollie adapter would be code for an account we cannot open. This spec keeps number `014` and its purpose (a second processor behind the same interface, so a Stripe rejection, reserve or outage does not stop sales; `plan/06` §9) and names a processor that can onboard a US LLC.

**Which fallback, and why.** Researched 2026-10-24 against a US LLC selling to EU and UK consumers in GBP, EUR and PLN:

| Processor | Onboards a US LLC? | Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay | BLIK · P24 · iDEAL · Bancontact · Klarna for a US-registered business | Fit for a solo founder | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Airwallex** | Yes: US payment acceptance since 2024; single-member LLCs accepted ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240424584632/en/Airwallex-Expands-Payment-Acceptance-to-the-U.S.-Cementing-Its-Position-as-a-Leading-One-Stop-Financial-Platform-for-Borderless-Businesses); [Airwallex US account](https://www.airwallex.com/us/business-account/global-accounts/usd-account)) | Yes, US listed for all three ([methods overview](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/payments/payment-methods)) | All five list US as a business location (BLIK, iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna on the overview; P24 on its own page, [P24](https://airwallex.com/docs/payments__eu-and-uk__przelewy24-or-p24)) | Self-serve signup, free sandbox, PaymentIntent API close to Stripe's (server-created intent, `client_secret`, `request_id` idempotency, refunds API, HMAC-signed webhooks) | **Recommended** |
| PayPal (Checkout) | Yes | Cards via PayPal's own fields; wallets vary | BLIK, P24, iDEAL listed for buyers, but "no support for cross-border shipping" for these methods ([PayPal APMs](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/checkout/apm)), which is our whole model | Easy signup, different API shape (orders, not intents) | Second choice; a possible later *method*, not the fallback |
| Adyen | Possibly, sales-led | Yes | Yes | Enterprise; unpublished minimums, weeks of onboarding ([review](https://www.businessexpert.co.uk/payment-processing/adyen-review/)) | No (ADR-0005 option 3 reasoning still holds) |
| Checkout.com | Sales-led | Yes | Most | Volume and risk-profile minimums exclude small merchants | No |
| Revolut Business (merchant) | No: merchant accounts need a Revolut Business account in a listed country, and the US is not listed ([Revolut help](https://help.revolut.com/en-SE/business/help/merchant-accounts/setting-up-a-merchant-account/who-can-apply-for-a-merchant-account)) | n/a | n/a | n/a | Ineligible |
| Mollie | No (EEA, CH, UK only) | n/a | n/a | n/a | Ineligible |

Airwallex is the only option that is self-serve for a new US LLC, covers the methods `plan/04` §10 lists for our launch and near-term markets, and has an intent model close enough to Stripe's that one adapter can pass spec 013's contract suite without changing the interface. Three facts are **not** yet confirmed and are blocking founder questions (§13 Q1): that Airwallex accepts a US LLC whose owners live outside the US, that a cross-border flower relay is inside its risk appetite (its terms keep a catch-all for "businesses outside of our risk appetite", [Airwallex terms](https://www.airwallex.com/en-eu/terms)), and the exact card fees for UK and EU cards charged by a US entity.

**Why now.** Spec 013 (approved) builds Stripe and names this spec as the second adapter. Spec 013 is the only way to take money; if Stripe declines the LLC's application, imposes a reserve that starves florist payouts, or has an outage on a peak day, the shop stops. The fallback must exist before the first peak (`plan/06` §7) and is built in sandbox while the accounts are under review.

## 2. Scope

- **Processor choice, per destination, by admin only.** A pure function `processorFor(destinationCountry, mode)` returns `airwallex` only when the founder has switched the fallback on for that destination in admin (flag `payments.fallback`, `country` scope) **and** the Airwallex kill switch `payments.airwallex` is on **and** an Airwallex payment step is registered for the mode. Otherwise `stripe`. It never reads anything about the buyer, the request, the card or a previous failure. No code switches processor automatically.
- **The provider is bound to the payment row.** `payment.provider` records who took the money; every later call (sync, reconcile, refund, cancel, dispute, sweep, replay, ledger check) goes to that provider through `providerFor(payment.provider)`, whatever the flags say now.
- **The Airwallex adapter** implementing spec 013's `PaymentProvider` unchanged, passing spec 013's exported contract suite against recorded responses at a pinned API version; integer-minor-unit to decimal conversion with no float arithmetic; automatic capture; 3DS requested on every card; the six non-personal metadata fields; idempotent `request_id` on every write; token authentication with an in-memory token cache.
- **The Airwallex payment steps** (`airwallexTestPaymentStep`, `airwallexLivePaymentStep`) and a routing step per mode that delegates to the Stripe or Airwallex step through `processorFor`, so spec 010's `CheckoutPaymentStep` registry and `checkoutMode()` are unchanged. Step 3 uses Airwallex's **hosted payment page** (§13 Q2): our page keeps the pre-contract block and the pay button; pressing it places the order and sends the buyer to Airwallex's page to enter card or wallet details; Airwallex returns the buyer to spec 013's return route.
- **Processor change mid-checkout**, made safe: a flag flip between step 3's render and the pay press re-renders step 3 with nothing charged; a placed, unpaid order moves to the new processor only after the old one confirms its intent cancelled.
- **Methods per buyer country** for Airwallex in spec 013's capability table: card, Apple Pay and Google Pay `available` for every country at launch (parity with spec 013 Q5); BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL, Bancontact and Klarna rows present with `available: false`.
- **Webhooks:** `POST /api/webhooks/airwallex` with HMAC-SHA256 verification of `x-timestamp` + raw body, a 300-second window, store before process in the shared `webhook_inbox`, the same `webhook.process` job routing to spec 013's reconcile, refund and dispute paths; replay through the adapter's `listEventsSince`.
- **Refunds and disputes** through spec 013's services, unchanged, for Airwallex payments.
- **A daily ledger check** for every registered provider: each provider-reported success has a matching `payment` or `refund` row with the same amount and currency, and the reverse.
- **Two alerts:** `payment.provider_unavailable` (so the founder knows when to flip) and the per-provider form of spec 013's `webhook.silent_24h`.
- **One migration** with its rollback (provider checks, inbox field check, the two flag rows, one index).
- **Docs:** RoPA rows, the payment-fallback runbook, `.env.example`, the cookie register, and a proposed ADR superseding ADR-0005's fallback half.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Changing spec 013's interface, lifecycle, reconcile table, refund rules or order rows.** This spec plugs into them. The three small seams it needs in spec 013's code (`providerFor`, the routing step, one additive `PaymentOutcome` variant) are listed in §12 as a requirement on spec 013, recorded as its §14 amendment.
- **Automatic failover per buyer or per error.** Forbidden by the founder's brief. A Stripe error shows spec 013's "payment unavailable, try again" state and raises an alert; only the founder flips.
- **BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna, PayPal on either processor.** Rows exist, `available: false`. Turning one on is a later task with its own AC (spec 013 §12 task 9 for Stripe; a sibling task here for Airwallex, §12).
- **The Phase 4 methods `plan/04` §10 routes through Mollie** (Bizum, Vipps, MobilePay, Swish via Mollie). With Mollie unavailable they need a new source; that is a Phase 4 payments spec, not this one.
- **The Stripe consequences of the US LLC** (Stripe, Inc. instead of Stripe Payments Europe as the contracting entity, RoPA wording, card fees, which methods a US Stripe account offers). Those belong to spec 013 and the ADR-0021 follow-up; flagged in §13 Q9.
- **An embedded Airwallex card form (Drop-in element).** Option B of §13 Q2, a later spec if the fallback becomes long-lived.
- **Fraud rules on Airwallex beyond 3DS on every card and Airwallex's own risk engine.** Our own pre-checks and the manual-review queue stay with spec 021.
- **Florist payouts** through Airwallex → spec 030.
- **Removing the dormant Mollie values** (`'mollie'` in the provider checks, `paymentProcessors`, the `payments.mollie` flag row). Left in place, unused (§13 Q8).

## 4. User stories

- As the **founder**, when Stripe is down or suspends the account, I open admin, switch the fallback on for Poland, and the next buyer sending flowers to Poland pays through Airwallex, with the same price, the same pages and the same confirmation.
- As the **founder**, when Stripe is back, I switch the fallback off; orders already paid through Airwallex still refund, dispute and reconcile through Airwallex.
- As the **founder**, I get one alert when a processor keeps failing, so I know when to flip, and one each morning if the money our database says we took differs from what a processor says we took.
- As a **buyer in the UK sending to Poland**, I see the same price on the product page, at step 3 and on my bank statement, whichever processor takes it; I pass 3DS once and am charged at once.
- As a **buyer whose step 3 was open when the founder flipped**, I am told the payment options changed and asked to try again; I am never charged twice and never charged by two processors.
- As a **buyer**, I never see a different list of payment methods because of my IP or my card.
- As **Googlebot**, I see nothing new: no indexable page references an Airwallex origin.
- As the **implementer of spec 016 or 018**, nothing changes for me: the events, statuses and refunds are spec 013's.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

One migration, `NNNN_payments_fallback.sql` (the next free number when it merges; `0017_payments_a7` is on main) with `NNNN_payments_fallback.down.sql`. No new table; no change to `order_status`, `order_transition` or any order column.

1. **`payment.provider`**: `payment_provider_check` becomes `provider IN ('stripe', 'mollie', 'airwallex')`. `UNIQUE (provider_ref)` stays (Airwallex intent ids and Stripe ids do not share a format; a collision would be refused, which is the safe outcome).
2. **`webhook_inbox.provider`**: `webhook_inbox_provider_check` is recreated as the set on main at merge time plus `'airwallex'` (today `('stripe','mollie')`; spec 016's own migration adds `'resend'`; whichever merges second takes the union).
3. **`webhook_inbox` field check**: `webhook_inbox_stripe_fields_check` (0017) is replaced by `webhook_inbox_card_provider_fields_check`: `provider NOT IN ('stripe','airwallex') OR (livemode IS NOT NULL AND event_type IS NOT NULL)`.
4. **Flags**: two `feature_flag` rows, disabled, the way spec 002 seeded `payments.stripe`: `payments.airwallex` (global kill switch) and `payments.fallback` (takes `country` scope rows; the ref is the **destination** country).
5. **Index**: `payment_provider_created_at_idx ON payment (provider, created_at)` for the ledger check and the silence monitor.

**Rollback** (reverse order). Guard first: if any `payment` or `webhook_inbox` row has `provider = 'airwallex'` and `livemode = true`, the down file raises `FALLBACK_ROLLBACK_LIVE_ROWS` and changes nothing (a real payment is part of the accounting record, `plan/07` §10). Otherwise: drop the index; delete the two flags' scope rows and flag rows; delete test-mode Airwallex rows in key order (`refund`, `payment_dispute`, `payment`, `webhook_inbox`); restore 0017's `webhook_inbox_stripe_fields_check`; restore both provider checks to their pre-migration sets. The migrate, rollback, migrate round trip of spec 002 runs over it.

### 5.2 API / server actions / jobs / events (zod schemas named)

```
src/modules/payments/
  processor.ts         processorFor(destinationCountry, mode), providerFor(name), PROCESSORS
  routed-step.ts       routedTestPaymentStep, routedLivePaymentStep (delegate by processorFor)
  airwallex/
    adapter.ts         AirwallexProvider implements PaymentProvider
    amount.ts          toAirwallexAmount(Money), fromAirwallexAmount(raw, currency)
    auth.ts            token fetch + in-memory cache
    status.ts          Airwallex status → ProviderIntent status table
    version.ts         pinned API version (sent as x-api-version)
    webhook.ts         verify (HMAC), AirwallexEventSchema, normalise → ProviderEvent
    steps.ts           airwallexTestPaymentStep, airwallexLivePaymentStep
  ledger.ts            payments.ledger_check job
src/app/api/webhooks/airwallex/route.ts   POST, thin: payments.ingestWebhook('airwallex', request)
```

**Choosing the processor**
- `processorFor(destinationCountry: CountryCode, mode: 'test' | 'live') → 'stripe' | 'airwallex'`. Reads `payments.fallback` at the destination's `country` scope, `payments.airwallex` global, and whether the Airwallex step for `mode` is registered. Its parameter list is exactly these two: no buyer, request, header, card or history parameter exists, so it cannot be per buyer (type test). It is the **only** reader of `payments.fallback` and `payments.airwallex` (source scan).
- The routed step's `prepare(draftId, quote)` calls `processorFor` and returns that processor's `PaymentPreparation`, tagged with `processor`. The tag travels in step 3's form as a hidden field, validated by zod as one of the two names.
- `confirm(orderId, input)` calls `processorFor` again. If it differs from the submitted tag it returns `ProcessorChanged` (new additive member of spec 013's `PaymentOutcome`): no intent is created on either side and step 3 re-renders with the current processor and `payments.error.methodsChanged`. The order may already be `placed` (spec 013 places before confirming); a placed order with no payment row simply renders step 3 again.
- **A placed order whose live payment is on the other processor** (the buyer was mid-payment when the founder flipped): when step 3 re-renders for it, `switchProcessor(orderId)` runs under the order row lock. It retrieves the old intent; only if it is `requires_payment_method` or `requires_action` does it call `oldProvider.cancel(ref, 'void:{paymentId}')`, and only when that returns `canceled` does it set the old payment `cancelled` and let the new processor's step create a new `pending` payment. In every other case (cancel refused or erroring, intent `processing`, `succeeded`, or provider unreachable) the order stays on the old processor and spec 013's reconcile and sweep apply. Spec 013's partial unique index (one live payment per order) makes two live payments impossible (§13 Q4).
- **No automatic switch.** Provider errors are counted for `payment.provider_unavailable` (§11) and nothing else; the buyer sees spec 013's `payments.error.unavailable`.

**Using the right provider for an existing payment.** `providerFor(payment.provider)` returns the registered adapter for that name or throws `PROVIDER_NOT_REGISTERED` (a bug or missing env: the job retries and ends `dead` with its alert). Spec 013's `syncFromProvider`, `reconcile`, `refund`, the cancel handler, the sweep, the dispute path and `jobs:replay-webhooks` use it instead of a fixed Stripe instance (requirement on spec 013, §12). `payments.airwallex` off stops **new** Airwallex payments only; webhooks, refunds and reconciliation of existing Airwallex payments keep running while the env keys exist.

**The Airwallex adapter** (`AirwallexProvider implements PaymentProvider`, spec 013's interface unchanged)
- **Auth:** `POST /api/v1/authentication/login` with `x-client-id` and `x-api-key`; the returned token is cached in memory until 60 s before its `expires_at` (one replica, ADR-0018). A 401 clears the cache and retries once.
- **Pinned API version** in `version.ts`, sent on every call; contract fixtures live under `tests/contract/fixtures/airwallex/<api-version>/`.
- **`createIntent(CreateIntentInput)`** → `POST /api/v1/pa/payment_intents/create` with: `request_id` = `pi:create:{paymentId}`; `amount` = `toAirwallexAmount(order total)`; `currency` = the order's buyer currency; `merchant_order_id` = the order reference (`FO-XXXX-XXXX`); `metadata` = exactly spec 013's six non-personal keys (`order_id`, `payment_id`, `destination_country`, `buyer_country_declared`, `is_first_order`, `order_mode`); `return_url` = `/{locale}/checkout/return`; automatic capture (no manual-capture option is ever sent; `CreateIntentInput.captureMethod` stays the literal `'automatic'`); 3DS requested on every card payment, the equivalent of spec 013 Q1's `request_three_d_secure: 'any'` (the exact field of the pinned version is fixed in task 1 and recorded in the runbook). **Never sent:** `customer`, `order.shipping`, products, any name, email, phone, address or card message (spec 013 Q4 applies unchanged).
- **`retrieveIntent`, `cancel`, `capture`** map to the intent retrieve, cancel and capture endpoints with `request_id` = `void:{paymentId}` / `capture:{paymentId}`. Capture exists only for spec 013's `requires_capture` recovery and is never expected.
- **`refund(ref, Money, idempotencyKey, metadata)`** → `POST /api/v1/pa/refunds/create` with `payment_intent_id`, `amount` = `toAirwallexAmount`, `request_id` = the refund row id (spec 013's rule), `reason` = the internal reason code, `metadata` = `{ order_id, refund_id }`. Refunds go to the original method only.
- **Status mapping** (`status.ts`, one exhaustive table; an unknown value throws `UnknownProviderStatus`, never a silent default):

| Airwallex intent status | `ProviderIntent.status` |
|---|---|
| `REQUIRES_PAYMENT_METHOD` | `requires_payment_method` |
| `REQUIRES_CUSTOMER_ACTION` | `requires_action` |
| `PENDING` | `processing` |
| `REQUIRES_CAPTURE` | `requires_capture` |
| `SUCCEEDED` | `succeeded` |
| `CANCELLED` | `canceled` |

  Refund statuses map `RECEIVED`, `ACCEPTED` → `pending`; `SETTLED` (or `SUCCEEDED` in older versions; only the pinned version's word is in the table) → `succeeded`; `FAILED` → `failed`. Card brand and last four come from the latest payment attempt's card object; `methodType` from its payment method type (`card`, `applepay`, `googlepay` at launch) mapped to our method ids.
- **Money, never a float.** Airwallex takes amounts in major units as a JSON number ([Airwallex API, payment intents](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/api/payments/payment_intents/create)). `toAirwallexAmount(Money)` builds the decimal **text**, always with exactly two decimals (`45.99`, `0.10`, `1.00`), from the integer by integer division and remainder (exponent 2 for GBP, EUR, PLN from an explicit table; any other currency throws `UnsupportedCurrency`) and the request body is serialised so that text appears verbatim. `fromAirwallexAmount(raw, currency)` reads the number's shortest decimal text, checks it against `^\d+(\.\d{1,2})?$`, and builds the integer from its digits. No `* 100`, `/ 100` or `Math.round` on money exists in `airwallex/` (`fo/no-float-money` passes).
- **`verifyAndParseWebhook(rawBody, headers)`:** reads `x-timestamp` (Unix milliseconds) and `x-signature` (hex); refuses a missing header, a timestamp more than 300 s from now either way, or a signature that is not the hex HMAC-SHA256 of `x-timestamp` followed by the raw body under the environment's secret, compared in constant time with lengths checked first ([Airwallex webhooks](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/developer-tools/webhooks/listen-for-webhook-events)). It then parses `AirwallexEventSchema` (zod over the fields we read) and normalises to `ProviderEvent`.
- **`listEventsSince(date)`:** Airwallex has no public event-list API (events are re-triggered from its dashboard), so the adapter lists payment intents and refunds updated since `date` and returns one synthetic `ProviderEvent` per object and current status, with id `sync:{objectId}:{status}`. Processing always retrieves the object (spec 013's reconcile rule), so a synthetic event has the same effect as the real one, and a second replay is a duplicate key. Disputes are not listed; a missed dispute event is re-triggered from Airwallex's dashboard (runbook).

**Step 3 with Airwallex (hosted payment page)**
1. `prepare` returns `{ processor: 'airwallex', mode, amountMinor, currency, methodTypes }`; no intent exists yet and no Airwallex script is needed to render step 3.
2. The buyer presses the pay button (spec 010's wording and amount, unchanged). `placeOrderAction` places the order (spec 010), then `confirm` runs spec 013's `startPayment` through `providerFor('airwallex')`: a `pending` payment row with `provider = 'airwallex'`, then the intent, idempotent on `pi:create:{paymentId}`. It returns `{ processor: 'airwallex', intentId, clientSecret }` to the island only.
3. The island sends the buyer to Airwallex's hosted page for that intent with Airwallex.js's redirect call (or a server-built URL if the pinned version documents one; task 1 decides and records it). The hosted page collects card, Apple Pay or Google Pay details, runs 3DS and charges.
4. Airwallex returns the buyer to `/{locale}/checkout/return`; spec 013's return route ignores every query parameter, runs `syncFromProvider` (which now resolves the provider from the payment row) and 303s to `/{locale}/checkout/done` with no query string. A webhook may arrive first; reconcile is idempotent either way.
5. A buyer who abandons the hosted page and comes back sees spec 013's "placed, unpaid" step 3 with a retry that returns to the same intent.

**Methods per buyer country.** Spec 013's `methodsFor(buyerCountryDeclared, currency)` gains a processor dimension: `methodsFor(processor, buyerCountryDeclared, currency)` ∩ `available` ∩ `payments.method.{id}` flags. Airwallex rows: `card`, `apple_pay`, `google_pay` available for every buyer country in GBP, EUR, PLN; `blik` (PL, PLN), `p24` (PL, PLN and EUR), `ideal` (NL, EUR), `bancontact` (BE, EUR), `klarna` (per Klarna's market list) with `available: false`. The list depends on the **declared** buyer country and the order currency, never on IP (ADR-0006, `plan/07` §3). `src/config/payment-methods.ts`'s `paymentProcessors` gains `'airwallex'`; the footer registry's single `processor` field keeps naming the primary (§13 Q5).

**Webhook route.** `POST /api/webhooks/airwallex` (Node runtime, `force-dynamic`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`): raw body (over 1 MB → 413); verify (400, nothing stored, on any failure); `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (provider, event_id) DO NOTHING` with `provider = 'airwallex'`, `event_id` = the payload's `id`, `livemode` = the **environment's** mode (Airwallex's demo and production environments have separate hosts and secrets, so an event verified by this environment's secret is of this environment's mode), `event_type`, `object_ref`; enqueue `webhook.process` with `singletonKey` = the inbox id; then 200. A database failure answers 500 so Airwallex retries. `webhook.process` selects the schema by `provider` and routes to spec 013's paths. **Subscribed events** (only these, to keep `raw` small): payment intent succeeded, cancelled, requires-customer-action and requires-capture; payment attempt failed; refund received, accepted, settled and failed; dispute opened, updated and closed (exact names of the pinned version recorded in `webhook.ts` and the runbook).

**Jobs**
- `payments.ledger_check` (pg-boss, daily 03:00 UTC, read-only, actor none): for each registered provider with `payment` rows in the window (the previous UTC day), calls `listEventsSince(windowStart)` and compares, per currency: each provider-reported succeeded intent has a `payment` row with that `provider_ref`, status `captured`, and equal `amount_minor` and currency; each `captured` row in the window appears at the provider; the same for refunds against `refund` rows `succeeded`. Any difference raises `payment.ledger_mismatch` with provider, currency, kind (`missing_local`, `missing_provider`, `amount_differs`), count and the summed minor amounts, never a name or email. A clean run logs one `info` line with counts.

**Environment variables** (secrets only in env, `lib/env.ts` validates, `.env.example` lists them, the founder sets them in Railway): `AIRWALLEX_ENV` (`demo` | `prod`), `AIRWALLEX_CLIENT_ID`, `AIRWALLEX_API_KEY`, `AIRWALLEX_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. `validateEnv`: all four or none (a partial set is refused); `AIRWALLEX_ENV = prod` is refused in every `APP_ENV` other than `production`, and `demo` is refused in `production`. ## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Data flows → RoPA** (`docs/compliance/ropa.md`), two rows:
  1. **Airwallex, live**: the Airwallex entity named in the US merchant agreement (expected Airwallex US, LLC; the founder confirms from the signed agreement) as processor for payment processing and independent controller for its own KYC, fraud and regulatory duties. Subjects: buyers whose destination is switched to the fallback. Sent: amount, currency, the order reference, the six non-personal metadata fields; on Airwallex's own page the buyer enters card or wallet details and any billing details the method needs; device and IP signals gathered by Airwallex's scripts. **Not sent:** recipient name, address, phone, card message, buyer name or email from our side. Transfer: to the US and Airwallex's group (UK, Singapore, Australia, Hong Kong per its privacy notice); mechanism per Airwallex's DPA (SCCs or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the founder files the DPA and records which, `plan/07` §1.4). Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) for payment; Art. 6(1)(f) and Recital 47 for fraud prevention. Kept by us: the `payment`, `refund`, `payment_dispute` rows (accounting record, `plan/07` §10) and `webhook_inbox.raw` 12 months.
  2. **Airwallex, sandbox** (staging and PR environments): the same categories, Airwallex's test cards only, Art. 6(1)(f), spec 010's test-order retention.
  The privacy notice (spec 041) lists Airwallex as a processor beside Stripe.
- **Controller:** the controller is now a US LLC (ADR-0021); GDPR and UK GDPR still apply to EU and UK buyers (Art. 3(2)). The Art. 27 representative question belongs to ADR-0021's follow-ups, not here.
- **Minimisation:** recipient data never reaches Airwallex (spec 013 Q4 extended); subscribed events are limited.
- **Price display and the button** (`plan/07` §4, CRD Art. 8(2)): the amount charged is the order total in the shown currency on either processor; the button on our page names the obligation to pay and the amount before the buyer leaves for the hosted page; no surcharge for any method (PSD2, CRD Art. 19); reconcile cancels on a mismatch.
- **Contract formation and cancellation:** unchanged from spec 013 §8 (charged at placement, free cancellation until we start preparing, full refund to the original method, lawyer-gated wording).
- **Geo-blocking** (EU 2018/302, `plan/07` §3, ADR-0006): methods depend on the declared buyer country and currency only, never IP; the processor depends on the **destination**, which the buyer chooses, never on the buyer's location; at launch both processors offer the same methods, so no buyer sees fewer options.
- **PCI DSS:** card data is entered only on Airwallex's hosted page and never touches our servers, logs or pages (SAQ A).
- **Security:** signature verified before storage; timestamps outside 300 s refused; constant-time compare; live credentials refused outside production; idempotent `request_id` on every write; no amount from the client; the bearer token and API key never logged.
- **Logs and PII:** spec 013 §11's allow-list plus `processor`; the redaction list gains `x-signature`, `x-api-key`, `authorization`, `token`, `client_secret` (already) and `clientSecret` (already). Nothing from a webhook body is logged.
- **Cookies** (`plan/07` §5): any cookie Airwallex's script or hosted page sets on our origin is recorded in sandbox by task 1, classified (essential if fraud prevention) and added to `src/config/cookies.ts` and the cookies page; cookies on Airwallex's own domain are Airwallex's and named in the privacy notice.
- **Accessibility** (`plan/07` §8, EAA): our step 3 states pass axe with zero serious or critical issues in four locales; the hosted page is a third-party component of our service, so its sandbox axe result is recorded, and a serious or critical failure goes to the founder as a decision (switch to option B of §13 Q2), not a silent accept.
- **Statement descriptor:** the founder sets the same descriptor ("FLOWERSOVERSEAS") on both processors, so a buyer recognises the charge and does not dispute it.
- **AML:** refunds only to the original method (`plan/07` §9).
- **Fraud tooling:** no Radar on the fallback; 3DS on every card shifts fraud liability to the issuer; Airwallex's risk engine runs; spec 021's own checks apply to both processors (§13 Q6).

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Choosing the processor**
- **AC-1** `processorFor(destination, mode)` returns `airwallex` exactly when `payments.fallback` is on for that destination's `country` scope, `payments.airwallex` is on, and the Airwallex step for `mode` is registered, over all eight combinations; otherwise `stripe`. A type test fails to compile a call with any third argument. Two requests that differ only in `cf-ipcountry`, `Accept-Language`, declared buyer country or card details produce the same processor. A source scan finds no reader of `payments.fallback` or `payments.airwallex` outside `processor.ts` and spec 012's flags page.
- **AC-2** With the Stripe fake answering every call with a 5xx or a timeout, 100 checkouts for a destination whose fallback is off create zero Airwallex intents and zero Airwallex `payment` rows; each shows `payments.error.unavailable`; flags are unchanged afterwards.
- **AC-3** For a payment with `provider = 'stripe'` and one with `provider = 'airwallex'`, after flipping `payments.fallback` and `payments.airwallex` on and off in every order, every provider call made by `syncFromProvider`, reconcile, `refund`, the cancel handler, the sweep, the dispute path, `jobs:replay-webhooks` and `payments.ledger_check` goes to the adapter named on the row (two recording fakes; zero calls on the other). With `payments.airwallex` off, an Airwallex webhook is still processed and an Airwallex refund still completes.
- **AC-4** Step 3 prepared for `stripe`, then the fallback switched on, then the pay press: `confirm` returns `ProcessorChanged`, zero intents exist on either fake, and step 3 re-renders for `airwallex` with `payments.error.methodsChanged` in the live region. For a `placed` order whose Stripe payment is `requires_payment_method`, re-rendering step 3 after the flip cancels the Stripe intent (key `void:{paymentId}`), sets that payment `cancelled` and creates one `pending` Airwallex payment; when the Stripe cancel is refused, errors, or the intent is `processing` or `succeeded`, the order stays on Stripe with no Airwallex call. Over 50 concurrent runs of re-render and a Stripe `succeeded` webhook, no order ever has two payments outside `cancelled`/`failed`, and no order is charged by both.

**Adapter**
- **AC-5** `AirwallexProvider` (against MSW-served recordings under `tests/contract/fixtures/airwallex/<api-version>/`, directory name equal to `version.ts`) passes spec 013's exported contract suite unchanged; a scan of the fixtures finds no email address or phone number other than Airwallex's documented test values.
- **AC-6** `toAirwallexAmount` and `fromAirwallexAmount` round-trip every integer from 1 to 10 000 000 minor units in GBP, EUR and PLN exactly (property test), with fixed cases 1 → `0.01`, 10 → `0.10`, 100 → `1.00`, 4599 → `45.99`, 9999999 → `99999.99` (always exactly two decimals); `fromAirwallexAmount` reads `1`, `1.5` and `1.50` as 100, 150 and 150; the serialised request body contains the amount text verbatim; any other currency in `src/config/currencies.ts` throws `UnsupportedCurrency`; `fromAirwallexAmount` refuses `45.999`, `-1`, `1e3`; `fo/no-float-money` passes on `modules/payments/airwallex`.
- **AC-7** For every total in spec 005's fixture matrix in GBP, EUR and PLN, the recorded create-intent request carries `amount` and `currency` equal to the order's `buyer_total_minor` and `buyer_currency_code`, `request_id` = `pi:create:{paymentId}`, `merchant_order_id` = the order reference, `metadata` with exactly the six keys of §5.2, the 3DS-on-every-card field of the pinned version, `return_url` = `/{locale}/checkout/return`, and no `customer`, no `order`/`shipping` object and no value matching an email, phone or the fixture recipient's name or address. A second `confirm` for the same order makes no second create.
- **AC-8** Each Airwallex intent and refund status in §5.2's table maps to exactly the listed value; an unlisted status throws `UnknownProviderStatus` and the webhook's inbox row ends `failed`, never `processed`.
- **AC-9** In Airwallex's sandbox, an intent created by the adapter and paid on the hosted page with Airwallex's 3DS test card ends `SUCCEEDED` with the captured amount equal to the order total, never `REQUIRES_CAPTURE`. This proof belongs to task 1 and runs before the lifecycle task.

## 13. Open questions

Each has a recommended default; the spec is written against the defaults.

- **Q1 (blocking the ADR and live use). Airwallex as the fallback processor?** Mollie cannot onboard a US LLC (§1). Airwallex can, self-serve, with the methods we need. Unconfirmed: non-US-resident owners, flower relay risk appetite, UK and EU card fees, reserve. **Recommended: yes, Airwallex**, confirmed by founder action 3 in writing before the live credentials are created; a "no" from Airwallex makes **PayPal** the next candidate (accepting that its local methods refuse cross-border shipping, so it would be cards and the PayPal wallet only). The answer is recorded in a new ADR superseding ADR-0005's fallback half.
- **Q2. Hosted payment page or embedded card form?** (A) Airwallex's hosted page: least code, card data never on our page, every method Airwallex supports works with no step 3 change; the buyer leaves our site for the payment page. (B) Airwallex's embedded Drop-in element on our page: better continuity, more code, more CSP, and it needs an intent before it mounts, so it changes spec 013's place-then-pay order. **Recommended: (A)** for a fallback used during outages; revisit (B) if the fallback carries more than a few days of traffic in a quarter, or if the hosted page fails the recorded axe check.
- **Q3. Which methods on the fallback at launch?** **Recommended: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay only**, mirroring spec 013 Q5, so a flip never changes what a buyer can use. BLIK is the first later addition (Airwallex creates the intent first, so it avoids Stripe's deferred-intent limit, spec 013 §12 task 9).
- **Q4. Move a placed, unpaid order to the new processor after a flip?** **Recommended: yes, only after the old processor confirms its intent cancelled** (§5.2). Alternative: never move; the order waits for the 2-hour sweep and the buyer starts again, retyping recipient details.
- **Q5. What does the footer say about who processes cards?** The approved design prints "Card payments processed by Stripe"; with a fallback that is untrue for buyers of a switched destination. **Recommended: the footer names no processor** ("Secure card payments", spec 004 copy change in the founder's copy batch), and no buyer page names one. Alternative: name both.
- **Q6. Fraud tooling on the fallback.** No Radar rules on Airwallex. **Recommended: accept** 3DS on every card plus Airwallex's own risk engine plus spec 021's checks for both processors, since the fallback carries traffic only during an incident.
- **Q7. Cost.** A US-registered Airwallex account charging UK and EU cards is likely priced as international (one third-party figure is 4.3% + $0.30, unverified) against Stripe's rate. **Recommended: accept for the fallback**, and ask for a written quote in founder action 3.
- **Q8. The dormant Mollie values** (`'mollie'` in two checks and `paymentProcessors`, the `payments.mollie` flag row). **Recommended: leave them**; removal is a cleanup amendment to spec 002 once ADR-0021 and this spec's ADR are accepted, not worth a migration now.
- **Q9 (for spec 013 and ADR-0021, not decided here). The primary under a US LLC.** A US Stripe account contracts with Stripe, Inc., not Stripe Payments Europe, so spec 013's RoPA row, the transfer mechanism, card fees and the method list (Stripe says its European methods are available to businesses worldwide, [Stripe blog](https://stripe.com/blog/european-payment-methods-available-worldwide), and lists the US for BLIK, [Stripe BLIK](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/blik/accept-a-payment)) need a spec 013 amendment. **Recommended:** the orchestrator raises it with ADR-0021's follow-ups.
- **Q10. Spec 013 amendment.** This spec needs six small seams in spec 013 (§12). **Recommended: write them as spec 013 §14 in this spec's docs PR**, so both specs say the same thing; every spec 013 AC holds unchanged with the fallback off.
