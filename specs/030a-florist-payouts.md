## 1. Problem

Florists are agreeing to come on board, and each one is promised two things on `/for-florists` (spec 011): a fixed amount per bouquet, agreed before the first order, that "does not change with the exchange rate or with what the buyer paid", and payment "every week, by bank transfer in your currency, for the orders you delivered that week". On 2026-10-19 the founder added a third: the first five orders are paid up front when the florist accepts them, then weekly or per order, as the florist prefers. Nothing in the system records what a florist is owed. `payout` and `payout_line` exist (spec 002, migration `0005`) but nothing writes them; `order_line.partner_payout_minor` is never filled (`place` leaves it null, because no florist is chosen at placement); spec 016 leaves an `open` `payout_adjustment` row whenever a buyer cancels after a florist accepted, and nothing settles it.

`plan/09` Phase 1 AC 1 asks for one real order "delivered, photo uploaded, buyer notified, payout line created. Zero manual DB edits in the flow." `plan/06` §5.4 sets the rules: a payout line per order in the florist's currency, released only when the delivery photo exists (or the founder waives it with a reason), a weekly statement email, and a Wise batch CSV the founder uploads by hand in Phase 1 (the Wise API is Phase 2, roadmap item `030`). The gap list ranks this third on the launch path: without it a florist is paid from a spreadsheet, and a cancelled-after-acceptance order is forgotten.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the criterion.

**2.1 Payout terms per florist** (admin, `partner.payout` permission)
- Three new fields on a partner: `payout_cadence` (`weekly` or `per_order`, default `weekly`), `prepaid_order_limit` (integer 0 to 50, default 5, the founder's 2026-10-19 rule) and `wise_recipient_id` (the florist's saved-recipient id in our Wise Business account; digits only). **No IBAN, account number or other bank detail is stored**: the florist's bank details live in Wise only (§13 Q7).

**2.2 The amount is fixed when the florist accepts**
- On `assignment.accepted` for a `live` order, each `product` line of the order gets `order_line.partner_payout_minor` (the **unit** amount) and `payout_currency_code` from the accepting florist's `partner_catalog_mapping` for that product and tier. It is written once: a later mapping change (spec 012b lets a florist's price change) never changes it, and a database trigger refuses to overwrite it.
- `addon` and `surcharge` lines get no amount (no add-on or surcharge payout exists in the data, §13 Q3); an order that has any is flagged for the founder's review (2.4).
- `test` and `demo` orders get nothing: no amount, no line, no payout.

**2.3 One payout line per order, at the right moment**
- **Prepaid** (the florist's first `prepaid_order_limit` accepted live orders): the `order` line is written at acceptance, in a payout of kind `prepaid` holding that one order, ready for the founder at once.
- **Otherwise, on the delivery photo**: on `order.photo_uploaded` for a `live` order, the `order` line is written: into the florist's open weekly payout (cadence `weekly`) or into a new one-order payout of kind `per_order` (cadence `per_order`).
- **Photo waiver**: the founder may accrue a `delivered` order that has no photo, with a written reason (`plan/06` §5.4).
- The line amount is the sum over product lines of unit amount times quantity. An order is paid once: the existing `payout_line_order_once_idx` admits one `order` line per order, so a prepaid order gets no second line at its photo.

**2.4 Weekly payouts, adjustments and review**
- Each florist has at most one **open** weekly payout. It covers the days from the end of the previous weekly payout to the Sunday of the current ISO week, in the florist's country time zone. An hourly job closes it once that Sunday has passed in the florist's zone, if its total is above zero; a total of zero or less rolls over to the next Sunday (that is how a claw-back carries forward).
- The founder settles or waives each `open` `payout_adjustment` (spec 002 §14 A10.3). Settling writes an `adjustment` line, with the order and the amount the founder enters, into the florist's open weekly payout.
- The founder may add an `adjustment` line (positive or negative) or a `goodwill` line (positive) to any draft payout, always with a reason.
- An `order` line whose order had add-on or surcharge lines carries `review_reason = 'unpriced_lines'` until the founder resolves it (typically after adding an `adjustment` line for the add-on).
- Lines are frozen once a payout leaves `draft`; the payout total is kept equal to the sum of its lines by the database.

**2.5 Statements**
- The founder sends a statement for a closed draft payout from admin. Sending refuses while the payout is open, its total is not above zero, a review flag is unresolved or, for a weekly payout, the florist has an `open` adjustment created on or before the period end.
- Sending assigns a statement number `P-NNNNNN`, moves the payout to `statement_sent`, renders a tagged PDF into the private bucket and emails the florist's contact address in the florist's language (en, pl or de) with the PDF attached. The statement is a **payout statement, not an invoice**, and carries no VAT figure (§13 Q1, Q2).

**2.6 Wise bulk-payment CSV and payment**
- The founder selects sent payouts and creates an **export**; the CSV follows Wise's saved-recipients batch template (header from config, one row per payout, fixed target amount in the florist's currency, our source currency, the statement number as payment reference). It is generated on request, byte-identical on every download, never stored and never logged.
- A payout can be in one active export only; an export can be cancelled while none of its payouts is paid. The founder uploads the file to Wise by hand, then marks payouts paid.

**2.7 Alerts and the founder's lists**
- Founder emails: a prepaid payout is ready; weekly payouts are ready for review; a `live` order with a photo has had no `order` line for 60 minutes.
- `/admin/payouts` lists open, ready, sent, exported and paid payouts, open adjustments, and delivered orders without a photo for more than 2 hours (the waiver list).

**2.8 Admin pages**: `/admin/payouts`, `/admin/payouts/[id]`, `/admin/payouts/exports/[id]`, and a "Payout terms" block on spec 012's `/admin/partners/[code]`.

## 3. Non-goals

- **The Wise API**, automatic transfers, webhooks from Wise, and Stripe Connect payouts → spec **030** (Phase 2; `plan/09` Phase 2, `plan/13` C2).
- **Self-billing invoices** (a statement that is the florist's VAT invoice, with its own numbering and wording) → spec **030**, only after the accountant answers `plan/06` §4 item 8 (§13 Q1). 030a's statement is a remittance statement.
- **Payout history and statements in the florist portal** → spec **027b**. Florists receive their statements by email in Phase 1.
- **Add-on and surcharge payout prices** as data (an add-on mapping per florist) → spec **027b** or a 012b amendment (§13 Q3). 030a flags those orders for review.
- **Refunds, cancellations, reroutes and the admin order action panel** → spec **020a**. A refund changes no payout automatically (§13 Q13).
- **The order close timer** (`photo_uploaded → closed`) → spec **022a**. 030a accrues on the photo, not on `closed` (§14, building).
- **Accounting export** (payouts with their EUR cost, invoices, VAT by country) → spec **039**. 030a records no EUR cost of a transfer.
- **Florist bank details** of any kind. The application form already asks for none (spec 011); 030a stores only the Wise recipient id.
- **A payout calculator or any payout figure on a public page** (spec 011 keeps its one authored example).

## 4. User stories

- As a **florist**, I am paid exactly the amount I agreed for each bouquet, in złoty, whatever the exchange rate, and I get a statement in my language listing each order with its date and amount.
- As a **new florist**, my first five orders are paid as soon as I accept them, so I do not finance the first bouquets myself.
- As a **florist paid per order**, I am paid for each order once its photo is in, not at the end of the week.
- As the **founder**, every Monday I open one page, see which statements are ready, fix any flagged line, send the statements, download one CSV, upload it to Wise and mark the payouts paid, without touching the database.
- As the **founder**, when a buyer cancels after a florist accepted, the case waits for me on the payouts page until I settle or waive it, and the florist's next statement cannot go out while it waits.
- As the **accountant**, every payout has a number, its lines, the orders they come from and who sent and paid it, kept for ten years.
- As a **buyer** or **recipient**, nothing changes: no payout wording reaches me, and my name, address and phone never appear on a florist's statement.

### 5.2 Module, commands, jobs, contracts

All code lives in `src/modules/partners/payouts/` (the `partners` module owns payouts, `plan/01` §5), exported through `src/modules/partners/index.ts`. `app/admin/**` calls it through thin server actions whose first statement is `requirePermission(…)`, then `withAdminContext` (spec 012).

**Config** `src/config/payouts.ts` (no database; `check:no-db`): `PayoutsConfigSchema` with `prepaidOrderLimitDefault: 5`, `missingLineAlertMinutes: 60`, `waiverListAfterMinutes: 120`, `wise: { sourceCurrency: 'EUR', header: [...] (the exact header row of Wise's saved-recipients batch template, §13 Q7), maxRows: 1000 }`. The header strings are authored once and asserted against a committed copy of the template's header row (`tests/fixtures/wise-batch-header.csv`, header only, no data).

**Pure functions** (unit-tested, no I/O):
- `payoutForOrder(lines, mapping)` → `{ amountMinor: bigint, currency, unpriced: boolean }`: the sum over `product` lines of unit amount times `qty`; `unpriced` is true when any `addon` or `surcharge` line exists; throws `PAYOUT_AMOUNT_UNKNOWN` when a product line has no unit amount.
- `weeklyPeriod(localDate)` → `{ start, end }` of the ISO week (Monday to Sunday) containing a calendar date.
- `localDateIn(instant, ianaZone)` through the i18n module's zone helpers (spec 009); no hour arithmetic, so DST nights are the zone's job.
- `minorToDecimalString(amountMinor, exponent)` → `"149.00"` for `14900n, 2`, `"149"` for exponent 0, `"1.234"` for `1234n, 3`; refuses a negative amount or a non-integer. Machine format for the CSV only (§7).
- `wiseCsv(rows, header)` → the file: RFC 4180 quoting, CRLF line ends, UTF-8 without BOM, rows in statement-number order; any cell beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or carriage return is prefixed with `'` (CSV formula injection).
- `statementNumber(seq)` → `P-` and six digits.

**Commands** (each in one transaction; zod schemas named):
- `savePayoutTerms(partnerId, PayoutTermsEditSchema, actor)`: cadence, prepaid limit, Wise recipient id; with spec 012's `updated_at` concurrency check; audited.
- `fixPayoutAtAcceptance(orderId, assignmentId)`: the `payouts` outbox handler for `assignment.accepted` (`live` only). Locks the accepting partner row `FOR UPDATE`; writes the unit amount and currency on each `product` line still null, from that partner's mapping (product, tier); then, if the partner's count of `prepaid` payouts is below its `prepaid_order_limit`, creates a `prepaid` payout (period = acceptance date in the partner's zone, `closed_at = now()`) with the order's `order` line, flagged `unpriced_lines` when it applies, and enqueues founder email F1. A second run finds the amounts written and the line present and does nothing.
- `accrueOrder(orderId, source: 'photo' | 'waiver')`: the `payouts` outbox handler for `order.photo_uploaded` (`live` only), and the body of the waiver. No-op when the order already has an `order` line. Otherwise, with the partner row locked: cadence `per_order` → a `per_order` payout (period = accrual date in the partner's zone, `closed_at = now()`) with the line; cadence `weekly` → the line goes into the partner's open weekly payout, created when absent with `period_start` = the day after the previous weekly payout's `period_end` (or the accrual date for the first) and `period_end` = the Sunday of the accrual date's ISO week. Missing unit amounts raise `PAYOUT_AMOUNT_UNKNOWN` (the dispatcher retries; F3 alerts).
- `accrueWithoutPhoto(orderId, PhotoWaiverSchema { reason: string 10..500 }, actor)`: refuses `PAYOUT_WAIVER_ILLEGAL` unless the order is `live` and its status is `delivered`; calls `accrueOrder(orderId, 'waiver')`; the reason goes to the audit log.
- `addManualLine(payoutId, ManualPayoutLineSchema { kind: 'adjustment' | 'goodwill', amountMinor: bigint, orderId?: uuid, note: string 3..500 }, actor)`: currency is the payout's; `goodwill` above zero, `adjustment` non-zero (spec 002's check).
- `removeManualLine(lineId, actor)`: `adjustment` and `goodwill` lines of a draft only, and not a line a settled `payout_adjustment` points to.
- `resolveReview(lineId, actor)`: sets `reviewed_at`, `reviewed_by`.
- `settleAdjustment(adjustmentId, AdjustmentSettleSchema { amountMinor: bigint non-zero, note: string 3..500 }, actor)`: the row must be `open`; inserts an `adjustment` line with the row's `order_id` into the partner's open weekly payout (created as in `accrueOrder` when absent, whatever the cadence), sets the row `settled`, `payout_line_id`, `resolved_at`. `waiveAdjustment(adjustmentId, AdjustmentWaiveSchema { reason: string 10..500 }, actor)` sets `waived`, `resolved_at`; the reason goes to the audit log. A resolved row refuses both with `PAYOUT_ADJUSTMENT_RESOLVED`.
- `closeDuePayouts(now)`: the `payouts.close` job, hourly. For each open weekly payout whose `period_end` is before the partner's local date: total above zero → `closed_at = now()`; otherwise `period_end` moves to the Sunday of the current local ISO week. One F2 email per run that closed at least one payout. Idempotent.
- `sendStatement(payoutId, actor)`: refuses, in this order, `PAYOUT_STATUS_ILLEGAL` (not `draft`), `PAYOUT_NOT_CLOSED`, `PAYOUT_TOTAL_NOT_POSITIVE`, `PAYOUT_REVIEW_OPEN`, `PAYOUT_ADJUSTMENT_OPEN` (weekly payouts: an `open` adjustment of the partner with `created_at` on or before the end of `period_end` in the partner's zone). Then sets `statement_number = statementNumber(nextval(...))`, `status = 'statement_sent'`, `sent_at`, and enqueues `payouts.statement_email { payoutId }` (`PayoutStatementJobSchema`).
- `payouts.statement_email` job: renders the PDF with spec 018's PDFKit setup (tagged, `lang`, title, embedded Noto Sans subset, A4) from the payout's rows only, stores it private at `payouts/{yyyy}/{payoutId}.pdf` through spec 002's `Storage` seam as a `media_asset` (`visibility = 'private'`), sets `statement_media_asset_id`, and sends the statement email through spec 012's `Mailer` with spec 016's `notification_delivery` (`source_key = job:payouts.statement_email:{payoutId}`, `template_key = florist-payout-statement`, `audience = 'florist'`, `recipient_ref` = the partner id), so a retry sends once. Recipient: `fulfillment_partner.contact_email`; locale: the `locale_code` of the partner's earliest owner `partner_member`, else the default locale of the partner's country.
- `createWiseExport(WiseExportCreateSchema { payoutIds: uuid[] 1..1000 }, actor)`: every payout `statement_sent`, not in an active export, its partner has a `wise_recipient_id` (else `PAYOUT_WISE_RECIPIENT_MISSING`, naming the partner codes); creates `payout_export` with `source_currency_code` from config and sets `export_id`.
- `wiseExportFile(exportId, actor)` → the CSV bytes: one row per payout, columns filled as: name = `legal_name`; recipient id = `wise_recipient_id`; source currency = the export's; target currency and amount currency = the payout's currency (a **fixed target** payment: the florist receives exactly the amount and we bear Wise's fee, §13 Q8); amount = `minorToDecimalString(total_minor, exponent of the currency)`; payment reference = the statement number. Columns the template has and 030a does not fill are left empty. Each download is logged with `logAdminRead('payout_export', id)`.
- `cancelExport(exportId, actor)`: refuses `PAYOUT_EXPORT_PAID` when any payout in it is `paid`; else sets `cancelled_at` and clears `export_id` on its payouts.
- `markPaid(payoutId, actor)`: refuses unless `statement_sent` and in an export that is not cancelled; sets `paid` and `paid_at`. `markExportPaid(exportId, actor)` does it for every `statement_sent` payout of the export.
- `payouts.watch` job (hourly, beside `payouts.close`): a `live` order with an `order.photo_uploaded` event older than `missingLineAlertMinutes` and no `order` line raises Sentry `alert=payouts.line_missing` and founder email F3, once per order (keyed by order id).

**Outbox** (spec 010's `outboxTargets`, an additive registration): target `payouts` for `(assignment.accepted, live)` and `(order.photo_uploaded, live)`. With `payouts.accrue` off the rows wait `pending` (held, never dropped), as spec 016's `notifications.email` does.

**Permissions** (spec 012's matrix, additive): `payouts.manage` (admin and finance) for every command above except `savePayoutTerms`, which uses the existing `partner.payout` (admin and finance). Ops and content roles get 403.

**Founder emails** (English, spec 016's founder email layout, to `OPS_ALERT_EMAIL`): F1 `payout-prepaid-ready` (partner code, statement link, amount); F2 `payouts-ready` (count of closed payouts, link to `/admin/payouts`); F3 `payout-line-missing` (order reference, link to `/admin/orders/{id}`). Partner codes and order references only; no names or contact data.

## 8. Compliance considerations

- **Data flows (RoPA, new row "Florist payouts"):** subjects: florists who trade as sole traders, and the contact person of each florist business. Categories: legal name, business contact email, Wise recipient id, payout amounts, order references, delivery dates, product names. **Not processed:** IBAN or any bank detail, recipient or buyer data. Purpose: paying florists under the florist agreement and keeping the accounting record. Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) contract; Art. 6(1)(c) for the accounting record. Recipients: Wise (the founder uploads the CSV; Wise's role as payment institution is to be confirmed by the lawyer, likely an independent controller), Resend (processor, existing), Cloudflare R2 private bucket (processor, existing row 7), the accountant. Retention: payouts, lines, exports and statement PDFs 10 years (`plan/07` §1.2 "Contract + 10 years financial"), as a `retention_policy` row. Transfers: none new beyond Wise's own terms (lawyer confirms the Wise entity serving an Estonian business). The RoPA row is written in the PR that ships the migration.
- **Recipient minimisation:** statements and founder emails carry no recipient or buyer field; the statement reminds the florist to delete recipient details of paid orders (`plan/07` §1.3; wording for the lawyer with the partner agreement, `plan/13` B3).
- **Tax and invoicing:** no tax rule is decided here. The statement is a remittance statement that says it is not an invoice and shows no VAT; whether florists invoice us or we self-bill, and whether the agreed amount includes the florist's VAT, are the accountant's (`plan/06` §4 item 8; §13 Q1, Q2). Nothing in 030a has to change for either answer except copy and, for self-billing, spec 030.
- **Price display (consumer):** not touched. Florist amounts are business-to-business figures, never shown to a buyer; no buyer copy is added, so the relay-language check (`src/config/honesty.ts`) has nothing new to scan on buyer surfaces.
- **Consumer information, consent gating:** none.
- **Accessibility:** admin pages to WCAG 2.2 AA (labelled fields, table headers, focus on dialog open and error summary); the statement email has semantic HTML and a plain-text part; the PDF is tagged with `lang`, a title and reading order (as spec 018's invoice).
- **Logs and PII:** log lines carry payout, export, order and partner ids, partner codes, counts and amounts; never a legal name, an email, a Wise recipient id or a CSV cell. The CSV is generated in memory, streamed and never written to disk, R2 or a log. Each download is in the audit log.
- **Security:** every command requires `payouts.manage` or `partner.payout`; CSV cells are neutralised against formula injection; statement PDFs are private with short signed URLs; database triggers stop a sent statement from being edited and a fixed amount from being changed.

## 13. Open questions

Each has a recommended default; implementation can start on the defaults, and only Q1, Q2 and Q7 must be settled before the first live statement.

- **Q1 (accountant): self-billing or the florist's invoice?** `plan/06` §4 item 8. *Default:* the statement is a payout statement that says it is not an invoice and shows no VAT; florists invoice us under their own rules if their law requires it (`plan/07` §1.1, Model A); self-billing is spec 030, after the accountant's written answer.
- **Q2 (accountant and florist agreement): does the agreed amount include the florist's VAT?** *Default:* the mapping amount is the total we transfer; we neither add nor remove VAT; the florist agreement says it is all-inclusive.
- **Q3 (founder): what does a florist get for add-ons and surcharges?** *Default:* nothing automatic; such orders are flagged and the founder adds an `adjustment` line with the agreed amount; add-on payout prices as data come with spec 027b.
- **Q4 (founder, florist agreement): what is a florist paid when the buyer cancels after acceptance?** *Default:* the founder decides each case on the payouts page (settle with an amount, or waive); no amount is pre-filled.
- **Q5 (founder): a prepaid order that is then cancelled or not delivered.** *Default:* the prepaid payout stands; the founder recovers it with a negative `adjustment` (the weekly payout then rolls over until positive) or waives it.
- **Q6 (founder): when do weekly payouts close?** *Default:* the first hourly run after Sunday ends in the florist's zone; F2 tells the founder on Monday morning; the target is statements sent and paid by Tuesday.
- **Q7 (founder): which Wise template?** *Default:* "send to saved recipients", so we hold only a recipient id and no bank details; the founder commits the template's header row as `tests/fixtures/wise-batch-header.csv` before the first export.
- **Q8 (founder): who pays Wise's fee?** *Default:* we do (fixed target amount), so the florist receives exactly the agreed amount, as spec 011's copy promises.
- **Q9 (founder): which balance do we pay from?** *Default:* EUR (`wise.sourceCurrency`); a PLN balance later is a config change.
- **Q10 (founder): statement language.** *Default:* the earliest owner member's language, else the country default (`pl` for Poland).
- **Q11 (founder): photo waiver.** *Default:* allowed only from `delivered`, with a reason of at least 10 characters, listed after 2 hours without a photo.
- **Q12 (lawyer, with the partner agreement, `plan/13` B3): the delete-recipient-details sentence.** *Default:* included, as a reminder of the agreement's duty (`plan/07` §1.3).
- **Q13 (founder): a partial refund to the buyer after delivery.** *Default:* the florist's payout does not change automatically; the founder adds a negative `adjustment` when the agreement allows it.

## 14. Self-check

Written by the spec writer from the advisor's four angles (founder 2026-10-22: no advisor agent).

**Building.** Every write is in the `partners` module behind zod schemas and admin permissions; no order status is written (ADR-0009): 030a only reads events through the outbox. Money is `bigint` minor units with the partner's currency, the total is kept by a trigger, an order is paid once by the existing index, and the fixed amount, the lifecycle and sent statements are protected by triggers, so a bug in a later spec cannot quietly change what a florist was told. Two deliberate departures from the plan: `plan/01` §4.3 says the payout is "fixed at order time", but no florist is chosen at placement, so it is fixed at acceptance, the first moment both the florist and its price are known; `plan/01` §8 accrues on `closed`, but the close timer is spec 022a's and does not exist, and `plan/06` §5.4 releases on the photo, so 030a accrues on `order.photo_uploaded`. Risks: the order of dependencies (§12; A10's migration and spec 016's `notification_delivery` come first), the shared PDF renderer with spec 018, and the DST night of 25 October, which the close-job test pins. Concurrency is covered by row locks on the partner and the open-payout index (AC-6, AC-15).

**Google ranking.** Nothing public changes: admin routes are blocked and `noindex`, PDFs are private, and no script, font or page reaches an indexable URL. No effect on CWV, sitemaps or hreflang.

**Law and compliance.** No tax rule is invented: the statement is a remittance statement that says it is not an invoice and carries no VAT, the two VAT questions go to the accountant with safe defaults (Q1, Q2), and self-billing stays in spec 030. Bank details are never stored (Wise saved recipients), the CSV is never stored or logged and is neutralised against formula injection, the RoPA gains one row with a 10-year retention, and recipient data never reaches a statement. Wise's controller role and the delete-reminder sentence are for the lawyer.

**The customer.** Buyers and recipients see nothing new; no payout wording, florist name or relay language reaches a buyer surface. Florists get exactly the amount they set, in złoty, unchanged by the exchange rate or by later price edits; their first five orders are paid at acceptance; their statement arrives in their own language; and a cancellation after acceptance can no longer be forgotten, because it blocks their next statement until the founder settles it.
