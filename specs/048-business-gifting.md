## 1. Problem

Companies send flowers too: to an employee's family abroad after a birth or a death, to a client in Warsaw for a contract signed, to every member of a remote team in Poland on Women's Day. A business order is worth more than a consumer order (several gifts, repeat dates, no price shopping once a supplier is trusted), and the founder approved a business page on 2026-10-24 (idea I). Today a company that lands on the site sees a consumer shop and no way to say "we need 40 bouquets in 6 countries on 8 March". It either orders 40 times by card or leaves.

Every incumbent has a business door, and none of them starts with software. Research (§14) shows the common minimum is **a dedicated page plus a person to talk to**: Interflora Australia asks companies wanting three or more gifts to email, and sets a 50-orders-a-year floor for its corporate service; Interflora New Zealand offers an account manager and "streamlined invoicing"; Bloom & Wild lists a business email and phone and offers a free business account for regular orders; Euroflorist offers a corporate account with a credit check, consolidated or per-order invoices and an employee discount, and lets companies without an account pay by invoice through Billie; Fleurop Switzerland offers a team account, 5 % off and branded flowers. Invoicing on account, consolidated invoices and bulk upload come **after** the conversation, for customers who already order. So the minimum that earns business revenue now is an indexable page that ranks for "corporate flowers Poland / Europe" and an enquiry form that reaches the founder in minutes. Invoicing on account and CSV bulk orders are specified here as defaults for a later slice (§5.5), because each needs the accountant (VAT invoices from a US LLC to EU companies, ADR-0021) and the lawyer (credit terms) first.

**The enquiry form (writes `business_enquiry`)**
- A plain `<form method="post">` handled by a Next server action on the same page, working with JavaScript disabled. Fields, in two fieldsets ("Your company", "Your gifts"), each at most eight inputs (`plan/04` §9):
  1. company name (required, 1 to 120 graphemes);
  2. your name (required, 1 to 120);
  3. work email (required, `z.email()`, at most 254);
  4. phone (optional, `^\+?[0-9 ()-]{7,20}$`, not normalised: the founder calls it);
  5. where your company is (required, ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 from the full country list, in `collator(locale)` order);
  6. where the gifts go (required, one or more of the seeded destination countries, plus "Somewhere else");
  7. how many gifts a year (required, one of `1-10`, `11-50`, `51-200`, `200+`);
  8. what for (optional, any of: employees, clients, team occasions, condolences, events);
  9. first date you need (optional, ISO date, today or later in the page's locale reading);
  10. anything else (optional, at most 1000 graphemes);
  11. `hp` honeypot (hidden from people and assistive tech; `z.string().max(0)`).
- One zod schema, `BusinessEnquiryInputSchema` (`.strict()`). An invalid submission re-renders the page with an error summary that takes focus and links to each field, a message under each field, and every answer preserved. A valid one writes one row and answers **303** to the sent page, whose URL carries no query string.
- **Abuse controls, none of which reads an IP address or a user agent** (spec 004 §8's rule, spec 011's precedent): the honeypot (non-empty: answer as success, write nothing, send nothing); a per-instance fixed-window limit through `createRateLimiter` (`src/lib/csp-report.ts`; global on Railway's single replica, ADR-0018), default 20 submissions per 10 minutes, then **429** with the phone and WhatsApp fallback; a bounded body read through `readBoundedBody` (64 KB, then **413**); and a link rule: a message carrying more than 2 URLs is stored with `status = 'spam'` and sends no alert. No CAPTCHA or Turnstile (a third-party script and a processor for a form with no spam yet; the runbook says when to revisit).
- **The write** goes through a `BusinessEnquirySink` seam (the `ReminderSink` precedent, `src/lib/reminders.ts`). `dbBusinessEnquirySink` inserts one row under `withDbContext({ role: "public" })` with no `RETURNING` (the public role has no `SELECT`).
- **The notice at collection** (GDPR Art. 13) sits directly above the submit button: the controller line `company.controller` (spec 041; the LLC under ADR-0021), purpose ("to answer your enquiry and, if you order, to set up your orders"), legal basis (steps you asked us to take before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)), retention (12 months after your last contact with us), recipients by category (our email and hosting providers), your rights and how to use them. A link to the privacy notice replaces the controller line where `infoPageExists("privacy", locale)` is true. `privacy_notice_version` is stored on the row.
- **No consent checkbox.** Processing an enquiry the person sent is Art. 6(1)(b); a consent box would assert the wrong basis and make the form look like a marketing sign-up. No marketing opt-in is offered: nothing sends marketing before spec 033 (spec 010 §3's reasoning).
- **The founder alert:** each stored, non-spam enquiry sends exactly one email through spec 012's `Mailer` to `BUSINESS_ENQUIRIES_NOTIFY_EMAIL` (new, validated by `lib/env.ts`, listed in `.env.example`), tag `business_enquiry_alert`, carrying the enquiry's fields and a link to its admin detail page. A failed alert never loses the enquiry: the row is already committed, and the failure is a Sentry warning with no field value. No email is sent to the enquirer (§13 Q3).
- **The form switch:** a feature flag `business.enquiry_form` (global, default `off`) in spec 012's registry (`src/config/feature-flags.ts`), flipped in `/admin/flags`. With `off`, the page renders the "talk to us directly" block in the form's place and no `<form>`; the server action answers a closed error and writes nothing. It flips `on` once the production database has §5.1's migration. This is a data flip in admin, as `CLAUDE.md` requires.

**Admin (`/admin/business-enquiries`, spec 012's shell and sign-in)**
- List: newest first, 50 per page, filter by status and destination country; a row shows created time, company name, volume band, destination countries, status. No email or phone in the list.
- Detail: every field, status history, and the status control: `new → contacted → won | lost`, any status `→ spam`; `lost` and `spam` need a reason (`status_reason`). Opening the detail writes `audit_log` `business_enquiry.viewed` (spec 012 §2's read-logging rule); every change writes an `audit_log` row with personal fields masked.
- One nav item "Business enquiries" with a count of `new` rows.

**Retention**
- `business_enquiry` rows are deleted 12 months after `updated_at` by spec 002's `retention.sweep`, through one new `retention_policy` row. This is the only copy of the rule.

**Gates, docs, artboards**
- e2e JS-on and JS-off in `en-gb` and `pl`; axe on the page, its error state and the sent page in four locales; visual baselines desktop and mobile; Lighthouse on `/en-gb/corporate-flowers`; the crawl and `seo:validate` cover the new URLs; `docs/runbooks/business-enquiries.md`; RoPA row; artboards `docs/design/wireframes/business-page.dc.html` (desktop, mobile, error state, flag-off state), `business-sent.dc.html` and `admin-business-enquiries.dc.html` drawn before `/plan-tasks`.

## 4. User stories

- As an **office manager in London** whose colleague's mother has died in Kraków, I find "corporate flowers Poland", read in a minute how to send flowers on the company's behalf, and send them by card the same day.
- As an **HR lead planning Women's Day gifts for 30 staff in Poland and Germany**, I fill in one short form with countries, volume and date, and I know who will answer and when.
- As the **founder**, I get an email within seconds of an enquiry with everything I need to call back, and I see every enquiry and its status in admin.
- As a **visitor with a screen reader**, the form has labels, a reachable error summary and keeps my answers when I make a mistake.
- As **Googlebot**, I find one indexable, server-rendered page per reviewed locale with reciprocal hreflang, a self-canonical and visible FAQ, and no sent or admin page.
- As a **spammer**, my honeypot or link-stuffed submission costs the founder nothing.

- Sent page: SSG, `noindex`. Admin pages: SSR `no-store`, auth-gated, `noindex` (spec 012).
- Client JS added: **0 bytes**. The page stays inside spec 004's first-load ceiling.
- CSP: no new origin.

### 5.5 Slice B: invoicing on account and bulk orders (scope boundary with defaults; not built now)

These defaults let the founder answer one question later ("B defaults?") instead of a new spec. Each needs its own amendment round with acceptance criteria before `/plan-tasks`.

1. **Precondition.** The accountant's ruling in `docs/compliance/vat-signoff.md` covers B2B sales by the LLC (whether a company buyer gets an invoice showing the destination's VAT, and in which language and currency), and a lawyer-reviewed business terms document exists (`content/legal/{locale}/business-terms.md`, legal family). Until both exist slice B cannot start.
2. **Business account.** An admin-created `business_account` (company legal name, registry number, VAT number where it has one, billing address, invoice email, PO reference required yes/no, payment terms, credit limit as integer minor units plus currency, status `active | paused | closed`). Created by the founder after a `won` enquiry; never self-service.
3. **Ordering on account.** A signed-in member of a business account (spec 018's account sign-in) sees "Pay by invoice" as a payment step in checkout (spec 013's `CheckoutPaymentStep` registry: a new `invoicePaymentStep`). The order is placed and reaches `paid_unrouted` through `orderService.transition` with `funding: 'account'`, only while the account's open balance plus this order stays within its credit limit. Price shown = price charged: the same all-in price as everyone; no hidden fees.
4. **Consolidated invoice.** One invoice per account per calendar month, listing every order with its VAT by rate, due 14 days after issue, payable by bank transfer (or Stripe Invoicing's hosted page). Overdue by 14 days pauses the account. Credit notes for cancelled orders as today (spec 018).
5. **Bulk orders by CSV.** Upload a CSV (template download; UTF-8; at most 200 rows): one row per gift with recipient name, phone, address fields of the destination's format (`address-formats.ts` columns), destination country, product SKU, tier, delivery date, card message, sign-as. Each row is validated like checkout step 1 and 2 (same zod schemas, same `canServe`, same date and cutoff rules) and shown back with per-row errors; nothing is placed until every row is valid and the buyer confirms the one total. Placement creates one order per row through `orderService.place` and one payment (card) or one account charge for the total. A partial failure places nothing.
6. **Discounts.** None by default; a negotiated percentage would be a per-account discount code (idea F's machinery), shown on the product price before the buyer commits.

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

- **Indexability.** `/{locale}/corporate-flowers` is indexable in a locale only when its content file exists and is `reviewed: true` (spec 041's rule); otherwise `noindex,follow` (file exists, unreviewed) or 404 (no file). The sent page is always `noindex,nofollow`, header and meta. Admin routes are already disallowed and `noindex`. Registered in `modules/seo/indexability.ts` as `SeoPageType` `business` (content-gated) and `businessSent` (constant `noindex`).
- **Canonical and hreflang.** Self-canonical, lowercase, no parameters. hreflang from `modules/seo/hreflang.ts` over the locales where the page is indexable, reciprocal, with `x-default` → `/en/corporate-flowers` (`plan/02` §8). An unreviewed `de` or `pl` file is not an alternate.
- **URL pattern and localised slugs.** §2's table; ASCII, lowercase, hyphenated, unique per locale, asserted by `i18n:check`; composed by `localePath()`. `corporate-flowers` matches the head query in English ("corporate flowers"), `kwiaty-dla-firm` the Polish ("kwiaty dla firm"), `blumen-fuer-firmen` the German ("Blumen für Firmen"); the native reviewer confirms `de` and `pl` before first indexing, because changing an indexed slug costs a redirect forever (§13 Q1).
- **Title and H1 targets.** Title "Corporate flowers to Poland and Europe | Flowers Overseas" pattern (`seoTitle` at most 60 characters); intents: "corporate flowers Poland", "send flowers to employees abroad", "business flowers Europe", "flowers for clients abroad".
- **Schema types.** `WebPage` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` (only the visible FAQ, text identical). Never `Product`, `Offer`, `Service` with a price, `LocalBusiness` or `FloristShop` (`plan/02` §9).
- **Sitemap membership.** `/sitemaps/{loc}/static.xml` for each locale where indexable, with `xhtml:link` alternates from the same data as the head; `<lastmod>` is the content file's `updatedAt`. The sent page never.
- **Internal links.** In: footer item "For business" in every locale where the page exists (spec 004 site-link registry; a site link with no page renders nothing, spec 004 §14 A20), and one in-body link from the help page. Out: each live destination's country page (`plan/02` §11: authority to money pages), the help page, the delivery page. No link to the sent page from anywhere (it is a redirect target).
- **Thin-content risk and mitigation.** One page per locale, authored, not templated per country; at least 400 words of unique prose in `en` (a `pages:check` floor for the `business` family), 6 to 10 FAQ items, and the form. No per-country variants (§3).
- **CWV budget impact.** No island, no new script, no image above the fold (a text hero). LCP is the H1 text; target LCP < 2.0 s, CLS < 0.05 on mobile, asserted by Lighthouse CI on `/en-gb/corporate-flowers`.

## 7. i18n considerations (mandatory)

- **New message keys.** `business.form.*` (labels, hints, options, errors, submit, honeypot label), `business.notice.*` (the Art. 13 notice), `business.sent.*`, `business.closed.*`, `business.limited.*`, `admin.business.*` (list, detail, statuses, reasons, nav). All ICU, typed, zero literals. `en` is authored and goes to the founder as one copy batch (Appendix A); `en-gb` overrides only where British wording differs; `de` and `pl` drafted by `pnpm i18n:draft` and marked unreviewed. Copy headroom (spec 003 §14 A17) is tight: the form reuses reviewed checkout keys where the label is identical (name, email, phone) and reports every new key's text in the PR.
- **Prose.** `content/pages/{locale}/business.md`; `en` written by the founder's batch; `de` and `pl` are human-written or human-edited (`plan/02` §12: no machine translation on indexable prose) and stay `noindex` until `reviewed`.
- **Locale formatting.** Country names through `Intl.DisplayNames` in `format.ts`; the country select sorted by `collator(locale)`; the first-date field is a native `<input type="date">` and is shown back to the founder with `formatDate` in `en`; volume bands rendered through ICU (`1-10` is a value, the label is "1 to 10 a year").
- **Phones and names.** Free-text, NFC-normalised, diacritics preserved (Polish and German company names). No address is asked.
- **RTL.** Logical properties only; company and contact names in `<bdi>` in admin; `/ar-XB` visual baseline for the page and its error state.
- **Translation review plan and `noindex` gating.** Indexing per locale is gated by `reviewed` (above). Form and notice keys follow spec 003's review flags; the Art. 13 notice wording is part of the privacy review (lawyer item `plan/07` §11.2).

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Data flows added → RoPA** (`docs/compliance/ropa.md`, row added by the task that first writes the table): **Business enquiries.** Subjects: people at companies who enquire. Categories: company name, contact name, work email, optional phone, company country, destinations, volume band, needs, first date, free-text message. Purpose: answer the enquiry and prepare orders. Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) (steps at the data subject's request before a contract). Retention: 12 months after the last change. Recipients: the founder; processors Neon (Frankfurt), Railway (Amsterdam), Cloudflare, Resend (EU region). Controller: Flowers Overseas LLC (ADR-0021); the EU and UK Art. 27 representatives ADR-0021 requires are named in the privacy notice once appointed (a precondition of the privacy page, not of this form, whose inline notice names the controller and the contact route).
- **Lawful basis and consent gating.** No consent is collected or needed (§2). No consent-requiring script loads; no cookie is set by the page or the form.
- **Price display.** The page shows no price. The slice B defaults keep one all-in price for businesses (`plan/07` §4).
- **Consumer information.** No contract is formed by an enquiry, so no pre-contract block. The page promises only a reply (and the reply time once approved, which the founder must be able to keep: honesty rule, spec 041). Business buyers who order online do so through the normal checkout and are treated as consumers (the stronger regime), so nothing in the consumer flow changes.
- **Geo-blocking (EU 2018/302).** Any company country may be chosen; nothing is redirected or gated by location (ADR-0006).
- **Brand voice.** No buyer-facing string or prose says or implies "partner", "florist accepts", "network", "relay", "vendor" or "third party" (`RELAY_PATTERNS`, `BANNED_VOICE_WORDS`); the page says "we" make and deliver.
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA, `plan/07` §8).** §5.3; axe zero serious or critical on the page, its error state, its flag-off state and the sent page in four locales; keyboard-only submission; the honeypot is invisible to assistive tech.
- **Logs and PII.** Logged: `request_id`, outcome kind, `locale`, `volume_band`, error codes; counters `business_enquiry_received: 1`, `business_enquiry_spam: 1`. Never: company name, contact name, email, phone, message. The logger's redaction list gains `company_name` and `message` (`*name`, `email` and `phone` are already covered). Sentry's `beforeSend` scrubs request bodies on the business route. No personal value in any URL (the sent page has no query string; admin detail URLs carry the row id only).
- **Security.** Server action Origin check; zod `.strict()`; bounded body; the limiter; React escaping; the alert email escapes every field (React Email); no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**The page**
- **AC-1** With JavaScript disabled, `GET /en-gb/corporate-flowers` and `GET /en/corporate-flowers` answer 200 with server-rendered HTML containing the H1, every section of the content file, the FAQ text, and (flag on) a `<form method="post">` with the eleven fields of §2.
- **AC-2** For `de` and `pl`: with no content file the URL answers 404; with an unreviewed file it answers 200 with `noindex,follow` in `X-Robots-Tag` and meta, and it is absent from the sitemap and from every other locale's hreflang set; with a reviewed file it is indexable.
- **AC-3** An indexable business page carries a self-canonical, a reciprocal hreflang set over exactly the indexable locales plus `x-default` → `/en/corporate-flowers`, and appears in that locale's `static.xml` with matching `xhtml:link` alternates; `seo:validate` passes over the built set.
- **AC-4** The page's JSON-LD contains exactly `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage`; every FAQ `name` and `acceptedAnswer.text` equals the visible text; no `Product`, `Offer`, `LocalBusiness` or `FloristShop` node exists.
- **AC-5** The "where we deliver" block links to the country page of every destination whose `corridorState()` is `live`, and to no other; with zero live destinations the block is absent from the HTML.
- **AC-6** The footer of every locale where the page exists links to it; the help page links to it; no `<a href>` anywhere points at the sent page.
- **AC-7** `pnpm pages:check` fails, naming file, field and rule, on a `business` fixture containing each of: a banned voice word, a `RELAY_PATTERNS` match, a price literal, a scale claim, fewer than 400 words in `en`, an FAQ outside 6 to 10 items; and passes on the committed files.

**The form**
- **AC-8** A valid submission writes exactly one `business_enquiry` row with every field as submitted (NFC, email lower-cased), `status = 'new'` and the current `privacy_notice_version`, and answers 303 to `/{locale}/corporate-flowers/sent` with no query string.
- **AC-9** An invalid submission writes nothing, answers 200 with an error summary that is the first focusable element after the heading and links to each invalid field, a message under each field, and every submitted value preserved.
- **AC-10** A non-empty honeypot answers 303 to the sent page and writes no row and sends no email; the submission after the limiter's window allowance answers 429 with the phone and WhatsApp fallback and writes nothing; a body over 64 KB answers 413; a body with a field the schema does not know writes nothing; none of these paths reads `x-forwarded-for`, `cf-connecting-ip` or `user-agent`.
- **AC-11** A message with three or more URLs is stored with `status = 'spam'` and `status_reason = 'links'` and sends no email; a message with two URLs is stored as `new` and sends the alert.
- **AC-12** Each stored `new` enquiry sends exactly one email with tag `business_enquiry_alert` to `BUSINESS_ENQUIRIES_NOTIFY_EMAIL`, containing every field and the admin detail URL; when the mailer throws, the row is still stored, the visitor still reaches the sent page, and Sentry records a warning containing no field value. `validateEnv` rejects a missing or malformed `BUSINESS_ENQUIRIES_NOTIFY_EMAIL` in `staging` and `production`, and `.env.example` lists it.
- **AC-13** The Art. 13 notice renders directly above the submit button with the controller line (or the privacy link where `infoPageExists("privacy", locale)`), purpose, basis, retention, recipients and rights; the form has no checkbox for consent or marketing.
- **AC-14** With flag `business.enquiry_form` off, the page renders the direct-contact block and no `<form>`, and invoking the server action directly returns `closed` and writes nothing; flipping the flag in `/admin/flags` renders the form after the page's next request, with no deploy.

**Admin**
- **AC-15** `/admin/business-enquiries` without an admin session redirects to admin sign-in; with one it lists enquiries newest first, 50 per page, filterable by status and destination, and no list row contains an email address or a phone number.
- **AC-16** Opening an enquiry's detail writes one `audit_log` row `business_enquiry.viewed`; each legal status move succeeds and writes one masked `audit_log` row; `lost` and `spam` without a reason are refused; every illegal move (for example `new → won`) raises `BUSINESS_ENQUIRY_STATUS_ILLEGAL` and writes nothing.
- **AC-17** Under `withDbContext({ role: "public" })` an `INSERT` succeeds and a `SELECT`, `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `business_enquiry` fails; under the `partner` role every statement fails.

**Retention, migration, privacy**
- **AC-18** `retention.sweep` deletes a row whose `updated_at` is 12 months and one day old and keeps one 12 months minus one day old.
- **AC-19** The migration applies, rolls back and re-applies cleanly in spec 002's round-trip test; `db:check` passes (RLS coverage, rollback file present).
- **AC-20** Over the submission paths (valid, invalid, honeypot, spam, limited, mailer failure), the captured logger output and Sentry events contain none of the submitted company name, contact name, email, phone or message.

**Quality gates**
- **AC-21** axe reports zero serious or critical issues on the page (form state, error state, flag-off state) and the sent page in `en`, `en-gb`, `de`, `pl`; the form can be completed and submitted with the keyboard alone.
- **AC-22** Visual baselines exist for the page (desktop, mobile, error state) in `en-gb`, `pl`, `de` and `/ar-XB`, and for the sent page in `en-gb`.
- **AC-23** Lighthouse CI on `/en-gb/corporate-flowers` (mobile) reports LCP < 2.0 s, CLS < 0.05 and a performance score of at least 95, and the route's client JS is unchanged from the site's shared baseline (no new chunk).
- **AC-24** No component under the new routes contains a literal user-facing string or a physical CSS property (`i18n:check`, `lint` with `fo/no-physical-css`), and `RELAY_PATTERNS` finds nothing in any `business.*` or `admin.business.*` message value in any locale.

## 12. Rollout

- **Order:** (1) migration + module + admin (flag off); (2) page and prose for `en`/`en-gb` (indexable once the founder approves the copy batch); (3) flip `business.enquiry_form` on in production after the migration is applied; (4) `de` and `pl` prose when the native reviewers are engaged.
- **Flags:** `business.enquiry_form` (global). The page itself is gated by its content file and `reviewed` flag.
- **Environments:** staging first with the capturing mailer; production with Resend.
- **Rollback:** flag off (form gone, page stays); remove the site link to hide the page; the down migration drops the table only after its rows are exported for the founder if any exist (runbook step).
- **Task outline for `/plan-tasks`** (review-only class except task 1, which touches a migration and a data flow, so it is self-reviewed under DoD item 4's breaker areas): 1. migration, module, sink, alert, retention, env (AC-8 to AC-12, AC-17 to AC-20); 2. page, registry, segment, prose plumbing, SEO, footer, pages:check family (AC-1 to AC-7, AC-13, AC-14, AC-21 to AC-24); 3. admin list and detail (AC-15, AC-16). Tasks 1 and 3 share `modules/business` and go to one agent.
- **Docs:** `docs/runbooks/business-enquiries.md` (reading and answering, statuses, retention, spam handling, when to add Turnstile), RoPA row, `.env.example`, codebase map regenerated.
