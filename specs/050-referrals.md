## 1. Problem

`plan/09` names referral as the Phase 1 growth channel: Polish families in the UK tell each other where they send flowers home, and "a referral code on every confirmation" turns each happy buyer into the next one. Nothing exists for it. There is no code, no link, no way to reduce a price at checkout for a friend, no store credit, no ledger to show what we owe in credit, and no way to measure whether referral pays.

The founder is worried about margin, so the model is built to cost money only when a new customer pays: the friend's reduction is a small fixed amount on a first order above a minimum, and the referrer's reward is store credit (not cash), granted only after the friend's flowers are delivered, spendable only on a later order, capped and expiring. Getting it wrong costs money (credit minted for self-referrals or refunded orders, credit spent twice, a charge that differs from the price shown) or legal standing (a tracking cookie without consent, advertising in a transactional email, unfair programme terms, a misleading "give X, get X" headline). This spec makes each of those impossible by construction or refuses it with a test.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the criterion.

**2.1 The programme, as data** (AC-1 to AC-3)
- One switch, the high-risk feature flag `referrals.enabled` (global, default **off**), flipped in spec 012's `/admin/flags` or from `/admin/referrals`. Turning it on is refused until every sold currency has amounts, the programme terms are published, and the tax question is confirmed (§5.2 `referralSwitchReadiness`). Turning it off is always allowed and stops new codes, new cookies and new friend reductions at once; referrals already placed are still rewarded and credit already granted stays usable (honouring promises, §8).
- Amounts per currency in integer minor units, editable by an admin: the reward (the same figure is the friend's reduction and the referrer's credit) and the minimum order value. Recommended defaults: **GBP 5.00 off a GBP 30.00 order, EUR 6.00 off EUR 35.00, PLN 25.00 off PLN 150.00**. Programme settings: credit valid **6 calendar months**, **10** rewards per referrer, credit may pay at most **50 %** of a purchase, cookie life **30 days**, at most **20** friend placements per code per 24 hours. Never a percentage reduction.

**2.2 Codes and sharing** (AC-4 to AC-8)
- Each customer who has a paid live purchase gets one opaque 8-character code (CSPRNG, Crockford base32, nothing derived from a name or email), created on first display and tied to the currency of that purchase.
- A "give {amount}, get {amount}" block, with its conditions next to the headline, on: the order confirmation's paid states (spec 013), the account (spec 018), and the B1 confirmation email **only for buyers who have given marketing consent** (§8, §13 Q10). Share by copy link, a WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/?text=`), and the device share sheet where the browser offers it. The code is also shown so a friend can type it at checkout (spec 047's code field).
- We never send anything to a friend. No field asks for a friend's email or phone.

**2.3 The link and the cookie** (AC-9 to AC-12)
- `GET /{locale}/invite/{code}`: for an active code with the programme on, sets two first-party cookies (`fo_ref`, the code, `HttpOnly`; `fo_ref_n`, the digit `1`, readable by a tiny island) and answers **302 to the clean locale home `/{locale}`**. Every other case gives the same 302 without cookies. The response is `no-store`, `noindex`, and the path is disallowed in `robots.txt`, so no indexable duplicate exists.
- The cookies are **strictly necessary** for a service the friend expressly asked for by opening the offer link, used for that one purpose only (§8 "Cookie basis", with sources); they are listed as essential and never read by analytics.
- Public cached pages stay byte-identical for every visitor. A post-paint island shows a dismissible "Your friend's welcome offer is saved" note when `fo_ref_n` is present, with no network request and no layout shift.

**2.4 The friend's reduction at checkout** (AC-13 to AC-23)
- Applied when: the programme is on, a valid code comes from `fo_ref` or is typed, the purchase is `live` (or `test` on staging), the basket's list total is at least the minimum, the buyer is a new customer, and the buyer is not the referrer. It shows in the basket and at step 3 as its own line; the total, the Pay button, the pre-contract block, the PaymentIntent and `purchase.total_minor` all carry the reduced amount (price shown = price charged).
- The reduction is split across the purchase's orders and their lines (largest remainder), VAT is computed on the reduced amounts per rate, and **the florist payout never changes**.
- Anything that changes between the review and the Pay press (switch off, code disabled, credit expired, the friend's other basket took the offer) refuses placement with spec 010's price-changed notice; nothing is charged that was not shown.

**2.5 The referrer's store credit** (AC-24 to AC-36)
- A referral is created `pending` at placement. It becomes `rewarded` (one credit grant) when the first order of the friend's purchase reaches `delivered`, read from `order.delivered` through the outbox (ADR-0009), and only while what the friend kept paying plus the reduction is still at least the minimum. It becomes `void` (no credit ever) or `revoked` (unspent credit removed, never a debt) on non-payment, cancellation, refunds below the minimum, a chargeback, or a self-referral found through the payment fingerprint.
- Credit is a ledger of grants and movements in integer minor units per currency. It is used only by a **signed-in** buyer, only on purchases in its own currency, applied by default with "Keep my credit for later", up to 50 % of the purchase and never below the currency's minimum charge, oldest expiry first. It expires 6 calendar months after the grant. It is never cash, never withdrawable, never transferable.

**2.6 Abuse controls** (AC-15, AC-16, AC-21, AC-22, AC-26, AC-29)
- Self-referral blocked by email match key (case, `+tag`, Gmail dots), buyer phone, and the payment provider's card fingerprint where offered (checked after capture; voids the referral). Same recipient address and phone are **recorded as a signal for admin review, not blocked** (two siblings sending to the same mother is the programme's best case, §13 Q8).
- One friend reduction per new customer; two referrers claiming one friend resolve to exactly one referral; per-code daily velocity cap; per-referrer reward cap; landing rate limit at the edge.

**2.7 Admin** (AC-37 to AC-39)
- `/admin/referrals` (admin only): settings, the switch readiness, metrics per currency for a date range (referred purchases by status, revenue kept, friend reductions, credit granted, redeemed, expired, revoked, outstanding), the referral list with signals, and two actions: void a pending referral, disable a code. No control mints credit.
- Spec 030b's finance view gains the reduction lines, the referral cost per referred order, the margin per referred order, and store credit outstanding as a liability (requirement on 030b, §5.2).

## 3. Non-goals

- **Cash rewards, payouts to referrers, withdrawals, credit transfers between customers.** Credit is a reduction on our own sales only.
- **Percentage reductions.** Never (founder). Spec 047 owns any percentage discount code.
- **Emailing, texting or messaging a friend, collecting a friend's contact details, or an "invite by email" form.** The referrer shares by their own means.
- **Gift cards or sold vouchers** → `plan/05` row 24 (Phase 4); a sold voucher is a different VAT and consumer-law object.
- **Credit minted by an admin** (goodwill credit, apology vouchers) → spec 020 or a later spec; `plan/06` §5.2's apology voucher stays unbuilt.
- **Multi-level or team rewards, leaderboards, prize draws, tiers.** Single level: the person whose code was used.
- **Florist-to-florist referrals** → `plan/06` sourcing (founder-run).
- **Converting credit between currencies** (§13 Q2 records the alternative).
- **Telling the referrer who bought.** The referrer sees amounts and counts, never a friend's name, email, order or recipient.
- **Syncing referral data to a CRM** → roadmap `032`.
- **Reminder or "your credit expires soon" emails** (§13 Q20).
- **The discount-code engine itself** (code field, reduction lines, allocation, VAT recalculation, invoice line) → spec **047**; this spec registers two reduction kinds in it (§5.2).

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer in London** who just sent roses to Gdańsk, I see "Give £5, get £5" under my order, with the conditions in one line, and I send my link to my cousin on WhatsApp in two taps.
- As **that cousin**, I open the link on my phone, land on the normal home page, see a small note that my friend's offer is saved, and at checkout I see "Welcome offer from a friend" take £5 off a £45 bouquet; I pay exactly the amount on the button.
- As the **referrer**, once my cousin's flowers are delivered I sign in and see £5 of store credit with its use-by date; on my next order it comes off automatically, and I can keep it for later.
- As a **friend** whose basket is under the minimum, I see how much more I need to add to use the offer, in a positive sentence.
- As the **founder**, the programme costs nothing until a new customer pays; I switch it on in admin after about 20 orders, change amounts without a release, and see referred orders, revenue, cost, credit outstanding and margin per referred order.
- As the **founder**, a buyer who refers themselves with a second email gets no credit, and a friend's refund takes back unspent credit without ever charging the referrer.
- As **Googlebot**, I never see an invite URL, a parameter duplicate or a changed price on a cached page.
- As a **florist**, my payout for a bouquet is the same whether or not the buyer used an offer.

**`referralFriendSource`** (stage `offer`). `evaluate`: code from the draft (`referral_code_id`, set from `fo_ref` on the first no-store basket or checkout render, or from the typed code), unless `referral_removed`. **The outbox consumer** (`referrals` target; rows of spec 013's `outboxTargets` for `test` and `live`, never `demo`): `order.captured` (row 2 only), `order.delivered` (row 20), `order.abandoned`, `order.payment_failed`, `order.cancelled`, `order.cancelled_refunded`, `order.refunded_partial`, `order.refunded_full`, `order.chargeback_opened`. Rules, with `kept = Σ buyer_total_minor of the purchase's orders − Σ succeeded refunds` and `qualifies = kept + friend_reduction_minor ≥ min_order_minor`:

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

**Rules check (`CLAUDE.md` non-negotiables).** Price shown = price charged: the reduced total is shown before the Pay press and charged exactly (AC-13, AC-20). Schema price = visible price on public pages (AC-40). Money in integer minor units with ISO currency everywhere, no conversion. Order status only through `orderService.transition`: this module calls none (AC-25). Go-live is a data flip (AC-1, AC-3). No IP logic (amounts per currency only; the edge rate limit uses the IP only to count, ADR-0006). No PII in logs, URLs or analytics (AC-42). Secrets in env (`FINGERPRINT_HMAC_KEY`, AC-47). No rule is bent, so no ADR is proposed.

**Cookie basis (researched; ⚖️ lawyer to confirm with the legal set).**
- ePrivacy Art. 5(3) as transposed (UK PECR reg. 6(4)(b); DE TDDDG §25(2) no. 2; PL Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej art. 399, which replaced Prawo telekomunikacyjne art. 173 in November 2024; FR Loi Informatique et Libertés art. 82) exempts storage that is strictly necessary for a service the user expressly requests.
- The CNIL's cookie FAQ states that trackers used to bill **affiliation** are not exempt, but that trackers for **cashback or reward** services the user signed up for are exempt, relying on the Conseil d'État decision of 8 April 2022, "even if these trackers are also used to remunerate the publishers" ([CNIL FAQ](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies/FAQ)).
- The ICO says necessity is judged "from the point of view of the subscriber or user, not your own", and that a technology used for any other purpose as well loses the exception ([ICO, what are the exceptions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/)).
- **Our position:** the friend opens a link whose whole message is "{amount} off your first order"; keeping that offer until checkout is the service they asked for. So `fo_ref` (the code) and `fo_ref_n` (a presence digit) are strictly necessary **on these conditions, each tested**: first-party; one purpose (apply the friend's offer at checkout and record which code the friend used when they place the order); never read by analytics, never sent to GA4, Stripe metadata or Sentry; no identifier of the friend; 30 days; the offer is shown and removable at checkout, and can be typed instead, so the record comes from the friend's own checkout choice. They join the essential list of `plan/07` §5 and the cookie register.
- **If the lawyer disagrees** (the referrer's credit looks like affiliation remuneration), the fallback needs no redesign: the landing renders a no-store page with one "Claim {amount} off" button that sets the cookie on press (an express request), or the friend types the code (§13 Q9).

**Email.** The friend is never emailed (no address is collected). Promotion inside a transactional email is advertising: the BGH held advertising in an automatic reply email unlawful where the recipient had objected (VI ZR 134/15, 15 December 2015); UK soft opt-in (PECR reg. 22(3)) and EU ePrivacy Art. 13(2) require an opt-out at collection and in every message, and checkout offers no marketing choice (spec 018 amendment R4). So the block enters B1 only for buyers with `marketing_consent` (§13 Q10 records the founder's original wish and the opt-out alternative, ⚖️). No grant or expiry email at launch: the ICO treats messages that encourage spending a balance as direct marketing (§13 Q20).

**Consumer law and programme terms** (`legal/referral`, ⚖️ lawyer review with the legal set; seller Flowers Overseas LLC per ADR-0021 through spec 041's `company`).
- **Material information next to the claim** (UCPD Art. 7 misleading omissions; UK DMCC Act 2024 Part 4 Ch. 1): the conditions (first order, minimum, credit after delivery, signed-in use, 6 months, cap) sit directly under "Give {amount}, get {amount}", not behind a link.
- **Fair and transparent terms** (UK CRA 2015 s. 62 and s. 68; Directive 93/13/EEC Art. 3 and 5): plain amounts table; credit is not cash, not transferable, not withdrawable; expiry stated with the date shown per grant; revocation only for the listed reasons (non-payment, cancellation, refund below the minimum, chargeback, self-referral); we may change or end the programme for future referrals with notice on the terms page, and every pending referral and every granted credit keeps its terms (avoids the grey-list term letting a trader alter terms without a valid reason: CRA 2015 Sch. 2 para. 11; Directive 93/13 Annex 1(j)). Not a pyramid scheme (UCPD Annex I point 14): nobody pays to take part.
- **Price display:** the reduction appears only at checkout as its own line; product pages never announce a reduction, so Omnibus Art. 6a's prior-price rule is not triggered; the Commission's Art. 6a guidance (OJ C 526, 29.12.2021) treats genuinely personalised reductions as outside it (⚖️ confirm).
- **Geo-blocking (EU 2018/302):** the same offer for every buyer whatever their residence; amounts follow the currency the buyer pays in.
- **Withdrawal and remedies:** unchanged; remedies apply to the price paid.

**VAT (🧾 accountant, a `taxQuestions.referralVat` sign-off is a switch-on gate).** The friend's reduction is a discount granted at the time of supply, excluded from the taxable amount (Directive 2006/112/EC Art. 73 and 79(b); UK VATA 1994 s. 19). Store credit given free and redeemed later is treated the same way (Boots, C-126/88; Argos, C-288/94), and the founder's instruction is VAT on the discounted amount. Open for the accountant: whether the referrer's credit is consideration for an introduction (HMRC VAT Notice 700/7, business promotions), which would put VAT on the full price of the referrer's later order. The florist payout never changes (AC-19).

**Data flows (RoPA row "Referral programme", in the PR of task 1).** Subjects: buyers who share (referrers) and buyers who use an offer (friends). Categories: code, the referrer-friend link, snapshotted amounts, status and reasons, signals (codes only), the credit ledger, HMAC of the card fingerprint, the two cookies. Purposes: running the programme; preventing self-referral and repeat-card abuse. Bases: Art. 6(1)(b) (programme terms, a contract with each participant); Art. 6(1)(f) for abuse signals and the fingerprint HMAC (fraud prevention, Recital 47), balanced by keeping only codes and a keyed hash. Recipients: Neon (existing); WhatsApp only when the referrer chooses to share. Retention: §5.1 (36 months, fingerprint 13 months). Rights: export and erasure include codes, referrals and the ledger (spec 018 task 5); the referrer never learns a friend's identity from us. Privacy policy: a referral section (spec 041).

**Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA):** share controls are real buttons and links with visible labels; "Link copied" announced through `aria-live="polite"`; the link field is labelled and selectable; WhatsApp link names its destination; targets at least 44 × 44 CSS px; `SavedOfferNote` is a labelled region, does not steal focus, Close is first in its tab order; reductions and states as text, never colour alone; axe in CI on every new state.

**Logs and PII:** ids only (`referral_id`, `referral_code_id`, `purchase_id`, `order_id`, `grant_id`), statuses, reasons, amounts and currencies; never the code, the link, an email, phone, name, recipient field, raw fingerprint or its HMAC; the invite path segment is redacted in app logs and Sentry.

## 13. Open questions (each with the recommended default this spec is written against)

- **Q1. Amounts.** Default: GBP 5 off a GBP 30 order, EUR 6 off EUR 35, PLN 25 off PLN 150; the referrer gets the same figure in their code's currency. All editable in admin.
- **Q2. Credit currency.** Default: credit is in the currency of the referrer's first paid purchase and works only on purchases in that currency; no conversion. Alternative: convert at the ECB rate on use (adds FX rounding and a rate dependency).
- **Q3. How much of an order credit can pay.** Default: at most 50 % of the purchase, and never below the payment minimum (GBP 0.30, EUR 0.50, PLN 2.00). Alternative: up to the whole order less the minimum.
- **Q4. Cap per referrer.** Default: 10 rewards in total (GBP 50 at the default amount); friends keep getting their offer after the cap. Alternative: 10 per calendar year.
- **Q5. Credit life.** Default: 6 calendar months from the grant's day, ending at midnight UTC, the date shown on every grant.
- **Q6. When credit is added.** Default (your instruction): when the friend's first order is delivered; later refunds below the minimum or a chargeback take back what is unspent. Alternative: wait for the order to close after its review window (fewer clawbacks, slower reward).
- **Q7. Partial refunds of the friend's order.** Default: the reward stands while what the friend kept paying plus the offer is at least the minimum order value.
- **Q8. Same recipient address or phone as the referrer's orders.** Default: recorded as a signal for you to review before delivery, not blocked (two siblings sending to the same mother is the programme's best case). Same email or phone as the referrer blocks at checkout; the same card voids the reward after payment.
- **Q9. Cookie basis.** Default: strictly necessary, no consent banner change, on the conditions of §8, confirmed by the lawyer with the legal set. Fallback if the lawyer disagrees: a "Claim {amount} off" button on a landing page sets the cookie, or the friend types the code.
- **Q10. The confirmation email.** You asked for the block in the confirmation email. Default: only for buyers who have agreed to marketing emails, because an offer inside a transactional email is advertising (BGH VI ZR 134/15; PECR reg. 22) and checkout asks no marketing question. Everyone sees it on the confirmation page and in the account. Alternative (⚖️, lawyer first): every buyer, with an opt-out link in each email and an opt-out at checkout.
- **Q11. Where the link lands.** Default: the home page in the link's language. Alternative: the country page of the referrer's last destination.
- **Q12. The "offer saved" note on public pages.** Default: yes, a small note after the page has loaded, no layout shift. Alternative: nothing until the basket.
- **Q13. With a discount code.** Default: one offer per order (the larger applies; a tie goes to the friend's offer); store credit works together with a discount code. (A buyer holding credit has ordered before, so credit and the friend's offer never meet.)
- **Q14. Applying credit.** Default: applied automatically for signed-in buyers, with "Keep my credit for later".
- **Q15. Credit used on an order that is then cancelled or refunded in full.** Default: given back as credit valid at least 30 more days; partial refunds go back to the card only.
