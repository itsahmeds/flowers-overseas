## 1. Problem

Google shows a "Shopping" tab and product panels with photos and prices on ordinary searches, and since 2020 products appear there for free ("free listings") when a shop sends Google Merchant Center a product feed. For a gift bought on impulse ("kwiaty na urodziny", "Blumen Strauß Rosen") these panels sit above the organic results our corridor and product pages compete for. The founder approved this channel on 2026-10-24 (idea D) as free traffic with photos and prices, the same traffic paid Shopping ads would buy later from the same feed.

Nothing in the repository produces a feed today. The data a feed needs already exists and is already held to the rules that matter: the catalogue, prices, photos and countries live in the database (spec 012b), every product page renders one all-in price that equals its JSON-LD `Offer.price` and the checkout total (spec 005, spec 009 AC-31, `plan/02` §9), and indexability is one predicate (spec 007). The risk is a second, drifting copy of those facts: Google disapproves items (and can suspend the account for misrepresentation) when the feed price, availability, shipping cost or business identity disagree with the landing page. So the feed must be a projection of exactly what each product page renders, generated by the same code, never a separately authored list. This spec defines that projection, its gates, its tests, and the founder's Merchant Center steps.

## 2. Scope

1. **A feed per (destination country, page locale)**, served at `GET /feeds/google/{feedKey}.xml` (RSS 2.0 with the `g:` namespace), where `feedKey` is `{iso2}-{locale}` in lower case (`pl-pl`, `de-de`, `de-en`, `gb-en-gb`). A pair is **eligible** only when the destination country is `live` and the locale is a launch locale whose HTML default currency (`plan/03` §1) equals the destination's currency, so every price in the feed is the page's **native** price, never an FX conversion (§13 Q2). Today that means: Poland → `pl-pl` only (PLN); a EUR destination such as Germany → `de-de` and `de-en`; the United Kingdom → `gb-en-gb` (GBP). An ineligible key answers 404.
2. **One item per product per eligible feed**, the product's **default size** only (§13 Q4). Its price is the exact visible default price of the product page at `link`, VAT and delivery included, in the destination's currency; it is the same integer the page's `Offer.price` and the checkout read (price shown = price charged = schema price = feed price).
3. **The inclusion predicate** (§5.2): an item exists only when its product page exists now (database AND build manifest, spec 012b §2.3), is `index,follow` by spec 007's one verdict, emits its `Product` + `Offer` node (spec 009's gate: live destination, live date picker, offer equals the buy section's price), shows a native price, has an approved **real photograph** as primary image at least 800 px on each side (§13 Q3), and its title and description pass the relay-language, price-literal and length checks. Each product that fails is listed with a reason code, never silently dropped.
4. **Rollout gates as data, not code**: the feed carries zero items unless (a) `isIndexingEnvironment()` is true (production on the canonical host: the site is open to search engines), (b) the per-country flag `merchant.google_feed` is on, (c) `checkout.open` is on for the country and `checkout.demo_guard` is off (a buyer can really pay). Turning the flag off empties the feed, which removes the listings at Google's next fetch.
5. **Attributes** mapped from data (§5.2 table): `id`, `title`, `description`, `link`, `image_link`, `additional_image_link`, `availability`, `price`, `brand`, `condition`, `identifier_exists`, `google_product_category` (2899, "Arts & Entertainment > Party & Celebration > Gift Giving > Fresh Cut Flowers"), `product_type`, `shipping` (delivery included, price 0 in the item's currency), `handling_cutoff_time`, `ships_from_country`.
6. **Freshness**: the feed is a cached route handler (1 hour backstop) invalidated by the same tags as the country's product pages, so a price, copy, photo, flag or go-live change reaches the feed URL within 60 s.
7. **Landing-page hygiene for Google's crawlers**: Google's click-tracking parameter `srsltid` and the `utm_*` set change nothing a crawler compares (canonical, robots directive, visible price, JSON-LD); `robots.txt` in the indexing environment lets `Googlebot` and `Storebot-Google` fetch product pages and the feed path; no edge rule challenges them (spec 040 already forbids Bot Fight Mode).
8. **Price parity tests**: an end-to-end test that, for every would-be item in the e2e environment, opens `link` and compares the rendered visible price and canonical with the feed; and a **daily verification job** in production that does the same against the live site, plus JSON-LD and availability, and records the result.
9. **Admin page `/admin/feeds`**: every eligible feed with its URL (to paste into Merchant Center), flag state, item count, the excluded products with reason codes, a labelled "would-be" preview that ignores only the three whole-feed gates (flag, indexing environment, real checkout) and applies every per-product term, and the last verification runs.
10. **Runbook** `docs/runbooks/google-merchant-center.md`: the founder's account setup, the go-live checklist (§12 gates), the weekly disapproval check, and the rollback.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Google Ads / Shopping ads, conversion tracking, Performance Max.** The same feed serves them later; linking an Ads account, consent-gated conversion tags and budgets are a later spec (`plan/04` analytics stays off).
- **Merchant API push or status pull** (service account, keys). The Content API for Shopping reached sunset on 2026-08-18; its successor needs a service-account key, which agents never handle. Disapproval monitoring here is email notifications plus the runbook's weekly check plus our own daily parity job (§13 Q6). Pulling product statuses into admin is a later spec.
- **Targeting shoppers outside the destination country** (for example UK shoppers sending to Poland) (§13 Q1).
- **Sizes as variants** (`item_group_id`, one item per size) (§13 Q4).
- **Sale prices** (`sale_price`), promotions, `custom_label_*`, local inventory ads, `video_link`, `lifestyle_image_link`, product reviews feed. No was/now pricing exists on the site; Omnibus rules would apply the day one does (`plan/07` §2.1).
- **Other engines** (Microsoft Merchant Center, Meta catalogue, Pinterest). The XML is close to theirs; each is its own spec.
- **New public copy.** Titles and descriptions are the reviewed product copy; no destination suffix or marketing text is added (§13 Q9).
- **Changing product page markup, price rules or the indexability engine.** This spec reads them. The one permitted touch on the product page is a non-visual `data-` attribute on the buy section's default price if no stable test hook exists (§5.3).

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer in Poland** searching "bukiet róż z dostawą", I see a Flowers Overseas bouquet with its real photo and the price in złoty; I tap it, land on the Polish product page, and the price I saw is the price on the page and the price I pay, with delivery included.
- As the **founder**, I open `/admin/feeds`, copy the Poland feed URL into Merchant Center once, and from then on a new florist bouquet, a price change or a new live country reaches Google with no code change; when something is excluded I can read why.
- As the **founder**, when Google emails me about a disapproval, the runbook tells me where to look, and our own daily check has usually caught a price mismatch first.
- As **Storebot-Google**, every landing page I crawl is server-rendered, canonical, unblocked, and shows the feed's price and availability in visible text and JSON-LD.
- As the **founder before launch**, nothing reaches Google while the site is hidden, even if I add the feed URL to Merchant Center early: the feed is empty.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

One migration, numbered at implementation after the last merged one, with a hand-written `.down.sql` run by the round-trip test; no `float`, `enum` or extension.

1. **`NNNN_merchant_feed_check`**: `merchant_feed_check(id uuid PK DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(), feed_key text NOT NULL CHECK (feed_key ~ '^[a-z]{2}-[a-z]{2}(-[a-z]{2})?$'), started_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(), finished_at timestamptz NULL, items_in_feed integer NOT NULL CHECK (items_in_feed >= 0), items_checked integer NOT NULL CHECK (items_checked >= 0), mismatch_count integer NOT NULL CHECK (mismatch_count >= 0), mismatches jsonb NOT NULL DEFAULT '[]'::jsonb, status text NOT NULL CHECK (status IN ('running','passed','failed','error')), error_code text NULL)`, index `(feed_key, started_at DESC)`. Each `mismatches` element is `{ itemId, field, feedValue, pageValue }` with `field` one of `price`, `currency`, `availability`, `canonical`, `jsonld_price`, `http_status`, `image_status`: product identifiers and public values only. RLS: admin reads; the job writes through `withSystemContext('merchant.feed.verify')`. A `retention_policy` row deletes rows older than 90 days through spec 012b's `retention.sweep`. Spec 012's audit trigger is **not** attached (machine-written telemetry, not an admin action). Rollback: delete the retention row, drop the table.

**Registry and seed (no migration):** `src/config/feature-flags.ts` gains `merchant.google_feed` (`scopeKinds: ["country"]`, `risk: "standard"`, `dirties: ["catalog"]`, description: "Google Merchant Center feed for this destination: on serves items, off serves an empty feed so Google removes the listings at its next fetch"); its seed rows are off for every country. No column is added to `product`, `country` or `media_asset`: every fact the feed needs exists.

### 5.4 Rendering & caching (per plan/01 §3)

- The feed is a **route handler**, not a page: generated on request on a cache miss, cached at the origin (`revalidate` 3600 as a backstop, the data reads wrapped in the same tagged cache as the product views) and at the Cloudflare edge (`s-maxage=3600`). Google fetches at most a few times a day, so the origin cost is a handful of builds per day per feed.
- **Invalidation reuses spec 012b's tag table**: anything that dirties `catalog:{iso2}` (price change, copy or photo change, review decision, country status flip, florist saleability changes, and now the `merchant.google_feed` toggle) also purges that country's feed URLs, and a verified publish's `sitemap` invalidation purges every feed, which is how a new or removed product reaches the feed. No new tag.
- **Exit signal** to move generation to a pg-boss job writing the XML to R2: a feed above 5,000 items or a p95 build above 5 s in the generation log line (§11). Not expected for years at relay scale.
- The verification job reads the public site through the edge like any visitor; it never bypasses the cache, so it checks what Google sees.
- Admin pages: SSR `no-store`, Cloudflare bypass (spec 040), as spec 012.

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

- **Indexability is unchanged and is the feed's input.** An item exists only for a product page that spec 007's verdict makes `index,follow`; the feed adds no page, no URL and no robots branch. Before the indexing flip every product page is `noindex`, so every feed is empty by construction (AC-4), and the feed cannot advertise a page Google may not index.
- **Canonical**: `link` is the page's own canonical (ADR-0001 apex, `plan/02` §7 lowercase, no trailing slash, no parameters). One canonical per (locale, country, product), which is exactly one feed item (`plan/02` §4.2). Cross-locale duplicates are separate items in separate data sources with their own content language, which is Google's documented multi-language setup, not duplication.
- **Tracking parameters**: Google appends `srsltid` to free-listing clicks (Merchant Center auto-tagging) and the runbook keeps auto-tagging on. A product URL carrying `srsltid` or `utm_*` must render the same canonical, robots directive, visible price and JSON-LD as the bare URL (AC-19); spec 040 A7 already rests correctness on body identity, and ADR-0020's hashed CSP means there is no per-request nonce to differ. `src/config/url-keys.ts` stays `["page","sort"]`: these keys are neutralised, never honoured.
- **hreflang and sitemaps**: unchanged. The feed URL is not in any sitemap (`plan/02` §10: sitemaps list indexable HTML only) and carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; `robots.txt` does not disallow `/feeds/` so Google's fetcher is never refused.
- **Schema**: the feed and the `Product`/`Offer` JSON-LD are two serialisations of one `productOfferNode()` result, so `plan/02` §15's "structured data ≠ visible content" guard now also covers "feed ≠ structured data". Keeping Merchant Center's automatic item updates on lets Google correct price and availability from that JSON-LD between fetches.
- **Crawlability for Shopping**: `Storebot-Google` and `Googlebot` must reach product pages and images. `robots.txt` in the indexing environment allows them (AC-20); the media origin (`media.flowersoverseas.com`, R2) serves images without a robots block; spec 040 keeps Bot Fight Mode and challenges off for verified crawlers, and Google notes its fetcher runs from US addresses, which ADR-0006 and spec 040 never block.
- **Thin content**: none added. Product copy is the reviewed, validated copy (spec 012b validators, 60 to 90 words).
- **CWV**: no public page gains bytes or JavaScript. The optional `data-price` attribute is a few bytes on one element.
- **Internal links**: none in or out; the feed is not linked from any page.

## 7. i18n considerations (mandatory)

- **Feed language = page language.** Google requires the feed, landing page and checkout to share one language: a `pl-pl` feed carries Polish titles and descriptions and links to `/pl/...` pages whose checkout is Polish. The content language is set per data source (`pl`, `de`, `en`; `en-gb` maps to `en`).
- **Currency = page currency = destination currency.** Only native-price pairs are eligible (§2 item 1), so no FX conversion, rounding to x.90, or weekday ECB rebuild can make the feed and the page disagree (`plan/03` §1 invariant 3). This is why Poland has a Polish feed only: no English page renders PLN by default (§13 Q2).
- **Formatting**: the feed's `price` is a machine format Google defines (period decimal, ISO code), produced by the existing `moneyDecimalString` from integer minor units; it is not user-facing formatting, so `Intl` does not apply there. The verification job compares the page's visible price with `formatMoney(price, locale)` from `src/modules/i18n/format.ts`, so locale formatting is still checked (`229,00 zł`, `49,90 €`, `£45.00`).
- **Text encoding**: UTF-8, NFC, with Polish and German diacritics round-tripping (`ą ę ł ń ó ś ź ż`, `ä ö ü ß`); slugs in `link` are ASCII by `plan/02` §4.
- **Review gating**: unreviewed copy makes the page `noindex` (`plan/03` §6 step 4), which excludes the item (`not_indexable`). Machine-drafted text therefore never reaches Google.
- **New message keys**: admin only, `messages/admin/en.json` under `admin.feeds` (spec 003 A17's `admin` surface, outside every public locale's unreviewed share). **No public message key is added** (AC-23).
- **RTL**: none; logical properties on the admin pages.
- **Address and phone formats**: none captured or shown.

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Data flows (RoPA)**: **no personal data** enters the feed, the route, the job or the check table: product, price, URL and image data only. Google receives public catalogue data, as any crawler does; no buyer, recipient or florist personal data, no cookie, no tag. `docs/compliance/ropa.md` is unchanged; the PR states so. Linking Google Ads and adding conversion tags is a later spec with consent gating (`plan/07` §5).
- **Price display (`plan/07` §4, CRD Art. 6, PAngV, UK DMCC drip pricing)**: the listed price is the all-in price (VAT and delivery included) charged at checkout; shipping is declared as 0 because delivery is included, never because it is hidden. Date surcharges (Sunday, peak days) are optional upgrades shown on the date chip before selection; the default price the listing shows is the price of the default date. If the page's preselected date carries a surcharge, spec 009 withholds the `Offer` and the item leaves the feed rather than list a price the page does not show (§13 Q8).
- **No reference or "was" prices**: no `sale_price`, so Omnibus 30-day lowest-price rules are not engaged.
- **Truthful images (misrepresentation)**: only real photographs enter the feed (§13 Q3). AI-generated images carry an "example arrangement" label on our page (ADR-0014) that a Google listing cannot show, and Google requires AI images to keep IPTC `DigitalSourceType` metadata, which spec 012b strips at upload. Excluding them is both honest and compliant.
- **Brand and relay (founder ruling 2026-10-14)**: no florist name, code, credit or relay wording reaches Google: titles and descriptions pass `relayMatches` per locale and the florist-name check (AC-15), on top of spec 012b's save-time ban list. `ships_from_country` is the destination, which is true and says nothing about who makes the bouquet.
- **Seller identity**: Merchant Center's business name, address and contact must match the site's legal pages (imprint, terms). The founder registers the account in the name of Flowers Overseas LLC, so the feed goes live only after the site's company identity names the LLC (§12 gate G3, the pending entity ADR). Listing under one entity while the site names another is a misrepresentation risk.
- **Geo-blocking (EU 2018/302)**: a listing targeted at a country is advertising, not access control; the landing page is open to every visitor, nothing redirects by IP (ADR-0006), and prices vary by destination only (`plan/07` §3).
- **Withdrawal and returns**: the account-level return policy the founder sets is "no returns" for perishable goods with a link to the guarantee page, matching `plan/07` §2.1 (Art. 16(d) CRD; reg. 28(1)(c) CCRs) and the PDP sentence. No refund or failure wording is added anywhere a buyer sees after purchase.
- **Sanctions**: a sanctioned destination is never `live` (`plan/07` §9), so it never has a feed.
- **Accessibility**: the two admin pages target WCAG 2.2 AA (axe in CI, table headers and captions, labelled copy control with a polite status message, 24 × 24 px targets).
- **Logs and PII**: the generation and verification log lines carry feed key, counts, durations and reason codes only; no URL query strings are logged; mismatch rows hold public values only.
- **Security**: the feed is public by design (it repeats public pages; §13 Q5); it is a GET with no parameters beyond the key, cached at the edge, so it adds no write surface and no secret. The admin pages check `feeds.view` first (spec 012's source scan covers them).

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**The route and its gates**

- **AC-1** `GET /feeds/google/{feedKey}.xml` for an eligible key answers 200 with `Content-Type: application/xml; charset=utf-8`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` and `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=3600`, and a body that parses as RSS 2.0 declaring `xmlns:g="http://base.google.com/ns/1.0"` in which every `<item>` passes `GoogleFeedItemSchema`. Any other key, including a malformed one, answers 404.
- **AC-2** `eligibleFeedKeys()` returns exactly the pairs (live country, launch locale) whose locale default currency equals the country's currency. With Poland live it contains `pl-pl` and not `pl-en`, `pl-en-gb` or `pl-de`; with Germany live it contains `de-de` and `de-en`; a `demo` or `disabled` country contributes no key.
- **AC-3** With every other gate open, the flag `merchant.google_feed` off for a country makes that country's feeds valid documents with zero items; turning it on (through `/admin/flags`) makes the items appear within 60 s without a deploy.
- **AC-4** When `isIndexingEnvironment()` is false (any environment other than production on `flowersoverseas.com`), every feed has zero items, whatever the flag and the catalogue say.
- **AC-5** With the flag on and the environment indexing, a country whose `checkout.open` is off, or any state where `checkout.demo_guard` is on, has zero items.

**Items**

- **AC-6** A product priced in the feed's country appears as an item if and only if every term of §5.2's inclusion predicate holds; for each term, a fixture where only that term fails excludes the product with that term's reason code, and the exclusion is listed on `/admin/feeds/[feedKey]`.
- **AC-7** `id` is `{SKU}.{ISO2}.{locale}`, is at most 50 characters, is identical across two generations of the same data, and is unique across all items of all feeds.
- **AC-8** `title` equals the product page's `<h1>` text and `description` equals the page's product description with markup stripped and whitespace collapsed, both from the same `ProductView`; title ≤ 150 and description ≤ 5000 characters.
- **AC-9** `link` equals the `href` of the product page's `<link rel="canonical">`: `https://flowersoverseas.com/...`, no query string, no fragment.
- **AC-10** `price` equals `{Offer.price} {Offer.priceCurrency}` of the same page's JSON-LD and equals the buy section's default visible price (same integer minor units, same ISO currency); it is produced from integer minor units by `moneyDecimalString`, with no float anywhere on the path.
- **AC-11** `availability` is `in_stock` when the page's `Offer.availability` is `InStock` and `out_of_stock` when it is `OutOfStock`; any other value excludes the item (`availability_unsupported`).
- **AC-12** Every item has exactly one `shipping` for its feed's country with price `0.00` in the item's own currency, `min_handling_time` and `max_handling_time` 0, and transit times derived from the country's operations as §5.2 defines; `handling_cutoff_time` equals the cutoff and zone the page prints, and is absent when the page prints none; `ships_from_country` is the destination ISO2.
- **AC-13** Every item has `brand` = the trading-name constant (`Flowers Overseas`), `condition` = `new`, `identifier_exists` = `no`, `google_product_category` = `2899`, a `product_type`, and no `gtin`, `mpn` or `sale_price`.
- **AC-14** `image_link` is the approved primary image's public variant on the media origin, both sides ≥ 800 px, `https:`; a product whose primary image is `source = 'ai'`, unapproved, or has no variant that large is excluded; `additional_image_link` holds at most 10 further approved non-AI images in page order.
- **AC-15** No item in any feed has a title or description that `relayMatches` flags for the feed's locale, that matches `PRICE_LITERAL_PATTERN`, or that contains the legal name or code of any non-demo florist; such a product is excluded with `copy_rejected`.

**Freshness and crawlers**

- **AC-16** The sitemap index and every sitemap child list no `/feeds/` URL, in every environment.
- **AC-17** After a price supersede (spec 012b `supersedePrice`) for a product in a flagged live country, `urlsForTag('catalog:{iso2}')` includes that country's feed URLs, and the next fetch of the feed shows the new price.
- **AC-18** The e2e parity test opens the `link` of every item of the e2e environment's preview feed and finds, on each page, the canonical equal to `link` and the visible default price equal to the feed price formatted for the feed's locale; it asserts at least one item and fails when the preview feed is empty.
- **AC-19** A product page requested with `?srsltid=abc123&utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic` renders the same `<link rel="canonical">`, the same robots directive, the same visible default price and the same JSON-LD as the bare URL.
- **AC-20** In the indexing environment, `robots.txt` allows user agents `Googlebot` and `Storebot-Google` to fetch a product page path, the same path with `?srsltid=abc123`, and `/feeds/google/pl-pl.xml`. (Outside it, `Disallow: /` stays, and the feed is empty by AC-4.)

**Verification, admin, data**

- **AC-21** The `merchant.feed.verify` job, run against pages that match the feed, writes one `merchant_feed_check` row with `status = 'passed'` and `mismatch_count = 0`; when one page's visible price differs, the row is `failed` with exactly one mismatch naming that item and `field = 'price'`, and one Sentry event is captured; no row or event contains a query string, an email address or a person's name.
- **AC-22** `/admin/feeds` and `/admin/feeds/[feedKey]` render for `admin`, `content` and `ops`, answer 403 for `finance`, show every eligible feed's URL, flag state, the state of each whole-feed gate, served and preview counts, exclusions with reasons, and the last runs; the preview is labelled as a preview on every view; both pages pass axe with zero violations.
- **AC-23** The change adds no key to any public message namespace; every new key is under `admin.feeds` in `messages/admin/en.json`; `pnpm i18n:check` passes.
- **AC-24** `NNNN_merchant_feed_check` applies and its `.down.sql` restores the previous schema exactly (round-trip test), and the retention sweep deletes a row older than 90 days and keeps one younger.
- **AC-25** `googleFeedXml` escapes `&`, `<`, `>`, `"` and `'` in every text node, emits no CDATA, and round-trips a title containing `Róże & bratki <3` and `Blumenstrauß "Süß"` byte-exactly through an XML parser.

## 11. Observability

- **Log line per feed generation** (`merchant.feed.built`): `request_id`, `feed_key`, `items`, `excluded` (count per reason code), `duration_ms`, `preview` (bool). No URL, no query string, no copy text. The p95 of `duration_ms` and the max of `items` are the §5.4 exit signal.
- **Log line per verification run** (`merchant.feed.verified`): `feed_key`, `items_in_feed`, `items_checked`, `mismatch_count`, `status`.
- **Alerts**: a Sentry event on a `failed` or `error` verification (one per feed per day); a Sentry warning when a flagged feed in the indexing environment serves zero items for two consecutive daily runs (likely a gate closed by accident).
- **Outside our system**: Merchant Center's "Needs attention" list and its email notifications (founder turns them on, §12), and its performance report for free-listing clicks. GA4 is untouched by this spec.

## 12. Rollout

**Build order** (`/plan-tasks` owns the split): (1) `seo/merchant` pure serializer and schemas; (2) `catalog/merchantFeed.ts` predicate and mapping; (3) route, cache tags, flag registry entry; (4) migration and verification job; (5) admin pages (after `/design`); (6) runbook. Everything merges with the flag off; in every environment except production on the canonical host the feed is empty anyway (AC-4).

**Go-live gates** (all must hold before the founder turns `merchant.google_feed` on for a country; the runbook lists them as a checklist):

- **G1 The site is open to search engines.** The indexing flip of spec 007 §12 is done: `isIndexingEnvironment()` is true, `robots.txt` open, the `/seo-audit` passed. Before it, Google's crawler would be refused and every item disapproved; the feed is empty by AC-4 regardless.
- **G2 A real, paying destination.** The country is `live` through spec 012b's preflight, `checkout.open` is on for it, `checkout.demo_guard` is off and spec 013's real payments work: Google requires a working checkout at the advertised price.
- **G3 One seller identity.** The site's company identity (`src/config/company.ts`, imprint, terms) names Flowers Overseas LLC through its own ADR, so it matches the Merchant Center business information (§13 Q7).
- **G4 Real photographs.** `/admin/feeds/{key}` preview shows at least one item and no exclusion the founder did not expect.
- **G5 Parity green.** The last `merchant.feed.verify` run for the feed is `passed`. The job runs on flagged feeds only, so the order is: turn the flag on, wait for the next daily run (04:00), read it on `/admin/feeds`, and only then add the data source in Merchant Center. Until the data source exists, nobody fetches the feed.

**Founder steps in Merchant Center** (founder only; no agent signs in, and no key, password or token is created for or handled by an agent):

1. Sign in to `merchants.google.com` with the Google account that owns the Search Console domain property for `flowersoverseas.com` (`plan/02` §16). Create a Merchant Center account: business name shown to shoppers **Flowers Overseas**; legal business information **Flowers Overseas LLC** with the registered address; customer service email and the contact page URL, exactly as on the site's imprint and contact page.
2. Website: enter `https://flowersoverseas.com`, then **verify and claim** it. Preferred: the Search Console method (same Google account, one click). Fallback: the DNS TXT method, adding the record in Cloudflare yourself. Do not use the HTML-tag method (it would need a code change).
3. Return policy: for each country you list, choose **no returns** (perishable goods) and paste the guarantee page URL in that country's language.
4. Shipping: the feed declares shipping on every item (free, delivery included). Only if Merchant Center refuses to proceed without an account-level service, add one per country: free, handling 0 days, transit as the feed says, with the same cutoff.
5. Turn on **free listings** for the countries you list. Leave Google Ads unlinked.
6. Keep **automatic item updates** (price and availability) and **auto-tagging** on (the defaults).
7. Add a product source per feed: "Add products from a file", "enter a link to your file", paste the URL shown on `/admin/feeds` (for Poland `https://flowersoverseas.com/feeds/google/pl-pl.xml`), country **Poland**, language **Polish**, daily fetch at **06:00 Europe/Amsterdam**, no username or password. Press "Fetch now" once.
8. Turn on email notifications for product and account issues.
9. Weekly (runbook): open "Needs attention", compare with `/admin/feeds/{key}`'s last runs, and record anything unexpected in the runbook's log section.

**Rollback**: turn `merchant.google_feed` off for the country (the next fetch removes every item; for an instant stop, also pause the data source in Merchant Center). Code rollback is reverting the task PRs plus the migration's `.down.sql`; nothing else holds state.

**Exit signal** (spec `implemented`): T-01 to T-26 green in CI; on production, after G1 to G5 for Poland, Merchant Center shows the items as approved for free listings with zero price or availability mismatches for 7 consecutive days, and `merchant_feed_check` shows 7 `passed` runs.

## 13. Open questions

Each carries the default this spec is written against; the spec stays `proposed` until the founder rules.

- **Q1. Which shoppers see a listing?** Google shows a listing to shoppers in the data source's target country, and the shipping country must be one we deliver to. **Recommended default: target country = destination country only** (Polish listings for shoppers in Poland). Showing Poland deliveries to shoppers in the UK (the diaspora corridor, ADR-0002) would declare shipping to a country we do not deliver in, which Google can treat as inaccurate shipping. Revisit with Google support's written answer in the Shopping-ads spec.
- **Q2. Languages per destination.** **Recommended default: every launch locale whose page shows the destination's own currency**: Poland gets a Polish feed only; a EUR destination gets German and English. The alternative, English Poland listings priced in EUR through Google's currency conversion, makes Google show an estimated złoty price that drifts from what we charge, and our EUR prices for Poland move with each weekday ECB rebuild.
- **Q3. AI-generated photos.** **Recommended default: real photographs only** (`photo` or `partner`); a bouquet whose only photo is AI-generated stays on the site but not in the feed. Our pages label AI images as example arrangements and a listing cannot carry that label; Google also requires AI images to keep IPTC metadata that our upload pipeline removes.
- **Q4. Sizes.** **Recommended default: the default size only**, one item per product. Sizes as variants need `item_group_id` and a landing URL that preselects each size, which is a product page change for little gain at today's catalogue size.
- **Q5. Public or password-protected feed URL.** **Recommended default: public**, `noindex`, cached at the edge. It repeats what public pages show, and a password would be one more secret for you to create, store and type. Alternative: HTTP basic auth with a password you set in Railway and Merchant Center yourself.
- **Q6. How disapprovals are watched.** **Recommended default: Merchant Center's emails plus a weekly look (runbook) plus our own daily price check.** Pulling Google's product statuses into admin needs the Merchant API and a Google service-account key; that is a later spec, and the key would be yours to create and paste into Railway.
- **Q7. Business identity.** **Recommended default: register Merchant Center as Flowers Overseas LLC, and switch the feed on only after the site's company details name the LLC** (gate G3, which waits for the entity ADR). Alternative: start under the current identity and change both later, which Google may re-review.
- **Q8. Peak days.** On a day when the earliest bookable date carries a surcharge (for example around Valentine's Day), spec 009 shows the dated total and withholds the `Offer`; this spec then drops the item for that day. **Recommended default: keep this fail-closed rule now**, and settle the peak-day price policy with spec 009 before 1 February 2027.
- **Q9. Titles.** **Recommended default: the product name exactly as on the page** (no new copy to write or review). Alternative: append a reviewed, per-locale suffix such as "with delivery in Poland", which needs new public message keys that the copy share has little room for.
