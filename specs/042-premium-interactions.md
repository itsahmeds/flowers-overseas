## 1. Problem

The founder asked, 2026-10-19, in chat: "anything else that we missed motion or design or
animation wise? -- also i meant.. like something that we didn't build or even think of.. that
could have been done?". The orchestrator listed six ideas:

1. **Photo-to-page morph.** Click a bouquet and its photo glides up into the product page (CSS
   View Transitions, cross-document, on a navigation from our own page only; never on a first
   load).
2. **A live card preview.** As the buyer types the card message, the card fills in beside it.
3. **Second photo on hover.** A card shows a close-up or another angle on hover.
4. **Soft photo loading.** A blurred version of each photo first, then sharp, instead of a blank
   paper box.
5. **A swipe gallery on product pages for phones**, with smooth snapping.
6. **The route line** drawing between the sender's and the recipient's countries on country pages.

**The request, verbatim.** The founder replied: "fix and do all  this tooo." and then "we will
only launch after that bro." The replies reached the spec writer through the orchestrator's work
order. This spec records them as the request; approving this spec is the founder's own act, on
its PR.

**What exists already, so the spec builds the difference and not a second copy.**
- Idea 2 is half built. The product page draws `PrintedCardPreview` (Caveat face, mark, upright
  card) and its one island, `CardMessageField`, mirrors the typed text into it
  (`src/modules/ui/product/PrintedCardPreview.tsx`, `CardMessageField.tsx`; spec 004 §14 A21
  clauses 3 and 5). Checkout step 2 draws its own preview in the display face, mirrored by
  `CardCounter` (`src/modules/ui/checkout/TextAreaWithCounter.tsx` L118 to L138; spec 010 §2
  L50). The two previews do not look alike, nothing moves when the sample gives way to the
  buyer's words, and the name "handwritten-style" collides with the rule that cards are printed
  (§13 Q4).
- Idea 5 is half built. The phone gallery is a real scroller with mandatory snap
  (`src/modules/ui/primitives/HorizontalScroller.tsx`), and its dots are in-page links that never
  say which photo is showing, because no island follows the scroll (`Gallery.tsx` L148 to L153).
  Second photographs exist: `seed/data/media.json` holds an approved `productDetail` photograph
  for 84 products, but `seed/data/media-variants.json` gives each only a 384 px variant, while
  heroes go to 828 px. Swiped full width on a phone, or shown over a card on hover, a 384 px
  photograph looks soft, so ideas 3 and 5 wait on a `media:variants` run that gives detail
  photographs the hero's ladder (advisor memo 2026-10-20, F1; §13 Q6).
- Ideas 1, 3, 4 and 6 do not exist. The home already draws a decorative dashed route
  (`.hm-route`, desktop only) and the 404 draws one in (motion 13); idea 6 extends that language
  to the country pages.

**Why it is a spec and not six small tweaks.** Each idea sits next to a rule that protects
ranking: the LCP element painted at its final position in its first frame (spec 004 §14 A23
clause 11, AC-44), one priority image per page and nothing downloaded that is not displayed (A24
clause 5), one island per product page (spec 009 AC-14), no island on listings and corridors
(spec 008 AC-23, spec 007 AC-24), the 131,072 B and 133,120 B script budgets (A1,
A-founder-2026-10-18a), the deferral of non-critical chunks until the LCP paint (A-founder-2026-10-15b,
AC-55), and the zero copy headroom on `en-gb` (`.claude/state/wo-1018-common.md` L8). Priority 1
(organic ranking) outranks priority 2 (conversion, which is where premium feel lives), so every
idea below is built to leave first-load numbers where they are. Where an idea cannot be built
without touching one of those rules, §13 asks the founder; nothing here resolves such a conflict
silently.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the AC.

- **Shared limits** binding all six ideas (§5.3.0): per-feature byte caps, first-load metrics
  unchanged, transform and opacity only, a reduced-motion form for each motion, no new counted
  message key, server HTML text and links unchanged (AC-1 to AC-6).
- **Idea 1, the morph:** a cross-document view transition from a product card's photograph to
  the product page's hero, on a same-origin navigation from a card only, from `lg` up only
  (AC-7 to AC-9).
- **Idea 2, the card preview:** one preview component and one face on the product page and at
  checkout step 2, and a soft hand-over from the sample to the buyer's words (AC-10, AC-11).
- **Idea 3, the second photo:** a hover-only crossfade to the product's second approved
  photograph on a fine pointer, downloaded only on hover (AC-12 to AC-14). **Blocked** on the
  detail photographs' hero-ladder variants (AC-20) and the founder's judgement of them (§13 Q6).
- **Idea 4, soft loading:** a tiny blurred placeholder under every non-priority photograph and a
  colour fill under the priority one, generated by the media pipeline at seed time (AC-15 to
  AC-17).
- **Idea 5, the phone gallery:** dots that follow the swipe and a smooth scroll on a dot tap,
  inside the product page's one island or in CSS (AC-18); its second slides use the detail
  photographs at the hero's ladder (AC-20).
- **Idea 6, the route line:** a decorative dashed line drawn once on the corridor page's first
  screen (AC-19).
- The designer's artboards for all six in `docs/design/` before `/plan-tasks` (CLAUDE.md); this
  spec states limits and values, the artboards fix the look.

## 4. User stories

- **Idea 1.** As a buyer browsing a grid on a large screen, when I click a bouquet its photograph
  carries me into its page, so I never lose my place and the site feels made with care.
- **Idea 2.** As a buyer writing a card, I see my words appear on the card as I type, on the
  product page and again at checkout, looking the same in both places and plainly labelled as
  printed, so I know exactly what my recipient will read.
- **Idea 3.** As a buyer with a mouse, when I rest on a bouquet I see it from another angle, so I
  can judge it before I click.
- **Idea 4.** As a buyer on a slow phone connection, I see a soft version of each bouquet at once
  instead of empty boxes, so the page feels alive while it loads.
- **Idea 5.** As a buyer on a phone, I swipe through a bouquet's photographs, each one settles in
  place, and the dots tell me which one I am on.
- **Idea 6.** As a buyer on a country page, a line draws itself from me to the place I am sending
  to, so the page says "far away, made close" without a word.
- **Googlebot.** As Googlebot, I get the same server HTML, the same links, the same LCP element
  and the same first-load bytes as before; nothing I index moves into the client.
- **A visitor who asked for less motion** gets every feature as a still state: no morph, no
  drawing, no zoom, at most an opacity change of 120 ms or less.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

**No database table, column or migration.** Justification: every photograph on a page is resolved
from the seed manifest at build time with zero queries (spec 006 §5.4; `manifest.ts` L5 to L11),
and the placeholders of idea 4 belong to that manifest. Rollback is a revert of the seed file and
the reader.

Seed data (spec 006 §14 amendment requested, §13 Q9):
- **`seed/data/media-lqip.json`** (new): one row per approved asset: `assetId`, `placeholder`
  (a `data:image/webp;base64,...` URI of at most **300 decoded bytes**, at most 16 x 20 px for a
  4∶5 asset), `fill` (the asset's mean colour as an sRGB triplet for the priority fill). Generated
  by the pipeline, never by hand.
- Idea 3's second photograph is **not a new field**: it is the first approved asset after the
  hero in the manifest's order (`galleryFor`'s `thumbs[0]`, `src/modules/catalog/product.ts`
  L1273 to L1287) that is displayable with alt text in the page's locale. No asset, no hover
  layer. Today that is the product's approved `productDetail` photograph (84 products).
- **Variants (advisor memo 2026-10-20, F1).** A `media:variants` run gives every approved
  `productDetail` asset the same width ladder as the product hero in
  `seed/data/media-variants.json` (today they stop at 384 px; heroes go to 828 px). This is a
  seed-data change through spec 006's existing step, not a schema change. Tasks 042-3 and 042-5
  ship only after that run, and `pnpm seed:check` keeps the ladder complete (AC-20).

### 5.2 API / server actions / jobs / events (zod schemas named)

- No route, server action, job or event.
- `scripts/media-lqip.ts` (`pnpm media:lqip`): reads each approved asset's **published variants**
  (the files `seed/data/media-variants.json` lists), never the `.local/` originals, which exist
  only in the main checkout (advisor memo 2026-10-20, F5); it runs the same in any worktree and in
  CI, and writes `seed/data/media-lqip.json`. `sharp` runs here only, never on the render path.
  Validated by **`MediaLqipFileSchema`** (zod, in the seed schemas beside spec 006's, checked by
  `pnpm seed:check`, never imported at render, as `manifest.ts` L12 to L18 requires).
- `src/config/interactions.ts` (new, no database, covered by `pnpm check:no-db`): one typed
  boolean per idea (`morph`, `cardPreviewMotion`, `secondPhoto`, `softLoading`, `galleryDots`,
  `routeLine`), all read at build time. It is the kill switch of §12. It also holds
  `secondPhotoExcluded`, a typed list of product codes that get no hover layer (§5.3.3), set to
  `FO-PT-005`, `FO-GS-002` and `FO-GS-010` until TASK-250 is `done`.

#### 5.3.1 Idea 1: the photo-to-page morph

- **Where.** From a moving or priority product card on any listing, hub or home rail (spec 008
  `ProductCard`, a plain `<a>` today, `ProductCard.tsx` L262 to L268) to that product's page
  hero (`Gallery.tsx`, the `hero` slot).
- **Trigger.** Activation of the card link (click or Enter) causing a same-origin document
  navigation, at a viewport of `lg` or wider, in a browser that supports cross-document view
  transitions. Never on a first load, a reload, an entry from another site, a typed URL, any
  navigation not started from a card, or a viewport below `lg`.
- **How.** Both documents opt in with `@view-transition { navigation: auto; }` inside both
  `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and the `lg` breakpoint's `min-width` query (advisor
  memo 2026-10-20, F2).
  Phones get no morph until spec 023's RUM shows its mobile LCP cost (§3). The product hero
  carries the product's `view-transition-name`; on the old page the name is set **only on the
  activated card's photograph box, in a `pageswap` handler** (the 400 B cap), never as a static
  per-card name. Reason: one product can appear twice on one page (the home rails), and two
  elements with one name abort the transition. A click before the deferred handler loads (L6)
  sets no name and navigates as today. The document root does not crossfade
  (`view-transition-name: none` on the root or equivalent): only the photograph moves, and the
  rest of the page swaps at once. Unmatched names on the old page render nothing during the
  transition.
- **Values.** `--duration-slow` 320 ms, `--ease-emphasised`, once. The photograph keeps its
  aspect ratio at every frame (no stretch; on a phone the hero's box is taller than 4∶5, so the
  snapshot is cropped as `object-fit: cover`, not scaled unevenly). Its end box equals the hero's
  box.
- **Reduced motion.** No view transition at all; the page loads as today.
- **Fine pointer vs touch.** The pointer does not matter (it is a navigation, not a hover); the
  viewport does: from `lg` up only.
- **Unsupported browsers** (Firefox; Safari before 18.2) navigate exactly as today.
- **The conflict.** On the product page the hero is the LCP element. A morph shows a snapshot
  of it moving for 320 ms before it rests, which A23 clause 11 forbids as written ("painted at its
  final position and opacity in the first frame it renders"). Lab LCP (Lighthouse loads cold, with
  no previous page) cannot see it; field LCP (CrUX counts same-origin document navigations) can.
  A third-party RUM test (cited in the advisor memo) found about 70 ms on mobile LCP and about
  5 ms on desktop, which is why phones wait. §13 Q1 records the narrow exception.

#### 5.3.2 Idea 2: the card preview fills in

- **Where.** The product page's card section (`PrintedCardPreview`, `CardMessageField`) and
  checkout step 2 (`TextAreaWithCounter` with `preview`).
- **One preview.** Both pages render one shared preview component: the upright white card, the
  mark, the message line and (at checkout) the signature line, in the face the founder rules
  (§13 Q4; default: Caveat on the message line, as the product page has it). Its caption is the
  existing printed label on each page: `catalog` "Printed on our card · included" on the product
  page and checkout's `card.preview` (the `checkout` surface, which does not count toward A17).
- **Trigger and motion.** On the first input that makes the field non-empty, the sample sentence
  fades out and the buyer's words fade in: `opacity`, `--duration-base` 200 ms,
  `--ease-standard`. Clearing the field fades the sample back. Each keystroke after that updates
  the text at once, with no per-letter animation (a per-letter effect would split graphemes into
  separate nodes, which breaks screen readers and the grapheme count). The card itself does not
  move.
- **Reduced motion.** The swap is instant (L7 allows 120 ms; it uses none).
- **Phone.** The preview stays in view while typing: on a phone it sits above the field (the
  designer draws it), so the on-screen keyboard does not hide it. No sticky element is added
  (spec 010 AC-39's step 3 rule is untouched).
- **Without JavaScript.** The product page shows the labelled sample (A21 clause 5); checkout
  shows the server's kept value (spec 010 AC-10).
- **Unchanged.** The product page's `<textarea>` has no `name` and goes nowhere; no storage, no
  network (spec 009 AC-14); the 200-grapheme limit (spec 004 AC-51); `<bdi>` on user text.
- **The conflicts.** "Handwritten-style" is the idea's wording and the founder ruled cards are
  printed (spec 004 L848 to L852, T-17); Caveat is allowed on the product page only (A21 clause
  3, L786 to L804) and costs up to 30 KB of font at checkout; the two previews disagree today on
  the face. §13 Q4.

#### 5.3.3 Idea 3: the second photo on hover (blocked on imagery)

- **Where.** Every moving product card (not the priority card) whose product has a second
  approved, displayable asset with alt text in the page's locale, at the hero's ladder (§5.1).
- **Not these three (advisor memo 2026-10-20, F6).** TASK-250's products, FO-PT-005, FO-GS-002
  and FO-GS-010, whose catalogue photographs do not show the product, get no hover layer while
  TASK-250 is not `done`. They are listed in `secondPhotoExcluded` (§5.2); emptying the list is a
  one-line PR after TASK-250 closes.
- **Trigger.** Hover with a fine pointer only (L8). Not on focus (A-founder-2026-10-19e clause 5:
  focus starts no motion), not on touch.
- **How.** One `aria-hidden` layer inside the photograph's clipped 4∶5 box, above the photograph
  and under the example label, so it zooms with motion 6's `scale(1.06)`. The second photograph's
  URL is carried on the card as a CSS custom property and used as the layer's `background-image`
  only inside the hover rule, so **no byte of it is requested before the first hover** of that
  card (ADR-0016 keeps `style-src 'unsafe-inline'`, L85). One variant of the card slot's ladder,
  AVIF first through `image-set()`. The layer fades `opacity` 0 to 1 on hover-in and back on
  hover-out.
- **Duration.** Default `--duration-hover` 500 ms with `--ease-photo`, so it moves with the zoom;
  that needs A-founder-2026-10-19e clause 4 to list the layer beside the photograph (§13 Q5;
  fallback `--duration-slow` 320 ms).
- **Reduced motion.** The layer shows at once on hover with no transition, and no zoom (motion
  6's reduced form, unchanged).
- **Coarse pointer.** Nothing: no layer request, no change. The phone sees second photos in the
  product page's swipe gallery (idea 5).
- **Honesty.** The second photograph is an example arrangement too: the card's example label
  stays visible above the layer, the asset is `reviewState` approved, and the product page's
  provenance note already lists every gallery asset (`Gallery.tsx` L136 to L140).
- **Order.** Builds after TASK-277 (the premium hover) merges, because both write the card's
  hover rules and `tests/e2e/motion.spec.ts`.

#### 5.3.4 Idea 4: soft photo loading

- **Where.** Every photograph rendered through `MediaAsset` (cards, rails, the product gallery,
  hub lead figures, the home hero).
- **Non-priority photographs.** The box paints the asset's `placeholder` (§5.1), upscaled and
  soft, as its background, under the `<img>`. When the image is below the fold, motion 11's
  existing fade (opacity 0 to 1, `--duration-slow`) plays over the placeholder instead of over
  the plain paper colour. Above the fold, a non-priority image paints at once over it.
- **The priority photograph** (the page's LCP image, AC-36 as A24 clause 5 amends it) gets only
  the asset's `fill` colour, never an image placeholder. *Reading:* a blurred image the size of
  the LCP box could itself be reported as the LCP candidate on some widths (its bits per pixel
  can exceed Chrome's 0.05 low-entropy cut at small card sizes), which would make the measured
  LCP earlier than the real photograph. A colour cannot be a candidate. Overrulable in one word
  (§13 Q10).
- **Reduced motion.** Motion 11's reduced form: the sharp image appears when loaded, over the
  placeholder.
- **Honesty.** The placeholder is a blurred copy of the same example photograph; the honesty label
  is unchanged.
- **No JavaScript** and no `next/image` `placeholder="blur"` runtime: the data is in the manifest
  and rendered by the server.

#### 5.3.5 Idea 5: the phone swipe gallery

- **Where.** The product page gallery below `lg` (today's `HorizontalScroller` with `GalleryDots`),
  for a product with two or more photographs. A product with one photograph renders no dots, as
  today. With the approved detail photographs, 84 products have a second slide; each slide
  uses the hero's ladder, so a full-width slide is never drawn from the 384 px variant (§5.1,
  AC-20).
- **Snap.** Mandatory, one photograph per screen (unchanged); `scroll-snap-stop: always` so a fast
  flick moves one photograph.
- **Dots follow.** After a swipe settles, exactly one dot is current: filled, a 2 px ring and
  `aria-current="true"`; the others are outline only (not colour alone, WCAG 1.4.1). The
  mechanism is the implementer's: CSS scroll markers where the browser has them, else a scroll
