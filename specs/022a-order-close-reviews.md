## 1. Problem

Orders never close today. Spec 002 §14 A6 row 26 (`photo_uploaded ▶ closed`, `order.closed`, system only) has no job behind it, and neither do rows 29 (`resolved ▶ closed`) and 33 (`refunded_full ▶ closed`). Spec 013 §3 and spec 016 §3 both handed "the job that closes orders" to the review-window spec, and spec 018's erasure and the payouts spec (`plan/01` §8 `payout.accrue` "on `closed`") wait on it. Without it every delivered order sits in `photo_uploaded` for ever.

Nothing asks a buyer how we did, so the Phase 1 exit criterion (`plan/09`: 10 paid orders delivered with a 4.5 average buyer rating) cannot be measured, `rankCandidates` (spec 016 §5.2) ranks every florist on the null default, and the site has no social proof. Reviews are also where consumer law is sharpest: the EU Omnibus amendments to the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (Art. 7(6); Annex I points 23b and 23c) and the UK DMCC Act 2024 (Schedule 20 banned practices, in force 6 April 2025) require that we say whether and how reviews come from real buyers, never publish fake or selectively gathered reviews, never hide the negative ones, and never reward a review without saying so. Google's review-snippet rules forbid self-serving review markup on `Organization` or `LocalBusiness` and markup for reviews not visible on the page. This spec delivers the close, the invitation, the rating and the display as one flow that is verified by construction.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the criterion.

**The close (`src/modules/orders/close/`)**
- A pure `closeDueAt(facts)` decides when an order may close: `photo_uploaded` at the `order.photo_uploaded` event instant plus `complaintWindowHours` (72, `src/config/company.ts`, spec 018 Q1) as absolute hours; `resolved` and `refunded_full` at their entering event's instant. Any other status: never.
- A sweep job `order.close_sweep`, enqueued by the minute tick, closes every due order through `orderService.transition(orderId, 'order.closed', 'system:order.close')`, one transaction per order, under `FOR UPDATE`, skipping (and retrying on a later run) an order with a `requested` or `pending` refund (spec 013 §12's requirement). No raw status write exists.
- `order.closed` sends no buyer, florist or ops message.

**The invitation (`src/modules/reviews/`, `emails/`)**
- On `order.photo_uploaded` for a `live` order, a `reviews` outbox handler inserts one `review_invite` row with `send_after` = photo instant + 24 hours and `expires_at` = photo instant + 30 days.
- The `review.request` job (declared in `src/jobs/index.ts`, registered here), run by the minute tick, sends each due invite as buyer email **B9 `review-invite`** through spec 016's pipeline (`notification_delivery`, `Mailer`, idempotency key), in `order.locale_code`. Eligibility reads no order status after the photo, no rating and no complaint: every delivered `live` order is invited once, unless the buyer opted out, the customer is redacted, or the invite expired while `reviews.invite` was off.
- The review link carries a deterministic token, `HMAC-SHA256(REVIEW_TOKEN_SECRET, inviteId)`, base64url; only its SHA-256 is stored. A retried send renders a byte-identical body, so Resend's idempotency rule never strands a live link.
- **Trustpilot**: with `reviews.trustpilot` on and `TRUSTPILOT_AFS_ADDRESS` set, B9 carries a BCC to Trustpilot's automatic feedback address and its structured snippet (buyer first name, email, order reference), for every invite B9 sends. Trustpilot then sends its own verified invitation (§13 Q2).
- Florists never solicit reviews: no florist template or florist page carries a review or rating request.

**The review form (`/{locale}/review/{token}`)**
- `GET` renders the form, with the star chosen in the email (`?r=1` to `5`) preselected, and writes nothing (mail scanners open links).
- `POST` (server action, zod `ReviewSubmission`) writes one `review` (source `internal`, `verified_order = true`, `moderation_status = 'pending'`) and appends `review.created` through `appendEvent` with actor `buyer`. One review per order.
- Expired, used and unknown tokens get a calm page and write nothing. An opt-out page (`POST` only) stops future invites to that buyer.

**Moderation (`/admin/reviews`)**
- A queue, oldest pending first, and a detail page, under the new permission `reviews.moderate` (admin, ops). Actions: **publish**; **publish without text** (reason `personal_data`, `abuse` or `illegal`; the stars stay public and counted); **remove** (reason `not_genuine`, `duplicate` or `test_order`, with a note); **reply** (shown as our reply). No reason refers to the rating or the tone. Rating and text are immutable. Every write is audited by spec 012's trigger.
- The page shows the Phase 1 exit metric: the average of every first-party rating not removed, to two decimals, with the count.

**The florist rating (`src/modules/partners/rating.ts`)**
- `rating_90d_bp` = round(2000 × mean rating) over the partner's first-party ratings of the last 90 days that are not removed (5.00 = 10000, 4.50 = 9000), `NULL` under 3 ratings. `acceptance_rate_bp` = round(10000 × accepted / answered offers) over 90 days, `NULL` under 5 answered offers. Written nightly and after every submission or moderation touching that partner.

**Display (server-rendered, no client JavaScript)**
- **Review policy** `/{locale}/legal/reviews` (`/de/rechtliches/bewertungsrichtlinie`, `/pl/informacje-prawne/zasady-opinii`): a legal document in spec 041's machinery, existing once `reviews.invite` is on or a published review exists.
- **Reviews page** `/{locale}/reviews` (`/de/bewertungen`, `/pl/opinie`): exists with at least 1 published review; every published review, newest first, 20 per page; summary (average, count, distribution 5 to 1).
- **PDP block**: with at least 3 published reviews of the product, stars and count by the title and the 5 newest reviews below the fold. **Corridor block**: with at least 3 published reviews of orders to that country, the same block, no markup.
- **Schema**: `aggregateRating` and named `review` entries only on a PDP's `Product` node (when spec 009 emits one), equal to what the page shows. No review markup anywhere else.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Payout lines on close** (`payout.accrue`) → the payouts spec (030a). It subscribes to `order.closed`; this spec only writes the event.
- **Photo-missing alert** for a `delivered` order with no photo (it can never close, D6) → spec 025.
- **The admin dispute and resolve buttons** (rows 27 to 29 by admin) → spec 020. This spec closes `resolved` orders once they exist.
- **Florist ratings view, rating-based pausing** (`plan/06` §5.4 "below 4.2 for 30 days → paused"), timeliness, photo and substitution scores → spec 027b. Here: an ops alert only (§11).
- **Recipient rating and thank-you via the card QR** → spec 029.
- **Importing Trustpilot reviews, a Trustpilot score strip or widget, a home-page review carousel** (`plan/04` §3) → later, once Trustpilot holds 25 reviews (`plan/04` trust strip rule). No Trustpilot script loads anywhere.
- **Buyer photos in reviews, editing a submitted review, review translation.** Reviews show in the language written, with `lang`.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` headers** → a later Mailer change; the in-email opt-out link covers the duty (§13 Q3).
- **Florist profile pages with reviews** (`plan/05` #27) → Phase 2.

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer**, a day after the delivery photo I get one short email asking how we did; one tap on a star opens a page where I can add a few words and send it.
- As a **buyer who does not want to be asked**, one link in that email stops it, and nothing else changes.
- As a **visitor**, I see real reviews from people who ordered, the low ones too, with the month and the destination, and a page that tells me how they are checked.
- As the **founder**, I see new reviews in admin, publish them, withhold text that names a person, reply, and read the exit-metric average; I can never edit or bury a rating.
- As **routing**, I rank florists on a rating that reflects what buyers said in the last 90 days.
- As **Googlebot**, I find review markup only where a product's own reviews are visible, never on the organisation.

### 5.2 Jobs, commands, contracts (zod schemas named)

**Consumed:** `orderService.transition` and `appendEvent` (spec 010); `outboxTargets` (a new `reviews` target for `order.photo_uploaded`, `live` only); spec 016's `notificationsFor` data and send pipeline; `Mailer.send` with an additive `bcc?: string` taken only from env (request on spec 012, §12); `succeededRefunds` and refund status (spec 013); `invalidate(tags)` (`src/lib/cache.ts`); spec 041's info-page registry and `pageIndexability()`.

**Events:** `review.created` (status-preserving, payload `ReviewCreatedV1 { mode, reviewId, rating }`), registered in spec 010's registry with `STATUS_PRESERVING_ACTORS['review.created'] = ['buyer']`. `order.closed` payload `OrderClosedV1 { mode, from: 'photo_uploaded' | 'resolved' | 'refunded_full' }`.

**Jobs** (`src/jobs/index.ts`; envelopes per spec 002):
- `order.close_sweep` (`{}`; minute tick, `singletonKey`): selects orders in the three statuses whose `closeDueAt` has passed, oldest first, at most 200 per run; per order, one transaction: lock, re-read status and refunds, transition or skip. An order that moved on (for example to `disputed`) is skipped without error.
- `review.request` (`{}`; minute tick, `singletonKey`): due invites (`send_after <= now`, `sent_at IS NULL`, `skipped_reason IS NULL`), at most 50 per run; skip with a reason, or send B9 and set `sent_at`. With `reviews.invite` off the run does nothing; invites wait and expire at `expires_at`.
- `partners.rating_refresh` (`{ partnerId?: uuid }`; nightly 03:00 UTC for all non-demo partners, and enqueued for one partner after a submission or a moderation).

**Commands:** `submitReview(token, ReviewSubmission)`; `optOutOfReviewInvites(token)`; `moderateReview(reviewId, ModerationAction, admin)` with `ModerationAction` a discriminated union (`publish` | `publish_without_text {reason}` | `remove {reason, note}` | `reply {text}`); `reviews.redactForCustomer(customerId)` for spec 018's erasure; `reviewSummary(scope)` and `listPublicReviews(scope, page)` reading `public_review` only, where scope is `all` | `{ productId }` | `{ countryIso2 }`.

**`ReviewSubmission`**: `rating` int 1 to 5; `text` optional, trimmed, at most 1000 graphemes (`Intl.Segmenter`); `showFirstName` boolean, default false. The denormalised columns are filled server-side: country and delivery date from the order, `product_id` from the first product `order_line` by line number, `partner_id` from the order's accepted, unwithdrawn assignment (else `NULL`).

**B9 `review-invite`** (row added to spec 016's `notificationsFor` data; `live` only): subject and hero "How did we do?"; one line "Tap a star to tell us about your order."; five star links to `/{locale}/review/{token}?r=n`; "It takes less than a minute."; a link "How we check reviews" to the policy; the opt-out link "Prefer not to be asked? Stop these emails." No recipient name, no product upsell, no incentive, no refund, problem or failure wording (founder rule, 2026-10-14). Copy is proposed and ships `reviewed: false` (§7).

**Constants** (`src/config/reviews.ts`): invite delay 24 h; invite life 30 days; text 1000 graphemes; block minimum 3; reviews-page index minimum 10 published reviews with visible text; rating window 90 days; rating minimum 3; acceptance minimum 5; pending alert 72 h.

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

- **Indexability.** Reviews page: `byRule` in `src/modules/seo/indexability.ts`; exists (200) with at least 1 published review, else 404 (`plan/02` §6: pages that fail the rule are not created); `index,follow` only with at least 10 published reviews with visible text, the page's UI strings `reviewed`, the locale indexable and the indexing environment (§13 Q5). Policy: spec 041 legal rule (`noindex` until `lawyerReviewed`). Review form and opt-out: `noindex`, `no-store`; robots `Disallow: /*/review/` (the trailing slash keeps `/en/reviews` crawlable).
- **Canonical and pagination.** Self-canonical; `?page=N` indexable and self-canonical, `?page=1` 301s to the bare URL (`plan/02` §7); `?stars=n` `noindex,follow`, canonical to the bare URL.
- **hreflang.** `alternatesFor()` over indexable locales; the same review set appears on every locale's page, so the alternates are true equivalents.
- **URLs.** `reviews` joins `PATH_SEGMENT_KEYS` (`reviews`, `bewertungen`, `opinie`); the policy is a leaf under `legal` (`reviews`, `bewertungsrichtlinie`, `zasady-opinii`); `review` is a non-localised segment like `track`.
- **Schema.** Only on a PDP `Product` node that spec 009 already emits, and only with at least 3 published reviews of that product: `aggregateRating` (`ratingValue` to one decimal, `ratingCount`, `bestRating` 5, `worstRating` 1) equal to the visible summary, and up to 5 `review` entries for reviews whose author chose to show a first name, each equal to a visible card. **Never** on `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `WebPage` or the reviews page (Google: self-serving reviews are ineligible), never Trustpilot's numbers (Google: do not aggregate reviews from other sites), never on corridor pages. This replaces `plan/02` §9's "Reviews page: `Organization` + `aggregateRating`" row (§13 Q11). Reviews page: `BreadcrumbList` only.
- **Sitemap.** Reviews page in `static.xml` when indexable, `lastmod` = newest `published_at`; policy per spec 041.
- **Internal links.** In: footer, PDP and corridor blocks. Out: the policy from every block and the page; products are not linked from cards (no product name on a card, which keeps duplicate text low).
- **Thin content.** The index threshold of 10 reviews with text; blocks need 3. Seed data has zero reviews, so nothing renders in demo or CI fixtures.
- **CWV.** Server-rendered text and inline SVG stars, no script, blocks below the fold, no layout shift (fixed star box). No third-party script. Lighthouse adds `/en/reviews` once a fixture with 20 reviews exists.

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Verified reviews by construction (UCPD Art. 7(6), Annex I 23b; DMCC Sch. 20).** A first-party review can only be written with a token minted for a delivered `live` order; the database refuses an `internal` review without `verified_order` and an order. Every card says "Verified buyer" and shows the delivery month and destination (`plan/07` §7). The policy page states how this works.
- **No selective invitation, no suppression.** Every delivered `live` order is invited, whatever happened after delivery (a complaint, a refund, a chargeback); the Trustpilot invitation goes to the same set and is never conditioned on our on-site rating (review gating is misleading and against Trustpilot's guidelines). Moderation reasons are closed and none concerns the rating; withholding text keeps the stars public and counted; removal is only for reviews that are not genuine, with a note, audited; the reviews page sorts by date, never by rating, and shows the 1-star bar.
- **No incentives (Annex I 23c; DMCC).** No voucher, discount, draw or gift for a review, in any copy or code path; florists never ask (scan over florist templates).
- **Consumer information.** The policy (legal family, lawyer-gated) covers: who can review; how we check; what we publish and when; the moderation reasons; replies; that we neither pay nor reward reviews; Trustpilot's separate invitation and its own rules; how to report a review. ⚖️ The lawyer reads it in the B4 review.
- **GDPR data flows (→ RoPA, three rows).** (1) Review invitation: buyer email and first name, order reference; basis legitimate interest with the ePrivacy existing-customer conditions (notice at checkout, opt-out in every email; ⚖️ §13 Q3 because German case law treats feedback requests as advertising); retention: the invite row 2 years, no address stored. (2) First-party review: rating, text, optional first name; basis consent to publication given on submission, withdrawable by a request that redacts the text and name; retention while published, redacted on erasure. (3) Trustpilot (Denmark): buyer first name, email, order reference via the BCC; Trustpilot is a processor for invitations and an independent controller for reviews on its platform; DPA filed in `docs/compliance/` before `reviews.trustpilot` turns on. Privacy notice and spec 041's processor-coverage test gain Trustpilot.
- **Checkout notice** (spec 010 pay step, additive, §13 Q3 default): "After delivery we will email you once to ask how we did. You can stop these emails at any time." Positive wording, no tick box.
- **DSA notice and action.** Each card carries a "Report" link (§13 Q8); `docs/runbooks/content-takedown.md` gains the review steps.
- **Erasure.** `reviews.redactForCustomer` nulls text, display name and sets `redacted_at`; the stars stay as an anonymous verified rating. Spec 018's erasure calls it.
- **Price display.** No money in this spec.
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA).** Native radio inputs with visible labels and a fieldset legend; error tied with `aria-describedby`; star images carry text alternatives ("Rated 4 out of 5"); focus order; contrast of filled and empty stars at 3:1 against the ground.
- **Logs and PII.** No token, email, name or review text in logs, Sentry or analytics; review id and order id only. The token is in a URL by design (a bearer link like spec 018's tracking link), not personal data, never logged (`Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`).
- **Founder rules.** No negative framing in B9, the form or the thanks page; no relay language (`RELAY_PATTERNS`) in our copy. A buyer's own words are not ours: review text is outside the relay and claim scans and is never edited.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Close**
- **AC-1** `closeDueAt` returns the `order.photo_uploaded` instant plus exactly 72 × 3600 s for `photo_uploaded` (also across a DST change in the destination zone), the entering event's instant for `resolved` and `refunded_full`, and `null` for each of the other 23 statuses of spec 002 A6.4.
- **AC-2** A sweep closes a due `photo_uploaded` order with one `order.closed` event (actor `system:order.close`, `from: 'photo_uploaded'`) through `orderService.transition`, and leaves an order due one second later untouched; a source scan finds no `status` write outside the engine in `src/modules/orders/close/`.
- **AC-3** A due order with a `requested` or `pending` refund is not closed; after the refund becomes `succeeded` or `failed`, the next sweep closes it.
- **AC-4** Two concurrent sweeps over the same due order write exactly one `order.closed`; an order moved to `disputed` after selection and before its lock is skipped with no event and no error.
- **AC-5** `order.closed` produces no row in `notificationsFor` for any audience and mode, and no `notification_delivery` row after a close.

**Invitation**
- **AC-6** `order.photo_uploaded` on a `live` order creates exactly one `review_invite` with `send_after` = photo + 24 h and `expires_at` = photo + 30 days, also when the outbox row is handled twice; `test` and `demo` orders create none.
- **AC-7** With `reviews.invite` on, a due invite sends one B9 in `order.locale_code` and sets `sent_at`; a second run sends nothing; a retry of the same send renders a byte-identical body (deterministic token); with the flag off nothing is sent and the invite stays unsent until `expires_at`, then is marked `expired`.
- **AC-8** Data-driven over every status reachable after `photo_uploaded` (closed, disputed, resolved, refunded_full, chargeback states) and over refunds partial and full, the invite is sent; it is skipped only for an opted-out buyer (`opted_out`), a redacted customer (`redacted`) or expiry (`expired`).
- **AC-9** Rendered B9 in `en`, `de` and `pl` contains the five links with `r=1` to `r=5`, the policy link and the opt-out link, and contains no match of the negative-framing list (refund, cancel, problem, complaint, sorry, unfortunately, wrong, failed and their `de`/`pl` forms), the incentive list (discount, voucher, code, coupon, prize, win, free and forms), `RELAY_PATTERNS`, the recipient's name, or U+2014.
- **AC-10** With `reviews.trustpilot` on and `TRUSTPILOT_AFS_ADDRESS` set, every B9 sent carries that BCC and the structured snippet with the order reference, and `trustpilot_bcc` is true; with either missing, no B9 carries a BCC; nothing on the send path reads a rating.
- **AC-11** No rendered florist template (V1 to V5) and no florist inbox page matches the review or rating claim patterns of `src/config/honesty.ts`.

**Form**
- **AC-12** `GET /{locale}/review/{token}?r=4` returns 200 with the fourth star checked, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, and leaves the counts of `review`, `review_invite.used_at` and `order_event` unchanged; `robots.txt` disallows `/*/review/` and not `/*/reviews`.
- **AC-13** A valid `POST` writes one `review` with source `internal`, `verified_order` true, the order id, the submitted rating and text, `display_name` = the buyer's first name only when `showFirstName` is true, `moderation_status` pending and the four denormalised columns; it sets `used_at` and appends one `review.created` (actor `buyer`) while the order's status is unchanged.
- **AC-14** A second `POST` with the same token, a `POST` after `expires_at`, and an unknown token each write nothing; expired and unknown render the same page; a text of 1001 graphemes (emoji and Polish letters included) and a rating outside 1 to 5 are refused by `ReviewSubmission` with a field error.
- **AC-15** The opt-out `POST` sets `customer.review_invite_opt_out_at`; that buyer's later invites are skipped as `opted_out`; the opt-out `GET` writes nothing.

**Moderation**
- **AC-16** `/admin/reviews` and every moderation action refuse a user without `reviews.moderate` (403, no write); the queue lists pending reviews oldest first; the header shows the average of all non-removed first-party ratings to two decimals and their count.
- **AC-17** Each of the four actions writes the expected columns and one `audit_log` row; `remove` without a reason or note, `publish_without_text` with a removal reason, and any reason outside the closed list are refused; an `UPDATE` of `rating` or `text` raises `REVIEW_CONTENT_IMMUTABLE`.
- **AC-18** A review published without text appears on the public page with its stars and the withheld line, counts in the public summary and in routing; a removed review appears nowhere public and counts in neither.
- **AC-19** Under `app.role = 'public'`, `public_review` returns published reviews only, returns `text` as null for a withheld review, exposes none of `order_id`, `partner_id`, `moderation_note` or `moderated_by`, and a direct `SELECT` on `review` returns no row.
- **AC-20** A review pending for more than 72 hours raises `alert=reviews.pending_overdue` once per review.

**Rating**
- **AC-21** `partnerRatingBp` returns `null` for 2 ratings, 10000 for [5, 5, 5], 8667 for [5, 4, 4], 9000 for [5, 4, 5, 4]; it excludes removed reviews and reviews created more than 90 days before `now`; `acceptanceRateBp` returns `null` under 5 answered offers, excludes withdrawn offers, and returns 6667 for 4 accepted, 1 declined and 1 timed out.

**Display**
- **AC-23** The policy page exists at its three URLs exactly when `reviews.invite` is on or a published review exists; it carries the eight sections of §8 "Consumer information", is `noindex` while `lawyerReviewed` is false, and the footer links to it only when it exists.

- **AC-27** JSON-LD: a PDP that emits `Product` and has 3 or more published reviews carries `aggregateRating` equal to its visible summary and at most 5 `review` entries, each equal to a visible named card; every other page type (home, about, corridor, reviews page, occasion, category, info pages) carries no `aggregateRating`, `Review` or `review` and no `Organization` with either; no JSON-LD anywhere contains a Trustpilot figure.

- **AC-29** `reviews.redactForCustomer` nulls `text` and `display_name` and sets `redacted_at` on that customer's reviews, keeps `rating`, and the next render shows "Verified buyer" with no text.
- **AC-30** Publishing, withholding, removing, replying and redacting each call `invalidate()` with `reviews`, `reviews:product:{id}` and `reviews:country:{iso2}` for that review.
- **AC-31** The review form, the reviews page and `/admin/reviews` pass axe with zero violations; the star group is operable by keyboard alone; `pnpm i18n:check` is green with the new keys and no literal strings.
- **AC-32** The checkout pay step renders `checkout.reviewNotice` in `en`, `en-gb`, `de` and `pl`, with no tick box.
- **AC-33** `docs/compliance/ropa.md` holds the three rows of §8, the privacy content files name Trustpilot (spec 041's processor-coverage test green), and `.env.example` and `src/lib/env.schema.ts` carry `REVIEW_TOKEN_SECRET` (required, at least 32 characters, refused as a placeholder in production) and `TRUSTPILOT_AFS_ADDRESS` (optional email).

## 13. Open questions

Each has a recommended default; the body is written to the default.

- **Q1. When does the review email go?** Default: **24 hours after the delivery photo**, as its own email (B4 stays photo only, founder 2026-10-14). Alternative: 48 hours.
- **Q2. How does Trustpilot invite?** Default: **Trustpilot's automatic feedback BCC on our review email, for every invited buyer** (free plan, 50 invitations a month; Trustpilot marks them verified). Alternatives: a Trustpilot link on the thanks page shown to every submitter whatever their rating; or no Trustpilot at launch. Never a link shown only after a high rating.
- **Q3. What makes the review email lawful in every market?** Default: **a notice line at checkout plus a stop link in every review email**, no tick box (existing-customer rule; ⚖️ the lawyer confirms, because German courts treat feedback emails as advertising). Alternative: an unticked opt-in box (fewer reviews).
- **Q4. Whose name shows on a review?** Default: **"Verified buyer" unless the buyer ticks "show my first name"**. Alternative: first name by default with an untick.
- **Q5. When may Google index the reviews page?** Default: **10 published reviews with text**; it exists from 1.
- **Q6. How many reviews before a product or country block appears?** Default: **3** (`plan/02` §9).
- **Q7. What does a delivered order's tracking page say after it closes?** Default: **keep "Delivered" with the photo** for an order closed from `photo_uploaded` with no full refund; "Closed" only for refunded or chargeback endings (a request on spec 018's label map; A6.4 unchanged).
- **Q8. How does someone report a review (DSA)?** Default: **a WhatsApp link with the review number prefilled**, the channel the help page already offers. Alternative: a short form (a new data flow).
- **Q9. What happens to a review nobody moderates?** Default: **an alert at 72 hours, no automatic publishing**; the policy promises a check within 3 working days.
- **Q10. How is the florist rating stored?** Default: **mean × 2000 in basis points (4.50 = 9000), 90 days, at least 3 ratings, else empty**; acceptance at least 5 answered offers.
- **Q11. May the plan change to match Google's rules?** Default: **yes**: `plan/02` §9 "Reviews page: `Organization` + `aggregateRating`" and `plan/07` §7 "Schema" read "review markup only on a product's own reviews; never on the organisation". The founder edits or approves the plan edit.
