# specs/
One spec per feature, numbered `NNN-<slug>.md`, created by `/spec`. Template: `_template.md`. A spec must be `approved` before `/plan-tasks` will break it into tasks. Numbers are reserved by the roadmap (`plan/09-roadmap.md`): 001–012 Phase 0, 013–025 Phase 1, 026–031 Phase 2, 032–039 Phase 3.

A letter suffix splits one reserved number when a spec's deferred half becomes its own spec:

| Spec | Split from | Phase | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| `012b-cms.md` | `012-admin-v0.md` §15 | 1 | Catalogue CMS: the database as master copy, publishing, review for catalogue text and photos, florist bouquets, prices, photos, country go-live (slice A); review everywhere (slice B) |

Outside the roadmap's reserved ranges: 040 hosting (Railway and Cloudflare), 041 trust, help and legal pages, 042 premium interactions (approved 2026-10-20), 043 site clarity (approved 2026-10-20): the header follows the destination in the URL, one state line per page, chips grouped by kind, photographed products first, no internal words on public pages; 044 blog: posts written, approved and published from admin on spec 012b's machinery; 046 Google Merchant feed (proposed): one product feed per live destination and page language for Google's free shopping listings, built from the same product-page data so the feed price is the visible, schema and charged price, and serving items only once the site is open to search engines and the destination takes real payments; 047 discount codes: admin-made percent or fixed-amount codes for launch promotions, entered on checkout step 3 and paid for by us, with the public price, the schema and the florist's pay unchanged; 048 business gifting: one indexable corporate-flowers page per locale with an enquiry form that lands in admin and emails the founder, with invoicing on account and CSV bulk orders kept as a later slice; 049 monthly flowers: prepaid plans of 3, 6 or 12 monthly deliveries to one recipient, charged once with no stored card, each delivery becoming an ordinary order through `orderService.transition` and the normal routing; 050 referrals: a fixed amount off a friend's first order above a minimum, and the same amount as store credit for the referrer once the friend's order is delivered, off until the founder switches it on.
