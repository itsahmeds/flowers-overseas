## 1. Problem

A paid order today goes nowhere after the money is secured. Spec 013 takes the payment and writes the outbox rows; spec 010 owns the engine that moves the order; spec 011 gives a florist an inbox where they can accept, decline, deliver and add the photo. Nothing picks the florist, nothing re-offers when one declines or does not answer, nothing tells the florist an order is waiting, nothing tells the buyer what is happening, and nobody notices when no florist takes an order. `plan/09` Phase 1 AC 1 asks for one real order "routed automatically, accepted by a florist via magic link, delivered, photo uploaded, buyer notified … zero manual DB edits", and AC 7 asks for alerts on SLA cascade exhaustion, webhook failure and "`paid` without `routed`" for more than 5 minutes. Without this spec the product, trust plus routing (`plan/00` §1), has no routing.

`plan/09` reserved two numbers: `016` (routing job, assignment SLA, timeouts, no-partner path) and `017` (notifications: Resend set, React Email, WhatsApp procedure, vendor magic-link flows). They are one flow (an offer is a notification, an exhaustion is an alert), so they are one spec. Number `017` is folded in here and stays unused; references to "spec 017" in specs 010, 011, 012 and ADR-0019 resolve to this file. ADR-0019 replaced the "vendor magic-link flows" with the one florist sign-in, so the florist half of `017` is now "order emails that sign the florist in and open the order in one tap" (spec 011 §12, ADR-0019 consequences).

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the AC.

**Routing (`src/modules/orders/routing/`; `plan/06` §5.2)**
- **Payment model (founder's ruling, §14).** Every card and wallet payment is captured at placement: automatic capture, no authorisation hold, no manual capture. An order therefore goes `placed → paid_unrouted` (`order.captured`, row 2 of spec 002 A6.3) and is then routed. The authorise-then-capture-on-acceptance path (`order.authorised`, row 1, and the `accepted` capture of rows 14 and 15 as a money step) **is not used at launch**: this spec has no handler, template or test for `order.authorised`, and the A6 rows and spec 002 §14 A8's `authorised` row stay as they are, unused. A cancel before production is a full refund through spec 013's refund path.
- Routing starts when a `live` or `test` order reaches **payment secured**, which at launch is `order.captured` from `placed` (row 2, to `paid_unrouted`). This is A6.1's reading of `plan/01` §8's "paid", and spec 013's Q1. **Both `test` and `live` orders are routed by this spec's router, and this spec registers the `routing` outbox handler** (orchestrator ruling from founder 16-11); spec 013 only hands the order off through the `routing` target and never calls `demoRoute` for them. Spec 010's `demoRoute` and spec 011's demo effect serve `demo` mode only; this spec never routes a `demo` order.
- A pure, unit-tested ranking picks one florist over **coverage**, **catalogue**, **blackout**, **capacity** on the delivery date, **sign-in reachability**, then rating, acceptance rate, payout and round-robin (§5.2).
- Each offer has an **SLA** (`plan/06` §5.2 rule 3): 60 minutes 08:00–20:00 destination local time, 30 minutes for same-day orders; orders secured at night are offered at 07:30 with the SLA ending 09:00.
- A reminder at half the SLA; on decline or timeout the next candidate is offered (the **cascade**), up to 5 offers or the routing deadline.
- **No-partner path**: exhaustion moves the order to `no_partner_available` and alerts the founder; the founder reroutes through spec 020's reroute action (which calls this spec's `manualReroute`), or, after a grace window, the order is cancelled with `order.cancelled` and spec 013's cancel handler refunds it in full. The buyer sees none of this until the cancellation: for the buyer the order reads "We have your order" throughout, and the cancellation reads as a plain cancellation with a full refund (B5). The **grace window** (founder 16-2, advisor's pick): for an order due today, 2 hours; for an order not due today, until 10:00 destination local time the next morning; both capped at the routing deadline (§5.2). An order that no florist covers at all goes to `no_partner_available` at once, through spec 002 §14 A8's `paid_unrouted` row (founder 16-9; the `authorised` row is unused at launch).
- **After acceptance**: this spec writes **no** event. On `assignment.accepted` spec 013 writes `order.captured` (row 14, no money moves, the money was taken at placement) and then `order.in_production` (row 16) in one transaction, which is what makes the florist's "Out for delivery" action (row 19) available; this spec only sends V3 to the florist on the `order.captured` from `accepted` row and B2 ("Being made") to the buyer on `order.in_production`. **Buyer messages fire on order, production and delivery events, never on an offer, an acceptance, a decline, a timeout, a reroute or the absence of a florist** (§5.2 `notificationsFor`).
- **Withdrawal**: when an order leaves fulfilment before delivery (cancelled, full refund, chargeback), the open or accepted assignment is withdrawn and the florist is told to stop. (A refused capture at acceptance, row 15, cannot happen at launch: nothing is captured at acceptance.)
- **Checkout coverage predicate** `canServe(...)`, the postcode coverage check spec 010 §3 assigned to this spec, for spec 010's checkout to call at step 1 and at placement (wired by spec 010 §14 A3, §12; founder 16-13: checkout refuses a postcode no florist covers).

**Notifications (`src/modules/notifications/`, `emails/`; ADR-0013, `plan/11` §5)**
- An outbox handler for the `notifications` target: picks templates from `(event, from_status, mode)`, resolves addresses at send time, renders React Email in the audience's language, sends through spec 012's one `Mailer`, and records each send in `notification_delivery`.
- The Phase 1 set (§5.2 table): buyer messages (order received with the contract confirmation, being made, on its way, delivered with photo, cancelled with a full refund, refund, and **the invoice or credit note email, B8**), florist messages (offer, reminder, order confirmed, withdrawn, photo reminder), the founder's no-partner alert and WhatsApp copies.
- **Every florist email signs the florist in and opens the order in one tap** (ADR-0019; spec 011 §12), through spec 012's sign-in core.
- The **Phase 1 WhatsApp procedure**: the founder receives a ready-to-paste message for each florist who chose WhatsApp, **only for the offer (V1) and the stop message (V4) at launch** (founder 16-5, advisor's pick); the reminder (V2) and photo reminder (V5) reach the florist by email only. Email always goes in parallel.
- **Resend webhooks**: signature-verified, stored before processing, idempotent, order-independent, with contract tests (`plan/09` AC 3).

**Alerts (`plan/09` AC 7, this spec's share)**
- Cascade exhausted (`order.no_partner_available`); an offer past its SLA with no timeout processed; a Resend webhook row dead; a florist email bounced. The "secured but not routed for 5 minutes" check and the Stripe webhook alerts ("no Stripe webhook for 24 hours") are spec 013's, built there now (spec 013 §13 13-8; `sla.watch`); this spec supplies the expected first-offer time that check needs (§5.2). Routing alerts here, payment alerts in 013, per founder 16-10; spec 012 §11's pointer to spec 025 is updated by §12's request.

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer**, I get an email when my order is in (with my confirmation and the terms), another when it is being made, one when it is on its way and one with the photo when it has arrived. I am never told how the order reaches the person who makes it.
- As a **buyer whose order we could not deliver**, I am told plainly that we could not deliver it and that I have been refunded in full, so I can still send something else in time.
- As a **florist**, I get a short email (and, if I chose it, a WhatsApp message from the founder) with the product, date, town, postcode and what I receive; one tap opens the order already signed in, and I accept or decline there.
- As a **florist who accepted**, I am told the moment the order is confirmed, and told at once to stop if it is cancelled before delivery.
- As the **founder**, I am alerted when no florist takes an order, when an offer is stuck past its time, or when Resend webhooks fail, and I can reroute or cancel from admin without a database edit.
- As a **recipient**, my address and phone never appear in an email or a WhatsApp message.

**Routing functions**
- `eligibleCandidates(order, partners, now)` — pure. Eligible when: the partner's status matches the order's mode (`live` → `active`, `test` → `demo`; spec 010's trigger enforces the same pair); a `partner_coverage` row matches the order's postcode zone or city; `partner_catalog_mapping.can_fulfil` is true for every product line and tier; no `partner_blackout` on the delivery date; accepted plus open offers on that date are below `min(fulfillment_partner.capacity_per_day, partner_coverage.capacity_per_day)`; it has not been offered this order; and it has at least one `partner_member` whose sign-in spec 011's `signInPolicy(environment)` allows (a florist who cannot sign in cannot act on the offer).
- `rankCandidates(candidates)` — pure, deterministic. Key in order: inside the best-rating band (rating ≥ 95 % of the best candidate's, `plan/06` §5.2 rule 2c) before outside it, then rating descending; acceptance rate descending; payout for this order ascending; last offer time ascending (round-robin); partner id. A null rating counts as 4.50 and fewer than 5 offers in 90 days as 100 % acceptance (§13 16-1).
- `offerWindow(now, deliveryDate, zone)` — pure, in the destination's IANA zone with spec 009's zone helpers. The rules apply in this order, and the first that matches wins. **Night first:** 20:00–07:30 → offer at the next 07:30, SLA to 08:00 for a same-day order and to 09:00 otherwise (no florist is woken, and a same-day order is not exhausted overnight). Then 07:30–08:00 → offer now, SLA to 08:00 for same-day and to 09:00 otherwise. Then same-day 08:00–20:00 → offer now, SLA 30 min. Then 08:00–20:00 → offer now, SLA 60 min. The SLA never passes the **routing deadline**: the delivery date at the country's same-day cutoff plus 2 hours (§13 16-2). Absolute instants are stored; DST is the zone's job, never added hours.
- `graceEnd(exhaustedAt, deliveryDate, zone)` — pure, same zone helpers; the instant at which an untaken `no_partner_available` order is cancelled (founder 16-2, advisor's pick). An order is **due today** when its delivery date is the destination-local date of `exhaustedAt`. **Due today:** `exhaustedAt` + 2 hours. **Not due today:** the first 10:00 destination-local that is at least 2 hours after `exhaustedAt` (an order exhausted at 21:00 waits until 10:00 the next morning; one exhausted at 06:00 until 10:00 the same morning). Both are **capped at the routing deadline**, so the result never passes it. Absolute instants; DST is the zone's job.
- `expectedFirstOfferAt(order)` — `offerWindow(...)`'s offer time from the moment the payment was secured; exported for spec 013's unrouted check so a night-deferred order does not alert at 23:00 (§12).
- `canServe({ countryIso2, postcode, cityId, deliveryDate, lines, mode })` → `{ ok: true } | { ok: false, reason: 'no_coverage' | 'no_capacity' }` — the same rules as `eligibleCandidates` minus "already offered", read under the system context on `no-store` routes only; never called while rendering a cached page.

**Jobs** (pg-boss on the Railway worker, ADR-0018; payloads are zod schemas; `order.route` is a job name, not an event):
- `order.route` (`{ orderId }`, `singletonKey = orderId`): enqueued by the `routing` outbox handler on payment secured, by `afterDecline`, and by `assignment.timeout`. In one transaction: lock the order and the chosen partner row `FOR UPDATE` (two routes cannot double-book capacity), then recount that partner's accepted plus open offers for the date **after** the lock and move to the next candidate if it is now full; pick the top candidate; insert `order_assignment` (`attempt` + 1, `offered_at`, `sla_expires_at`); write the events of the table above; schedule `assignment.remind` at half the SLA and `assignment.timeout` at `sla_expires_at` (both keyed by assignment id). A deferred offer reschedules the job with `startAfter` and writes nothing else. No candidate, 5 attempts made, or the deadline passed: `order.no_partner_available` and schedule `order.no_partner_expire` at `graceEnd`. The same applies when the `routing` handler moves an order straight to `no_partner_available` because `routing.auto` is off (orchestrator ruling on O-3, 2026-10-14).
- `assignment.remind`: if still unanswered and not withdrawn, send V2. Otherwise nothing.
- `assignment.timeout`: if still unanswered, set `response = 'timeout'`, write `assignment.timed_out`, send V4 to that florist, enqueue `order.route`. An answered or withdrawn assignment makes it a no-op. Accept and timeout racing on one assignment: both lock the assignment row; the first wins, the second finds it answered.
- `order.no_partner_expire`: at `graceEnd(exhausted_at, delivery date, zone)` (§13 16-2); if the order is still `no_partner_available`, call `cancelOrder(orderId, system:routing.no_partner_expiry, 'no_partner')`, which writes `order.cancelled`. Spec 013's cancel handler refunds in full; B5 goes to the buyer. If the order has moved on (rerouted, or cancelled by the buyer or an admin), nothing. **`routing.auto` off changes nothing here (orchestrator ruling on O-3, 2026-10-14):** the order gets the same `graceEnd` deadline from the moment it entered `no_partner_available`; if by then the founder has not rerouted or cancelled it, it is cancelled with `reason: no_partner` through `cancelOrder`, spec 013 refunds the full charged amount automatically, and the buyer receives B5's plain cancellation ("We could not deliver this order. We have refunded {amount}."). No charged order can wait without an end.
- `routing.sla_watch` (added to spec 013's `sla.watch` if it exists, else its own minute job): an assignment unanswered 5 minutes past `sla_expires_at` raises `alert=routing.sla_overdue` (the timeout job did not run).
- `assignment.photo_remind`: scheduled on `order.delivered`, fires 1 h later; sends V5 if the order is still `delivered`.

**The `routing` outbox handler** (idempotent on the outbox row):
- `order.captured` from `placed` → enqueue `order.route` (or, with `routing.auto` off for the destination, write `order.no_partner_available` at once and schedule `order.no_partner_expire` at `graceEnd`, with that moment as `exhaustedAt`). `order.authorised` has no handler (not written at launch).
- `order.captured` from `accepted` → nothing in the `routing` target: spec 013 has already written `order.in_production` in the acceptance transaction (this handler writes no event for it). V3 and B2 go through the `notifications` target (B2 on `order.in_production`).
- **Withdrawal**: `order.cancelled`, `order.refunded_full`, `order.chargeback_opened` and `order.cancelled_refunded`, each when the event's `from_status` is not `delivered`, `photo_uploaded`, `closed`, `disputed` or `resolved`: set `withdrawn_at` and `withdrawn_reason` on the order's open or accepted assignment, and send V4 to its florist. Pending remind, timeout and photo-reminder jobs then find it withdrawn and do nothing.
- `order.delivered` → schedule `assignment.photo_remind`.

**`manualReroute(orderId, partnerId, actor)`** (`src/modules/orders/routing/`, called by spec 020's action panel with `admin:{uuid}`): from `no_partner_available`, writes `order.manual_reroute`, then the first-offer steps to the named partner, which must pass `eligibleCandidates` with "already offered" relaxed (the founder may have phoned a florist who declined earlier). From `routed`, `manual_reroute` or any other state it refuses with `ORDER_TRANSITION_ILLEGAL` and writes nothing. Until spec 020 ships, nothing calls it, and an exhausted order is cancelled at the end of the grace window (§13 16-4).

**Notifications**
- `notificationsFor(event, fromStatus, mode)` — pure; the table below as data. `demo` → nothing. `test` → florist and ops rows only, never a buyer row (spec 010 AC-24; §13 16-11). `live` → every row. **No buyer row exists for any of `order.routed`, `assignment.offered`, `assignment.accepted`, `assignment.declined`, `assignment.timed_out`, `order.no_partner_available`, `order.manual_reroute`, or `order.captured` from `accepted`** (founder's ruling, §14): the buyer is told about an order, its production, its delivery, a cancellation or a refund, never about routing.
- The `notifications` outbox handler, and the jobs above for V2 and V5: for each row, resolve the address at send time (buyer → `order.buyer_snapshot` email, spec 010; florist → `users.email` of each `partner_member` of the assignment's partner; ops → `OPS_ALERT_EMAIL`), render in the audience's locale (buyer → `order.locale_code`; florist → that member's `partner_member.locale_code`; ops → `en`), insert `notification_delivery` as `queued` (or find the existing row by its unique key), call `Mailer.send` with `idempotencyKey = {source_key}:{template_key}:{recipient_ref}` (request on spec 012, §12), and record `provider_message_id` and `sent`. **A repeat** (outbox row handled again, job re-run) that finds the row `sent` or later skips it; one that finds it still `queued` sends again with the same key, because a crash between the insert and the send must not drop the email. **Resend's `409 invalid_idempotent_request`** (the key was already used, with a different body, within Resend's 24-hour key window) means the first attempt was accepted: the row is marked `sent` with no `provider_message_id`, and nothing is retried. A `409 concurrent_idempotent_requests` and any other error throw, so the dispatcher's retry and `dead` handling apply. **Chosen over pinning the body per attempt:** a florist email's body holds a freshly minted sign-in token, and pinning the body would mean storing a live token; treating the 409 as sent keeps no token at rest.

- **WhatsApp copy** (W): for a partner with `notification_channel = 'whatsapp'`, each **V1 (offer) and V4 (stop message) only** (founder 16-5, advisor's pick; V2 and V5 have no W copy at launch, and adding them later is a change to `notificationsFor`'s data, not to the pipeline) also sends the founder an email holding the florist's message, in the locale of the partner's earliest owner member, ready to paste. It carries the inbox address `{FLORIST_BASE_PATH}/orders/{assignmentId}` and **no sign-in token**, so no bearer credential passes through the founder's inbox or Meta.
- **The refund line** (spec 002 A6.4, advisor F4): B6, and any buyer email sent after a `refund` row with `status = 'succeeded'` exists, shows "Refunded {amount} on {date}" (refund minor units and currency through `Intl` in the buyer's locale; the refund's success date). A `refunded_full` order is never called "cancelled" in an email (D5).

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Routing**
- **AC-1** For a `live` or `test` order, `order.captured` from `placed` (the only payment-secured event at launch; `order.authorised` has no handler), leads, through the `routing` handler this spec registers, to exactly one `order.route` job run and one first offer, even when the outbox row is handled twice, and `demoRoute` is not called; for a `demo` order no job is enqueued and only `demoRoute` serves it.
- **AC-2** `eligibleCandidates` excludes, each on its own, a partner whose status does not match the order's mode, with no coverage for the order's postcode zone or city, unable to fulfil one product line, with a blackout on the date, at capacity on the date, already offered this order, or with no member `signInPolicy` allows.
- **AC-3** `rankCandidates` orders a fixture of five candidates exactly as §5.2's key says, including the 95 % rating band, the null-rating default of 4.50 and the under-5-offers default of 100 %.
- **AC-4** `offerWindow` returns the offer time and SLA end of §5.2 for same-day, daytime, 07:30–08:00 and night cases in Europe/Warsaw and Europe/London, including the nights of 25 Oct 2026 and 28 Mar 2027, and never an SLA end after the routing deadline; a same-day order secured at 06:00 is offered at 07:30 with its SLA ending at 08:00 (the night rule wins over same-day immediacy).
- **AC-5** A first offer on a `paid_unrouted` order writes, in one transaction, `order.routed` (actor `system:order.route`) and then `assignment.offered`, inserts one `order_assignment` with `attempt = 1`, `offered_at` and `sla_expires_at`, and queues V1 to each member of the partner (and W for a WhatsApp partner).
- **AC-6** A re-offer on a `routed` order writes `assignment.offered` and no `order.routed`, inserts the next `attempt`, and leaves the status `routed`.
- **AC-7** With no answer, V2 is sent at half the SLA; at `sla_expires_at` the assignment gets `response = 'timeout'`, `assignment.timed_out` is written by `system:assignment.timeout`, V4 goes to that florist and the next candidate is offered. A timeout on an answered or withdrawn assignment writes nothing.
- **AC-8** A decline through spec 011's `respondToOffer` on a `test` or `live` order leads to the next offer within one `order.route` run; the declining florist receives no V4.
- **AC-9** Two concurrent routes for orders on the same date cannot take a partner past its capacity (two workers).
- **AC-10** On a `routed` order, no candidate left, 5 offers made, or the deadline passed each lead to `order.no_partner_available` and A1 (an ops email with the admin order link and a Sentry event tagged `alert=routing.no_partner`).
- **AC-11** On a `paid_unrouted` order with no candidate at the first attempt, and on any paid order in a country with `routing.auto` off, the order moves to `no_partner_available` with A1 and no buyer message of any kind, and `order.no_partner_expire` is scheduled at `graceEnd` in both cases; with `routing.auto` off and no admin action, at `graceEnd` the order receives one `order.cancelled` with `reason: no_partner` through `cancelOrder` (actor `system:routing.no_partner_expiry`), spec 013 refunds the full charged amount, and the buyer receives B5 (AC-40); one second before `graceEnd`, or after an admin reroute or cancel, nothing is written by the job (orchestrator ruling on O-3, 2026-10-14).
