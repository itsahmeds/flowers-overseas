## 1. Problem

Florists are agreeing to work with us now (founder, 2026-10-22). ADR-0019 gave florists one way in: an email sign-in to the inbox, with no per-order links. Spec 011 built that inbox for Phase 0 only. It is mounted at `/demo/vendor-inbox`, `signInPolicy("production")` is empty, and every florist route answers 404 on the live site (spec 011 §2, §13 Q4). So a real florist, once signed and switched to `active` in admin, has no way to see an order. Spec 016's routing even skips a florist nobody can sign in for (its `eligibleCandidates`), so no real order would be offered at all.

There is a second gap the plan did not name. Sign-in is allowed only for an email with a `partner_member` row, and in production nothing creates one: spec 012's admin creates the partner with a contact email but no member, and spec 011's `florists:demo-member` script refuses to run in production. A real florist needs a way in that the founder controls from admin, with no code change and no database console.

This spec is the smallest change that lets a signed florist open, accept and fulfil a real order on flowersoverseas.com: move the inbox to `/vendor` (`plan/02` §4.1, `plan/05` #58), let `active` florists sign in on the live site, and let the founder grant that access in admin. Everything else of the Phase 2 portal stays later (§3).

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the criterion.

**The mount point moves to `/vendor`**
- `FLORIST_BASE_PATH` (`src/config/florists.ts`) becomes `/vendor`. Pages, the florist Auth.js base path (`/vendor/api/auth`), the sign-in pages (`/vendor/sign-in`), the language switch, the order pages (`/vendor/orders/{assignmentId}`), the photo route and the cookie path (`Path=/vendor`) all derive from it, as spec 011 built them. The route directory under `src/app` moves with it; spec 011's florist route test holds the tree equal to the constant. AC-1.
- `/demo/vendor-inbox` and every path under it answer 404 in every environment, with no redirect (§13 Q6). AC-2.

**Who may sign in on the live site**
- `signInPolicy`: `production` gives `["active", "paused"]`; `development`, `test`, `preview` and `staging` keep `["demo"]`; an unknown environment still reads as production (fail closed). §13 Q1. AC-3.
- The production 404 is removed: the florist surface answers in every environment. `/vendor` stays dynamic, `private, no-store` and `noindex` (spec 011 §5.4), and the locale proxy passes it through untouched. AC-4.
- **Status is read on every request.** `requireFloristContext()` keeps a membership only while its partner's status is in `signInPolicy(environment)` at that request. A member whose partner leaves the list (offboarded) is sent to sign-in on the next request and gets no new link. In production a `demo` partner's membership never yields a `partnerId`. AC-5.
- **A paused shop finishes what it accepted.** A member of a `paused` partner sees its orders and can mark out for delivery, delivered and add the photo. An open offer shows Decline and no Accept. An accept is refused unless the partner's status matches the order's mode at that moment (`live` needs `active`; `demo` and `test` need `demo`), the database's `ASSIGNMENT_MODE_MISMATCH` rule, checked under the command's row lock. The inbox shows a short paused notice. AC-6.

**The founder grants access in admin (§13 Q2)**
- The partner's contact email (spec 012 §2.5) is the one address that may sign in for that partner. Creating a partner (convert from an application included) or saving a changed contact email runs `syncPartnerOwnerMember` in the same audited admin transaction. It upserts the `users` row for the trimmed, lower-cased address and one `owner` `partner_member` row; it deletes the previous address's membership; and it deletes the previous user, with their sessions, when nothing else refers to them (no other membership, no `admin_profile`, no `user_role`, no `customer` row). Access therefore opens exactly when the founder flips the partner to `active`, and closes when the partner is offboarded or the address changes. AC-7.
- `partner_member` joins spec 012's audited tables: one migration attaches `audit_row_change('user_id', 'partner_id')`. AC-8.
- **Starting language** (§13 Q3): a new membership's `locale_code` is the language of the application the partner was converted from; otherwise `pl` for a partner in Poland, `de` in Germany or Austria, `en` elsewhere. The florist changes it with spec 011's switch; a re-sync after an address change keeps the previous member's choice. AC-9.
- **The founder can see the florist got in.** The first successful florist sign-in sets `partner_member.accepted_at`, once. The admin partner page shows a "Florist sign-in" block: the address, and "Not signed in yet" or "First signed in on {date}". AC-10.

**Proof and records**
- An integration journey under the production policy: a member of an `active` partner signs in, accepts a `live` order and fulfils it with a photo; a `demo` member in the same configuration receives no email. AC-11.
- Public pages unchanged; no public link, sitemap entry or index signal for `/vendor`. AC-12.
- A post-deploy smoke run on production. AC-13.
- Records: cookie register, RoPA, the florist runbook, the codebase map, and pointer amendments in specs 011 and 012. AC-14.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Staff invites,** more than one person per shop, owner and staff permissions, a partner switcher for chains: the rest of **026** (Phase 2; `027b` in the gap list).
- **Capacity and blackout calendar,** substitution notes, the delivery-failed report, the printed card with QR: **027a**. Payout history, statements, ratings, coverage and product toggles: **027b**.
- **Payouts,** including "the first five orders paid when accepted" (founder, 2026-10-19): **030a**. **Florist-set bouquets and prices:** spec **012b** slice A. The inbox keeps showing "You receive {payout}" from the partner's catalogue mapping, which the founder enters in admin.
- **Withdrawing open offers when a shop is paused,** and reassigning an offboarded florist's accepted orders: spec **020a** (reroute) and spec 016's cascade, which times out an open offer the paused shop cannot accept.
- **Order emails and their one-tap sign-in:** spec **016** (approved; its links derive from `FLORIST_BASE_PATH` and move with it).
- **A welcome email at activation** (§13 Q4), **a public "Florist sign-in" link** (§13 Q5), **a redirect from the old path** (§13 Q6).
- **A switch that closes the whole florist surface without a deploy;** passkeys (`plan/11` §1); the WhatsApp Cloud API (**028**).

## 4. User stories

- As a **florist the founder has just switched to active**, I open flowersoverseas.com/vendor on my phone, type my shop's email, tap the link, and see my orders in my language, with no app and no password.
- As that florist, when an order email arrives I tap it, accept, make and deliver the bouquet, and add the photo from the same page.
- As a **florist whose shop is paused** for a holiday, I can still finish tomorrow's accepted order and add its photo, and I am told plainly why no new orders arrive.
- As the **founder**, I give a florist access by entering their email on their partner page and switching them to active; I see whether they have signed in before their first order; changing the email moves access at once.
- As a **buyer**, nothing I see changes, and nothing tells me how my order is made.
- As **Googlebot**, I find no link to `/vendor`, and any `/vendor` response I fetch says `noindex`.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

One migration, `NNNN_florist_access.sql` with `NNNN_florist_access.down.sql`, numbered after the last one merged (spec 011's `0020_florists_v0` is in flight).

| Change | Why | Rollback |
|---|---|---|
| `CREATE TRIGGER partner_member_audit AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE ON public.partner_member FOR EACH ROW EXECUTE FUNCTION public.audit_row_change('user_id', 'partner_id')` | granting or removing a florist's access to recipient data is an admin write and gets the audit every other admin write gets (spec 012 §2.3, "a trigger, not a convention"). Writes outside the admin context (the first-sign-in stamp, the florist's language switch) return early in the trigger and are not audited, as on every audited table | `DROP TRIGGER partner_member_audit ON public.partner_member` |

`partner_member` has no personal column, so `audit_mask_columns` gains no entry. Nothing else changes: `invited_at` and `accepted_at` exist since `0005`, and `locale_code` arrives with spec 011's `0020`. No change to `order`, `order_event`, `order_assignment`, `order_transition` or any other order table. No seed. The round-trip test (migrate, roll back, migrate) covers the pair.

### 5.2 API / server actions / jobs / events (zod schemas named)

Signatures only.

- **`src/config/florists.ts`:** `basePath: "/vendor"`; `signIn.production: ["active", "paused"]`. `floristSurfaceOpen()` is removed with its callers, because the surface is open in every environment.
- **`requireFloristContext(): Promise<FloristContext>`** (spec 011 §5.2): memberships filtered by `signInPolicy(env)` against `fulfillment_partner.status` read in the same request; an empty result redirects to sign-in. `FloristContextSchema` is unchanged (it already carries `partnerStatus`).
- **`canAcceptFor(orderMode: OrderMode, partnerStatus: PartnerStatus): boolean`** (pure, `src/modules/orders/`): `live` with `active`; `demo` and `test` with `demo`. The list and order views use it to show or hide Accept, and `respondToOffer` calls it again under its `FOR UPDATE` lock. A refused accept raises `ASSIGNMENT_PARTNER_NOT_ACCEPTING`, writes nothing, and renders spec 011's "This order has changed since you opened it." Decline is not gated.
- **`syncPartnerOwnerMember(tx, partnerId: string): Promise<SyncOwnerResult>`** (`src/modules/partners/`), where `tx` is the transaction of spec 012's `withAdminContext` and `SyncOwnerResultSchema = z.object({ userId: z.uuid(), created: z.boolean(), revokedUserId: z.uuid().nullable(), deletedUser: z.boolean() }).strict()`. Called by spec 012's `createPartner`, `updatePartner` (only when the contact email changed) and `convertApplication`, inside their admin transaction, with audit actions `partner.member.grant` and `partner.member.revoke`. The address is normalised by `FloristSignInEmailSchema` (`z.email().max(254)`, trimmed, lower-cased), which matches `users.email`'s lower-case check.
- **`floristLanguageFor(input: { applicationLanguage: FloristLanguage | null; countryIso2: string }): FloristLanguage`** (pure).
- **`recordFirstFloristSignIn(userId: string): Promise<void>`:** called from the florist instance's sign-in event under the system context; sets `accepted_at = now()` where it is null, on that user's memberships whose partner the policy allows.
- **`floristSignInStatus(adminCtx, partnerId): Promise<{ email: string; firstSignedInAt: string | null }>`** for the admin block, read under the admin context.

No new job, event type, route handler or environment key. No change to the sign-in core, the link design, the send limit or the `Mailer`.

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

- **Indexability:** no indexable page is added or changed. Every `/vendor` response (page, redirect, 404, auth endpoint) carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, and `robots.txt` keeps `Disallow: /vendor/` (`plan/02` §4.1 and §7: "noindex header + robots disallow"). The bare `/vendor` is not matched by that line. It is only a redirect to the disallowed sign-in page for a visitor without a session, it carries `noindex` itself, and no public page links to it, so it stays crawlable rather than gaining a second robots line.
- **Canonical, hreflang, schema:** none on florist routes, which have no locale prefix (`plan/02` §4.1).
- **URL pattern:** `/vendor/*`, the pattern `plan/02` §4.1 and `plan/05` #58 reserve; ASCII, no locale, no personal data (assignment ids are UUIDs).
- **Sitemap:** no `/vendor` URL in any sitemap (`plan/02` §10; asserted by AC-12).
- **Internal links:** none in from public pages (§13 Q5); out-links from florist pages unchanged (`plan/02` §11).
- **Thin content and crawl budget:** nothing new is discoverable. The old `/demo/vendor-inbox` never answered on production, so its 404 loses nothing.
- **CWV:** public pages ship the same bytes; the paused notice is server-rendered text on a private page.

## 7. i18n considerations (mandatory)

- **New message keys:** `floristInbox.paused.notice` in `en`, `pl` and `de` (`reviewed: false`; the florist surface is outside the indexable share, spec 003 §14 A17 through spec 011 §14). Admin keys `admin.partners.signIn.heading`, `.address`, `.notYet`, `.firstSignedIn` and `.help` in `messages/admin/en.json` (English only, spec 012 §2.2). No value carries a banned word on a public surface; the admin catalogue's exemption covers "florist" and "partner".
- **The florist's language:** `partner_member.locale_code`, defaulted by `floristLanguageFor` (§13 Q3) and changed only by the florist's switch; before sign-in, `hl` or `en`. Never `Accept-Language`, never an IP (ADR-0006). Spec 016's florist emails read the same column, so the first offer email arrives in the florist's language even if they have never signed in.
- **Formatting:** the admin date through `Intl` in the admin's `time_zone` (spec 012), labelled with the zone; florist dates and times unchanged (destination zone, `plan/03` §10).
- **RTL:** logical properties only; no new layout.
- **Review and `noindex` gating:** the `pl` and `de` paused notice are machine-drafted and join spec 011's florist strings in the next native review batch (`plan/03` §6); they render on no indexable page.

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Data flows (RoPA):** spec 011's rows "Florist sign-in" and "Recipient data shown to a florist" now run in production. Their environment and recipient cells are updated, and "Florist sign-in" names the source of the address (the partner's contact email, entered by the founder). No new data category and no new processor: Neon, Railway, Cloudflare and Resend are already listed.
- **Lawful basis and the processor gate:** the recipient's name, phone, address and card message reach a real florist only after acceptance and only until the photo (spec 011), and a florist can sign in only while `active` or `paused`. Spec 012's status graph refuses `onboarding` to `active` without `terms_signed_at`, the partner agreement whose Art. 28 processor clauses `plan/07` §1.1 requires. The lawyer's review of that agreement (`plan/07` §11 item 1) is still open; the founder records a signed agreement before activating (§12).
- **Minimisation:** one person per shop, by an address the founder controls; access ends on the next request after offboarding or an address change; a removed address's `users` row is deleted when nothing else needs it.
- **Audit:** every grant and removal is an `audit_log` row naming the admin who made it (AC-8).
- **Cookies and consent:** the three `__Secure-fo-florist.*` cookies become `set` in production with `Path=/vendor`. They stay strictly necessary, set only under `/vendor`, never on a public path, so no consent is asked. `src/config/cookies.ts` and `docs/compliance/cookie-register.md` are updated; the rows stay owned by spec 011 (the register's spec field takes three digits).
- **Resend:** its DPA is filed before the first real florist email (spec 012's founder prerequisite, unchanged).
- **Price display, consumer information, geo-blocking:** n/a. The florist surface is business to business, sells nothing to consumers and shows no consumer price.
- **Accessibility:** the paused notice is text, not colour, placed at the top of the list; the admin block has a heading and text states; axe at 390 and 1440 px.
- **Logs and PII:** no email address in any log, URL or Sentry event (`florist_member_granted` logs `partner_id` only); the audit row records user and partner ids, never the address.
- **Security:** status checked on every request; accept re-checked under the row lock; the same sign-in page for every address (no enumeration); spec 012's send limit; CSRF and cookie scope unchanged; a florist session still authenticates nothing under `/admin`.
- **`CLAUDE.md` rules, checked:** private routes server-rendered and `noindex`; no IP redirect; status only through `orderService.transition` (the accept guard writes nothing itself); no literal strings; zod at every boundary; a migration with its rollback; partner go-live stays a data flip in admin (opening the live surface is a one-time platform change, after which each florist goes live by `fulfillment_partner.status`); no PII in logs or URLs; artboards before `/plan-tasks`.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-11** In an integration run with `APP_ENV=production` and the capturing mailer, a member of an `active` partner requests a link, confirms it (two `GET`s, then a `POST`), opens a `live` order's offer (built with spec 002's `walkOrderTo`), accepts it (one `assignment.accepted` event, actor `partner:{partnerId}`), and, with the order then walked to `in_production`, marks it out for delivery and delivered with a photo through `InMemoryStorage`; the order's event types from the offer onwards equal the fixture sequence exactly; a member of a `demo` partner in the same run receives no email.
- **AC-12** For the home page, a corridor guide and a product page, in four locales, the response with a `__Secure-fo-florist.session-token` cookie for `Path=/vendor` equals the response without it (status, headers, body); a crawl of every `<a href>` on those pages finds no `/vendor` target; no sitemap contains a `/vendor` URL; `robots.txt` in the indexing environment still contains `Disallow: /vendor/`.
- **AC-13** After the deploy that ships this spec, a read-only smoke run against `https://flowersoverseas.com` finds `/vendor/sign-in` answering 200 with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` and a `Cache-Control` containing `no-store`, `/vendor` redirecting to `/vendor/sign-in`, and `/demo/vendor-inbox` answering 404.
- **AC-14** `src/config/cookies.ts` lists the three florist cookies as `set` with scope `Path=/vendor` and `pnpm cookies:check` passes; `docs/compliance/ropa.md` carries the updates of §8; the florist runbook has the sections "Give a florist access on the live site", "Pause, offboard or move access" and "First-order check"; spec 011 §14 and spec 012 §14 carry the pointer amendments of §12; `pnpm codebase:map --check` passes.

## 13. Open questions

Each has a recommended default, which this spec is written against; the founder's answer replaces the default.

1. **Which florists can sign in on the live site?** *Default:* **active and paused.** A paused shop (a holiday, or a quality pause) can still finish orders it already accepted and add the photo, but cannot accept new ones, and routing sends it none. Onboarding shops cannot sign in yet, because there is nothing to see before the agreement is signed; offboarded shops cannot sign in at all.
2. **Who signs in for a shop?** *Default:* **one person, with the contact email you enter on the shop's page in admin.** Changing that email moves access to the new address at once. More people per shop (staff) comes with the Phase 2 portal.
3. **Which language does a new florist start in?** *Default:* **the language they chose on their application;** otherwise Polish for a shop in Poland, German for Germany or Austria, English elsewhere. They can switch at any time, and their order emails follow.
4. **Do we email the florist when you switch them to active?** *Default:* **no automatic email.** You send them the address flowersoverseas.com/vendor in your own message, and every order email signs them in with one tap anyway.
5. **A "Florist sign-in" link on the public for-florists page?** *Default:* **not now.** It would change an indexed page for something florists reach from their emails; it can come with the Phase 2 portal.
6. **The old demo address `/demo/vendor-inbox`.** *Default:* **it stops working (not found), with no redirect.** It never worked on the live site, and staging sign-in links expire within an hour anyway.
