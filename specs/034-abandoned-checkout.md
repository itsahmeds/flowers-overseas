## 1. Problem

Spec 010's basket amendment keeps a buyer's basket on the server for 30 days, behind a hashed cookie. Most people who start an order for someone abroad stop at least once: they go to find the recipient's postcode, ask a sibling to split the cost, or get interrupted on the bus. If they come back in the same browser, the basket is there. If they do not, or they come back on another device, the order is lost, and with it a birthday or an anniversary that mattered to them. The founder approved on 2026-10-24 a single reminder email for that buyer, **only with consent**.

An unfinished-basket email is direct marketing in every market we sell to: the UK regulator's definition of direct marketing covers it, the German data protection authority of North Rhine-Westphalia treated such emails as advertising in its 2025 report, and Polish law since November 2024 requires prior consent for any commercial information by email (§8.2). So the email can only go to a buyer who asked for it, the proof of that request must be kept, stopping it must take one click and work at once, and it must never reach someone who has already bought. It also must not teach buyers that walking away earns a discount, must not invent urgency, must never show a delivery date that can no longer be met, and must never expose the recipient's details to whoever happens to read the email. `plan/07` §1.2 already chose "explicit consent everywhere to keep one rule"; this spec applies that rule to the first marketing email the service sends.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the AC.

**When a basket counts as abandoned**
- A pure function `isAbandoned(draft, now)` decides it: the draft is `live`, not placed, has at least one line, has a saved buyer email (step 2 saved), carries a basket-reminder consent, and its last activity was at least **2 hours** and at most **20 hours** ago (AC-1). Demo and test drafts never qualify.
- A pg-boss job `basket-reminders.scan` (every 10 minutes) creates at most one `basket_reminder` row per draft; a job `basket-reminders.send` sends it after re-checking everything under a row lock (AC-2 to AC-5).

**Who may be emailed (consent)**
- One **unticked** box on checkout step 2, directly under the email field, for guests and signed-in buyers alike: "If I do not finish my order, email me once with a link back to my basket." It is never required to continue (AC-7).
- Ticking it writes the consent onto the draft and one `consent_log` row whose subject is a keyed hash of the email (no plaintext address in the proof); unticking writes a withdrawal (AC-8). The 24-hour sweep of the draft's personal fields clears the consent with them (AC-9).
- No other basis makes a basket eligible: not `customer.marketing_consent`, not the UK soft opt-in (AC-10; §8.2, §13 Q1).

**The email**
- One React Email template, `R1 basket-reminder`, sent through spec 012's `Mailer` via Resend, in the draft's locale (`en`, `en-gb`, `de`, `pl`), HTML and plain text (AC-11).
- It lists the basket's available bouquets (name, size, destination country, delivery date), one "Open my basket" button, and a footer saying why it was sent, that it is the only one, and how to stop it. **No price, no discount, no countdown, no stock claim, no recipient detail, no buyer name** (AC-11, AC-12).
- **Delivery-date awareness**: a chosen date whose cutoff has passed, or passes within the next 60 minutes, is never shown as the delivery date; the email shows the earliest date we can now deliver instead. Lines that can no longer be delivered at all are left out; if none remain, nothing is sent (AC-6).
- No open tracking, no click tracking, no per-recipient image URL (AC-13). `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) on every message, plus a visible link (AC-14).
- **One email per basket**, and at most one basket email per address in any 7 days (AC-3, AC-5).

**The link back (no personal data and no basket contents in the URL)**
- The button carries an opaque 256-bit token in the path, stored only as its SHA-256, valid 7 days after sending (AC-15).
- Opened in the browser that still holds the basket, it simply opens the basket. Opened anywhere else, it creates a new basket holding **only the bouquets** (product, size, destination, date, add-ons) and none of the recipient, card or buyer details, so a mistyped email address never shows a stranger who the flowers were for (AC-16, §13 Q4).
- An expired or unknown link, or one whose basket was already bought, lands on a neutral page with a way back to the shop, identical in every case (AC-17). A restored basket follows the basket's own date and price rules (AC-18).

**Stopping it (one click, honoured at once)**
- `POST` one-click unsubscribe from the mail client, and a page with one button from the visible link. Both withdraw the consent, record it, and add the address's hash to a suppression list in the same request, so no pending reminder for that address can go out afterwards (AC-19, AC-20).
- Bounces and spam complaints reported by Resend suppress the address permanently for basket emails (AC-21).

**Suppression after purchase and recovery measurement**
- Placing the basket, or buying with the same email in another basket, stops a pending reminder (AC-3, AC-22).
- A purchase placed from the reminded basket, or from a basket restored from its link, within 7 days of sending is counted as **recovered** (AC-22). Nothing here changes an order's status (AC-23).

**Admin metrics**
- `/admin/basket-reminders`: sent, skipped by reason, links opened, recovered purchases, recovery rate, recovered revenue per currency (integer minor units, through `formatMoney`), unsubscribes, bounces, complaints (AC-24).
- An automatic pause: if complaints or bounces cross a threshold, the system turns the feature flag off and alerts the founder (AC-25).

**Data, retention, records**
- One migration with its rollback (§5.1); retention rows for tokens, reminder rows, suppression and consent proof (AC-26, AC-27).
- RoPA row, privacy-policy paragraph request, runbook (AC-29).

## 3. Non-goals

- **A second reminder.** The data model allows a second (`sequence` 2), but this spec sends one. A second needs the buyer's email kept beyond spec 010's 24-hour rule, so it is a founder decision (§13 Q2) and its own amendment.
- **Discounts or free delivery in the email.** None, by default and by construction (AC-12). Spec 047 (discount codes, founder's Idea F, not yet written) may later offer a code as a lever; that needs its own amendment here, never in a first email, because a code for walking away teaches buyers to walk away (§13 Q6).
- **A marketing preference centre, newsletters, general marketing consent** → spec 033. **CRM sync** of reminders → spec 032. **Win-back** → spec 036. **Occasion reminders** → spec 018 C.7.4 and spec 035.
- **Browse abandonment** (emailing someone who looked at a product but never started a basket). Never: there is no consent and no email to send to.
- **Reminding an order that was placed but not paid.** That is spec 013's 2-hour payment sweep; a placed basket has a `purchase` and is out of scope here (AC-1).
- **A buyer who leaves before step 2.** No email is known and no consent is given, so nothing is sent. Signed-in buyers who leave at step 1 are in the same position (§13 Q1 records the alternative of a standing account consent).
- **SMS, WhatsApp, push notifications.** Email only.
- **Quiet hours** (holding a send overnight in the buyer's time zone). The buyer's time zone is not known; the residence country is for invoicing only (ADR-0006). Recorded in §13 Q3.
- **Analytics events and UTM parameters** for the email → spec 023, consent-gated as every GA4 event is. This spec counts in first-party tables only.
- **Reporting-currency totals** of recovered revenue → spec 030b's converter, if it is approved. This spec reports per currency.
- **A/B tests of copy or timing.**
- **Editing `CLAUDE.md`, `plan/*` or an accepted ADR.** The ADR-0013 boundary is addressed by a proposed new ADR (§13 Q8).

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer in Leeds** who started an order for my grandmother in Gdańsk, ticked "email me once", and then got called away, I get one friendly email two hours later that takes me straight back to my basket, and I finish the order.
- As a **buyer whose chosen date has passed** by the time the email arrives, I am told the earliest date we can now deliver, not a date that cannot happen.
- As a **buyer who did not tick the box**, I never get this email.
- As a **buyer who bought on my laptop** after leaving my phone's basket, I do not get a reminder for something I already bought.
- As a **person whose address a buyer mistyped**, the one email I receive names a bouquet and a country, never who it is for, and the link shows me nothing about the recipient.
- As a **buyer who wants no more of these**, one click in my mail app stops them, and they stop at once.
- As a **Polish or German buyer**, the email reads in my language with dates like "niedz., 14 lut" or "So., 14. Feb.".
- As the **founder**, I see on one admin page how many reminders went out, how many baskets came back, and how much revenue they brought, and the system pauses itself if people start marking the email as spam.
- As **Googlebot**, I never see any of this: the link routes are blocked and `noindex`.
- As a **recipient**, I am never contacted: the email goes only to the buyer.

**B. `basket_reminder` (new).**

`basket_reminder(id uuid PK DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(), draft_id uuid NULL REFERENCES checkout_draft(id) ON DELETE SET NULL, sequence smallint NOT NULL DEFAULT 1 CHECK (sequence BETWEEN 1 AND 2), locale_code text NOT NULL REFERENCES locale(code), status text NOT NULL CHECK (status IN ('scheduled','sent','skipped','failed')), skip_reason text NULL CHECK (skip_reason IN ('purchased','purchased_elsewhere','consent_withdrawn','suppressed','recent_reminder','no_available_line','no_email','too_late','flag_off')), scheduled_for timestamptz NOT NULL, sent_at timestamptz NULL, line_count smallint NULL CHECK (line_count BETWEEN 1 AND 5), email_hmac text NULL CHECK (email_hmac ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$'), restore_token_sha256 text NULL UNIQUE, restore_expires_at timestamptz NULL, unsubscribe_token_sha256 text NULL UNIQUE, provider_message_id text NULL UNIQUE, delivery_status text NULL CHECK (delivery_status IN ('sent','delivery_delayed','delivered','bounced','complained','failed')), restore_count integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, first_restored_at timestamptz NULL, recovered_purchase_id uuid NULL UNIQUE REFERENCES purchase(id), recovered_at timestamptz NULL, created_at, updated_at, UNIQUE (draft_id, sequence))`, with:
- `CHECK ((status = 'skipped') = (skip_reason IS NOT NULL))`, `CHECK ((status = 'sent') = (sent_at IS NOT NULL))`, `CHECK ((restore_token_sha256 IS NULL) = (restore_expires_at IS NULL))`, `CHECK ((recovered_purchase_id IS NULL) = (recovered_at IS NULL))`, `CHECK (recovered_purchase_id IS NULL OR status = 'sent')`.
- Indexes on `(status, scheduled_for)` and `(email_hmac, sent_at)`.
- **No email column, no name, no money column.** Recovered revenue is read from `purchase.total_minor` and its currency by join, so `db:check`'s money-pair gate is untouched and the amount can never disagree with the purchase.
- `sequence` exists so a second reminder (§13 Q2) is a config change plus an amendment, not a migration. `BASKET_REMINDER_MAX = 1` in config; a test pins it.
- RLS on: `system` (all), `admin` (select). No `customer`, `partner` or `public` policy. Added to `db/rls.ts`.

**C. `email_suppression` (new).** `email_suppression(email_hmac text NOT NULL CHECK (email_hmac ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$'), scope text NOT NULL CHECK (scope IN ('basket_reminder')), reason text NOT NULL CHECK (reason IN ('unsubscribed','bounced','complained')), created_at, updated_at, PRIMARY KEY (email_hmac, scope))`. An upsert keeps the strongest reason (`complained` over `bounced` over `unsubscribed`). RLS: `system` only. One scope today; spec 033 may add others.

**D. `consent_log` (spec 002, migration `0006`) gains the `contact` subject.**
- `ADD COLUMN subject_hmac text NULL` with an index.
- `consent_log_subject_kind_check` becomes `subject_kind IN ('visitor','customer','recipient','contact')`.
- `consent_log_subject_check` becomes: `visitor` → `anonymous_id` set, `subject_ref` and `subject_hmac` null; `contact` → `subject_hmac ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$'`, `subject_ref` and `anonymous_id` null; otherwise `subject_ref` set and `subject_hmac` null.
- A `contact` subject is an email address known only as `HMAC-SHA256(CONTACT_HMAC_SECRET, normalised email)`. That lets us prove, when someone asks or complains, that this address gave this consent at this time, without keeping a readable address for five years.
- `src/db/schema/customers.ts` `consentSubjectKinds` gains `contact`; `ropa.md` row 3 is updated (§8).

**E. Seeded rows.**
- `retention_policy`: `basket_reminder_tokens` (**clear** `restore_token_sha256`, `restore_expires_at`, `unsubscribe_token_sha256` and `email_hmac` **60 days** after `sent_at`, or after `created_at` when never sent; 60 days keeps unsubscribe and bounce handling working well past the 30 days mailbox providers expect); `basket_reminder` (**delete** 395 days after `created_at`, so a year of metrics plus a month is kept); `email_suppression` (**delete** 5 years after `updated_at`, matching consent proof). The existing `consent_records` row (1 825 days) covers the `contact` rows.
- `feature_flag` `marketing.basket_reminder` (global, default **off**).

**Rollback.** The down file, in reverse: deletes the seeded rows; drops `email_suppression`; restores `consent_log`'s two checks and drops `subject_hmac` (it first **refuses with `BASKET_REMINDER_CONSENT_ROWS_EXIST`** if any `contact` row exists, because those are records kept under a legal obligation; after the first real consent this is a forward fix, never a rollback); drops `checkout_draft.restored_from_reminder_id`; drops `basket_reminder`; restores the `pending_notice` check (setting new values to `NULL`); drops the two consent columns. No order, purchase or line is touched by either file.

**Pure functions (signatures only)**
- `isAbandoned(draft: DraftEligibilityView, now: Date): boolean`: true exactly when: `mode = 'live'`; `purchase_id IS NULL`; at least one line; `buyer_saved` with an email in `buyer`; `reminder_consent_at` set; `now - last_activity_at >= ABANDON_AFTER` and `<= SEND_LATEST`. The upper bound keeps a reminder from arriving the next day and keeps the send well inside the 24-hour life of the buyer's details.
- `reminderLineStates(lines: DraftLine[], now: Date, window: DeliveryWindowFn): ReminderLine[]`: for each line, using spec 009's `deliveryWindow()` evaluated at `now + CUTOFF_MARGIN` in the destination's zone: `kept` (date set and still deliverable), `moved` (date set and no longer deliverable; carries `nextDate`, the first deliverable date), `open` (no date chosen), `unavailable` (product retired, tier unpriced, destination's mode not `live`, or no deliverable date in the window). The margin exists because an email is read some time after it is sent; it is never shown to the buyer as a deadline.
- `contactHmac(email)`: HMAC-SHA256 with `CONTACT_HMAC_SECRET` over the address normalised as spec 010 normalises it (trim, lowercase), lowercase hex.

**Consent at step 2 (a requirement on spec 010's step 2, §12)**
- `CardAndBuyerStepSchema` gains `basketReminder: z.literal('on').optional()` (the checkbox's form value; absent means not ticked). Rendered only when `marketing.basket_reminder` is on **and** every `checkout.basketReminder.*` key is `reviewed` in the request's locale.
- `recordBasketReminderConsent(tx, { draftId, given: boolean, email, locale, wordingVersion, now })`: when the posted state differs from the draft's: sets or clears `reminder_consent_at` and `reminder_consent_version`, and appends one `consent_log` row: `subject_kind 'contact'`, `subject_hmac = contactHmac(email)`, `choices = { basket_reminder: given, wording_key: 'checkout.basketReminder.label', wording_hash: <source_hash of that key in that locale>, locale }`, `policy_version` = the privacy notice version in force, `source_ip_truncated` null (as `ropa.md` row 3 requires). Same state: no row. A change of email at step 2 with the box still ticked writes a withdrawal for the old hash and a grant for the new one.
- Spec 010's 24-hour personal-field sweep (`checkout_draft_personal`) also clears `reminder_consent_at` and `reminder_consent_version`, because the consent belongs to an email the draft no longer holds.

**Jobs (spec 002's pg-boss layer; on Railway's single replica they run through the minute tick, spec 040)**
- `basket-reminders.scan` (cron `SCAN_CRON`): if the flag is off or either env var is missing (production and staging), logs `basket_reminder.scan_refused {reason}` and stops. Otherwise selects drafts where `isAbandoned` holds and no `basket_reminder` exists for `(draft_id, 1)`, inserts `status 'scheduled'`, `scheduled_for = now`, `locale_code = draft.locale_code` (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), and enqueues `basket-reminders.send { reminderId }` with `singletonKey = reminderId`. Then runs `autoPauseCheck(now)`.
- `basket-reminders.send { reminderId }` → `sendBasketReminder(reminderId, now)`, in one transaction: lock the reminder `FOR UPDATE` and its draft `FOR UPDATE`; return if the reminder is not `scheduled`. Then the checks, in this order, each ending in `skipped` with its reason and **no email**:
  1. flag off → `flag_off`;
  2. draft deleted, or `purchase_id` set → `purchased`;
  3. no email in `buyer` → `no_email`;
  4. consent cleared → `consent_withdrawn`;
  5. `now - last_activity_at > SEND_LATEST` → `too_late`;
  6. `email_suppression` holds `contactHmac(email)` for `basket_reminder` with reason `complained` or `bounced`, or with `unsubscribed` and an `updated_at` later than the draft's `reminder_consent_at` → `suppressed`;
  7. a `live` order exists whose `customer.email_normalised` equals the draft's email and whose purchase was created after the draft's `created_at` → `purchased_elsewhere`;
  8. another `basket_reminder` with the same `email_hmac` was sent within `PER_ADDRESS_GAP` → `recent_reminder`;
  9. `reminderLineStates` yields no line other than `unavailable` → `no_available_line`.
  If `now - last_activity_at < ABANDON_AFTER` (the buyer came back since it was scheduled), the row is **deleted** and nothing is sent; the next scan reconsiders the draft two hours after its new last activity. Otherwise: mint the restore and unsubscribe tokens (`crypto.getRandomValues`, 32 bytes, base64url), store their SHA-256 and `restore_expires_at = now + RESTORE_TTL`, set `email_hmac` and `line_count`, render R1 in `locale_code`, call `Mailer.send` with `tag 'basket_reminder'`, `stream 'marketing'`, the two `List-Unsubscribe` headers and `idempotencyKey = basket_reminder:{id}`, then set `status 'sent'`, `sent_at`, `provider_message_id`, `delivery_status 'sent'`. Resend's `409 invalid_idempotent_request` is treated as sent with no `provider_message_id`, as spec 016 does. Any other send error throws; after pg-boss's 3 retries the row becomes `failed` with a Sentry event. The draft is never modified by a send.
- `autoPauseCheck(now)`: over reminders sent in the last `AUTO_PAUSE.windowDays`: when at least `minSends` were sent and `complained / sent > complaintRate` or `bounced / sent > bounceRate`, sets `marketing.basket_reminder` off through spec 012's flag service with actor `system:basket_reminders.auto_pause` (so the flag's audit history shows it) and raises Sentry `alert=basket_reminders.paused`. It never turns the flag back on.

**Rendering contract of R1.** `BasketReminderEmailPropsSchema` (`.strict()`): `{ locale, lines: Array<{ productName, tierLabel, countryName, imageUrl, imageAlt, date: { kind: 'kept', isoDate } | { kind: 'moved', nextIsoDate } | { kind: 'open' } }> (1 to 5), restoreUrl, unsubscribeUrl, helpUrl, privacyUrl, company: CompanyIdentity }`. It has **no field for a price, an amount, a currency, a time remaining, a cutoff time, a stock count, a discount or a person's name**, so the template cannot render one (AC-12). `imageUrl` is the product's public `thumb` media URL, identical for every recipient. `restoreUrl` is `https://{host}/{locale}/basket/restore/{restoreToken}`; `unsubscribeUrl` is `https://{host}/{locale}/basket/unsubscribe/{unsubscribeToken}`. Dates are formatted inside the template through spec 003's `formatDate` with weekday and month name.

**Restore.** `GET /{locale}/basket/restore/{token}` (route handler, `no-store`; `ReminderTokenSchema = z.string().regex(/^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{43}$/)`) → `restoreBasket({ token, locale, cookieToken, now })`:
- No reminder with that hash, `restore_expires_at` passed, source draft deleted, or source draft placed → **303 to `/{locale}/basket/link-ended`**, the same response in every case, and nothing written.
- The browser's `fo_checkout` cookie names the source draft, unplaced → set `first_restored_at` if null, increment `restore_count`, set `pending_notice 'restored'`, **303 to `/{locale}/basket`**. Nothing else changes.
- The browser's cookie names a draft already restored from this reminder (`restored_from_reminder_id`), unplaced → 303 to `/{locale}/basket`, nothing written (a second click in the same browser makes no second basket).
- Otherwise → create a new draft whose lines copy the source lines' `destination_country_id`, `product_id`, `tier_key`, `delivery_date`, `addon_keys`, `last_seen_total_minor` and `last_seen_currency_code` (amounts the buyer saw, so spec 010's price-changed notice still works; fresh `position`s; no `quote`, `recipient`, `card` or `order_id`), with `currency_code` copied from the source draft and `locale_code` from the request, `buyer` null, no consent, `restored_from_reminder_id` set and `pending_notice 'restored_details'`; set `fo_checkout` and `fo_basket` as spec 010's start route does; set `first_restored_at` if null and increment `restore_count`; **303 to `/{locale}/basket`**. A browser that held another unplaced basket keeps it on the server, but its cookie now names the restored basket (§13 Q4 records this).
- The response sets `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` in addition to the site's headers, so the token never leaves in a `Referer`.

**Unsubscribe**
- `POST /api/basket-reminders/unsubscribe/{token}` (RFC 8058; `OneClickUnsubscribeBodySchema = z.object({ 'List-Unsubscribe': z.literal('One-Click') })` over `application/x-www-form-urlencoded`; no cookie, session or CSRF token needed, no Origin check because the mail provider posts it) → `unsubscribeBasketReminders({ token, channel: 'header', now })`; a `GET` answers 405.
- `/{locale}/basket/unsubscribe/{token}`: `GET` renders one button and changes nothing (link scanners in corporate mail systems fetch links, so a `GET` that unsubscribed would stop emails nobody asked to stop). The button posts a server action → `unsubscribeBasketReminders({ token, channel: 'page', now })`, then the done state.
- `unsubscribeBasketReminders` finds the reminder by token hash. If found: upserts `email_suppression (email_hmac, 'basket_reminder', 'unsubscribed')`; appends `consent_log` (`contact`, `{ basket_reminder: false, channel }`); clears the consent on the source draft if it still exists. All in one transaction, before the response. Any reminder still `scheduled` for a draft with the same email then ends `skipped 'suppressed'` at its send (check 6), because the suppression is newer than that draft's consent. If not found (unknown, or tokens already cleared at 60 days): writes nothing. **Both cases return the same 200 body**, so the endpoint reveals nothing.
- A later fresh tick of the box by the same address is a new, specific consent (a newer `consent_log` grant), and check 6 of the send lets it through; a `complained` or `bounced` suppression is never overridden.

**Resend webhooks (a requirement on spec 016, §12).** The existing processor, after verifying and storing the event as spec 016 does, also looks up `basket_reminder.provider_message_id`; when found, it applies the same status precedence to `delivery_status`, and on `email.bounced` (hard bounce) or `email.complained` upserts `email_suppression` with that reason from the row's `email_hmac`. `email.opened` and `email.clicked` stay ignored.

**Placement (a requirement on spec 010's placing task, §12).** Inside `placeOrderAction`'s transaction, after `placePurchase`:
- `cancelPendingForDraft(tx, draftId)` marks a `scheduled` reminder of the draft `skipped 'purchased'`.
- `attributeRecovery(tx, { draftId, purchaseId, mode, now })`, for `mode = 'live'` only: the reminder is the draft's own `sent` reminder, else the one named by `restored_from_reminder_id`; when it exists, has `sent_at >= now - ATTRIBUTION_WINDOW` and no `recovered_purchase_id`, it sets `recovered_purchase_id` and `recovered_at`. A purchase is attributed to at most one reminder (`UNIQUE`). It reads and writes only `basket_reminder`; it never touches an order, a status or an event.

**`Mailer` (a requirement on spec 012's core, §12, shared with spec 018 C.18).** `mailTags` gains `basket_reminder`. `MailMessageSchema` gains `headers?: { 'List-Unsubscribe': string; 'List-Unsubscribe-Post': 'List-Unsubscribe=One-Click' }` (no other header admitted), `stream?: 'transactional' | 'marketing'` (default `transactional`; `marketing` sends from `MARKETING_MAIL_FROM`) and `idempotencyKey?: string` (spec 016's request). The Resend request carries no tracking options.

**Env (`lib/env.ts`, `.env.example`).** `CONTACT_HMAC_SECRET` (base64, at least 32 bytes decoded) and `MARKETING_MAIL_FROM` (an RFC 5322 mailbox on a verified Resend domain, §13 Q7). Optional in development and test (a fixed test secret and the capturing mailer); in staging and production, absent values make the scan refuse (AC-28). The runbook records that the HMAC secret is never rotated except on compromise, and how to keep the previous one for lookups if it is.

**Admin.** `basketReminderMetrics(range: MetricsRange)` with `MetricsRangeSchema = z.enum(['7d','30d','90d'])`, returning `BasketReminderMetricsSchema`: `{ sent, skipped: Record<SkipReason, number>, failed, linksOpened, recoveredPurchases, recoveryRateBp, recoveredRevenue: Array<{ currency, totalMinor }>, unsubscribes, bounces, complaints }`, all integers; `recoveryRateBp = floor(recoveredPurchases * 10000 / sent)` (0 when nothing was sent). Revenue sums `purchase.total_minor` of recovered `live` purchases grouped by `purchase.buyer_currency_code`; never summed across currencies, never a float. Labelled "before refunds".

**Events.** None in `order_event` or the outbox. Log lines and Sentry only (§11).

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

- **Indexability.** Nothing here is indexable. Every new route answers `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, the matching robots meta on HTML, and `Cache-Control: no-store`; none has a canonical, an hreflang alternate or JSON-LD (AC-31).
- **URL pattern.** All buyer-facing routes sit under `/{locale}/basket/…` (`restore/{token}`, `link-ended`, `unsubscribe/{token}`), the English word in every locale as spec 010 §13 Q10 ruled for checkout. `plan/02` §4.1 ("cart: noindex + robots disallow") and spec 010's basket amendment already disallow `/*/basket` in `robots.txt`, a prefix rule that covers these paths; the API route sits under `/api/`, already disallowed. No localised slug, no query string.
- **Sitemaps.** None of these URLs is in any sitemap (validator in AC-31).
- **Internal links.** No indexable page links to these routes; they are reached only from an email or a redirect. So no crawl budget is spent and no signal flows to them. The tokens are unguessable, so the routes cannot be enumerated.
- **Thin content.** Not applicable: the pages are utility pages, blocked and `noindex`.
- **Cloudflare.** Spec 040's cache-bypass rule must cover `/basket` and `/*/basket` as a prefix (spec 010's basket amendment item 13 requests it); AC-31 checks that the restore, link-ended and unsubscribe paths answer `cf-cache-status: DYNAMIC` or `BYPASS` on staging, so a token response is never cached at the edge.
- **CWV.** No change to any indexable page. The step 2 box is server-rendered HTML with no script. Lighthouse budgets are unaffected; the existing checkout client-JS budget (spec 010 AC-38) is unchanged.
- **Email links.** Absolute `https://flowersoverseas.com/{locale}/…` URLs with no tracking parameters; mail-client link scanners that follow them land on `noindex`, robots-blocked pages.

### 8.2 Lawful basis and consent gating: the rules, and why one rule

| Market | Rule for an unfinished-basket email | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| EU, all | ePrivacy Directive Art. 13(1): electronic mail for direct marketing needs prior consent; Art. 13(2) allows the existing-customer exception only for details obtained "in the context of the sale of a product". An unfinished basket is not a sale | Consent |
| Germany | § 7(2) UWG: email advertising needs prior express consent; § 7(3) UWG (existing customers) needs the address obtained in connection with a sale. The Wettbewerbszentrale obtained cease-and-desist undertakings against basket-reminder emails sent without consent (2015), and the LDI NRW's report for 2025 treated such reminders as advertising, not service mail, and found neither Art. 6(1)(b) nor (f) GDPR available after a cancelled checkout ([IT-Recht Kanzlei, 22.07.2026](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/warenkorberinnerungen-per-email-unzulaessig.html); [mobiflip, 2015](https://www.mobiflip.de/e-commerce-warenkorb-erinnerungen-wettbewerbszentrale/); [datenschutz-notizen](https://www.datenschutz-notizen.de/die-warenkorb-abbrecher-e-mail-3730510/)) | Consent |
| Poland | Art. 398 of the Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej, in force 10 November 2024): commercial information and direct marketing by electronic means need the end user's prior consent ([prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/zgody-marketingowe-i-kontakty-handlowe-co-mowi-prawo-komunikacji-elektronicznej,530981.html); [iFirma](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/prawo-komunikacji-elektronicznej-juz-opublikowane-nowe-zasady-zgod-marketingowych-w-2024-roku/)) | Consent |
| UK | PECR reg. 22: consent, or the soft opt-in of reg. 22(3) (details obtained "in the course of the sale or negotiations for the sale", only our own similar products, **and a simple opt-out offered when the details were collected** and in every message). The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 raises PECR penalties to UK GDPR levels ([Stephenson Harwood](https://www.stephensonharwood.com/media/xellm2dd/the-data-use-and-access-act-2025-july-2025.pdf); most remaining provisions commenced 5 February 2026 per [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/02/09/uk-data-reform-becomes-reality-what-this-means-for-you-and-what-you-need-to-do-n-102mh5x); ⚖️ the exact commencement of the penalty uplift is for the lawyer to confirm) | Consent (the soft opt-in would still need a checkout control for the opt-out, so the control may as well be an opt-in that is valid everywhere) |
| France (future locale) | CPCE L.34-5: prior consent; CNIL commentary treats a basket reminder that urges purchase as prospecting. CNIL recommendation 2026-042 (published 14 April 2026) requires consent for tracking pixels in marketing email ([BCLP](https://www.bclplaw.com/fr/events-insights-news/tracking-pixels-in-emails.html); [donneespersonnelles.fr](https://donneespersonnelles.fr/panier-abandonne-email-consentement)) | Consent; no pixels at all (AC-13) |

**Why one rule.** `plan/07` §1.2 already chose explicit consent everywhere. The soft opt-in is UK-only, would need a different checkout per buyer country (and deciding a buyer's regime from their location edges toward ADR-0006's territory), still needs a control at collection, and is contested for unfinished sales. So: **one unticked, specific, unbundled box, valid in every market**, and no other basis (AC-10, §13 Q1). Consent is specific (it names this one email), informed (the hint says when, how many and how to stop), freely given (never required, no effect on the price or the order), unambiguous (an affirmative tick), and as easy to withdraw as to give (one click in the mail client). ⚖️ The lawyer is asked to read the box wording and the German position before the flag turns on for `de` (recommended, §13 Q11).

**Proof (Art. 7(1)).** `consent_log` keeps, per grant and withdrawal: the keyed hash of the address, the time, the wording key with its `source_hash` and locale (so the exact sentence shown can be reproduced), and the privacy notice version. No IP, no user agent.

### 8.3 Price display and consumer information

- The email shows **no price, amount or currency** (AC-11, AC-12). It is therefore not an invitation to purchase under UCPD Art. 7(4), and it cannot show a price that differs from the price charged. Its one money sentence is true for every basket: "Your basket shows the total, with VAT and delivery included, before you pay."
- The basket the link opens re-prices every line and shows spec 010's price-changed notice when anything moved (spec 010 AC-61); the charge is the total shown at step 3, as always.
- **No pressure tactics** (`plan/07` §4 "No fake urgency/scarcity"; UK DMCC Act 2024; UCPD Annex I point 7): no countdown, no "only a few left", no "prices may rise", no "last chance", no deadline. The props schema cannot carry a time remaining, a stock count or a discount (AC-12). The cutoff margin is internal and never shown.
- **Positive framing** (founder, 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-24): "Your basket is saved", "pick up where you left off". No guilt ("Did you forget?"), no loss framing.
- **No relay language** (spec 041 §14 A-founder-2026-10-14): the email and pages never mention a florist accepting, a partner, an offer or anyone else being paid; `relayMatches` finds nothing (AC-11).
- **Sender identity.** The footer carries the trader identity from `company.ts` (whatever ADR-0021's company amendment sets) and the privacy link, as every buyer email does (spec 016 layout).

Eligibility, timing and content depend only on the draft and the buyer's own choice. No branch reads the residence country, `Accept-Language`, an IP or a geo header (AC-30). A UK and a Polish buyer who tick the box get the same treatment.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

*When a basket counts as abandoned*
- **AC-1** `isAbandoned` returns true for a fixture draft that is `live`, unplaced, has one line, a saved email and consent, with last activity exactly 2 h 00 min and exactly 20 h 00 min ago; and false when any single condition is changed: last activity 1 h 59 min or 20 h 01 min ago, `mode` `demo` or `test`, `purchase_id` set, zero lines, no saved email, no consent.
- **AC-2** One run of `basket-reminders.scan` over a fixture of eligible and ineligible drafts inserts exactly one `scheduled` `basket_reminder` per eligible draft and none for the others; a second run inserts nothing; with `marketing.basket_reminder` off it inserts nothing and logs `basket_reminder.scan_refused`.
- **AC-3** `sendBasketReminder` on a `scheduled` reminder ends `skipped` with the expected reason and calls `Mailer.send` zero times for each of: flag off (`flag_off`); draft placed or deleted (`purchased`); no email (`no_email`); consent cleared (`consent_withdrawn`); last activity more than 20 h ago (`too_late`); the address suppressed as `complained`, as `bounced`, or as `unsubscribed` after the draft's consent (`suppressed`); a `live` purchase by the same customer email created after the draft (`purchased_elsewhere`); a reminder sent to the same address 6 days 23 h ago (`recent_reminder`); every line unavailable (`no_available_line`). With an `unsubscribed` suppression older than the draft's consent it sends. With the buyer active again 30 minutes before the send, the row is deleted and nothing is sent. Otherwise it calls `Mailer.send` exactly once and ends `sent` with `sent_at`, `line_count`, `email_hmac`, both token hashes and `restore_expires_at = sent_at + 7 days`.
- **AC-4** Two concurrent `basket-reminders.send` jobs for one reminder produce exactly one `Mailer.send` call; the call carries `idempotencyKey = basket_reminder:{id}`; a Resend `409 invalid_idempotent_request` leaves the row `sent` with a null `provider_message_id` and no retry; any other error leaves it `scheduled` for retry and, after the last retry, `failed` with one Sentry event.
- **AC-5** After a draft's reminder is `sent`, further activity and a further 2 h of inactivity produce no second `basket_reminder` for that draft; `BASKET_REMINDER_MAX` equals 1, pinned by a test.

*Delivery-date awareness*
- **AC-6** With an injected clock and spec 009's `deliveryWindow` over a Warsaw fixture: a line whose date is still deliverable at `now + 60 min` renders that date; a line whose cutoff is 59 minutes after `now`, or already passed, renders "The earliest delivery date is now {next}" with `next` equal to `deliveryWindow`'s first deliverable date, and its original date appears nowhere in the HTML or text; a line with no date renders the "choose a date" sentence; a line whose product is retired, tier unpriced or country not `live` is absent; with every line absent the reminder is `skipped 'no_available_line'`. The draft's lines are byte-equal before and after the send. The same holds across the last Sunday of March and of October in `Europe/Warsaw`.

*Consent*
- **AC-7** With the flag on and the locale's `checkout.basketReminder.*` keys reviewed, step 2's server HTML contains one `<input type="checkbox" name="basketReminder">` without `checked` and with a `<label>` and an `aria-describedby` hint, for a guest and for a signed-in buyer; it renders `checked` only when re-rendering the buyer's own ticked submission after a validation error or when the draft already holds their consent; step 2 saves and advances with the box unticked; with the flag off, or with any of those keys unreviewed in the request's locale, the box is absent. Works with JavaScript disabled.
- **AC-8** Saving step 2 with the box ticked sets `reminder_consent_at` and `reminder_consent_version` and appends one `consent_log` row with `subject_kind 'contact'`, `subject_hmac` equal to `contactHmac` of the email, `subject_ref` and `anonymous_id` null, `choices` holding `basket_reminder: true`, the wording key, its `source_hash` and the locale, and `source_ip_truncated` null; no `consent_log` column contains the plaintext address. Saving again with the same state appends nothing. Saving unticked clears both draft columns and appends one row with `basket_reminder: false`. Changing the email with the box ticked appends a withdrawal for the old hash and a grant for the new one. A raw insert of a `contact` row with a null or malformed `subject_hmac`, or with `subject_ref` set, raises the check violation.

## 13. Open questions

The spec stays `proposed` until the founder answers; each question has the default this spec is written against. A one-word "ok" per default is enough.

- **Q1. Who may receive it?** Default: **only buyers who tick the box on this basket** (one rule for every country, as `plan/07` already chose). Alternatives: (a) also UK buyers under the soft opt-in (needs an opt-out control at checkout anyway, contested for unfinished baskets, two rule sets); (b) also signed-in buyers who switched on a standing "basket reminders" setting in their account (reaches people who leave at step 1; one more setting in spec 018).
- **Q2. One email or two?** Default: **one**, about 2 hours after the buyer last did something, and the footer says it is the only one. Alternative: a second about 24 hours later (`plan/11` lists "2 h, 24 h"), which means keeping the buyer's email for 48 hours instead of 24 and rewording the box ("up to two emails").
- **Q3. When?** Default: **2 hours** after the last activity, never later than 20 hours, no overnight hold. Alternatives: 1 hour (more recoveries, more "I was just about to" annoyance) or 4 hours; or hold overnight sends until 08:00 in the buyer's language's main time zone.
- **Q4. What does the link bring back on another phone or computer?** Default: **the bouquets, dates and add-ons only**; the buyer types the recipient and their own details again, so a mistyped email address never shows a stranger who the flowers are for. In the original browser everything is still there. Alternative: bring back everything (fewer steps, but whoever opens the email sees the recipient's name, address, phone and card message).
- **Q5. Prices in the email?** Default: **no prices**; the basket shows the current total when they return. Alternative: show each bouquet's current all-in price, which must then be re-priced at send time and can still differ from what they see later.
- **Q6. A discount in the email?** Default: **never in this email**. If wanted later, spec 047's codes could be offered in a separate, later amendment, and never in a first reminder, because it teaches buyers to leave baskets.
- **Q7. Which address does it come from?** Default: **a separate sending subdomain** (for example `Flowers Overseas <hello@mail.flowersoverseas.com>`), so a spam complaint about a marketing email cannot hurt delivery of order confirmations. The founder adds three DNS records in Cloudflare and verifies the domain in Resend. Alternative: the same address as order emails.
- **Q8. Resend or the CRM?** ADR-0013 says marketing email lives in the CRM (spec 032, Phase 3). Default: **write a new ADR** that lets this one consented email go through Resend from the app until the CRM exists, on its own subdomain, and moves it when spec 032 lands. Alternative: wait for the CRM.
- **Q9. Automatic pause.** Default: **pause by itself** when, over the last 7 days with at least 50 sent, more than 0.3% are marked as spam (Gmail's limit) or more than 4% bounce; the founder turns it back on. Alternative: alert only.
- **Q10. The checkout rule "no marketing opt-in in checkout".** Spec 010 AC-12 and spec 018's R4 forbid it. Default: **allow exactly this one box** (both specs get a one-line exception). Alternative: put the box on the basket page instead (fewer people see it; no email is known there for guests, so it would need its own email field).
- **Q11. Lawyer.** Default: **ask the lawyer to read the box wording and the German position before turning it on for `de`; not blocking for `en`, `en-gb` and `pl`**, where the rule (prior consent) is clear. Alternative: lawyer before any locale.
- **Q12. Who sees the admin page?** Default: **`admin` role only** (revenue figures included). Alternative: also `ops` without the revenue column, once spec 030b's permission table is approved.
- **Q13. How often may one address get a basket email?** Default: **at most once in 7 days**, whatever the number of baskets. Alternative: once per basket.
