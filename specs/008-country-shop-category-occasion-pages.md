## 1. Problem

Spec 007 makes the corridor page the first thing production is allowed to index and ends, deliberately, at a slot: "the **shop entry** — the handoff to spec 008's country shop root and categories […] the product grid itself is 008's" (007 §2, §3). Spec 005 shipped 84 products, their tiers, their per-country all-in prices, `fromPrice`, `countProductsIn`, `countProductsFor` and `priceProjection` and renders **none** of them ("005 ships view models and no JSX", 005 §3). Spec 006 shipped the copy, the alt text, the provenance labels, `resolveLoader()` and a `Photo` that can finally show a photograph. Four specs have now produced a catalogue, a price for it, a picture of it and a page that links to it — and there is still no page in the repository on which a buyer can see a bouquet. `plan/09` reserves 29 Sep–5 Oct for "`008` country shop, category, occasion pages (ISR, noindex for demo)", the founder's 2026-09-14 ruling puts the browsable site first, and the 16 October demo is a shop that cannot currently be shown.

The failure mode this spec must avoid is the one `plan/02` §1 catalogues from the competitor study, and it is *sharper* here than on the corridor page. A corridor page is expensive to fabricate — it needs 600 words about a country. A **listing** page is free to fabricate: one template, one query, and FloraQueen's 12,610 programmatic collections or GBO's ~240k product×country pages appear in an afternoon. 23 categories × 7 destinations × 4 locales × an occasion set is 2,000+ URLs that a loop would emit tonight, most of them showing the same twelve bouquets under a different heading. So the design budget of this spec goes almost entirely into the three rules that decide **which listings exist at all** (a data-driven existence rule with a product-count floor, §2), **what a listing may claim** (a price that equals the price charged, a ranking we can describe truthfully, no rating, no badge, no "bestseller" we cannot evidence, §8), and **what a parameterised URL is** (sorted and filtered views are `noindex` and canonical to the base, never a second page, §6).

Two constraints bound the shape. **Nothing is purchasable**: spec 010 owns the basket and checkout, so a Phase 0 listing is a browsable catalogue with honest prices and no add-to-basket — which is exactly why ADR-0007 keeps the whole page type out of the index for the demo. And **nothing in Phase 0 is operationally live**: 007's `ActivePartnersProvider` returns `false` for every country, so no shop page may render a cutoff, a delivery date or a same-day badge, even though `plan/04` §6's card anatomy asks for one. The card that ships is the card we can stand behind.

## 2. Scope

Each bullet is testable; §9 names the AC. Nothing here requires a database, a partner, a payment provider or an image we do not have. This spec **consumes** spec 007's `modules/seo` and `modules/geo` surfaces and spec 005's `modules/catalog` read API; it re-specifies neither.

**Six page types, their URLs and their existence rules (`plan/02` §4.1, §6; `plan/05` rows 6, 7, 8, 10, 13, 14)**

All segments come from `locales.data.ts`'s already-authored `pathSegments` (`shopCategory` = `flowers` / `blumen` / `kwiaty`; `occasions` = `occasions` / `anlaesse` / `okazje`; `product` = `product` / `produkt`), all country slugs from `countries.ts`, all category and occasion slugs from spec 006's `copy/{locale}/*.json`, and every URL is composed only by `localePath()` — the single builder spec 003 §6 established and 007 kept.

| # | Page type | URL (`en-gb` example) | Exists iff |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Country shop root | `/en-gb/poland/flowers` | country **published** (`status === 'live' \|\| guidePublished`) and ≥1 product deliverable there (active product × active `country_price` row) and the locale is a launch locale |
| 7 | Country category | `/en-gb/poland/flowers/roses` | the shop root exists **and** `countProductsIn(category, iso2) ≥ N` (§13 Q7, default **6**) **and** the category has an authored slug in that locale |
| 8 | Country occasion | `/en-gb/poland/occasions/womens-day` | the shop root exists **and** `observed === true` for that (occasion, country) **and** `countProductsFor(occasion, iso2) ≥ N` **and** an authored slug |
| 10 | Category hub (no destination) | `/en-gb/flowers/roses` | the category has ≥1 product in ≥1 published country **and** an authored slug **and** an authored intro in that locale |
| 13 | Occasion hub (no destination) | `/en-gb/occasions/mothers-day` | the occasion is observed in ≥1 published country **and** an authored slug **and** an authored intro in that locale |
| 14 | Occasions index | `/en-gb/occasions` | ≥1 occasion hub exists in that locale |

- **The existence rule is structural, not a runtime branch.** `generateStaticParams` emits exactly the sets above and `dynamicParams = false`, so every other slug, every wrong segment, every casing variant and every category that falls below the floor is a hard 404 — `plan/02` §6's "pages that fail the rule are **not created** (404), not `noindex`" holds by construction, and the `plan/02` §4.1 "≥6 products" condition becomes an *existence* condition rather than an indexability condition, which is the only version of it that cannot produce a thin page for a crawler to find (§13 Q7).
- **The six-product floor is spec 005's function, not a judgement**: `countProductsIn()` / `countProductsFor()` already exist for exactly this ("005 supplies the counts; 008 enforces the threshold in its loader", 005 §2).
- **Segment collision is impossible by test, not by luck.** The first path segment after the locale is a country slug (`poland`), a page-type segment (`flowers`, `occasions`, `send-flowers-to`, `blog`, …) or a static page. A unit test asserts that no country slug in any locale equals any `PATH_SEGMENT_KEYS` value in that locale and that no category or occasion slug collides with a sibling slug in its namespace — the `countries.ts` per-locale uniqueness refinement extended one level out.
- **The PDP pattern is reserved here and owned by spec 009**: `/{locale}/{countrySlug}/{product}/{productSlug}` (`plan/02` §4.1). 008 adds the `productPath()` builder and the `product` link id, and renders product cards as links **only** when that link id is published — until 009 ships, a card is a non-interactive tile with the same DOM shape and no `<a>` (§13 Q8, AC-12). Never a link to a non-200 (spec 004 AC-14, 007 AC-17).

**The listing, its states and the product card (`plan/04` §6, `docs/design/README.md` §Density)**

- **One listing template, six page types.** `listingView()` in `src/modules/catalog` is the single view model the page, the JSON-LD builders and the sitemap all read — 005's `priceProjection` contract and 007's `corridorView()` precedent applied again, so structured data is incapable of describing something the page does not render (`plan/02` §15).
- **The product card contract** (identical on every country-scoped listing): photo slot through spec 006's `Photo` + `resolveLoader()` at 4∶5 with `sizes` from the named `grid` slot; the product name from `product_translation` in the page locale; **one all-in price** — the default tier's `priceProjection().displayPrice` through `formatMoney`, VAT and delivery included, with the `catalog.price.inclusive` wording beside it; the honesty/provenance label of spec 006 §2.5 wherever an `ai` asset is displayed. **Absent by design:** rating, stars, review count, "bestseller"/"popular" badge, delivery badge ("Today"/"Tomorrow" — `plan/04` §6 asks for it and we have no cutoff data, spec 006 §14 A4), countdown, strike-through, "was/now", add-to-basket (spec 010), wishlist, quick-view.
- **Why the card shows a price and not a "from" price.** The card price is the price of a real, purchasable configuration — the product's `is_default` tier (005 §13 Q6) — so `price shown = price charged` holds without a qualifier (`CLAUDE.md`, `plan/07` §4, `docs/design/README.md` "no 'from' where the price shown must equal the price charged"). "From" prices appear in exactly one place: the **category tile** on the country shop root, where the tile stands for a set and `fromPrice(category, iso2)` is the lowest default-tier price inside it — also a payable number.
- **States**, drawn on the artboards (§12): *populated* (the normal grid) · *single page* / *paginated* · *sorted* · *empty* (a country whose catalogue has no deliverable product: an honest sentence and links to the corridor page and the hubs — never a fake grid, `plan/02` §5) · *no photo* (spec 006's placeholder box, never a broken image) · *stale FX* (the projection falls back to the destination currency and the page says which currency it quotes, 005 §14 A3) · *demo/guide* (every Phase 0 page: prices shown, nothing purchasable, one sentence saying so) · *live* (Phase 1: the same template, the shop-entry CTA becomes a basket path when 010 lands — no template edit).
- **Hubs show no money.** A destination-less category or occasion hub shows product *names and photos* and **no price at all**, with one sentence saying why and a destination picker — 005 §13 Q10's binding answer ("no money on destination-less hubs in Phase 0") and `docs/design/README.md`'s one licensed exception to the price-on-every-card density rule. The occasion hub additionally carries the thing that makes it worth ranking: **the date of that occasion in every published country, computed by 007's `occasionDate`/`nextOccasions`** and formatted by `formatDate` (`plan/02` §6: "the hub explains all", and "a wrong Mother's Day is worse than no page").

**Sort, filters, pagination — server-rendered, URL-driven, no client fetch**

- **Sort** is a `<form method="get">` with a labelled `<select>` and a real submit button: it works with JavaScript disabled and with the keyboard alone, and it adds **zero** client JS. Options in Phase 0: the default order, price ascending, price descending. The default order is spec 005's deterministic `topProductsForPrebuild` ordering (a founder-set `sortIndex`, tie-broken by SKU) and is **labelled for what it is** — never "bestsellers", which we cannot evidence and which `plan/07` §2.1's ranking-transparency duty would require us to describe truthfully (§13 Q3).
- **Filters**: none ship in Phase 0 (§13 Q6). The browsing affordance is the category and occasion chip row — links to *real pages*, which is what `plan/02` §7 means by "facets worth ranking get a real authored path". Facet-shaped parameters are nevertheless parsed and canonicalised through 005's `resolveFacets()` so a hand-typed or externally-linked `?colour=red` renders the base listing, is `noindex,follow`, and canonicals to the unparameterised URL.
- **Pagination** is `?page=N` (`plan/02` §7), 12 products per page, server-rendered, with `?page=1` **301** to the bare URL, a `· Page N` title suffix, self-canonical per page, and a `<nav aria-label>` with real `<a>`s. No "load more", because a load-more button is a client fetch and a page of products a crawler cannot see (`plan/02` §14). The cacheability consequence of `?page=N` is decided in §5.4 and §13 Q2.

**Indexability, canonical, hreflang (`plan/02` §7; ADR-0007) — through 007's engine, unchanged**

- Every page type here registers a `PageDescriptor` with 007's `indexability()`. **No new `noindex` branch is written anywhere in this spec**; that is the whole point of 007 §2's "specs 008–011 register their page types here instead of writing their own branch", and AC-14 asserts that the rendered robots meta, the sitemap membership and the e2e-observed header agree for every combination.
- **Phase 0: every page this spec ships is `noindex,follow` and in no sitemap** — country-scoped pages because no country is operationally live (ADR-0007, `plan/02` §7's "demo-country shop pages"), hubs and the occasions index because the indexing environment does not exist yet (007 §2's `isIndexingEnvironment()`), and `de`/`pl` additionally because their locale is not indexable.
- **What becomes indexable, and when** (§6 states the predicates in full): at the indexing flip, the **category hubs, occasion hubs and the occasions index** in indexable locales with reviewed intros (they are true pages with authored copy and real per-country dates, and `plan/09` Phase 0 AC 1 names them); the **country shop root, country categories and country occasions** only when the country is genuinely `live` (007's `corridorState(iso2) === 'live'`, i.e. an active partner exists) — Phase 1, PL first.
- Sorted, filtered and parameterised URLs are `noindex,follow` with a canonical to the base URL, always. `?page=N` ≥ 2 is self-canonical and follows the country's own indexability (`plan/02` §7).
- hreflang, canonical and alternates come from 007's `alternatesFor()` / `canonicalUrl()` / `buildMetadata()` with **no second generator**. A locale in which the entity has no authored slug has no page and therefore no alternate.

**Schema (`plan/02` §9)**

- `BreadcrumbList` on all six page types, mirroring the visible breadcrumb exactly (007's builder, extended with the new crumb shapes).
- `ItemList` on every listing and on the occasions index: `itemListElement` of `ListItem { position, url, name }` in the rendered order, one entry per rendered card, **no price, no image, no rating** inside it — an `ItemList` describes an order, not an offer.
- **No `Product`, no `Offer`, no `AggregateOffer`, no `aggregateRating`, no `review`, no `Event`, no `LocalBusiness` on any page in this spec** (§13 Q5; 007 AC-16 extended). The recommendation recorded: a `Product`/`Offer` pair may be emitted only from the page where the product is purchasable, which is the PDP once spec 010's basket exists and a country is live — until then an `Offer` would advertise a transaction the site cannot perform, which is `plan/02` §15's first manual-action risk and `plan/07` §4's unfair-practice risk in one.

**Links — satisfying 007's contract (`plan/02` §11)**

- `site-links.ts` publishes the five ids 007 reserved and left unpublished: the country **shop root**, the **country categories** (up to 6 on the corridor), the indexable **country occasions**, the **occasion hubs** and the **occasions index**. Publishing them is a data flip; the corridor's markup does not change (007 AC-7, AC-20 re-run green).
- Down and across: shop root → its categories, its occasions, its products (when 009 publishes the link), the corridor page (breadcrumb); country category → shop root, sibling categories, corridor, products; country occasion → shop root, the occasion hub, products; category/occasion hub → every country page of that entity that exists, in `collator(locale)` order, plus the occasions index; occasions index → every occasion hub. Locale home and footer gain the hubs through the existing `site-links.ts` rows — again with no markup change.
- Crawl depth from any locale home to any page in this spec is ≤3 (`plan/02` §11), asserted by BFS in the crawl test.

**Gates, docs, artboards**

- Lighthouse URL set gains one country shop root, one country category, one occasion hub and the occasions index per indexable locale; axe gains all six types in their populated and empty states; visual baselines gain the six types × two platforms; the crawl test of 007 AC-17 is extended to the new routes; `seo:validate` gains the two new sitemap children and the `ItemList` fixtures.
- `pnpm seed:check` (spec 006's gate) is **extended** rather than duplicated: category and occasion slugs must exist for all four launch locales and satisfy the `plan/02` §4 slug rules; hub intros must be 40–120 words, ≥60% token-distinct from every other hub intro in the same locale, free of the nine banned words, free of a price literal and free of any delivery-timing claim (spec 006 §14 A4). No new CI job is created.
- `docs/runbooks/shop-pages.md` (how a category becomes a page, how a country's shop goes live, how to read an existence-rule failure, what the counts in the CI step summary mean), `docs/architecture.md` §2/§3, `docs/codebase-map.md` regenerated, `messages/*.json` namespaces, and the artboard extensions of §12.

## 3. Non-goals

- **The product detail page** → spec 009. 008 reserves the URL pattern, builds `productPath()`, publishes the link id when 009 lands, and renders the card.
- **Basket, add-to-basket, checkout, quote issuing** → spec 010 (`plan/04` §8). Phase 0 cards and listings have no purchase affordance at all, which is why `Offer` is absent (§13 Q5).
- **Search and typeahead beyond spec 007's destination finder**, and `WebSite.SearchAction` with it → a later spec; the `search` link id stays unpublished and `/search` stays robots-disallowed (007 §2).
- **Facet and filter UI, "same-day only", price bands, colour landing pages** → Phase 1+ (`plan/02` §7, `plan/10` §1.1). Facet *parameters* are parsed and neutralised here so they cannot become URLs.
- **Reviews, ratings, badges, bestseller ordering, trending, "X people bought this"** → real data only, specs 022/023/027 (`plan/07` §7, `plan/09` Phase 0 AC 6).
- **Delivery badges, cutoff countdowns and date chips on a card** → they need operational data no country has; spec 009 renders them on the PDP the day 007's `ActivePartnersProvider` returns true.
- **The city listing, the flower-type hub, the colour landing page, `/{locale}/{shopCategory}` as a categories index** → Phase 1+ and, in the last case, §13 Q4: no country-less categories index ships, so `/{locale}/flowers` is a 404.
- **A client-side currency repaint island and the embedded `priceTable`** → §13 Q9: not in Phase 0; the price is rendered in the locale's default currency (005 §7) and the currency chip stays the text spec 004 shipped.
- **The database, migrations and the admin that will own these flags** → spec 002 / spec 012. This spec adds no migration and requests two spec amendments instead (§5.1).
- **Editing `CLAUDE.md`, `plan/*` or an accepted ADR.** One bounded, stated deviation is carried (§12): the `site-links.ts` config flags, on the spec 003/004/007 precedent.

## 4. User stories

- As a **buyer who has landed on the Poland guide**, "See bouquets for Poland" takes me to a grid of real bouquets with one price each that already includes VAT and delivery, and nothing on the page pretends I can order today when you tell me plainly that you are still choosing florists.
- As a **buyer searching "roses delivery"** with no country in mind, the roses hub shows me what you make and asks me where it is going, instead of quoting a price that would be wrong for every destination but one.
- As a **buyer searching "when is Mother's Day in Germany"**, the occasion hub gives me the date for every country you cover, computed rather than copied, and links me to the page for the country I care about.
- As a **buyer on `/pl/`**, the category and occasion URLs are Polish words, the product counts decline correctly ("2 bukiety / 5 bukietów"), and the prices are in złoty.
- As **Googlebot**, every listing URL is complete server-rendered HTML with one self-canonical, a reciprocal hreflang cluster, a `BreadcrumbList` and an `ItemList` that match the visible page, no `Offer` for anything I cannot buy, and no sorted or filtered variant that claims to be a separate page.
- As the **founder taking Poland live**, the shop, its categories and its occasions become indexable because a partner row appeared — no template edit, no sitemap to remember, no page that has to be written.
- As the **seo-auditor agent**, "how many listing URLs exist, and does that exceed the countries and categories that pass the floor?" is one function and one number in the CI step summary.
- As a **screen-reader user**, the grid is a list, every card is a heading plus a price, the sort control is a labelled form I can operate with the keyboard, and no image carries English alt text on a Polish page.
- As the **implementer of spec 009**, the card, the grid, the breadcrumb, the price projection and the URL builder already exist and my page is the one thing missing.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data model changes (tables, columns, migrations + rollback)

**No migration runs in this spec.** Spec 002 is unprovisioned; the persistence targets (`category`, `category_translation`, `occasion`, `occasion_country`, `product_category`, `product_occasion`, `country_price`) are already specified there and are **not restated**. Phase 0 reads committed data through spec 005's `CatalogueProvider` / `PriceProvider` seam and spec 006's copy files, and `pnpm check:no-db` covers every file this spec adds.

| Phase 0 (this spec) | Later owner | Contract that prevents drift |
|---|---|---|
| Category and occasion **slugs and intros** per locale in spec 006's `seed/data/copy/{locale}/{categories,occasions}.json` | spec 002's `category_translation` / `occasion_translation`; spec 012's admin editor | spec 006's `SeedCopySchema` + `to*TranslationRow()` projections, pinned by the existing column-list tests; `seed:check` gains the slug and intro rules (AC-2) |
| `listingView()` and the page descriptors | nothing — a pure projection | The view model is the only input to the page, the JSON-LD and the sitemap row (AC-16) |
| `site-links.ts` rows for the five 007 targets + `product` | spec 002's `feature_flag`, spec 012's admin | One predicate (`isPublished`), unchanged since spec 004 |

**Two amendment requests**, listed as founder decisions rather than applied here (the mechanism 005 §13 Q8 and 007 §5.1 used):

1. **To spec 005 §5.2** — `src/modules/catalog` gains `slugs.ts`: `slugFor(kind, key, locale)` and `resolveSlug(locale, kind, slug)` for `kind ∈ category | occasion | product`, plus `hasSlug(kind, key, locale)`. Neither 005 nor 006 exposes a slug→key resolver today, and a route cannot be built without one. It is additive, it lives **inside** the existing module (no new boundary, `plan/01` §5), and it reads the data 006 already owns. Rollback: delete the file.
2. **To spec 006 §2.3** — `seed:check` gains the four copy rules of §2 (slug present for all launch locales; slug rules; intro length; intro distinctness and honesty). Additive to an existing gate; rollback is reverting the rules.

**Rollback of this spec** is `git revert` of its task commits plus setting the `site-links.ts` rows back to `published: false`: no state, no migration, no external resource. Because no listing URL is announced before the indexing flip (§12), a revert cannot orphan an indexed URL.

### 5.2 API / server actions / jobs / events (zod schemas named)

No server action, no job, no event, no cookie read, no cookie write, and no route handler that accepts input. The one form on the page is a `GET` form whose parameters are parsed, canonicalised and then either honoured (sort, page) or neutralised (everything else).

Zod schemas, parsed at module load (copy data) or at the route boundary (`plan/12` §2):

- **`ListingParamsSchema`** — the route params: locale, optional country slug, page-type segment, entity slug. An unknown locale, a segment that belongs to another locale, an unknown or non-existent slug, an uppercase or trailing-slash variant → `notFound()`. Never a redirect (ADR-0006), never a case-fixing rewrite.
- **`ListingSearchParamsSchema`** — `{ page?: int ≥ 1, sort?: 'default' | 'price-asc' | 'price-desc' }`, **strict**: every other parameter is dropped into 005's `resolveFacets()` and contributes only `indexable: false`. `page = 1` produces a 301 to the bare URL; a page beyond the last is a 404, not an empty grid.
- **`ListingViewSchema`** — `{ pageType, locale, country?, entity?, h1, intro?, breadcrumb[], items: ProductCardView[], tiles: CategoryTileView[], occasionDates?: {iso2, date}[], total, page, pageCount, sort, links, indexability }`. The single view model.
- **`ProductCardViewSchema`** — `{ productId, name, href?, photo: { assetId | placeholder, alt, slot }, price: Money, priceLabelKey, provenance }`. There is **no** `badge`, `rating`, `reviewCount`, `oldPrice` or `ctaAddToBasket` field: the honesty rules of §8 are enforced by the type, not by review.
- **`CategoryTileViewSchema`** — `{ key, name, href, count, fromPrice?: Money }`.

Behavioural contracts worth pinning:

- `listingView()` is the **only** source for the page, the JSON-LD builders and the sitemap row (007's `corridorView()` rule, extended).
- `indexability()` is spec 007's, called and never reimplemented; `listingExists()` is pure and synchronous over the providers, so `generateStaticParams`, the sitemap builder, the link renderers and the e2e crawl get the same answer.
- Every price in a `ProductCardView` comes from `priceProjection()`; no component receives a number and no component calls `Intl` (`fo/no-adhoc-intl`, `fo/no-float-money`).
- A block whose data is missing renders **nothing** — no heading, no placeholder, no "coming soon" (spec 004 §5.3, 007 §5.2).

### 5.4 Rendering & caching (SSG/ISR/SSR, tags invalidated, per plan/01 §3)

- **Server components only. Zero new client islands and zero client-side data fetches** (007 AC-24, extended): the grid, the toolbar, the pagination, the occasion dates and the price all render with JavaScript disabled.
- **The bare URL of every page type is prebuilt** by `generateStaticParams` over the existence set with `dynamicParams = false`, revalidated at **3600 s** with tags `catalog:{iso2}` (spec 005 `cacheTagsFor`), `catalog:{iso2}:{locale}` and `sitemap` — `plan/01` §3's "category/collection 1 h + tag `catalog:{country}`" verbatim, through `src/lib/cache.ts` as the only invalidation seam.
- **Parameterised requests** (`?page=N`, `?sort=…`) are the one place `plan/02` §7 and Next's static/dynamic split disagree: reading `searchParams` makes the route dynamic. Default resolution (§13 Q2): the listing routes are **rendered on the server per request and cached at the edge by full URL** — `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400` — with every data read wrapped in the same tag-keyed cache, so the per-request work is a projection over already-cached data. Under ADR-0018 (Railway, **one replica**, behind Cloudflare) this is *more* correct than per-replica ISR, and invalidation is `revalidateTag` + the Cloudflare URL purge that ADR-0018 already requires `lib/cache.ts` to perform. The alternative — pagination in the path — is recorded in §13 Q2 because it would contradict `plan/02` §7.
- **No response varies by request header and no cached response sets or reads a cookie** (`plan/02` §14; spec 001 AC-15, 003 AC-12, 004 §5.4, 007 AC-23): no `Vary`, no `Set-Cookie`, byte-identical bodies with and without `fo_locale`, `fo_currency` and `fo_consent`. This is why the currency menu cannot be honoured server-side and why §13 Q9 defers the repaint island.
- **Images**: exactly one `priority` LCP candidate per page — the **first product photo** — with its `<link rel="preload" as="image" imagesrcset imagesizes>` emitted from the same manifest lookup that produced the `srcset` (spec 006 §2.5, AC-19). Everything below is `loading="lazy" decoding="async"`. Page size 12 keeps initial image transfer inside spec 006's ≤204 800 B per-page budget with ≤6 eager tiles.
- **Script budget unchanged**: ≤131 072 B Brotli first-load on every URL, no route regressing >5 KB against the committed baseline (spec 004 §14 A1).

## 6. SEO considerations (mandatory)

This is the page type most capable of destroying the domain's quality signal, so this section is a contract.

- **Indexability — one engine, six descriptors.** All through 007's `indexability()`; this spec writes no `noindex` branch.
  - *Country shop root / country category / country occasion*: `index,follow` iff the page **exists** (§2) **and** `corridorState(iso2) === 'live'` (country `status === 'live'` **and** an active partner — i.e. genuinely operational, not a registry label) **and** the product count ≥ 6 **and** the locale's catalogue copy for the rendered entities is `reviewed` **and** `isLocaleIndexable(locale)` **and** `isIndexingEnvironment()`. In Phase 0 the partner predicate is false for every country, so **every one of these pages is `noindex,follow` and in no sitemap** — ADR-0007 and `plan/02` §7's "demo-country shop pages" satisfied exactly, with no per-country code branch.
  - *Category hub / occasion hub / occasions index*: `index,follow` iff the page exists **and** its authored intro is `reviewed: true` **and** it links to ≥1 existing page **and** `isLocaleIndexable(locale)` **and** `isIndexingEnvironment()`. These are the page types `plan/09` Phase 0 AC 1 expects in the indexed set, and they are honest before any country is live because their content is authored copy plus computed dates, not a product grid pretending to be a shop.
  - *Any URL carrying a sort or facet parameter*: `noindex,follow`, always, canonical to the base. *`?page=N ≥ 2`*: self-canonical, robots directive inherited from the base page (`plan/02` §7).
- **Canonical.** Self-canonical, absolute, lowercase, no trailing slash, parameter-free except the honoured `page`, from 007's `canonicalUrl()`. No cross-locale canonical is expressible. Every other URL form 404s or 301s to the canonical form (`?page=1`).
- **hreflang set.** 007's `alternatesFor()` in `<head>` and in the sitemap, reciprocal, complete over indexable locales, `x-default → /en`, `en-150` never emitted, and **no alternate for a locale in which the entity has no authored slug** — which is the honest answer and the reason a hub's cluster may be smaller than four.
- **URL pattern and localised slugs.** `plan/02` §4.1 verbatim (§2's table). Country in every shop URL (`plan/02` §4.2 — price, currency, VAT wording and availability all depend on the destination); country-less hubs for category and occasion. Slugs are ASCII, lowercase, hyphenated, unique per locale per namespace, **human-authored, never machine-drafted** (`plan/02` §12, spec 003 §6) and gated by the extended `seed:check`. No price, no facet value, no sort key and no session identifier ever appears in a path.
- **Schema types.** `BreadcrumbList` (all six) and `ItemList` (listings + occasions index). Forbidden and asserted absent on every page in this spec: `Product`, `Offer`, `AggregateOffer`, `aggregateRating`, `review`, `Event`, `LocalBusiness`, `FloristShop` (§13 Q5; `plan/02` §9, §15; 007 AC-16 extended to the new routes). The `ItemList` order equals the rendered order, so the markup cannot describe a ranking the page does not show.
- **Sitemap membership.** Two new children per locale, added to the per-locale index **only when non-empty** (007's "an absent child is correct; an empty one is noise"): `/sitemaps/{loc}/categories.xml` (category hubs + country shop roots + indexable country categories) and `/sitemaps/{loc}/occasions.xml` (occasion hubs + the occasions index + indexable country occasions). `<lastmod>` is the real max `updatedAt` across the catalogue entity, its copy and its price data — never "now". Nothing `noindex` can appear because membership and the robots directive come from one predicate. In Phase 0 both children are empty and therefore absent.
- **Internal links in/out.** In: corridor page (007's reserved link ids, now published), locale home and footer (hubs), sibling categories, the occasion hubs. Out: as §2. Crawl depth from any locale home ≤3 (`plan/02` §11). **Zero links to a non-200 URL**, extended to the product link that does not exist until 009 ships (AC-12).
- **Thin content and duplication — the real risks.**
  (a) *Listing inflation*: the existence floor (≥6 products) is applied at `generateStaticParams`, so a thin listing has no URL to be thin at; the URL count is printed per locale in CI and compared against the floor by `seo-auditor`.
  (b) *Near-identical listings across countries*: the same twelve bouquets at seven country URLs. Mitigated exactly as `plan/02` §4.2 prescribes — the page carries a server-rendered country block (the authored corridor link, the country's own occasion dates, its VAT wording, its currency, its price column), country pages are indexable only for live countries so N stays 1 for a long time, and GSC's "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical" at >10% is the monitored trigger.
  (c) *Hub ↔ country-page duplication*: the hub carries what the country page cannot (dates for every country, a destination choice) and carries **no prices**, which is the same distinction `plan/02` §6 draws for occasions.
  (d) *Parameter duplication*: sort and facet URLs are `noindex` + canonicalised, never in a sitemap, never linked with a crawlable `<a>` (the toolbar is a `GET` form, so the sorted URL is produced by a submission, not by a link a crawler follows); `robots.txt` gains the facet parameter shapes to the existing `plan/02` §7 disallow list but **does not** block the sorted URLs, so their `noindex` can be seen.
  (e) *Machine-translated catalogue copy indexed*: structurally impossible — an unreviewed `de`/`pl` translation fails `isProductIndexable` (005 §6) and `isLocaleIndexable` is false for both locales.
- **CWV budget impact.** Zero new client JS; the LCP element moves from text to the **first product photo**, preloaded and sized from spec 006's manifest, which is the one meaningful change and the reason page size is 12 and eager tiles ≤6. Budgets are spec 004's, unchanged: performance/accessibility/best-practices ≥0.95, LCP <2.0 s, CLS <0.05, script ≤131 072 B Brotli, image transfer ≤204 800 B. `categories:seo` ≥0.95 is asserted only on indexable URLs (007 AC-25's rule inherited) — which in Phase 0 is none of these, recorded with its reason in `lighthouserc.json`.
- **Crawl efficiency.** No calendar URLs, no infinite parameter space (the search-param schema is strict), no redirect chains (one 301: `?page=1`), no soft-404 (a page past the last is a real 404), and no URL a crawler can reach that `indexability()` has not ruled on.

## 7. i18n considerations (mandatory)

- **New message keys / namespaces.** `shop.*` (the shop-root headings, the category-tile heading, the "you cannot order yet" demo sentence, the empty-state sentence, the ranking-disclosure sentence), `listing.*` (sort labels and the sort form's legend, pagination labels, the result-count line), `categoryHub.*` and `occasionHub.*` (h1 patterns, the "prices depend on where it is going" sentence, the destination-picker heading, the date-table caption), `occasionsIndex.*`, plus `a11y.*` additions (grid list label, pagination nav label, sort form label). All ICU, namespaced, typed, zero literals. `en-gb` overrides only where British wording differs. `de`/`pl` chrome re-drafted by `pnpm i18n:draft` and therefore unreviewed, which correctly keeps `isLocaleIndexable('de'|'pl')` false.
- **Slugs and intros are content, not chrome, and are never machine-drafted.** A URL is not a translation artefact (`countries.ts`'s own note; spec 003 §6; `plan/02` §12): the ~31 category and occasion slugs are authored by the founder for all four launch locales in spec 006's copy files, and the hub intros are authored `en` first with `de`/`pl` queued for the `plan/13` B12 native reviewers. A locale with no authored slug simply has no page there — the same honest answer 007 gives for corridor copy (§13 Q10).
- **Plurals are the acid test.** The product-count line ("5 bouquets", "5 bukietów") and the tile counts are ICU plurals with Polish few/many/other cases as required unit-test cases (`plan/03` §7); the count is never concatenated and never rendered as "5 product(s)".
- **Locale formatting.** Money through `formatMoney` only (005's rule; no `Intl` in a component); occasion dates through `formatDate` with month name and weekday (`plan/03` §7) — **no date literal in any component or message string**; category and occasion lists ordered by `collator(locale)` so Polish `ł` and German umlauts sort correctly; multi-item phrases via `Intl.ListFormat`; the VAT-and-delivery wording is the authored per-locale formula `catalog.price.inclusive` (005 §7), not a translation.
- **Currency.** The rendered currency is the **locale's default** (`/en` EUR, `/en-gb` GBP, `/de` EUR, `/pl` PLN), because cached HTML must be byte-identical for every visitor (`plan/02` §3, 005 §7). The destination country's own currency appears only in the stale-FX fallback, and the page says which currency it is quoting (005 §14 A3). The `fo_currency` cookie is **not** read on these pages in Phase 0 (§13 Q9).
- **RTL.** No physical CSS anywhere; the grid, the toolbar and the pagination are logical-property layouts; the price/name pair inside a card is wrapped in `<bdi>` (spec 003's rule, first applicable here); `/ar-XB` visual baselines gain the country shop root and the category hub.
- **Address/phone formats.** Not rendered here (no form beyond the `GET` sort). Unchanged.
- **Translation review plan and `noindex` gating.** Per-page: the hub intro's `reviewed` flag and the catalogue copy's `reviewed` flag. Per-locale: `isLocaleIndexable()`. Both are inputs to the one `indexability()` decision, so an authored-but-unreviewed page exists, renders and is `noindex,follow` until a native reviewer signs it. Category, occasion and tier vocabulary is added to `content/i18n/glossary.{locale}.md` in the same PR, because the reviewers translate against it.

## 8. Compliance considerations (mandatory)

- **Data flows added → RoPA: none.** These pages render public catalogue content, accept no personal data, set no cookie, read no cookie and add no processor. The only input is a `GET` form carrying a sort key and a page number — no free text, no identifier, nothing that could be personal data in a URL or a log (`CLAUDE.md`: no PII in logs, URLs, analytics). `docs/compliance/ropa.md` gains an explicit "no new processing" note for 008, as 006 and 007 did, so the absence is recorded rather than assumed.
- **Price display (`plan/07` §4, `CLAUDE.md`) — the core of this section.** Every price on every country-scoped surface is one all-in amount from 005's `priceProjection()`: integer minor units, VAT included, delivery included, equal to the amount that will be charged, rendered by `formatMoney` with the locale's VAT-and-delivery formula beside it. The card price is a **payable configuration** (the default tier), not an indicative minimum, which is why it carries no "from". The single "from" — the category tile — is the lowest payable default-tier price inside that tile, and it is labelled as a "from" price. Destination-less hubs show **no money at all** with a one-sentence explanation (005 §13 Q10), because a cross-country minimum converted at today's rate is a price no configuration matches — the precise shape the Price Indication Directive and the DMCC drip-pricing ban exist to prevent. No "was/now", no strike-through, no countdown, no urgency claim anywhere (the 30-day-lowest figure exists in 005 and no reduction UI ships).
- **Ranking transparency (Omnibus Art. 6a / CPCs; UK DMCC).** The default order is a founder-set curation index, and the page says so in one sentence in the toolbar. It is **not** described as "bestsellers", "popular" or "recommended for you": we have no sales, no personalisation and no paid placement, and a listing that claimed any of the three would be an unfair commercial practice with a true, cheap alternative available (§13 Q3). No paid placement exists to disclose. No ordering depends on anything about the visitor.
- **No purchase affordance, therefore no pre-contractual offer.** Phase 0 listings carry no add-to-basket, no "buy", no pre-ticked anything (CRD Art. 22 is unbreakable here because there is nothing to tick) and form no contract. The demo state says plainly that ordering is not open yet. The withdrawal-right and substitution disclosures belong to the PDP and checkout (009/010) and are not pre-empted here.
- **Reviews and trust (`plan/07` §7, `plan/09` Phase 0 AC 6).** Zero reviews, ratings, stars, review counts, testimonials, Trustpilot marks, florist names, florist photos, florist counts, delivery photos and "X sold" counters on any page or in any JSON-LD this spec renders — asserted over all six page types in all four locales, and unexpressible in `ProductCardViewSchema` (§5.2).
- **No delivery-timing claim.** No "Today"/"Tomorrow" badge, no cutoff, no countdown, no same-day wording, because no country has operational data and spec 006 §14 A4 already fixed the rule for seed copy. The delivery promise tile that `docs/design/README.md` §Density asks to repeat above every grid renders in its **facts-unknown** form (007's block, reused) until a country is live.
- **Geo-blocking (EU 2018/302, ADR-0006).** Every listing URL is reachable from anywhere, identical for everyone, never redirected and never varied by IP or by buyer country. Prices are keyed on the destination only (005 AC-18). A destination we do not serve gets an honest "not yet", which Art. 3 permits.
- **Accessibility (EAA / WCAG 2.1 AA).** List semantics for the grid; one `<h1>`; card heading + adjacent price (no information by position or colour alone); labelled sort form operable with the keyboard alone and functional with JS disabled; labelled pagination `<nav>` with `aria-current`; alt text from spec 006's per-locale data, never generated, and no `<img>` at all when the locale has no alt (spec 006 AC-18); focus-visible on every card, chip and control; 200% reflow; axe zero serious/critical with no exception list on all six types in populated and empty states, plus a manual VoiceOver pass on the country shop root recorded in the runbook.
- **Logs / PII.** No new request-scoped field. The sort key and page number may appear in a log line; nothing else from the request does, and neither can identify a person. Structured data contains no personal data and no fabricated entity.
- **Security.** No new dependency, no new origin, no inline script, no client island. `img-src` already allows the static `/media/` variants (spec 006); ADR-0016's allowlist is unchanged.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Existence, routing, slugs**

- **AC-1** For each launch locale, each of the six page types returns 200 for exactly the URL set its §2 existence rule defines and **404** for everything else: an unknown slug, a category below the floor, an occasion not observed in that country, an unpublished country, a segment belonging to another locale, an uppercase variant, a trailing slash, an unknown locale. No redirect, no soft-404, no empty grid standing in for a 404.
- **AC-2** `pnpm seed:check` fails, naming file, entity and rule, on each of: a category or occasion with no slug in a launch locale; a slug that is not lowercase ASCII-hyphen, or duplicated inside its namespace and locale, or equal to a `PATH_SEGMENT_KEYS` value or a country slug in that locale; a hub intro outside 40–120 words; a hub intro <60% token-distinct from another hub intro in the same locale; a banned word, a price literal or a delivery-timing claim in an intro. It passes on the committed corpus.
- **AC-3** `generateStaticParams` emits exactly the existence set for each of the six routes and `dynamicParams === false`; the built route manifest contains no listing URL outside it; the per-locale counts are printed to the CI step summary.
- **AC-4** A unit test proves no collision is possible: no country slug equals any path segment in its locale, no category slug collides with another category slug or with an occasion slug in the same locale, and `resolveSlug()` is the inverse of `slugFor()` over the whole corpus in all four locales.
- **AC-5** Data-flip proof: raising a fixture country's product coverage so a category crosses the floor adds its URL, its links from the shop root and the corridor, and (when the country is live) its sitemap row and hreflang entry — with **no change under `src/app/`** and no template edit.

**The listing, the card, the states**

- **AC-6** Every country-scoped card renders exactly: photo (or spec 006's placeholder), name, one all-in price via `formatMoney` with the `catalog.price.inclusive` label, and the provenance note where an `ai` asset is shown. ## 13. Open questions

Each carries the default this spec is written against; the spec stays `draft` until the founder rules.

- **Q1 — May a country shop exist where no corridor guide has been written?** Spec 007 gives a country a *corridor* page only where a human has written its guide in that locale, so `de` and `pl` have no corridor pages in Phase 0. **Default: yes, the shop is independent** — a listing's content is data (products, prices, occasion dates), not authored prose, so the shop root, categories and occasions exist in every launch locale where the country is published and the slugs are authored, and the breadcrumb's country crumb links to the corridor where it exists and is plain text where it does not (the rule the finder and the destinations grid already use). Alternative: gate the shop on the corridor, which is tidier but leaves `/de/` and `/pl/` with no shop at all for the 16 October demo.
- **Q2 — Pagination: `?page=N` (and therefore dynamic rendering), or pagination in the path (and therefore pure ISR)?** `plan/02` §7 fixes `?page=N`; reading a query parameter makes a Next route dynamic. **Default: keep `?page=N`** and render the listing routes on the server behind an edge cache keyed on the full URL (`s-maxage=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400`) with tag-keyed data caching underneath — which, under ADR-0018's single Railway replica behind Cloudflare, is more predictable than per-replica ISR and uses the purge path ADR-0018 already requires. Alternative: `/…/page/2` path segments, pure ISR everywhere, and an amendment to `plan/02` §7. A third option — "load more" — is rejected outright: it is a client fetch and a page of products a crawler cannot see.
- **Q3 — What is the default product order called?** `plan/04` §6 says "bestsellers for the destination"; we have no sales. **Default: a founder-set curation order, labelled plainly** (e.g. "Our order") with a one-sentence disclosure of what determines it, and only price-ascending and price-descending beside it. Calling it "bestsellers", "popular" or "recommended" before spec 022/023 have data would be an unfair commercial practice with a free honest alternative available. Alternative: order by price ascending by default and drop the concept — simpler, and it makes the cheapest bouquet the face of every page.
- **Q4 — Does the destination-less category hub list countries or products?** **Default: both, in that order — countries first.** The hub's job is to get the destination chosen (`plan/04` §4); it shows the destinations where that category exists, then the products **without prices** and with one sentence saying the price depends on where it is going (005 §13 Q10, binding). Also decided here: **no country-less categories index** ships (`/{locale}/flowers` is a 404), because `plan/05` has no such row and the hubs are reached from the home, the footer, the corridor and the shop roots. Alternative: a categories index page — one more template and one more thin-content surface for no query we can name.
- **Q5 — When may a `Product`/`Offer` pair be emitted?** **Default: not in this spec, and not until a product is genuinely purchasable** — a live country and spec 010's basket. `plan/02` §9 already forbids an `Offer` for a country that is not live; the stronger rule recorded here is that an `Offer` on a site with no purchase path advertises a transaction we cannot perform. Listings emit `ItemList` and `BreadcrumbList` only, and spec 009's PDP inherits the same rule until 010 ships. Alternative: emit `Product` without an `Offer` on the PDP for rich-result eligibility — worth revisiting in spec 009, not here.
- **Q6 — Do filters ship in Phase 0?** **Default: no.** Sort only; browsing happens through category and occasion chips that are links to real pages (`plan/02` §7's "facets worth ranking get a real authored path"). Facet-shaped parameters are still parsed, neutralised, `noindex`-ed and canonicalised so an external link cannot create a URL. Cost: a buyer cannot narrow by colour in the demo. Alternative: ship colour and price-band filters, which adds a task, a parameter space and a crawl-budget problem for 84 products.
- **Q7 — What is the product-count floor, and is it existence or indexability?** **Default: 6, applied to existence** for country categories and country occasions (`plan/02` §4.1's "≥6" and §6's "pages that fail the rule are not created"), and 1 for the shop root. A category with five products has no URL and its products are reached from the shop root. Alternative: existence at 3 and indexability at 6 — more pages for the demo, and a set of `noindex` pages whose only reason to exist is that we built the template.
- **Q8 — What does a product card do between 008 and 009?** **Default: nothing — it is a tile, not a link**, until spec 009 publishes the product link id; the flip is a data change with no markup change (spec 004 AC-11's proof, applied again). Since 008 and 009 land in the same week the window is short. Alternative: sequence 009's route first and have 008 land with working links — which reverses the roadmap's order and blocks the shop on the PDP.
- **Q9 — Does the currency menu do anything on a listing page in Phase 0?** **Default: no.** Prices render in the locale's default currency (005 §7's rule that keeps cached HTML byte-identical), the currency chip stays the text spec 004 shipped, and neither the `fo_currency` cookie nor the embedded `priceTable` is used here. The repaint island and the embedded table belong to the page where the currency decides a payment, which is the PDP and checkout. Cost: a `/de/` buyer sending to Poland sees euros, not złoty, which is the correct default anyway.
- **Q10 — Who authors the ~31 category and occasion slugs in `de` and `pl`, and when?** **Default: the founder, before task 2**, because a URL is never machine-drafted (`plan/02` §12; the same rule `countries.ts` records for country slugs) and because an unauthored slug means no page in that locale. Hub *intros* follow the corridor pattern: `en` authored now, `de`/`pl` queued for the `plan/13` B12 native reviewers and `noindex` until reviewed. Alternative: ship `de`/`pl` with English slugs — rejected; a German URL reading `/de/blumen/roses` is the kind of half-translated page `plan/02` §12 exists to prevent.
